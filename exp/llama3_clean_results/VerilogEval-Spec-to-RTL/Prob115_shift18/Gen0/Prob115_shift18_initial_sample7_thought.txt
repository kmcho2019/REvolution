The TopModule will implement a 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load. To achieve this, we need to consider the load, enable, and amount signals to perform the desired operation. The module will use an always block to synchronize the operations with the clock. If the load signal is high, the module will load the data into the shift register. If the enable signal is high, the module will perform the shift operation based on the amount signal. The amount signal will determine the direction and the number of positions to shift. We will use Verilog's built-in shift operators to perform the left and right shifts. For arithmetic right shift, we will use the >> operator with a sign extension to maintain the sign bit.
