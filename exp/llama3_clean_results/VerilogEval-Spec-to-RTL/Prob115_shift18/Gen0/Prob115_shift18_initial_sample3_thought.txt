The TopModule can be designed using a 64-bit register and a multiplexer to handle the load and shift operations. We will use an always block to update the register on every clock cycle based on the load, ena, and amount signals. We will use case statements to determine the shift direction and amount. For the arithmetic right shift, we will use a combination of shifting and filling the most significant bits with the sign bit (the most significant bit of the register). For the left shift, we will use a combination of shifting and filling the least significant bits with zeros.
