A novel approach to implementing the 64-bit arithmetic shift register is to utilize a combination of lookup tables (LUTs) and multiplexers to minimize the complexity of the shift logic. The idea is to divide the 64-bit data into smaller segments, perform the shift operation on each segment, and then reassemble the shifted data. This approach can potentially reduce the area usage and improve the performance of the shift register. Additionally, the use of LUTs can simplify the implementation of the arithmetic right shift operation.
