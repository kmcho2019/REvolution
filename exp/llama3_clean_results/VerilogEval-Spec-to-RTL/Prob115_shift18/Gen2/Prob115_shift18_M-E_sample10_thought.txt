The novel architectural idea is to utilize a single, unified shift operation that can handle all specified shift directions and amounts by employing a barrel shifter architecture. The barrel shifter will be designed to efficiently perform both left and right shifts by 1 or 8 bits, leveraging the properties of bitwise operations to minimize the complexity of the design. This approach simplifies the control logic and reduces the number of conditional statements, potentially improving the overall performance and area efficiency of the design. The design will also incorporate a loadable shift register, enabling it to load data directly from the input when the load signal is active.
