To implement a 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we can utilize a novel architectural approach based on a pipeline-based design. This concept involves dividing the shift operation into multiple stages, each handling a specific aspect of the shift, such as direction, amount, and data manipulation. By breaking down the operation into smaller, more manageable parts, we can improve performance, reduce power consumption, and optimize area usage. The pipeline stages will be designed to work in tandem, ensuring that the output of one stage is seamlessly passed to the next, allowing for efficient and accurate shift operations.
