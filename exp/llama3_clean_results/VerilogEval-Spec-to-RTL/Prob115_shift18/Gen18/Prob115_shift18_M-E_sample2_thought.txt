The core concept involves breaking down the shift operation into smaller, more manageable parts, and then recombining the results. This approach enables efficient and flexible shift operations. We will employ a combination of 8-bit shifters and multiplexers to achieve the desired functionality. Additionally, we will incorporate a control unit to manage the shift amount and direction, ensuring that the correct operation is performed based on the input signals. This modular design enables better optimization for performance, power, and area.
