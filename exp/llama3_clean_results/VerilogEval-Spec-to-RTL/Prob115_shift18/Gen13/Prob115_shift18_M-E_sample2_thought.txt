To implement a 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load, we can leverage a novel architectural idea based on a hybrid approach combining a multiplexer-based barrel shifter with a variable-length shift control unit. The core concept involves using a multiplexer array to select the appropriate bits to shift in or out, depending on the shift direction and amount. The control unit generates the necessary shift enable signals based on the input 'amount' and 'ena' signals. This approach allows for efficient and flexible shift operations, minimizing the number of combinational logic levels and reducing the area usage. By utilizing a multiplexer-based design, we can also simplify the logic for handling the different shift amounts and directions, making it easier to optimize for performance, power, and area.
