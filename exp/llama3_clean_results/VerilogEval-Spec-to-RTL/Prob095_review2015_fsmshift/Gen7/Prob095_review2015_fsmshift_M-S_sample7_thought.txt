To simplify the existing solution, we can utilize a single counter variable that is set to 4 upon reset, and then decrements every clock cycle until it reaches 0. Once it reaches 0, it will remain 0 until the next reset. We can directly assign the `shift_ena` signal based on whether the counter is greater than 0. This approach eliminates unnecessary complexity and achieves the desired functionality of enabling the shift register for exactly 4 clock cycles upon reset.
