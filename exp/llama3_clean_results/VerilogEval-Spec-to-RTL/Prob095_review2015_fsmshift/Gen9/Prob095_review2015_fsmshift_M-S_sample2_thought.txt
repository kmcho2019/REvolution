The counter will be initialized to 0 when the module is reset and will be incremented every clock cycle when the shift_ena output is asserted. The shift_ena output will be asserted when the counter is less than 4 and will be deasserted otherwise. This approach eliminates the need for multiple states and reduces the complexity of the code.
