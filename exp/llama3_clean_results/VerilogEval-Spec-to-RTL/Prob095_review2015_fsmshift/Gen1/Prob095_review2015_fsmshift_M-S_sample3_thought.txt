To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for a separate state machine and counter. Instead, we can use a single counter that is reset when the reset signal is asserted. The shift_ena signal can be asserted when the counter is greater than 0 and the reset signal is asserted, or when the counter is less than 4 and the reset signal was asserted in the previous clock cycle. This way, the shift_ena signal will be asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles after the reset signal is asserted.
