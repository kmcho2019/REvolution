To address the issue with the previous implementation, we can employ a finite state machine (FSM) approach to control the shift register. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFT. When the reset is asserted, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT state and stays there for exactly 4 clock cycles. During the SHIFT state, the shift_ena signal is asserted. After the 4 clock cycles, the FSM transitions back to the IDLE state and keeps the shift_ena signal deasserted. This approach ensures that the shift register is enabled for exactly 4 clock cycles after a reset and remains disabled otherwise.
