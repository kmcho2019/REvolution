To implement a module named TopModule that correctly enables a shift register for exactly 4 clock cycles after a reset, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with a more straightforward approach. This approach involves defining a state that counts down from 4 after a reset is asserted, enabling the shift register during this countdown. Once the countdown reaches 0, the shift register is disabled until the next reset. This solution simplifies the logic and directly achieves the desired behavior without needing a separate flag to manage the enablement of the shift register. The states can be as follows: IDLE (waiting for reset), COUNTDOWN (enabling shift register and counting down from 4), and DISABLED (shift register disabled after countdown reaches 0). The FSM transitions between these states based on the reset signal and the countdown value.
