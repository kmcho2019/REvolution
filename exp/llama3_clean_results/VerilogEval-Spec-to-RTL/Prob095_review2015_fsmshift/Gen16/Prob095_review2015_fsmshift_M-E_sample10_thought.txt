To address the issues in the existing solution, we will introduce a novel architectural idea that utilizes a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. The FSM will transition from IDLE to SHIFT when the reset signal is asserted, and then remain in the SHIFT state for exactly 4 clock cycles. During this time, the shift_ena signal will be asserted, enabling the shift register. After 4 clock cycles, the FSM will transition back to the IDLE state, where it will remain until the next reset signal is asserted. This approach ensures that the shift_ena signal is asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles after each reset, meeting the requirements. We will also utilize a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state.
