A simpler approach to solving this problem is to use a single counter that counts down from 3 (since the counter will be decremented before the first cycle of shift_ena being high) when the reset signal is asserted. This counter directly controls the shift_ena signal, eliminating the need for an additional state variable. When the counter reaches 0, it is not decremented further, and shift_ena remains low. This design minimizes the logic required and maintains the desired functionality.
