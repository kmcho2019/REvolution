To create a novel solution, we can use a simple counter-based approach without an FSM. The idea is to have a 2-bit counter that counts down from 3 to 0 when the reset signal is asserted. The `shift_ena` signal will be asserted as long as the counter is greater than 0. On every positive edge of the clock, if the reset is asserted, the counter is set to 3, and on subsequent clocks, it decrements until it reaches 0. If reset is not asserted and the counter is 0, `shift_ena` remains 0 until reset is asserted again.
