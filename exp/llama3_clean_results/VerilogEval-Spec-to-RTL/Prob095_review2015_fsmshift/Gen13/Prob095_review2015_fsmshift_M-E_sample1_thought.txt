We can implement a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: idle and shifting. The FSM starts in the idle state. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM transitions to the shifting state. In the shifting state, we use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles for which the shift register should be enabled. After 4 clock cycles, the FSM transitions back to the idle state and remains there until the reset signal is asserted again. This approach ensures that the shift register is enabled for exactly 4 clock cycles after the reset signal is asserted, and then remains disabled until the next reset. The use of an FSM allows for a clear and organized implementation of the required behavior.
