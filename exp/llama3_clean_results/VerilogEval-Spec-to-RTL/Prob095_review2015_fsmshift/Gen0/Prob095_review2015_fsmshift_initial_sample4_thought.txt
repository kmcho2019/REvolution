To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. The FSM will be in the IDLE state initially. When the reset signal is high, the FSM will transition to the SHIFT_ENABLE state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After 4 cycles, the FSM will go back to the IDLE state and deassert shift_ena. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles. The reset signal will be checked on every positive edge of the clock, and the FSM will reset synchronously.
