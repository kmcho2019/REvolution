To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. The FSM will start in the IDLE state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles when reset is asserted. After the 4 cycles, it will transition to the IDLE state and deassert shift_ena until the next reset. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles shift_ena has been asserted. The FSM will be implemented using a always_comb and always_ff block in Verilog.
