To solve this problem, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: an idle state and a shifting state. When the FSM is reset, it will transition into the shifting state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4th cycle, it will transition back to the idle state and de-assert shift_ena. The FSM will remain in the idle state until it is reset again.
