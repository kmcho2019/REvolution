To solve this problem, I propose a novel architectural idea using a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. The FSM will be in the IDLE state initially and will transition to the SHIFT state when the reset signal is asserted. In the SHIFT state, the FSM will assert the shift_ena signal for exactly 4 clock cycles and then return to the IDLE state. This approach ensures that the shift_ena signal is only asserted when the FSM is in the SHIFT state and for the required number of clock cycles. The FSM will be reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is asserted, allowing it to restart the process.
