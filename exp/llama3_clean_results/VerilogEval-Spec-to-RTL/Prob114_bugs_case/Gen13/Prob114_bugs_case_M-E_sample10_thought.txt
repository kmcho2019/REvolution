A novel architectural idea for the TopModule involves utilizing a parameterized array to store the scancodes and their corresponding output values. This approach eliminates the need for nested case statements, potentially reducing the complexity and area usage of the design. By using a for loop to iterate through the array, the module can check each scancode against the input code and update the output signals accordingly. This method also allows for easy expansion or modification of the scancode table without altering the underlying logic. Additionally, this approach can be more efficient in terms of area and power consumption, as it avoids the synthesis of large case statements and instead utilizes a more regular and predictable structure.
