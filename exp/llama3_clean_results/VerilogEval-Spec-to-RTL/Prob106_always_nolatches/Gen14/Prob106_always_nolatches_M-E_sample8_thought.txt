To create a novel architectural solution, we'll introduce a scancode register to store the last received scancode and use a combinational logic block to compare it with the target scancodes. This approach eliminates the need for separate modules for each arrow key and reduces the complexity of the design. The combinational logic block will use a priority encoder-like structure to detect the matching scancode and set the corresponding output. This method improves the design's scalability and maintainability by allowing easy addition of new scancodes without modifying the existing logic. Additionally, the use of a single combinational logic block reduces the switching activity and area usage, leading to improved PPA metrics.
