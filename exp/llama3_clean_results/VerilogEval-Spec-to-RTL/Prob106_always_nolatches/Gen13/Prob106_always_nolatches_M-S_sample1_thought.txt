To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, we can leverage a single always block within the TopModule to perform the scancode comparisons. This approach eliminates the need for multiple instances of the scancode_checker module, thus reducing the overall area consumption and potentially decreasing power consumption due to reduced switching activity. By directly comparing the input scancode against the target scancodes for each arrow key and asserting the corresponding output signals within a single always block, the design becomes more straightforward and easier to maintain.
