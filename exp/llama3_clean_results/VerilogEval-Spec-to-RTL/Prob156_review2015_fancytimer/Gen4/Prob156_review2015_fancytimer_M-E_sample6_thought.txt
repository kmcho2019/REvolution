In the START_PATTERN state, the FSM will shift in the next 4 bits from the input data to determine the delay value. 

After the delay value is loaded, the FSM will transition to the COUNTDOWN state, where it will count down from the delay value multiplied by 1000. 

During the countdown, the FSM will output the remaining time and assert the counting signal. 

When the countdown reaches zero, the FSM will transition to the DONE state, where it will assert the done signal and wait for the acknowledgment signal (ack) from the user. 

Once the acknowledgment signal is received, the FSM will transition back to the IDLE state and repeat the process.

This novel approach simplifies the state machine by reducing the number of states and transitions, making it more efficient and easier to implement.
