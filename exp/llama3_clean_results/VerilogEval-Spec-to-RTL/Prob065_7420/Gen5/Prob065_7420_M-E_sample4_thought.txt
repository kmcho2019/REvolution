To create a novel architectural idea for implementing the 7420 chip, we can utilize a hierarchical approach by defining the TopModule directly without creating a separate module for a single 4-input NAND gate. This approach involves directly instantiating the NAND logic within the TopModule, thus reducing the need for an extra module.
