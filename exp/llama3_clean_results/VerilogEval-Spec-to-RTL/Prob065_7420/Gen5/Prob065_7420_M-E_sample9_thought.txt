The existing solution implements the 7420 chip using two instances of a NAND4 module, each directly computing the NAND of four inputs. A novel approach could involve leveraging the properties of Boolean algebra to decompose the 4-input NAND gate into simpler gates, potentially reducing the area and power consumption. One such decomposition involves using a cascade of 2-input NAND gates. By applying the distributive property of Boolean algebra, a 4-input NAND can be expressed as a combination of 2-input NAND gates. This decomposition can lead to a different architectural implementation that might offer advantages in terms of area and power efficiency, especially when considering the synthesis and physical implementation aspects. This approach requires careful analysis to ensure that the decomposition does not adversely affect the performance (timing) of the circuit.
