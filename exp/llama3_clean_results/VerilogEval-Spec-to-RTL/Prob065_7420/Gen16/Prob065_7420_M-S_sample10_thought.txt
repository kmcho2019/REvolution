To simplify the given solution, the approach involves reducing unnecessary complexity while maintaining the required functionality of the 7420 chip (two 4-input NAND gates). Given that the current implementation already captures the required functionality, the focus shifts to streamlining the design. 

1. **Direct Implementation**: Instead of defining a separate module for the NAND4 gate, the NAND functionality can be directly implemented within the TopModule. This reduces the complexity of the overall design by minimizing the number of modules and instantiations.

2. **Simplified Logic**: The logic for the NAND gates can be directly applied to the outputs p1y and p2y without the need for additional modules or complex signal assignments.

By simplifying the design in this manner, the overall complexity is reduced, potentially leading to better synthesis results in terms of area and power consumption, while maintaining the functional correctness of the 7420 chip implementation.
