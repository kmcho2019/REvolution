The refactoring approach involves simplifying the existing code structure by directly implementing the dual 4-input NAND gate functionality within the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate DualNAND4 module. This is achieved by using the same logical operations (conjunction and negation) to compute the outputs p1y and p2y directly within the TopModule. The goal is to maintain the same functional behavior while reducing the complexity and potentially improving the synthesizability of the design.

The key insight is recognizing that the outputs of the two NAND gates are independent, allowing for a straightforward and concurrent computation of p1y and p2y based on their respective input sets. By directly assigning these computations to the outputs within the TopModule, the code becomes more concise and easier to understand, while the functionality remains unchanged.

This refactoring approach aligns with the principles of simplifying digital designs for better performance, area efficiency, and reduced power consumption, especially in contexts where the synthesis tool can effectively optimize simple, combinational logic.
