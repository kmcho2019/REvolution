Each output will be the result of a 4-input NAND operation on its respective set of inputs. For p1y, the NAND operation involves p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d. For p2y, it involves p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d. In Verilog, we can use the logical NAND operator (~&) to implement this.
