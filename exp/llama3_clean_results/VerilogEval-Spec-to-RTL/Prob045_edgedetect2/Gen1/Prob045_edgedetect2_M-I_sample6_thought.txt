The provided solution has a flaw in the declaration of the output port 'anyedge'. It should be declared as a 'reg' instead of being left undeclared (which by default in Verilog would make it a 'wire'). Also, the solution uses two always blocks, which can be optimized into a single always block to reduce the area usage and potentially improve the power consumption and performance. We can combine the two always blocks because both are triggered at the positive edge of the clock.
