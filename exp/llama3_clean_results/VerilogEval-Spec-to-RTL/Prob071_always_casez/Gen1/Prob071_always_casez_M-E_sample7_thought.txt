To implement the priority encoder, we can utilize a combinational logic approach that takes advantage of the properties of bit shifting and masking. We can create a chain of AND gates and OR gates to detect the position of the first '1' bit in the input vector. Each stage of the chain will check a different bit position, and if a '1' is found, the corresponding position will be propagated through the chain. This approach allows us to eliminate the need for a loop or break statement, making it suitable for Verilog synthesis.
