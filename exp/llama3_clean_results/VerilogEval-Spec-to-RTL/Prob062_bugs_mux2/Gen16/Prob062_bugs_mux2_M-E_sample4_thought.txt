To implement a 2-to-1 multiplexer, we can leverage the concept of bitwise operations to create a novel architecture. Instead of using a traditional case statement or ternary operator, we can utilize bitwise AND and OR operations to selectively enable or disable the bits of the input signals 'a' and 'b' based on the select signal 'sel'.
