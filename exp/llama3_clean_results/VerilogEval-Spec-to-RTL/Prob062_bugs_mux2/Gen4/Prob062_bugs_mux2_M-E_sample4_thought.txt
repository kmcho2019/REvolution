A novel approach to implementing the 8-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer involves utilizing the ternary operator, which is a concise way to express conditional logic in Verilog. This approach directly selects between the two input vectors `a` and `b` based on the `sel` signal without needing to perform bitwise operations. By using the ternary operator, the code can be made more readable and potentially more efficient in terms of synthesis area.
