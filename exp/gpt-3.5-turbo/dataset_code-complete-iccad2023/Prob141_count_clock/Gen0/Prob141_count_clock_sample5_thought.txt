We can use three separate BCD counters for the hours, minutes, and seconds. The seconds counter increments by 1 every time ena is asserted. When the seconds counter reaches 60, the minutes counter increments by 1 and resets the seconds counter. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 60, the hours counter increments by 1 and resets the minutes counter. The hours counter is set to 1 when it reaches 12. We also need to handle the AM/PM indicator appropriately based on the hour value.
