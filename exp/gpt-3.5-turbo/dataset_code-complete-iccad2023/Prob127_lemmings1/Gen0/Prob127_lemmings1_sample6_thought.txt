The Lemmings finite state machine can have two states: STATE_WALK_LEFT and STATE_WALK_RIGHT. In STATE_WALK_LEFT, Lemming will walk left unless bumped on the left, in which case it will switch to STATE_WALK_RIGHT. In STATE_WALK_RIGHT, Lemming will walk right unless bumped on the right, in which case it will switch to STATE_WALK_LEFT. The outputs walk_left and walk_right will be based on the current state.
