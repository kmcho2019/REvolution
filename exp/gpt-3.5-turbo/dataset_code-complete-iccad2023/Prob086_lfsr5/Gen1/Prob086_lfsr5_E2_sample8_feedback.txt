The issue lies in the LFSR shifting logic equation `q <= {q[4] ^ (q[2] ^ q[0]), q[4:1]}`. The way the XOR gates are implemented for taps at bit positions 5 and 3 is incorrect. The XOR operation for the tap at position 5 should include bit 4, and for the tap at position 3 should include bit 2. However, the current logic mixes these tap positions leading to incorrect shifting behavior, causing mismatches during simulation.
