The issue lies in the logic expression within the `else` block of the `always` block. The XOR operation `(q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2]) ^ q[0])` is incorrect for implementing a Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3. The incorrect handling of tap positions is causing the LFSR to not produce the expected output sequence. This leads to a large number of output mismatches during simulation, as indicated in the log. The incorrect behavior of the shift register is the primary reason for the failure.
