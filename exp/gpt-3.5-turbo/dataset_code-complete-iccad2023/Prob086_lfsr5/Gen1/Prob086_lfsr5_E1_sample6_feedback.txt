The issue lies in the LFSR shifting logic implementation. In the line `q <= {q[4] ^ (q[3] ^ q[0]), q[4:1]};`, the XOR operation among the bits is incorrect. In a Galois LFSR configuration, the XOR operation should only be done between the tap positions (bits 5 and 3) and the LSB (bit 0). However, in this implementation, the XOR operation is being performed among different bits, creating incorrect feedback for the LFSR. This mismatch in feedback causes the LFSR to not generate the correct sequence of states as intended.
