To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can separate the shift operation for the LFSR with taps from the shift operation for the ones without taps. The bits with taps will perform the XOR operation with the LSB output bit (q[0]) before shifting, while the other bits will only shift right. This approach will ensure correct feedback for the tap positions and maintain the maximal-length LFSR property.
