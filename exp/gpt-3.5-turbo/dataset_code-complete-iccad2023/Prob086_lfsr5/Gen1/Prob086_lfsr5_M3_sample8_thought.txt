To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3:
- Use a 5-bit shift register.
- Add XOR gates at positions 5 and 3 to XOR the values at those positions with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to generate the next state.
- Use an active-high synchronous reset signal to reset the LFSR output to 1.
