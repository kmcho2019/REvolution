To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we need a shift register where the value at taps 5 and 3 are XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to generate the next state. The shifting logic should correctly incorporate the XOR operations with the tap positions to ensure the LFSR follows a maximum-length sequence. An active-high synchronous reset will reset the LFSR output to 1 when the reset signal is asserted.
