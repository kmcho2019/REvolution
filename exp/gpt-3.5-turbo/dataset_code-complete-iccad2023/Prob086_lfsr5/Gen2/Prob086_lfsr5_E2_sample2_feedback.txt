The issue lies in the logic for updating the LFSR output in the `always` block. The calculation `{q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2]), q[4:1]}` in the `else` block is incorrect for a Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. The XOR operation `q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2])` will not produce the correct feedback value. This incorrect feedback calculation is causing the LFSR to deviate from the expected maximal-length sequence, resulting in numerous mismatches during simulation.
