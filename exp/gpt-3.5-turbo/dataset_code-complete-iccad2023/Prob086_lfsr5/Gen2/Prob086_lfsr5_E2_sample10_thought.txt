To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we should use XOR gates at the tap positions to introduce feedback into the shift register for generating the next state. To ensure maximum-length sequence, the taps at positions 5 and 3 should be used. Bit positions without taps should shift right unchanged. An active-high synchronous reset signal should reset the LFSR output to 1.
