The issue lies in the LFSR shifting logic. In the line `q <= {q[4] ^ q[2], q[4:1]};`, the XOR operation for the new value of the `q[2]` bit is incorrectly performed using `q[4]` instead of the correct tap position at `q[3]`. This leads to incorrect feedback in the LFSR, generating the large number of output mismatches observed during simulation. Consequently, the LFSR fails to produce the expected sequence of states and the designed maximal-length property is violated.
