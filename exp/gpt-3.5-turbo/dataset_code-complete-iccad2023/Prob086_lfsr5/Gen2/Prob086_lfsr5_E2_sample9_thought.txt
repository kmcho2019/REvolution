To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 correctly, the XOR operation should be performed between the LSB output bit (q[0]) and the tap positions 5 and 3. The shifting operation within the LFSR should ensure that bit positions without taps shift right unchanged, maintaining the Galois LFSR behavior. An active-high synchronous reset signal should reset the LFSR output to 1.
