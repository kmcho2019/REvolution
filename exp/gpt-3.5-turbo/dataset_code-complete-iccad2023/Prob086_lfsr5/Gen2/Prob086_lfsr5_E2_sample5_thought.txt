To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we need to correctly XOR the values at those tap positions with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to generate the next state. Proper shift calculation while maintaining the taps at the right positions is crucial for achieving the maximum-length sequence. Utilizing an active-high synchronous reset signal to reset the LFSR output to 1 is also necessary.
