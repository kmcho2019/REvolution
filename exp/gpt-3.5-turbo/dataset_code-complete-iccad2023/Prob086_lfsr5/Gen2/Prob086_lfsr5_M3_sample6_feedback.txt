The issue lies in the logic inside the `else` block of the `always` block. The expression `{q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2]), q[4:1]}` meant to represent the Galois LFSR shifting with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 is incorrect. The bitwise XOR operation `(q[4] ^ q[2])` is incorrectly used instead of `(q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0])`. This incorrect usage of XOR gates results in wrong feedback to the LFSR, causing the incorrect shift and mismatched outputs in the simulation.
