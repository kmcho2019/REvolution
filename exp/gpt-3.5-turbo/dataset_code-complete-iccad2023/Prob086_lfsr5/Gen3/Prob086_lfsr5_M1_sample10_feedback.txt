The issue lies in the LFSR shifting logic equation `{q[4] ^ (q[2] ^ q[0]), q[4], q[3], q[2], q[1]}`. The XOR gate should only be applied to the bits with tap positions, which are bit positions 5 and 3 in this case. However, the XOR gate is incorrectly also including bit position 0 in the XOR calculation, leading to incorrect LFSR behavior. This mismatch in the logic causes the LFSR to deviate from the expected maximal-length sequence, resulting in the vast number of mismatches in simulation.
