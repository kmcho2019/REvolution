The issue in the code lies in the shifting logic of the LFSR. The line `{q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0], q[4:1]}` is supposed to represent the Galois LFSR logic with XOR gates at tap positions 5 and 3, but it's incorrect. The XOR gates should be applied to q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0] with q[4] as the MSB and q[0] modified accordingly, but in the current code, the MSB is not properly updated based on the XOR of tap positions. This results in the LFSR not functioning as intended, leading to mismatches in the simulation results.
