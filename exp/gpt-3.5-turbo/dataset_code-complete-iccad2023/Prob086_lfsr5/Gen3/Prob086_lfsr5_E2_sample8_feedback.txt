The issue in the Verilog code lies in the LFSR shifting logic inside the always block. The expression `{q[4] ^ (q[2] ^ q[0]), q[4], q[3], q[2], q[1]}` does not correctly implement the Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. In a maximal-length Galois LFSR, the feedback polynomial should correspond to the taps specified. However, the current expression does not reflect the correct feedback mechanism for the taps at positions 5 and 3. This mismatch leads to incorrect LFSR behavior and the observed mismatches in the simulation log.
