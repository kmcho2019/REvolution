The issue lies in the feedback logic of the Galois LFSR implementation. The feedback logic expression `{q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0]), q[4:1]}` is incorrect. In a Galois LFSR, the tapped bits should be XORed with each other and then XORed with the LSB (q[0]), but the expression used does not follow this principle. This incorrect feedback logic results in incorrect state transitions and causes the large number of output mismatches observed during simulation. Therefore, the Galois LFSR logic needs to be revised to match the correct behavior of a maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3.
