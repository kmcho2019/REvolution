The issue in the code lies within the LFSR logic implementation. The line `q <= {q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0], q[4:1]};` is intended to implement the right shifting behavior of the Galois LFSR with XOR gates at tap positions 5 and 3. However, the bit positions for XOR operations are incorrect. For a 5-bit LFSR with tap positions at bits 5 and 3, the correct positions for XOR should be `q[4] ^ q[2]` instead of `q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0]`. This incorrect XOR operation is causing the incorrect shift register behavior leading to mismatches in the simulation output.
