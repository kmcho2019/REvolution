To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 correctly, we should use a dedicated feedback mechanism that XORs the relevant bit positions to produce the next state of the LFSR. Additionally, since a Galois LFSR shifts right, the shifting operation should be handled such that bit positions without taps are shifted unchanged. Proper synchronization of the reset signal is also crucial for the correct operation of the LFSR.
