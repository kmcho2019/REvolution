The issue in the code lies in the LFSR shifting logic. In the line `q <= {q[4] ^ q[2], q[4:1]};`, the concatenation operation combines `q[4] ^ q[2]` with `q[4:1]`. The intent here is to XOR `q[4]` with `q[2]` to obtain the next value for the bit position with a tap at position 3, but the syntax used does not achieve this. As a result, the shifting behavior based on taps at positions 5 and 3 is not correctly implemented. This causes mismatches in the simulation as the LFSR does not progress as expected.
