The issue lies in the logic of the LFSR implementation. In the else block of the always block, the XOR expression for the next state calculation is incorrect. The XOR operation should include the correct tap positions 5 and 3, but the current expression `{q[4] ^ (q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0]), q[4:1]}` does not cover this properly. This incorrect XOR calculation is leading to the 4000 mismatches observed during simulation. To match the design intent of creating a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, the XOR operation needs to be revised accordingly.
