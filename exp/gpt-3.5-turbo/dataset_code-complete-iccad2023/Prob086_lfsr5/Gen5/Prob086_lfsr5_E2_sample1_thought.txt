To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, calculate the next state by XORing the tapped bits (q[4] and q[2]) instead of directly XORing q[4] with q[2] and q[0]. This will ensure the LFSR cycles through the maximum-length sequence, resetting the output to 1 on active-high synchronous reset.
