The issue in the code lies in the combination of sequential and combinational logic. In the always_comb block, the 'next_state' is calculated as the XOR of 'd_ff' and 'in'. However, 'd_ff' is updated inside the always_ff block triggered by the positive edge of the clock. This creates a mismatch because 'd_ff' is updated later in the simulation compared to 'next_state'. To match the high-level design intent, 'next_state' should be calculated directly as the XOR of 'd_ff' and 'in' in the always_ff block, ensuring consistency in the signals.
