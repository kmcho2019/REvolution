To implement the Lemmings finite state machine, we can use five states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING, and SPLATTER. 

- In the WALK_LEFT state: Lemming walks left and switches to WALK_RIGHT if bumped on the left or if given a dig command.
- In the WALK_RIGHT state: Lemming walks right and switches to WALK_LEFT if bumped on the right or if given a dig command.
- In the FALLING state: Lemming falls and says "aaah!" until it hits the ground. If it falls for too long, it can splatter.
- In the DIGGING state: Lemming digs until it reaches the other side, then falls and resumes walking in the original direction.
- In the SPLATTER state: Lemming stops walking, falling, and digging indefinitely.

Transitions between states are based on the inputs (bump_left, bump_right, dig, ground) and the clock.
