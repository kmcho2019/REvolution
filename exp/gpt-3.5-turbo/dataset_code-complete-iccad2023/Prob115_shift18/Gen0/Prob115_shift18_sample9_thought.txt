For the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load, we can use a combination of shift left and shift right operations based on the "amount" input. To implement the shift operations, we need to consider the arithmetic shift right operation separately to handle sign extension. When the "load" signal is high, the data input should be loaded into the shift register. Otherwise, based on the "amount" input, we should perform either a left or right shift by 1 or 8 bits.
