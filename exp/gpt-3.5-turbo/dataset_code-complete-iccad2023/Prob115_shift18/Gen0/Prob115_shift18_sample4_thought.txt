To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load, we need to create a shift register that shifts left or right by 1 or 8 bits based on the amount input. We can use conditional statements in Verilog to determine the shift operation to be performed. In the case of load signal being high, we will load data[63:0] into the shift register without shifting. The arithmetic right shift can be implemented by replicating the sign bit as the shifter moves to the right.
