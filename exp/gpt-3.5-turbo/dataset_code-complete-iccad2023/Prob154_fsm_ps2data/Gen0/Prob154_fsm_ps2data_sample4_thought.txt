The finite state machine will have three states - IDLE, BYTE_1, BYTE_2. In the IDLE state, we wait for in[3] to be 1 indicating the start of a message. Then we transition to the BYTE_1 state and receive the first byte. In the BYTE_1 state, we transition to the BYTE_2 state and receive the second byte. Finally, in the BYTE_2 state, we receive the third byte and assert the done signal.
