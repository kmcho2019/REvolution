To implement the 4-bit shift register with the described functionality, we can create a Mealy state machine. The shift register will have three states: IDLE, SHIFT, and LOAD. The state machine will transition between these states based on the load and enable signals. In the IDLE state, the register will hold its current value. In the SHIFT state, the register will shift its contents to the right. In the LOAD state, the register will load new data into its contents.
