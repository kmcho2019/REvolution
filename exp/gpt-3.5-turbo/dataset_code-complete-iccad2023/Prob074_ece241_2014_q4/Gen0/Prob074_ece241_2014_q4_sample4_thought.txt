The circuit consists of three D flip-flops each connected to a two-input gate (XOR, AND, OR). The output of each flip-flop is fed into a three-input NOR gate to produce the final output Z. The flip-flops are initialized to 0 at the beginning.

The D flip-flop inputs are controlled by the three gates as described. A clock signal is used to determine the timing of the state transitions.

The circuit can be implemented by defining the D flip-flops, the gates, and the NOR gate. The flip-flop inputs can be set based on the inputs x and the outputs of the corresponding gates.
