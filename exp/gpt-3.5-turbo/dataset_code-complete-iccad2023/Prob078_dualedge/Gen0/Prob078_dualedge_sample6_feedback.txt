The simulation log shows 113 mismatches out of 224 samples, indicating a problem with the functional behavior of the flip-flop. The design attempts to mimic a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using separate always blocks sensitive to the rising and falling edges of the clock. This approach, however, leads to data loss since q1 and q2 are updated on different clock edges. Consequently, the output q does not accurately reflect the input d, causing the mismatches observed during simulation.
