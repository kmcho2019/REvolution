We can design a Moore state machine with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The machine transitions between these states based on inputs bump_left, bump_right, and ground. In the WALK_LEFT state, the Lemming walks left unless bumped on the right, then transitions to the WALK_RIGHT state. In the WALK_RIGHT state, the Lemming walks right unless bumped on the left, then transitions to the WALK_LEFT state. In the FALLING state, the Lemming is falling and outputs "aaah!" until the ground reappears, then transitions back to the previous walking state.
