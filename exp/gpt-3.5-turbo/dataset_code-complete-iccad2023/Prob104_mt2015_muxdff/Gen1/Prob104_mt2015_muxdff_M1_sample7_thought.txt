The TopModule will consist of a D flip-flop and a 2:1 multiplexer. The D flip-flop will load the input 'r_in' when 'L' is asserted. The output of the D flip-flop will be fed to both inputs of the 2:1 multiplexer, with a bitwise XOR operation applied on one of the inputs based on a specific bit pattern. The output of the multiplexer will be assigned to the output 'Q'.
