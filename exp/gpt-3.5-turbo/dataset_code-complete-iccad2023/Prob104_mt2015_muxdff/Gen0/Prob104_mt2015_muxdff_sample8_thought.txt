The TopModule will consist of a D flip-flop and a 2:1 multiplexer. The D flip-flop will load the input 'r_in' when 'L' is asserted, and its output will be connected to one of the inputs of the 2:1 multiplexer based on the value of 'L'. The other input of the multiplexer will be directly connected to the output of the flip-flop. The output of the multiplexer will be assigned to the output 'Q'.
