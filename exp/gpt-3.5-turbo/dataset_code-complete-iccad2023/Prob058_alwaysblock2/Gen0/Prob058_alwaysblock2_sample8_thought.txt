For an XOR gate, we can express the output as the result of an XOR operation between inputs 'a' and 'b'. The assign statement will directly assign the XOR result to 'out_assign'. The combinational always block will update 'out_always_comb' whenever 'a' or 'b' change. The clocked always block will sample the inputs 'a' and 'b' on the positive edge of the clock 'clk' and store the result in a flip-flop to produce 'out_always_ff'.
