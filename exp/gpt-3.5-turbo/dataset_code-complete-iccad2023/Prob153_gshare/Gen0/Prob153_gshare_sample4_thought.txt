1. Implement a 7-bit global branch history register to keep track of historical outcomes of branches.
2. Create a 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters to store the prediction outcomes for each entry.
3. Generate an index by hashing the xor of the 7-bit pc and 7-bit global history to access the prediction table.
4. Use separate interfaces for prediction and training operations.
5. Handle cases where training takes precedence over prediction or where training and prediction of the same entry occur simultaneously.
6. Update the prediction table during training and store the actual outcome for future predictions.
7. Ensure that the branch history register is updated at the next positive clock edge after a prediction is made.
