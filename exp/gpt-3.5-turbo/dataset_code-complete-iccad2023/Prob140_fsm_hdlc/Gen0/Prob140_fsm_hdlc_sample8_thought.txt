We can design a Moore-type finite state machine with four states - IDLE, SEEN_1, SEEN_2, and ERR. The machine will transition based on the input bit sequence and the current state, and the output signals will be generated based on the current state.

In the IDLE state, we are waiting for the first '0' or '1' to occur.
In the SEEN_1 state, we have seen one '1' and we wait to see what comes next.
In the SEEN_2 state, we have seen two '1's and wait to see what comes next while tracking the count of consecutive '1's.
In the ERR state, we have seen too many consecutive '1's and flag an error.

The FSM transitions when a new input bit is received, and it updates its state and outputs accordingly.
