The finite state machine can have 4 states: IDLE, START_BIT, DATA_BITS, and STOP_BIT. Transition from IDLE to START_BIT upon detecting a start bit (0). From START_BIT, transition to DATA_BITS for 8 clock cycles to collect data bits. After 8 bits, transition to STOP_BIT to verify the stop bit (1). If correct, go to IDLE and output 'done'. If incorrect, stay in STOP_BIT until the next start bit.
