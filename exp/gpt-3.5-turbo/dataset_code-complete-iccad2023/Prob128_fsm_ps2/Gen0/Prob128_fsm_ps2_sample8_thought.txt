We can design a finite state machine with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and CHECK_DONE. In the IDLE state, we wait for the first byte with in[3]=1. Once we find it, we move to the BYTE1 state where we receive the next two bytes. After receiving the third byte, we move to the CHECK_DONE state to ensure that in[3]=1 in the next cycle, indicating the end of the message.
