We can design a simple finite state machine with three states: IDLE, WAIT_FOR_FIRST_BYTE_WITH_IN3_HIGH, and WAIT_FOR_SECOND_BYTE_AND_SIGNAL_DONE. In the IDLE state, we wait for the first byte with in[3]=1. Once we find it, we transition to the WAIT_FOR_FIRST_BYTE_WITH_IN3_HIGH state. In this state, we wait for the next two bytes and then transition to the WAIT_FOR_SECOND_BYTE_AND_SIGNAL_DONE state to signal the end of the message.
