We can design a simple finite state machine with four states: IDLE, FIRST_BYTE, SECOND_BYTE, and THIRD_BYTE. In the IDLE state, we wait for the first byte with in[3]=1. Subsequently, we move to FIRST_BYTE, SECOND_BYTE, and THIRD_BYTE states as we receive the next bytes sequentially. Upon receiving the third byte, we transition back to the IDLE state.
