We can design a finite state machine with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and CHECK_DONE. In the IDLE state, we wait for the first byte with in[3]=1. Once the first byte is detected, we move to the BYTE1 state to receive the next two bytes. After receiving the third byte, we transition to the CHECK_DONE state to ensure that in[3]=1 in the next cycle, signifying the end of the message.
