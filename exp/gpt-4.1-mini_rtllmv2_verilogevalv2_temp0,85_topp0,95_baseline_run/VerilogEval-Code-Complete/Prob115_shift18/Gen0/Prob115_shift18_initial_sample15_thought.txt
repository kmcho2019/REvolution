Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and enable. On load high, load the input data into q. When ena is high and load is low, shift q according to amount:
- For left shifts (00 and 01), shift q left by 1 or 8 bits, filling vacated LSBs with 0.
- For right shifts (10 and 11), perform arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits, filling vacated MSBs with sign bit q[63].
