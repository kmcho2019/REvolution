Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift operations controlled by the enable and amount signals. When load is high, q is loaded directly with data. Otherwise, if enable is high, q is shifted according to amount: left shift by 1 or 8 bits, or arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits. For arithmetic right shifts, the vacated bits are filled with the sign bit (q[63]). The shift operations are done combinationally inside the clocked always block.
