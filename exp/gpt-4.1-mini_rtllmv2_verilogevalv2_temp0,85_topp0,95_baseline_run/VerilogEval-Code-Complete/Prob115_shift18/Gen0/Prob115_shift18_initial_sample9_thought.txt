Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift operations. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load the input data into q. Else if ena is high, perform the shift operation based on amount: shift left by 1 or 8 bits, or shift right by 1 or 8 bits. For right shifts, perform arithmetic right shift, which means replicate the sign bit on left side. Left shifts fill with zeros from the right. The output q is updated accordingly.
