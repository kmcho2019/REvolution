Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift operations. On the rising clock edge, if load is high, load the input data directly. If ena is high and load is low, perform a shift operation based on the amount selector: shift left by 1 or 8 bits, or shift right (arithmetic) by 1 or 8 bits. For left shifts, fill with zeros on the right. For arithmetic right shifts, replicate the MSB (sign bit) on the left. If neither load nor ena is active, maintain the current value.
