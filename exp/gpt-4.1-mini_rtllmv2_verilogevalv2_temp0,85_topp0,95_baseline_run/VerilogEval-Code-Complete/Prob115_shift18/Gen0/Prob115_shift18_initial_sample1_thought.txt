Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load. On the rising edge of clk:
- If load is high, load q with data.
- Else if ena is high, shift q according to amount:
  * For left shifts (amount 00, 01), shift q left by 1 or 8 bits, filling with zeros.
  * For right shifts (amount 10, 11), perform arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits, preserving the sign bit.
- If ena is low and load is low, hold the current value.
