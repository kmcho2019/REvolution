Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load. On each clock edge, if load is high, load data into q. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift q according to amount. For left shifts (00 or 01), shift q left by 1 or 8 bits, filling with zeros. For right shifts (10 or 11), shift q right arithmetically by 1 or 8 bits, filling with the sign bit q[63].
