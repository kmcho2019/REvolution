Implement a synchronous 64-bit shift register with load and enable control. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load data into q. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift q according to amount:
- For shift left by 1 or 8 bits, shift q left and fill the vacated bits with 0.
- For arithmetic right shifts by 1 or 8 bits, shift q right and fill the vacated upper bits with the original sign bit q[63].
If ena is low and load is low, hold q.
