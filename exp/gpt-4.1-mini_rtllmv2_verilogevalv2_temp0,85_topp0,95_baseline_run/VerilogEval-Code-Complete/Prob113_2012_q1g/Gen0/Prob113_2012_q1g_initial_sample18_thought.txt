00 |  1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |   (x1 x2 = 00)
01 |  0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |   (01)

We can try to simplify the function by grouping cells:

Groups:

  - This is x1=1, x3=0

2) Group of three 1's at row 11, columns 00,01,11:

  - Since the 1's are in 00,01,11 (x3x4 = 00, 01, 11)

  - Actually, 3 cells can't form a group of power of two for K-map grouping, so better to group pairs.
