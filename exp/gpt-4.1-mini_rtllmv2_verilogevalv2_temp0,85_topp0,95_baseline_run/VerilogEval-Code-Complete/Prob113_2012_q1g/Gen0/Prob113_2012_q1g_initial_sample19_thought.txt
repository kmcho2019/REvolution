First, interpret the Karnaugh map with variables x1x2 as row indices and x3x4 as column indices. The rows and columns are in Gray code order:

Rows (x1x2): 00, 01, 11, 10
Columns (x3x4): 00, 01, 11, 10

From the map:

x1x2\x3x4 | 00  01  11  10
----------------------------
00        | 1   0   0   1
01        | 0   0   0   0
11        | 1   1   1   0
10        | 1   1   0   1

Now write minterms (1s):

- (00,00): x1=0,x2=0,x3=0,x4=0
- (00,10): x1=0,x2=0,x3=1,x4=0
- (11,00): x1=1,x2=1,x3=0,x4=0
- (11,01): x1=1,x2=1,x3=0,x4=1
- (11,11): x1=1,x2=1,x3=1,x4=1
- (10,00): x1=1,x2=0,x3=0,x4=0
- (10,01): x1=1,x2=0,x3=0,x4=1
- (10,10): x1=1,x2=0,x3=1,x4=0

Now group 1s for simplification:

Group 1: Cells (11,00),(11,01),(10,00),(10,01) => x1=1, x3=0 (x2 and x4 vary)
Group 2: Cells (11,11) alone => x1=1, x2=1, x3=1, x4=1
Group 3: Cells (00,00),(00,10),(10,10) => x3= variable but note only (00,00) and (00,10) share x1=0, x2=0; x4=0 common
Cells (00,00) and (00,10): x1=0, x2=0, x4=0
Cell (10,10): x1=1, x2=0, x3=1, x4=0 -- separate from previous group
Also cell (10,10) grouped with (00,10) is not possible due to row difference.

Group 4: (10,10) alone: x1=1, x2=0, x3=1, x4=0
Group 5: (00,10) and (00,00): x1=0,x2=0,x4=0 (covers those two cells)

Also, (00,10) and (10,10) share x3=1,x4=0 but different rows.

Reconsider grouping:

- Group A: (11,00),(11,01),(10,00),(10,01) => x1=1,x3=0
- Group B: (11,11) alone
- Group C: (00,00),(00,10) => x1=0,x2=0,x4=0
- Group D: (10,10) alone

Final expression:

f = (x1 & ~x3) | (x1 & x2 & x3 & x4) | (~x1 & ~x2 & ~x4) | (x1 & ~x2 & x3 & ~x4)
