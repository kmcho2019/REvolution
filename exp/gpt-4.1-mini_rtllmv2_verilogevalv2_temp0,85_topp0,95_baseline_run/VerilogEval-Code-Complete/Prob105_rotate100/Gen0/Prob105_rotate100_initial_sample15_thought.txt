Implement a synchronous 100-bit rotator with a clocked always block triggered on the rising edge of clk. On each clock cycle, if load is high, synchronously load q with data. Otherwise, check ena: if ena is 2'b01, rotate right by one bit by shifting q right by one and putting q[0] at q[99]. If ena is 2'b10, rotate left by one bit by shifting q left by one and putting q[99] at q[0]. For ena 2'b00 or 2'b11, keep q unchanged.
