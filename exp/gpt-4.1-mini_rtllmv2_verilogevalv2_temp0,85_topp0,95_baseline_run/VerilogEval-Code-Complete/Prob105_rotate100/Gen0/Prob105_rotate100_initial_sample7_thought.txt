Implement a 100-bit register with synchronous loading and rotation. On the rising edge of clk:
- If load is high, load q with data.
- Else if ena = 2'b01, rotate right by 1 bit: the least significant bit moves to the most significant bit position, and others shift right.
- Else if ena = 2'b10, rotate left by 1 bit: the most significant bit moves to the least significant bit position, and others shift left.
- Else (ena = 00 or 11), no change in q.
