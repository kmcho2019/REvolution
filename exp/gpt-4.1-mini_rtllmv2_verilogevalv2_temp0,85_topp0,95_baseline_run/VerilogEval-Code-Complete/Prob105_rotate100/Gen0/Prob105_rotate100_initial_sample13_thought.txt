Implement a 100-bit register with synchronous load and rotate functionality. On the rising clock edge, if load is high, load the input data directly into q. Otherwise, depending on ena:
- if ena == 2'b01, rotate right by 1 bit: the least significant bit of q becomes the most significant bit after rotation.
- if ena == 2'b10, rotate left by 1 bit: the most significant bit of q becomes the least significant bit after rotation.
- if ena is 00 or 11, maintain the current q.
