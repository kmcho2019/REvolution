Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT. When ground is 1, the Lemming walks either left or right, switching direction if bumped on either side or both. When ground is 0, transition to FALLING state, remembering the previous walking direction. While in FALLING state, ignore bumps and output aaah=1. When ground returns to 1, transition back to the stored walking direction state without changing it.
