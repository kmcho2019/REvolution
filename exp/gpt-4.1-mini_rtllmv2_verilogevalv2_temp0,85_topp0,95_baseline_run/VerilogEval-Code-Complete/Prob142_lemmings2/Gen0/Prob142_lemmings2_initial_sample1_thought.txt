We implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The current direction (walk_left or walk_right) is remembered in the walking states. When ground=0, the FSM transitions to FALLING and asserts aaah=1. While falling, bumps do not affect direction or state. When ground=1 again, the FSM returns to the walking state with the previous walking direction. Bumps while walking cause direction switch. Asynchronous active-high reset sets the FSM to WALK_LEFT. Outputs are derived from the state.
