We model the Lemming behavior with a Moore FSM with three states:
1. WALK_LEFT: walking left
2. WALK_RIGHT: walking right
3. FALLING: falling ("aaah")

The FSM transitions as follows:
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT:
  - If ground=0, move to FALLING, remember the current walking direction to resume later.
  - Else if bumped (bump_left or bump_right), switch direction.
- From FALLING:
  - Stay in FALLING while ground=0.
  - When ground=1, return to previous walking direction state.
  
Bumps while falling do not affect direction. Bumps coincident with ground change to falling or recovery do not affect direction either.

The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah reflect the current state:
- WALK_LEFT: walk_left=1, walk_right=0, aaah=0
- WALK_RIGHT: walk_left=0, walk_right=1, aaah=0
- FALLING: walk_left=0, walk_right=0, aaah=1

areset asynchronously resets to WALK_LEFT.

We'll encode states with two bits:
- 2'b00 = WALK_LEFT
- 2'b01 = WALK_RIGHT
- 2'b10 = FALLING

We keep track of the walking direction before falling to restore after fall.
