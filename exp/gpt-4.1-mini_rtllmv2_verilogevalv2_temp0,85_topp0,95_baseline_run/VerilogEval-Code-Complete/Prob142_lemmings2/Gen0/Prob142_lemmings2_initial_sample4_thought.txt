Model the Lemming's behavior as a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, transition to FALLING, preserving the last walking direction. While falling, bump inputs do not affect direction or cause state changes. When ground returns to 1, transition back to the previous walking direction state. When on ground, bumps cause direction switching: bump_left causes walking right, bump_right causes walking left, and bump on both sides also causes direction switch. The outputs are derived directly from the state: walk_left=1 in WALK_LEFT, walk_right=1 in WALK_RIGHT, aaah=1 in FALLING.
