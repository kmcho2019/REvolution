Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The Lemming starts in WALK_LEFT after async reset. When on the ground (ground=1), it walks either left or right, switching direction if bumped on either side (or both). When ground=0, it transitions to FALLING state, outputting aaah=1, and remembers its previous walking direction internally. While falling, bump inputs are ignored. When ground returns to 1, it returns to the saved walking direction state. Outputs depend only on the current state.
