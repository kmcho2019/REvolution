We implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs depend only on the current state. On reset, the Lemming starts WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, it transitions to FALLING and asserts aaah. When ground=1, it returns to the previous walking state (left or right) without changing direction. While walking, bump_left or bump_right (or both) causes direction to flip, but bumps during falling do not change direction. Bumps simultaneous with ground change do not affect direction either. We store the walking direction before falling to restore it after falling.
