We model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. On reset, the Lemming starts walking left. When on the ground (ground=1), the Lemming is in either walk_left or walk_right state, and switches direction if bumped on either side (bump_left or bump_right = 1). If ground becomes 0, the Lemming goes to the falling state and outputs aaah=1. While falling, bump inputs do not change direction. When ground returns to 1, the Lemming returns to the walking state it was in before falling, resuming its previous walking direction. The FSM is asynchronous reset active high, with synchronous state transitions on clk's rising edge.
