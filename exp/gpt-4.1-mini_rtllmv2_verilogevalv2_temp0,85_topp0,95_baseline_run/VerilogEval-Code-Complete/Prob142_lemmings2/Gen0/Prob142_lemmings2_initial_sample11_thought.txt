We will model the Lemming's behavior as a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The state transitions depend on the inputs bump_left, bump_right, and ground. 

- Initially, the FSM starts in WALK_LEFT due to asynchronous reset.
- When on the ground (ground=1), the Lemming walks either left or right.
- If bumped on the left, it switches to walking right; if bumped on the right, it switches to walking left; if bumped on both sides, it still switches directions.
- If ground goes to 0, the Lemming transitions to FALLING state, preserving its walking direction internally.
- While falling, bump inputs do not affect the walking direction.
- When ground becomes 1 again, the FSM returns from FALLING to the previous walking direction state.
- Outputs are combinational from the current state: walk_left and walk_right are one-hot encoded, and aaah is asserted only when falling.

The design uses a state register updated on the clock edge, and asynchronous reset to WALK_LEFT.
