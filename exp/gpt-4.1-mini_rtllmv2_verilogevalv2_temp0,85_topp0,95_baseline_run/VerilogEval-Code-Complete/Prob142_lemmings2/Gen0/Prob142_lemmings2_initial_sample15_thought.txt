We design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the state is WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, the Lemming falls (FALLING state) and outputs aaah=1. While falling, bump inputs and ground changes do not affect walking direction. When ground returns to 1, the Lemming resumes walking in the previous walking direction. Bumps cause direction change only when walking and ground=1. We keep track of walking direction before falling to restore it after falling.
