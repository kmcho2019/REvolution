Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. When ground=0, transition from either walking state to FALLING and assert aaah=1. While in FALLING, direction is remembered but not changed by bumps. When ground=1, transition back to the stored walking state. Bumps affect direction only in walking states and not during falling or during ground transitions. Asynchronous reset sets state to WALK_LEFT.
