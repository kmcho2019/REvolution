Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. 
- On reset, start in WALK_LEFT.
- If ground=0, transition to FALLING state, remember the walking direction before falling.
- In FALLING state, output aaah=1, walk_left and walk_right=0.
- If ground=1 and currently FALLING, transition back to the remembered walking direction.
- When ground=1 and in walking states, bump_left or bump_right (or both) cause direction change.
- Bumps during FALLING or during transition cycles do not affect direction.
