First, interpret the Karnaugh map to write out the minterms where the output is 1. The K-map is for variables a,b as columns (00,01,11,10) and c,d as rows (00,01,11,10). We can list the minterms (a,b,c,d) corresponding to 1s and then find the simplified boolean expression. Alternatively, group 1s in the K-map to find prime implicants and write a minimal expression.

The K-map:

00 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
01 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

Convert to minterms (a,b,c,d):

Indexing order: a,b (col), c,d (row), variables in order a b c d

Each cell corresponds to a 4-bit input: a b c d

Where cd is row, ab is column.
