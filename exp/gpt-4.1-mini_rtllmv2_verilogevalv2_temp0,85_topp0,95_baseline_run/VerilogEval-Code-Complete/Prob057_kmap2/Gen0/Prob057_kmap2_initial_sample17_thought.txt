Now from the Karnaugh map, let's find the simplified Boolean expression using grouping:

Grouping 1: The four 1's at positions (cd=00, ab=00,01,10) and (cd=01, ab=00,10) form a group:
These correspond to minterms m0,m1,m2,m4,m6
This group covers c=0 (since c=0 for rows 00 and 01), and for columns (00,01,10) which means b can be 0 or 1, so b is irrelevant. a is 0 or 1, also irrelevant except in the column 11 where 0's are.

This group can be simplified to ~c

Check that row 11 is c=1, row 10 is c=1 (no 1's), so the only rows with c=0 are 00 and 01, all 1's in those except at 01,01=0 and 01,11=0

But 01,01=0 and 01,11=0 breaks the group. So can't group all of c=0.

Try smaller groups.

Group 1: The four 1's at cd=00, ab=00,01,10 (positions m0,m1,m2)

cd=00 means c=0, d=0

ab=00,01,10 => a,b = 00,01,10, which means b is 0 or 1, a is 0 or 1 but excluding 11.

These 3 minterms can be grouped as:

c=0, d=0, b don't care, a don't care but excluding ab=11

Since ab=11 is not included (which is zero), we can't group all four columns.

Group of 2: m0 and m1 (cd=00, ab=00 and 01): c=0,d=0, a=0,b=0 or 1

Simplified: ~c & ~d & ~a

Wait a minute, a=0 for ab=00 and 01 (bits are a,b)

ab=00: a=0,b=0

So a=0,b don't care

So this group is ~c & ~d & ~a

Similarly, m1 and m2 can't group because a changes from 0 to 1.
