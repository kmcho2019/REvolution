First, interpret the Karnaugh map as a 4-variable truth table with inputs a,b,c,d. The map rows represent cd and columns represent ab, with the given cell values as outputs.

Map:
    ab
cd  00  01  11  10
00: 1   1   0   1
01: 1   0   0   1
11: 0   1   1   1
10: 1   1   0   0

Indexing (cd rows, ab columns):
cd=00, ab=00 -> out=1
cd=00, ab=01 -> out=1
cd=00, ab=11 -> out=0
cd=00, ab=10 -> out=1
cd=01, ab=00 -> out=1
cd=01, ab=01 -> out=0
cd=01, ab=11 -> out=0
cd=01, ab=10 -> out=1
cd=11, ab=00 -> out=0
cd=11, ab=01 -> out=1
cd=11, ab=11 -> out=1
cd=11, ab=10 -> out=1
cd=10, ab=00 -> out=1
cd=10, ab=01 -> out=1
cd=10, ab=11 -> out=0
cd=10, ab=10 -> out=0

We can write minterms where output=1.

Variables: a,b,c,d (assume a is MSB of ab, b LSB; c MSB of cd, d LSB)

Minterms (output=1):

From rows and columns:

cd=00 (c=0,d=0)
 ab=00 (a=0,b=0): 1 -> m0 = !a & !b & !c & !d
 ab=01 (a=0,b=1): 1 -> m1 = !a & b & !c & !d
 ab=11 (a=1,b=1): 0
 ab=10 (a=1,b=0): 1 -> m2 = a & !b & !c & !d

cd=01 (c=0,d=1)
 ab=00 (a=0,b=0): 1 -> m4 = !a & !b & !c & d
 ab=01 (a=0,b=1): 0
 ab=11 (a=1,b=1): 0
 ab=10 (a=1,b=0): 1 -> m6 = a & !b & !c & d

cd=11 (c=1,d=1)
 ab=00 (a=0,b=0): 0
 ab=01 (a=0,b=1): 1 -> m13 = !a & b & c & d
 ab=11 (a=1,b=1): 1 -> m15 = a & b & c & d
 ab=10 (a=1,b=0): 1 -> m14 = a & !b & c & d

cd=10 (c=1,d=0)
 ab=00 (a=0,b=0): 1 -> m8 = !a & !b & c & !d
 ab=01 (a=0,b=1): 1 -> m9 = !a & b & c & !d
 ab=11 (a=1,b=1): 0
 ab=10 (a=1,b=0): 0

Minterm numbers: 0,1,2,4,6,8,9,13,14,15

We can write the function as the sum of these minterms or simplify by grouping:

We see the groups:
- For c=0,d=0: m0,m1,m2 (ab=00,01,10) are 1, only ab=11 is 0.
- For c=0,d=1: m4 and m6=1 (ab=00,10)
- For c=1,d=0: m8,m9=1 (ab=00,01)
- For c=1,d=1: m13,m14,m15=1 (ab=01,10,11)

Looking for groups:

Group 1: m0,m1,m2,m4,m6 (c=0) and d varies, mostly a and b

Try grouping on c and d:

Observe that output=1 when (c=0 and b=0) or (d=0 and b=0) or (c=1 and d=1 and (b=1 or a=1)).

Alternatively, let's try to find simplified expression by Sum of Products.

Alternatively, use the K-map and write simplified SOP terms.

Using K-map groups:

- Group of 4: m0,m1,m4,m5 (but m5=0), so invalid.
- Group of 2: m0,m1 (row cd=00 col ab=00,01) -> !a & !c & !d (since b varies)
- Group of 2: m8,m9 (row cd=10 col ab=00,01) -> !a & c & !d
- Group of 4: m13,m14,m15,m9? m9 is 1 but different row. Better group m13,m14,m15 (cd=11, ab=01,10,11) -> c & d & (b + a)

Simplify:

Term1: !a & !c & !d (covers m0,m1)
Term2: a & !b & !c & !d (m2)
Term3: !a & !b & !c & d (m4)
Term4: a & !b & !c & d (m6)
Term5: !a & !b & c & !d (m8)
Term6: !a & b & c & !d (m9)
Term7: !a & b & c & d (m13)
Term8: a & !b & c & d (m14)
Term9: a & b & c & d (m15)

Alternatively, combine:

From terms (m0,m1) -> !a & !c & !d
From terms (m2,m6) -> a & !b & !c & (d or !d) = a & !b & !c
From terms (m4) -> !a & !b & !c & d
From terms (m8,m9) -> !a & c & !d
From terms (m13,m14,m15) -> c & d & ( ( !a & b ) or ( a & !b ) or ( a & b ) ) = c & d & (a | b)

Finally, write function as:

out = (!a & !c & !d)          // covers m0,m1
    | (a & !b & !c)           // covers m2,m6
    | (!a & !b & !c & d)      // m4
    | (!a & c & !d)           // m8,m9
    | (c & d & (a | b));      // m13,m14,m15

We can implement this as combinational logic.
