Design a finite state machine that tracks the water level based on sensors s[3:1]. The states represent the water levels: Above s[3], Between s[3] and s[2], Between s[2] and s[1], and Below s[1]. The FSM transitions depend on the current sensor inputs. The previous water level state is stored to determine if the level is rising or falling. If rising (new level > previous level), dfr (supplemental valve) is asserted. Outputs fr1, fr2, fr3 are asserted as per the nominal flow rate for the water level. The reset sets the FSM to the lowest level state (Below s[1]) with all valves open (fr1, fr2, fr3, dfr asserted).
