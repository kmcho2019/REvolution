Implement a finite state machine that tracks the current water level zone based on sensor inputs and the previous water level zone to determine if the flow rate should be increased by opening the supplemental flow valve (dfr). The water level zones are defined by which sensors are asserted. The FSM should remember the previous zone to detect if the level is rising (previous zone lower than current zone) or falling. The outputs fr1, fr2, fr3 correspond to nominal flow valves that are asserted depending on the current water level zone according to the table. When above the highest sensor (all sensors asserted), no flow valves are open. When below lowest sensor (none asserted), all flow valves are asserted. On reset, set the FSM to the lowest water level (no sensors asserted), with all three flow valves and dfr asserted. The FSM updates on each clock synchronous with reset. Compare previous and current water level zones to assert dfr if the water level is rising.
