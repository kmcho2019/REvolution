Model the system as a synchronous FSM that remembers the previous water level category based on sensors s[3:1]. The level is categorized into four states:

- STATE_0: No sensors asserted (water below s[1])
- STATE_1: Only s[1] asserted (water between s[2] and s[1])
- STATE_2: s[1] and s[2] asserted (water between s[3] and s[2])
- STATE_3: s[1], s[2], s[3] all asserted (water above s[3])

On every clock cycle, determine the current level from sensors and compare with the previous level. The outputs fr1, fr2, fr3 correspond to nominal flow valves and dfr to supplemental flow valve.

- When above s[3], flow rate is zero (all outputs zero)
- When below s[1], maximum flow (fr1, fr2, fr3 all asserted)
- Between s[1] and s[2], nominal flow rate fr1 and fr2 asserted
- Between s[2] and s[3], nominal flow rate fr1 asserted
- Supplemental flow valve (dfr) is asserted if water level increased since last state.

On synchronous reset, initialize the state to STATE_0 (lowest water level) with all valves open (fr1, fr2, fr3, dfr asserted).

This structure cleanly implements the required logic, tracks previous water level to decide dfr, and outputs according to the table.
