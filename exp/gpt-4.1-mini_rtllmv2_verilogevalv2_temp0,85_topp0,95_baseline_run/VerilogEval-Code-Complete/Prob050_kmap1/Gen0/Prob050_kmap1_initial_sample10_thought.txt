Analyze the Karnaugh map to find the minimized Boolean expression for the output. The map is:

       a
  bc  0   1
 00 | 0 | 1
 01 | 1 | 1
 11 | 1 | 1
 10 | 1 | 1

When a=0 and bc=00, output=0. Otherwise output=1.

From the map:
- For a=1, output=1 always (covers all bc).
- For a=0, output=1 except when bc=00 (b=0,c=0).
So output = a + (b OR c).

Therefore, implement out = a | b | c.
