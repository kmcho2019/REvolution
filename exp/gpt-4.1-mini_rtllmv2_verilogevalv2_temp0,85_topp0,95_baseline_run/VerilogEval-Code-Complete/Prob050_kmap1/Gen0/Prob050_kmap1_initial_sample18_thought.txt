Analyze the Karnaugh map to find the minimized Boolean expression. The K-map is:

      a
   bc  0   1
   00 | 0 | 1 |
   01 | 1 | 1 |
   11 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 1 | 1 |

Cells with output 1 are:

- (bc=00, a=1) => b=0, c=0, a=1
- (bc=01, a=0 or 1) => b=0, c=1, a=don't care
- (bc=11, a=0 or 1) => b=1, c=1, a=don't care
- (bc=10, a=0 or 1) => b=1, c=0, a=don't care

The only zero is at (b=0, c=0, a=0).

Thus, the output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0.

So the output can be expressed as the negation of that minterm:

out = !(~a & ~b & ~c) = a + b + c

Hence, the output is the OR of inputs a, b, and c.
