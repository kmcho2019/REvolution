The circuit consists of three D flip-flops, each driven by a two-input gate with input x and feedback from the flip-flop output or its complement. Specifically:
- DFF1 input is XOR of x and Q1
- DFF2 input is AND of x and ~Q2
- DFF3 input is OR of x and ~Q3
The outputs Q1, Q2, Q3 then feed a 3-input NOR gate to produce output z.
All flip-flops are reset to zero initially.
We will implement this synchronous logic with a clocked always block, and output z as the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.
