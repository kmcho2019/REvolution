Implement three D flip-flops each driven by the output of a two-input gate:
- FF1 D input: XOR of x and FF1 output
- FF2 D input: AND of x and complement of FF2 output
- FF3 D input: OR of x and complement of FF3 output

The flip-flops are reset to 0 at start. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.

The design uses synchronous reset on posedge clk to initialize flip-flops to 0.

The module TopModule has inputs clk and x, and output z.
