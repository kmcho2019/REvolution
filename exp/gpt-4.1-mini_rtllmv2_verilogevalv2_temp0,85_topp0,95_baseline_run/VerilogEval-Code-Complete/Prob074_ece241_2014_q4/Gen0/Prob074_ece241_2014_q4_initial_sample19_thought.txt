Implement three D flip-flops with asynchronous reset initialized to zero. For each flip-flop, compute the D input from input x and the flip-flop's current output or its complement as follows:
- D1 = x XOR Q1
- D2 = x AND ~Q2
- D3 = x OR ~Q3
On the rising edge of clk, update the flip-flops with their respective D inputs. Finally, output z is the NOR of Q1, Q2, and Q3.
