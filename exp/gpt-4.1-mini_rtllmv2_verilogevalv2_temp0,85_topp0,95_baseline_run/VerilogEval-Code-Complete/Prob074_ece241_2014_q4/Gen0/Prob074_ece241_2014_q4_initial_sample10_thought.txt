Create three D flip-flops, each with input from a two-input gate: XOR, AND, OR.
- For the XOR gate: inputs are x and the flip-flop's own Q output.
- For the AND gate: inputs are x and the complement of its Q output.
- For the OR gate: inputs are x and the complement of its Q output.
On each clock edge, update the flip-flops with the corresponding gate outputs.
The output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.
Initialize all flip-flops to zero asynchronously.
