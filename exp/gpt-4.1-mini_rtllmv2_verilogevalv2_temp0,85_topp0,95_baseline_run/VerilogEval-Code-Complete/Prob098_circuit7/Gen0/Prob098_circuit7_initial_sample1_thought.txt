The output q changes only on the rising edge of clk. Initially q is unknown. 
At the first rising edge with a=0, q becomes 1. It remains 1 as long as a=0 on the clock edges.
When a changes to 1 at a rising edge, q becomes 0 and remains 0 thereafter. 
So the circuit sets q to 1 on the first rising edge if a=0, and resets it to 0 when a=1 at any rising edge. 
Effectively, q is a latch that sets to 1 on a=0, and clears to 0 on a=1 at rising edges of clk.
