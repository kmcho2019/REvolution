The circuit updates output q at the positive clock edge based on input a and the current state of q. Observing the waveform: 

- At the first rising edge with a=0, q becomes 1.
- When a=1 at the rising edge, q becomes 0.
- q holds its value on subsequent clock cycles until a changes and triggers a change in q at the next rising edge.

This behavior matches q being the negation of a latched at the positive clock edge, i.e., q <= ~a at every posedge clk.

So the implementation is a simple positive edge-triggered D flip-flop with q assigned to ~a.
