The output q changes only on the rising edge of clk. Observing the waveform:
- At 15ns (clk rising edge, a=0), q becomes 1.
- At 45ns (clk rising edge, a=1), q becomes 0.
- Between rising edges, q holds its value.

From this behavior, the output q is the inverse of the input a sampled on the rising edge of clk. So, q <= ~a at every rising edge of clk.
