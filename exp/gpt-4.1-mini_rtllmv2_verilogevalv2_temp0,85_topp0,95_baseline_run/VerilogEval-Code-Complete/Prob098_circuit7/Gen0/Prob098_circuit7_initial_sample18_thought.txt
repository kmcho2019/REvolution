The output q updates only on the rising edge of clk and holds its value otherwise. From the waveform, at clk rising edges:
- When a=0, q remains set to 1 once it becomes 1.
- When a=1, q is reset to 0.
Thus, q behaves as a reset-dominant latch triggered on the rising edge of clk: if a=1, q=0; if a=0, q=1 and holds until reset.
