The 7420 chip contains two separate 4-input NAND gates. Each gate outputs the NAND of its four inputs. The outputs p1y and p2y should be the NAND of their corresponding inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d), respectively. The NAND gate output is 0 only if all inputs are 1; otherwise, it is 1.
