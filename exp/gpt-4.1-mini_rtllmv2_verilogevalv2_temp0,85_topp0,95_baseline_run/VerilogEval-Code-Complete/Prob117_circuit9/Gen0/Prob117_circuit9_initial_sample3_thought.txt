The circuit updates the 3-bit register q on the rising edge of clk. When a=1, q is held constant at the value 4 (binary 100). When a=0, q increments by 1 mod 7 (0 to 6) each clock cycle, wrapping from 6 back to 0. Initially, q is undefined (x). So the behavior is: on clk rising edge, if a==1, q=4; else q increments modulo 7.
