The circuit updates on the rising edge of clk. When 'a' is high, q is loaded with 4 and held constant. When 'a' is low, q increments by 1 each clock cycle, wrapping around after 6 back to 0. The initial value is unknown (x). Thus, q acts as a counter that stays at 4 when a=1, and counts up modulo 7 (0 to 6) when a=0.
