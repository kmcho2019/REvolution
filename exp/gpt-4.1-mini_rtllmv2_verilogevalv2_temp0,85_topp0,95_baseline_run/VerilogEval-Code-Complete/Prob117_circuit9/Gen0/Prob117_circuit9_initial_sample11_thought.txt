The circuit samples input 'a' at the rising edge of 'clk'. When 'a' is 1, 'q' holds the value 4 constantly. When 'a' transitions to 0, 'q' starts counting up by 1 at every rising clock edge, wrapping from 6 back to 0. The initial value of 'q' is unknown (x), but we can initialize it to 4 since that's the stable output when 'a' is 1. So, if 'a' is 1, assign 4 to q; else increment q with wrap-around from 6 to 0.
