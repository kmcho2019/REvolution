The circuit updates its 3-bit output q only on the rising edge of clk. When input 'a' is high (1), q remains constant at 4. When 'a' becomes low (0), q increments by 1 on each rising clock edge, wrapping back to 0 after reaching 6. So, q is held at 4 while a=1, and counts up from 4 to 6 and then to 0, 1, etc., when a=0. Initialize q to 4.
