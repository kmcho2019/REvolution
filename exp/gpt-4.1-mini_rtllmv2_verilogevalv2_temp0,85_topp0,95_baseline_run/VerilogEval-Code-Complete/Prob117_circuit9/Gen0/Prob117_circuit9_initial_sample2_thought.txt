The output q is a 3-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk. When input a is high, q holds its value (4 in the waveform) and does not change. When a goes low, q increments by 1 at each rising clock edge, cycling from 7 back to 0. From the waveform, q starts at 4, holds when a=1, and increments when a=0. The increment is modulo 8. So the circuit latches q=4 when a=1 and counts up when a=0.
