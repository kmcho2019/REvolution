The output q remains constant at 4 while input a is 1. Once a becomes 0, q increments by 1 on each rising edge of clk, wrapping from 6 back to 0. The initial value of q at first clk rising edge is 4 when a=1. So the circuit holds q at 4 when a=1, and when a=0, it increments q on each clk rising edge modulo 7.
