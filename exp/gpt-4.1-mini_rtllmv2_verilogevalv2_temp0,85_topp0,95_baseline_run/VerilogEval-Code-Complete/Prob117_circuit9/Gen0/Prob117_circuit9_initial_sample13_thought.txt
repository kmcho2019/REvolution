The output q is a 3-bit register that holds a value updated on the rising edge of clk. When input 'a' is high (1), q remains constant at 4 (binary 100). When 'a' transitions to low (0), q increments by 1 each clock cycle, wrapping around modulo 7 (0 to 6). This matches the waveform where q stays at 4 while a=1, then counts up from 4 to 6, then rolls over to 0, 1, etc., when a=0.
