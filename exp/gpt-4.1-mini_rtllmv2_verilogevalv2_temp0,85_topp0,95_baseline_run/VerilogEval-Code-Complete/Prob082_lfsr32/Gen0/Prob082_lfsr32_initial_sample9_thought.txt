Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock cycle. The LFSR taps are at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based indexing), which correspond to bits 31, 21, 1, and 0 in zero-based indexing. The feedback bit is the XOR of the LSB (q[0]) with each tap bit before shifting. The shift proceeds from MSB down to LSB, where bits with taps XOR with the output bit q[0], and bits without taps just take the value of the bit to their left. The reset is synchronous and active high, setting q to 32'h1.
