Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. Since Verilog arrays are 0-indexed, these positions correspond to bits 31, 21, 1, and 0. On each clock cycle, if reset is asserted, synchronously reset q to 32'h1. Otherwise, shift q right by 1 bit, and update the tapped bits by XORing with the LSB before the shift. The LSB (q[0]) is the feedback bit that affects the tapped bits.
