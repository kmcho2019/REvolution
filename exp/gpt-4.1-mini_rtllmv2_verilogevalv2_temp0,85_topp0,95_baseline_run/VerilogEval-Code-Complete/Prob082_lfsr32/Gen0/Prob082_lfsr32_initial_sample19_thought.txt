Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 by shifting right each clock cycle. The LSB (q[0]) is used to determine feedback: if q[0] is 1, the bits at the tap positions are XORed with q[0] to form the next state of those bits. The reset is synchronous and active high, setting q to 32'h1.
