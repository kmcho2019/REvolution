Implement a 4-digit BCD counter where each digit is 4 bits, counting from 0000 to 9999.
- The ones digit increments every clock cycle.
- Each higher digit increments when the digit below it rolls over from 9 to 0.
- Provide enable signals `ena[3:1]` which go high for one clock cycle when their respective digit should increment.
- Include a synchronous active-high reset that clears the count to zero.
