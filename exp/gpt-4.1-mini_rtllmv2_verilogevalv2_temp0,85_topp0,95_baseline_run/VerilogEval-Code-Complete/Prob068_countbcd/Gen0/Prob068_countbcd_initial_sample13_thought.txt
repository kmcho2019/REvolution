Implement a 4-digit BCD counter using a 16-bit register q, where each 4 bits represent one BCD digit (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands). On every positive clock edge, if reset is high, clear q to 0. Otherwise, increment the counter by 1 in BCD format, properly carrying from ones to tens, tens to hundreds, and hundreds to thousands. Generate enable signals ena[1], ena[2], and ena[3] corresponding to when the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits should increment respectively, i.e., when the lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0.
