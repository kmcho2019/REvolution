Create a 4-digit BCD counter with digits q[3:0] (ones), q[7:4] (tens), q[11:8] (hundreds), and q[15:12] (thousands). On each clock, increment the ones digit. When it wraps from 9 to 0, enable and increment the tens digit (ena[1]). Similarly, when tens wraps from 9 to 0, enable and increment hundreds (ena[2]). When hundreds wraps, enable and increment thousands (ena[3]). Implement synchronous active-high reset to clear all digits. The ena signals go high for one clock cycle when the corresponding digit increments.
