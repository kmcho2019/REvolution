Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with synchronous active-high reset. The counter increments the ones digit (q[3:0]) every clock cycle. When a digit reaches 9 and rolls over to 0, it enables the next digit to increment on the next clock. We produce enable signals (ena[3], ena[2], ena[1]) indicating when digits 3, 2, and 1 should increment. The counter rolls over from 9999 back to 0000.
