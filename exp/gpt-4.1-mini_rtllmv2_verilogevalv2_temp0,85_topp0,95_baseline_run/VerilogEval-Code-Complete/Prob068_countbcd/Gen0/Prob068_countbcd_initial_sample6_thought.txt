Create a 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q[3:0], q[7:4], q[11:8], q[15:12]. Increment the ones digit on every clock if not reset. When ones digit reaches 9 and increments, enable the tens digit (ena[1]) to increment next cycle. Similarly, when tens digit reaches 9 and increments, enable hundreds digit (ena[2]) to increment, and so on. The ena signals are combinational outputs indicating when the upper digit should increment, i.e., when the lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0. The counter resets synchronously when reset is high, setting all digits to 0.
