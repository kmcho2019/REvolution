Implement a synchronous 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q[3:0], q[7:4], q[11:8], q[15:12]. On each clock cycle, increment the ones digit. When a digit reaches 9 and rolls over to 0, enable the next digit to increment by setting ena for that digit. The ena signals are generated based on whether the previous digit rolled over from 9 to 0. Reset synchronously clears all digits to 0.
