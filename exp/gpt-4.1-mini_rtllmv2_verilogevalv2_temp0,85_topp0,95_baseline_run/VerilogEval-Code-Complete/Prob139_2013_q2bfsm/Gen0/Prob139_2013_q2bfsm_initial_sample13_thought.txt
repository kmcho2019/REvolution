We design a synchronous FSM with active low synchronous reset. The FSM has states:
- A: Initial state, stays here while resetn=0. On resetn=1, transition to B.
- B: Output f=1 for one clock cycle, then transition to C.
- C: Monitor x input to detect the sequence 1,0,1 over three clock cycles using internal counters or states.
- D: Output g=1 for one clock cycle after detecting 1,0,1 on x, then move to E.
- E: Maintain g=1 and monitor y input for up to two clock cycles. If y=1 within these two cycles, stay in E permanently with g=1. If not, go to F.
- F: g=0 permanently until reset.

Outputs f and g are registered and updated synchronously based on the FSM state.

We'll implement the FSM using a state register and combinational logic for next state and outputs.
