Implement a 4-bit register q updated on the rising edge of clk. When shift_ena is high, shift q left by one bit and input 'data' into the least significant bit (MSB first means the new bit goes into q[3], shifting the rest down). When count_ena is high, decrement the 4-bit value in q by 1. Since shift_ena and count_ena are never high together, no priority is needed for simultaneous high inputs.
