Design a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. On an asynchronous positive edge reset (areset), the FSM returns to WALK_LEFT. On each clock edge, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted (including both), the FSM toggles direction: if walking left, switch to walking right, and vice versa. Outputs are driven by the current state: walk_left is high in WALK_LEFT state, walk_right is high in WALK_RIGHT state.
