We will model the Lemming as a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The state transitions occur on the rising edge of clk or asynchronously reset by areset. On reset, the FSM goes to WALK_LEFT. If bumped on the left (bump_left=1), the Lemming will walk right; if bumped on the right (bump_right=1), the Lemming will walk left. If bumped on both sides, it also switches direction. Otherwise, it maintains its current state. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are derived directly from the current state.
