To generate shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles after reset, we use a 2-bit counter that increments each clock cycle while counting from 0 to 3. When reset is asserted, the counter starts at 0 and shift_ena is asserted for the first 4 cycles (counter < 4). After reaching 4, shift_ena goes low and remains low until the next reset. The reset is synchronous and active high.
