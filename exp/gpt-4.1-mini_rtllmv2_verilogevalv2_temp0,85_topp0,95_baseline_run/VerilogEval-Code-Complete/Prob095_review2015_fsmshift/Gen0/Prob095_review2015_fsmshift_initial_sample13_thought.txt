To implement shift_ena being asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles after reset, we can use a 3-bit counter that counts from 0 to 3. On reset (synchronous, active high), the counter is reset to 0 and shift_ena is asserted. Each clock cycle after reset increments the counter. While the counter is less than 4, shift_ena remains high; after that, it goes low forever until the next reset.
