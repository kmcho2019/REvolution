To implement a synchronous active-high reset that asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles after reset, we will use a 3-bit counter that starts counting from 4 down to 0. When reset is asserted, the counter is loaded with 4, and shift_ena is asserted as long as the counter is not zero. Each clock cycle, the counter decrements by 1. Once it reaches zero, shift_ena is deasserted and remains low until the next reset.
