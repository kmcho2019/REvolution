To create a synchronous active-high reset that triggers the shift_ena signal for exactly 4 clock cycles, we use a 3-bit counter that counts from 0 to 3. On reset, the counter is loaded with 0 and shift_ena is asserted. For the next 3 clock cycles, the counter increments, keeping shift_ena high. After reaching 4 clock cycles, shift_ena goes low and remains low until the next reset. This is implemented with a synchronous always block triggered on the clock's rising edge.
