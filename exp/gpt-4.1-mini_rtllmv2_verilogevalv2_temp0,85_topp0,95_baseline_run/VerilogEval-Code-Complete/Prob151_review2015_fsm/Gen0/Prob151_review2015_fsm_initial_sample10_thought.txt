We will implement a finite-state machine with the following states:
1. IDLE: searching for the pattern 1101 on the serial input `data`. We'll shift in bits and detect the pattern continuously.
2. SHIFT: after detecting the pattern, assert `shift_ena` for exactly 4 clock cycles to shift in the delay value.
3. COUNTING: assert `counting` and wait until `done_counting` is asserted.
4. DONE: assert `done` to notify user, then wait for `ack` input.
On synchronous reset, return to IDLE state.

For detecting the pattern 1101 in serial input, maintain a 4-bit shift register that shifts in `data` every clock. When the 4 bits equal 1101, transition from IDLE to SHIFT.

We'll implement a counter in SHIFT state to count 4 cycles of shift_ena assertion.

Signals:
- state: FSM state register
- pattern_reg: 4-bit shift register holding last 4 bits of input data
- shift_counter: counts 4 cycles of shift_ena

Outputs are asserted only in their respective states.

This approach ensures that the FSM meets the requirements and cleanly sequences through the steps.
