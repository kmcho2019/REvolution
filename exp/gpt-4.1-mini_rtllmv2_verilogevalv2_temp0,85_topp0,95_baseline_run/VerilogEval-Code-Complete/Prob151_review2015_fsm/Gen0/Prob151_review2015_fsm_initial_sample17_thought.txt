The FSM has the following states:
- SEARCH: waiting for the pattern 1101 on the serial data input.
- SHIFT: after detecting 1101, assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles to shift in duration bits.
- COUNT: assert counting until done_counting input is high.
- DONE: assert done and wait for ack input to reset to SEARCH.

To detect the pattern 1101, keep track of the last 4 bits shifted in serially using a 4-bit shift register updated every clock cycle. When the register matches 1101, move to SHIFT.

In SHIFT state, keep a 2-bit counter for 4 cycles of asserting shift_ena, then move to COUNT.

In COUNT, assert counting until done_counting is high, then move to DONE.

In DONE, assert done until ack is high, then reset to SEARCH.

Use synchronous reset to initialize FSM and the shift register to 0.
