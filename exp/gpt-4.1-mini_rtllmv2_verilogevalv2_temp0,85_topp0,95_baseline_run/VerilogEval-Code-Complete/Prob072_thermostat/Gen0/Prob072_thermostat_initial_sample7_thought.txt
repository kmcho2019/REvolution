Implement combinational logic that checks the mode to decide whether to operate the heater or air conditioner. In heating mode (mode=1), turn on the heater if too_cold=1 and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode (mode=0), turn on the air conditioner if too_hot=1 and turn off the heater. The fan is turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if fan_on is asserted by the user.
