To implement the function from the Karnaugh map with don't-care conditions, first interpret the map as follows:

x[3]x[4] rows: 00, 01, 11, 10  
x[1]x[2] columns: 00, 01, 11, 10  

Fill in the values with d=don't care:

       00  01  11  10
00 |  d | 0 | d | d |
01 | 0 | d | 1 | 0 |
11 | 1 | 1 | d | d |
10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | d |

We want to find a minimal sum-of-products expression for f, using the don't-cares to simplify.

Step 1: List all 1's (minterms):
- (x3x4, x1x2) = (01,11) => x3=0,x4=1,x1=1,x2=1
- (11,00), (11,01), (10,00), (10,01), (11,00) and (11,01) are 1's
- Also (10,00)=1 and (10,01)=1

From the map:
- (11,00) = 1
- (11,01) = 1
- (10,00) = 1
- (10,01) = 1
- (01,11) = 1

Step 2: Try to find groups:

Group 1: (11,00),(11,01),(10,00),(10,01) -> Covers x3x4 = 1x, x1x2 = 00 or 01  
- x3 = 1 (since rows 11 and 10 both have x3=1)
- x4 varies (1 or 0)
- x1 = 0 (since columns 00 and 01 have x1=0)
- x2 varies (0 or 1)

So group1: x3 & ~x1

Group 2: (01,11) = x3=0,x4=1,x1=1,x2=1

No other 1's nearby with don't care to group with, so single minterm: ~x3 & x4 & x1 & x2

Step 3: Final expression:

f = (x3 & ~x1) | (~x3 & x4 & x1 & x2)
