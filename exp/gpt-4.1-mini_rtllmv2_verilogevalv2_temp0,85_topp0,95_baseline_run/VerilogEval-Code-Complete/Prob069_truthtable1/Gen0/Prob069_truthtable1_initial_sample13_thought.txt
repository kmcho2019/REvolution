Analyze the truth table and derive the boolean expression for f. The output f is 1 for input combinations: 010, 011, 101, and 111. These correspond to the minterms: 
- x3=0, x2=1, x1=0  -> ~x3 & x2 & ~x1
- x3=0, x2=1, x1=1  -> ~x3 & x2 & x1
- x3=1, x2=0, x1=1  -> x3 & ~x2 & x1
- x3=1, x2=1, x1=1  -> x3 & x2 & x1

We can simplify it as: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)

Implement this logic combinationally in Verilog.
