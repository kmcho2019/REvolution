Analyze the truth table and derive a simplified Boolean expression for f. The minterms where f=1 are:
(0,1,0), (0,1,1), (1,0,1), (1,1,1).

From the table:
f = x2 & ~x1 | x2 & x1 | x3 & ~x2 & x1 | x3 & x2 & x1

Simplify:
f = (x2 & ~x1) + (x2 & x1) + (x3 & ~x2 & x1) + (x3 & x2 & x1)
= x2 + x3 & x1

So the simplified expression is:
f = x2 | (x3 & x1)

Implement this combinational logic in Verilog.
