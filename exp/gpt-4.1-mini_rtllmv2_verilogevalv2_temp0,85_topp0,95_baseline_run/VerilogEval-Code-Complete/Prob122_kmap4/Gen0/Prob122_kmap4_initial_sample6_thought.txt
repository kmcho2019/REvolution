The given Karnaugh map is 4-variable with variables a,b for columns and c,d for rows.

K-map:
       ab
cd    00  01  11  10
00 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
01 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
11 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
10 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |

From the K-map, the output is 1 when the number of 1s in inputs (a,b,c,d) is odd:
- Check each cell where output=1:
  For example, at cd=00 (c=0,d=0), ab=01 (a=0,b=1) → total ones=1 → odd
  At cd=01 (0,1), ab=00 (0,0) → ones=1 → odd
  At cd=01 (0,1), ab=11 (1,1) → ones=3 → odd
  At cd=10 (1,0), ab=00 (0,0) → ones=1 → odd
  etc.

This pattern corresponds to the XOR of all inputs: out = a ^ b ^ c ^ d.

Thus, the implementation is simply an XOR of all inputs.
