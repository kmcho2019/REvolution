The Karnaugh map has inputs a,b on columns and c,d on rows in the order shown.

The map values are:
cd\ab | 00 | 01 | 11 | 10
  00  | 0  | 1  | 0  | 1
  01  | 1  | 0  | 1  | 0
  11  | 0  | 1  | 0  | 1
  10  | 1  | 0  | 1  | 0

Observing the pattern, the output is 1 if the number of 1s in inputs a,b,c,d is odd. 
This corresponds to the parity function (odd parity).

Thus, out = a ^ b ^ c ^ d

We implement this by assigning out to the XOR of the inputs.
