This dual driving can lead to race conditions or glitches on 'done', as the combinational block overwrites the registered 'done'. The simulation mismatch hints strongly at this timing problem.

However, the `next_state` transition to check for the stop bit happens when `bit_count == 8`. Additionally, the start bit detection and counting logic do not include any synchronization or sampling delay, which may cause the FSM to miss or misinterpret bits.
