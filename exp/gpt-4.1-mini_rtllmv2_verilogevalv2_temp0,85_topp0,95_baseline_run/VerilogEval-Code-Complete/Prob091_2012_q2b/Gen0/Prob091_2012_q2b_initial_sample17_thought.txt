We have a one-hot encoded state machine with states A, B, C, D, E, F represented by y[0] to y[5]. We need to find the next state inputs for flip-flops y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D).

From the state transition table:
- For B (y[1]): next B is reached from A on input w=1.
- For D (y[3]): next D is reached from B on w=0, from C on w=0, from E on w=0, and from F on w=0.

Therefore:
- Y1 (input to flip-flop y[1]) = y[0] & w
- Y3 (input to flip-flop y[3]) = (y[1] & ~w) | (y[2] & ~w) | (y[4] & ~w) | (y[5] & ~w)
= ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

We write these as continuous assignments in Verilog.
