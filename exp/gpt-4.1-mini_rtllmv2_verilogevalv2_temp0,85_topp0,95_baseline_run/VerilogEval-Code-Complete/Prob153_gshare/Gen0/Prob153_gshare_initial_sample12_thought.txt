Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history register. The index to the PHT (Pattern History Table) is formed by XORing the 7-bit PC and the 7-bit global history register. The PHT has 128 entries, each a 2-bit saturating counter representing branch prediction states. On a prediction request (predict_valid), output the prediction and the current global history, and update the global history with the predicted outcome on the next clock edge. On a training request (train_valid), update the corresponding PHT entry according to the actual outcome. If the branch was mispredicted (train_mispredicted), restore the global history to the train_history (the state after the branch). If prediction and training happen simultaneously and training is for a misprediction, training update and history restore take precedence over prediction update. The PHT updates and global history updates occur on the positive clock edge. Prediction reads are combinational and reflect the PHT state before any updates. Reset is asynchronous active-high and clears the global history and resets all PHT entries to weakly not taken (2'b01).
