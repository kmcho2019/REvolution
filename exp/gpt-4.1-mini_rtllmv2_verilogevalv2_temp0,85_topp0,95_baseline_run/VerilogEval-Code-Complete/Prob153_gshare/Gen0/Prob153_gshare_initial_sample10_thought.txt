Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry (2^7) pattern history table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters. The index into the PHT is the XOR of the 7-bit PC and 7-bit history.

Prediction interface:
- When predict_valid is asserted, the predictor outputs the prediction bit and the history register used for the prediction.
- At the next clock edge, the global history register is updated by shifting in the predicted branch direction.
- If a training and prediction happen in the same cycle, training takes precedence in updating the history register.

Training interface:
- On train_valid, update the 2-bit counter at the PHT entry indexed by XOR of train_pc and train_history.
- If the branch was mispredicted, restore the global history register to train_history.
- The PHT update and history update happen on the next clock edge.

Important details:
- Prediction outputs the state of the PHT before any updates.
- PHT updates occur on the next positive clock edge.
- The global history register update from prediction or training also happens on the clock edge after the inputs are sampled.
- Asynchronous active-high reset clears the global history register and initializes the PHT counters to weakly taken (2'b10).

The module will have a synchronous process for updating the PHT and global history register, and combinational logic for computing the prediction outputs.
