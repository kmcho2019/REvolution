Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history. The 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history are XORed to form a 7-bit index into a 128-entry PHT (pattern history table) of 2-bit saturating counters.

Prediction interface:
- On predict_valid, compute index = predict_pc ^ ghr, output prediction based on PHT entry.
- Output the predicted taken bit and the current ghr used for prediction.
- On next clock edge, update ghr with the predicted branch direction if no training misprediction overrides.

Training interface:
- On train_valid, use train_pc and train_history to index into PHT and update its 2-bit counter based on train_taken.
- If train_mispredicted, restore ghr to train_history (the history after the mispredicted branch).
- Training takes precedence over prediction in updating ghr.
- PHT updates happen synchronously on the clock edge.

The module has asynchronous active-high reset:
- On reset, clear ghr and initialize PHT entries to weakly taken (e.g., 2'b10).

Additional details:
- When prediction and training happen in the same cycle for the same PHT entry, prediction sees PHT before training.
- If training and prediction update ghr at the same cycle, training's ghr update overrides prediction's.
