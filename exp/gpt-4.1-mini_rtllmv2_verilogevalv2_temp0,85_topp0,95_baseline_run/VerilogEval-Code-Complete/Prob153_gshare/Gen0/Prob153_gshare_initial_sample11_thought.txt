Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 128-entry pattern history table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by the XOR of the 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history. The global history register (7 bits) is updated on predictions or training with precedence to training if both happen in the same cycle. Prediction outputs the taken bit and the history used for that prediction. Training updates the saturating counter and, if mispredicted, recovers the history register to the train_history state. All PHT updates and history updates occur on the positive clock edge. The asynchronous reset clears the history and initializes PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10).
