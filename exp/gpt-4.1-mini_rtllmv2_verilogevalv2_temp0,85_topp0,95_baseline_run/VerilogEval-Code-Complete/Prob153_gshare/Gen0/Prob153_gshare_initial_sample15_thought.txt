Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history, XORed to form a 7-bit index into a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters. The predictor has two interfaces: prediction and training. Prediction returns the predicted branch direction and the history used, then updates the branch history register with the predicted outcome on the next clock edge. Training updates the PHT based on the actual outcome and recovers the branch history register if there was a misprediction. If training and prediction requests collide, training takes precedence in updating the history register. The PHT updates and history updates occur on the positive clock edge, with asynchronous active-high reset for initialization.
