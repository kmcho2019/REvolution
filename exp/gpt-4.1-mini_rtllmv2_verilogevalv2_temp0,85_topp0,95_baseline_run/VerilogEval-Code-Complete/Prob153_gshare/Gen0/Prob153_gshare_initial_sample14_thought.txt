Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history XORed to index a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters. Maintain a 7-bit global history register updated each cycle with the prediction outcome, except when training with a misprediction which recovers the history to the trained branch's post-branch history. Prediction returns the predicted branch direction and the history used for prediction before updating the history at the clock edge. Training updates the PHT entry based on the actual outcome and if mispredicted recovers the global history. When prediction and training occur simultaneously, training takes precedence in updating the history, but prediction reads PHT state before training. The design uses asynchronous reset, updates PHT and history at posedge clk, and provides combinational prediction outputs.
