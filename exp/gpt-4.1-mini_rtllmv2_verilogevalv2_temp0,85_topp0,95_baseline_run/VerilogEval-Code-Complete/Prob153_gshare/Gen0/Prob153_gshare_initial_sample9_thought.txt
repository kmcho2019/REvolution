Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history XORed to index a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters. The global history register (GHR) is 7 bits. For prediction requests (predict_valid), output the predicted direction (taken if saturating counter MSB=1) and the GHR used for prediction, then update the GHR with the predicted direction on the next clock edge. For training requests (train_valid), update the PHT entry based on the actual outcome, and if mispredicted, recover the GHR to the given train_history. If prediction and training requests happen in the same cycle, training takes precedence in updating the GHR. PHT updates occur on the clock edge, and predictions see the PHT state before training updates. Asynchronous reset initializes GHR to 0 and PHT entries to weakly not taken (2'b01). Prediction and training indexing use XOR of PC and history.
