To add two 8-bit 2's complement numbers and detect overflow, we sum a and b into a 9-bit result to capture any carry out. The 8-bit sum s is the lower 8 bits of the result. Overflow in signed addition occurs if the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of the sign bit. Equivalently, overflow = (sign of a == sign of b) && (sign of s != sign of a).
