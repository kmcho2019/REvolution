To add two 8-bit signed (2's complement) numbers and detect overflow, perform the addition with a 9-bit result to capture the carry out. Overflow in 2's complement addition occurs when the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of the sign bit. Another way is to check if the sign of the result is incorrect based on the signs of the inputs: overflow occurs if a and b have the same sign, but the sum has a different sign. Implement this logic and output the 8-bit sum and the overflow flag.
