To add two 8-bit two's complement numbers and detect signed overflow, perform the addition with a wider bit width to capture the carry out. Signed overflow occurs if the sign of the sum is incorrect based on the signs of the inputs: specifically, if the two inputs have the same sign but the sum has a different sign. We can detect overflow by checking the carry into the sign bit and the carry out of the sign bit or by comparing the sign bits of inputs and output.
