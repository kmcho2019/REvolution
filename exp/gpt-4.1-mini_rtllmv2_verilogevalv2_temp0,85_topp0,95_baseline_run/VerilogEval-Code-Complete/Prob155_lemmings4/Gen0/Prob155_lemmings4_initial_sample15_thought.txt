We model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with states representing the current action and direction: walking left or right, falling (with direction), digging (with direction), and splattered. Walking states allow direction changes on bumps; falling and digging states ignore bumps. Falling time is counted, and splatter occurs if fall duration exceeds 20 cycles on ground contact. On asynchronous reset, the state is walk_left. Outputs depend solely on the state. Priority on transitions when walking: fall > dig > bump-induced direction change. Digging starts only on ground and walking state with dig=1. Falling starts on ground=0. After falling or digging ends, walking resumes in the original direction. Being bumped while falling or digging has no effect. Splattered state disables all outputs.
