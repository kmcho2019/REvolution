We will design a Moore FSM to model the Lemming's behavior with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- FALLING_LEFT: falling while previously walking left
- FALLING_RIGHT: falling while previously walking right
- DIGGING_LEFT: digging while walking left on ground
- DIGGING_RIGHT: digging while walking right on ground
- SPLATTERED: once splattered, no outputs active forever

The FSM transitions according to the priority:
1. If on ground=0 and not already falling or splattered, transition to falling state corresponding to current walking direction.
2. If dig=1 and currently walking on ground (not falling) and not splattered, transition to digging in current walking direction.
3. If bumped on left or right while walking on ground, switch walking direction.
4. Falling duration is counted by a counter; if it reaches >20 cycles and then ground=1, splatter and transition to SPLATTERED.

During falling or digging, bumps and dig inputs are ignored. On ground re-appearance, falling transitions back to walking in the original direction.

Outputs depend only on state:
- WALK_LEFT: walk_left=1
- WALK_RIGHT: walk_right=1
- FALLING_LEFT or FALLING_RIGHT: aaah=1
- DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT: digging=1
- SPLATTERED: all outputs=0

We will implement asynchronous reset (areset) to WALK_LEFT state and clear counters.

The state encoding will be one-hot for simplicity and clarity.
