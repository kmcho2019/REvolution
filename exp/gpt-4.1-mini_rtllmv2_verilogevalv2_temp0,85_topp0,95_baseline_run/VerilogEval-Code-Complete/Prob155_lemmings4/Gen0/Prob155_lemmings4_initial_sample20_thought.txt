To model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM, we define states capturing walking direction (left or right), falling, digging, and splattered (dead). Walking states keep track of direction and handle bumps to switch direction. Falling state counts fall cycles; if over 20 and ground hits, transition to splattered. Digging starts only when ground=1 and walking, and continues until ground=0, then fall. Outputs depend solely on state. Inputs affecting state transitions are evaluated on clock edge. An asynchronous active-high reset forces the Lemming into walking left state. Priority for transitions is fall > dig > bump direction switch. Bumps do not affect direction during falling or digging. The fall counter resets when not falling, increments while falling, and triggers splatter if >20 upon ground contact. Splattered state is terminal until reset.
