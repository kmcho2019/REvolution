Design a Moore FSM with states representing direction (left/right) and activity (walking, falling, digging, splattered). Use a fall counter to track duration of fall; if it exceeds 20 upon landing, transition to splattered state. On asynchronous reset, initialize to walking left. Handle input precedence: falling > digging > direction change. Outputs depend only on the current state. Bumps affect walking states only, not falling or digging. Digging only starts if on ground and walking. Falling overrides all actions; when ground disappears, start falling and increment fall counter each cycle; when ground returns, if fall count > 20, splatter; else resume previous walking direction. Digging continues until ground disappears, then fall. Maintain direction during fall and digging for resuming walking. The FSM states are: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALL_LEFT, FALL_RIGHT, DIG_LEFT, DIG_RIGHT, SPLATTERED. Transition logic is based on inputs and current state.
