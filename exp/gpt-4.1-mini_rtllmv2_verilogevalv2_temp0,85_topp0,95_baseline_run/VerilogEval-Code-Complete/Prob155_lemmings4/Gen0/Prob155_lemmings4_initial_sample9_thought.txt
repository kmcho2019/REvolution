Design a Moore FSM with distinct states to represent walking left, walking right, falling, digging, and splattered. Walking states track direction. Falling counts cycles to detect splatter condition. Inputs bump_left/right cause direction changes only when walking. Falling and digging override walking and direction changes according to priority. The FSM transitions asynchronously reset to walking left. Outputs are solely determined by current state. A counter tracks fall duration; if >20 cycles when ground reappears, go to splattered state. Once splattered, stay there forever until reset.
