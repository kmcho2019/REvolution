Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. The clock increments seconds on ena pulses. Seconds count from 00 to 59 in BCD, rolling over to 00 and incrementing minutes. Minutes similarly count from 00 to 59 in BCD, rolling over to 00 and incrementing hours. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When hours increment from 11 to 12, toggle PM. When hours roll over from 12 to 01, continue counting normally. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM. All counters are implemented in BCD, with proper carry and roll-over handling.
