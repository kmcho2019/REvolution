Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. The clock increments seconds, minutes, and hours on the 'ena' pulse (once per second). Seconds and minutes count 00 to 59 in BCD; hours count 01 to 12 in BCD. The PM signal toggles every 12 hours (when hour wraps from 11 to 12 or from 12 to 1). On reset, set time to 12:00 AM. Use BCD counting with carry propagation to the next digit.
