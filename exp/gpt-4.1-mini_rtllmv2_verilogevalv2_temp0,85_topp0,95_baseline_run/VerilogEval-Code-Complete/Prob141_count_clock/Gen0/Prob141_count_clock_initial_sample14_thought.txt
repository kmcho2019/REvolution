Implement three cascaded BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each second increment (when ena is high) increments the seconds counter. When seconds reach 59 and increment, minutes increment. When minutes reach 59 and increment, hours increment. The hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD, with a separate pm flag toggling when going from 11 to 12. On reset, set hh to 12 (0x12), mm and ss to 0, and pm to 0 (AM).
