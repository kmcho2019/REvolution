Implement a synchronous resettable 12-hour clock counter with AM/PM indicator. The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and the hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. On each enable pulse (once per second), increment seconds. When seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes. When minutes roll over from 59 to 00, increment hours. When hours roll over from 12 to 01, toggle the PM indicator. The reset synchronously sets the clock to 12:00 AM (12:00:00, pm=0). Each BCD digit is 4 bits, so 8 bits for hh/mm/ss represent two digits.
