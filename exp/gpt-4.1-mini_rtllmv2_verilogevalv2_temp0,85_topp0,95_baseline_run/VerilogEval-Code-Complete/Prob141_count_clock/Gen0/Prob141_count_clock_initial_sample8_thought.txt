Implement a BCD-based 12-hour clock counting seconds, minutes, and hours with AM/PM indicator. On each ena pulse, increment seconds; when seconds reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment minutes; similarly for minutes rolling over to hours. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD format. PM toggles every time hour rolls from 11 to 12. Reset sets time to 12:00 AM. Since BCD is used, each digit is 4 bits, so we must handle each digit separately with proper rollovers and increments.
