Implement a synchronous counter that increments seconds by 1 BCD digit at each pulse on ena. When seconds reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment minutes similarly. When minutes reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment hours in 12-hour format (01-12) with BCD digits. The pm signal toggles when hours roll from 11 to 12. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM.
