Implement BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, and hours count from 01 to 12. Each digit is 4 bits, so two digits per unit (e.g., ss[7:4] is tens, ss[3:0] is ones). On each enable pulse (ena), increment seconds. If seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes. If minutes roll over from 59 to 00, increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12, and after 12 roll over to 1. The PM flag toggles every 12 hours (when hour rolls from 11 to 12, then next step from 12 to 1 toggles PM). Reset is synchronous and asynchronous to enable; when reset asserted, set time to 12:00:00 AM.
