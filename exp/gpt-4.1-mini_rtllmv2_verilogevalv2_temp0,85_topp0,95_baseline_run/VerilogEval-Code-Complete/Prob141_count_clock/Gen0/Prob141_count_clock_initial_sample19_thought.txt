Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator using BCD counters. The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, and hours count from 01 to 12. On reset, the clock is set to 12:00 AM. On each enable pulse (once per second), seconds increment; when seconds roll over from 59 to 00, minutes increment; similarly, when minutes roll over from 59 to 00, hours increment. The hour counter cycles through 01-12. When transitioning from 11 to 12, PM toggles. When going from 12 to 1, AM/PM remains the same. The PM bit indicates PM when asserted, else AM.
