To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indication and BCD outputs, we need to create counters for seconds, minutes, and hours that increment on the ena pulse. The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and the hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. We will update seconds first; upon overflow, increment minutes; upon minutes overflow, increment hours. The PM indicator toggles when the hour counter rolls over from 11 to 12. The reset signal synchronously resets the clock to 12:00 AM. The hh, mm, and ss outputs are each two-digit BCD values, with the high nibble representing the tens digit and the low nibble representing the ones digit.
