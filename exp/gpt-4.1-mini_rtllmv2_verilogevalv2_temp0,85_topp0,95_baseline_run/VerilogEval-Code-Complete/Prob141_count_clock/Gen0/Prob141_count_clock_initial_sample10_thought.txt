Implement a synchronous, active-high resettable 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator. Use BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59; hours count from 01 to 12. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 00 and increment minutes. When minutes reach 59 and increment, reset minutes to 00 and increment hours. Hours increment from 01 to 12, after 11:59:59 AM the clock should switch to PM, and after 11:59:59 PM switch back to AM (toggle pm signal). Reset sets time to 12:00 AM regardless of enable signal.
