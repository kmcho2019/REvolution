Implement three cascaded BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter counts in BCD format. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, and upon reaching 59 and incrementing, roll over to 00 and increment the next higher unit. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD, rolling over to 01 after 12. The PM indicator toggles every time the hour rolls from 11 to 12 (meaning after 11:59:59). Synchronous active-high reset sets the clock to 12:00 AM.
