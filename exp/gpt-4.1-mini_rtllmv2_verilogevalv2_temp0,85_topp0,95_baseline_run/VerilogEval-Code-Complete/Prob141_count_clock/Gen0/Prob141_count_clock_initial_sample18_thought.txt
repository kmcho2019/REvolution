Implement a synchronous clock counting module with separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. On each enable pulse (once per second), increment seconds; if seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes; if minutes roll over from 59 to 00, increment hours. The hour counter runs from 1 to 12 in BCD, rolling over from 12 to 1. The pm flag toggles on the hour rollover from 11 to 12 (i.e., when the hour increments from 11 to 12, pm toggles). Reset synchronously sets time to 12:00:00 AM (pm=0, hh=12, mm=00, ss=00). The BCD counters handle decimal digit increments with proper carry and rollovers.
