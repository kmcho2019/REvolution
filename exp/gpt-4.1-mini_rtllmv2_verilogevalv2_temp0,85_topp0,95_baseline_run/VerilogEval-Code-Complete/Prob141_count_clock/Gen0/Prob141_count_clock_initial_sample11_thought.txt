We will implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD digits for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters increment on the ena pulse (once per second). The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and the hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When seconds reach 59 and increment, minutes increment; similarly for minutes to hours. The hour rolls from 12 back to 1 and toggles the pm bit to switch between AM and PM. On reset (active high synchronous), the clock sets to 12:00 AM. We will handle BCD increments and rollovers carefully.
