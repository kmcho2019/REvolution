Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock counter with BCD-encoded hours, minutes, and seconds. On each enable pulse (once per second), increment seconds, rolling over from 59 to 00 and incrementing minutes. Minutes roll over from 59 to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12, with a wrap-around back to 1. The AM/PM indicator toggles when the hour rolls over from 11 to 12. Reset synchronously sets the time to 12:00 AM. All counters use BCD encoding with two digits per time unit.
