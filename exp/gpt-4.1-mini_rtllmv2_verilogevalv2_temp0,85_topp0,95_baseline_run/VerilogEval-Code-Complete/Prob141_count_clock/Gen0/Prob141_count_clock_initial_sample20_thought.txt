Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The clock increments once per second when ena is high. The seconds count from 00 to 59 in BCD, minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment minutes similarly. When minutes reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 01 to 12, rolling over to 01 after 12. The pm flag toggles when hours roll from 11 to 12 (transition from AM to PM or PM to AM). On reset, set time to 12:00:00 AM. Use synchronous reset with highest priority.
