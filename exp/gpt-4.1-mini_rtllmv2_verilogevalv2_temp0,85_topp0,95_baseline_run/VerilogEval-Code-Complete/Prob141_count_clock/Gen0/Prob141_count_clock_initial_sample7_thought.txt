Implement the 12-hour clock by creating separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each digit is represented as 4 bits (BCD). Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, with proper BCD rollovers: units digit counts 0-9, tens digit counts 0-5. Hours count 01 to 12. We toggle the pm indicator each time the hour rolls from 11 to 12 (noon or midnight transition). Reset synchronously sets the clock to 12:00 AM. The clock increments by one second on each ena pulse.
