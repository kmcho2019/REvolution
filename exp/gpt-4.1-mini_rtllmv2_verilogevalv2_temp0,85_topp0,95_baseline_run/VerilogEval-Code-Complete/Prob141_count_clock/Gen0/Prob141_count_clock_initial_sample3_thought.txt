Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indication. On reset, set the time to 12:00:00 AM (hh=0x12, mm=0x00, ss=0x00, pm=0). On each enable pulse (once per second), increment seconds BCD. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 00 and increment minutes BCD. When minutes reach 59 and increment, reset minutes to 00 and increment hours BCD. For hours, count from 1 to 12 in BCD. When hours increment from 11 to 12, toggle PM signal. When hours increment from 12 to 1, continue toggling PM (12-hour cycle). Use BCD counting carefully with carry conditions for each digit. Since hours are 01-12, handle that special case with two BCD digits (0x01 to 0x12).
