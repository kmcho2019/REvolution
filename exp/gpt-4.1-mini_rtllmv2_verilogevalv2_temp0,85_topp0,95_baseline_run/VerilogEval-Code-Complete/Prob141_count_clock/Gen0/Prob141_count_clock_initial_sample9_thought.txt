Implement three BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter will increment when enabled and roll over appropriately. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, so when the ones digit hits 9 and increments, it resets to 0 and the tens digit increments. When seconds reach 59 and increment, minutes increment, and similarly for hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD with special rollover rules: from 12, it rolls over to 1 and toggles the pm indicator. The reset synchronously sets the time to 12:00 AM.
