Design a 3-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Initially in IDLE, ignore bytes until we receive one with in[3]=1 (start of message). Upon receiving this first byte, move to BYTE2 state. Then accept the next byte and move to BYTE3. Then accept the third byte and assert done for one cycle, returning to IDLE to look for next message. Reset synchronously sets FSM to IDLE and done to 0.
