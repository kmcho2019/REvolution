Implement the XOR gate in three ways:
1. Using an assign statement directly assigning the XOR of a and b to out_assign.
2. Using a combinational always block (always @*) to assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_comb.
3. Using a clocked always block (always @(posedge clk)) to register the XOR of a and b, producing a delayed output on out_always_ff.
