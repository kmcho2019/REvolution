The output z is determined by inputs x and y in a specific pattern shown in the waveform. Observing the waveform:

- At times when both x=0 and y=0, z=1
- When x=1 and y=0, z=0
- When x=0 and y=1, z=0
- When x=1 and y=1, z=1

This corresponds exactly to the XNOR of x and y (z = ~(x ^ y)), or equivalently z = (x & y) | (~x & ~y).

Implementing z as the XNOR of x and y will produce the waveform shown.
