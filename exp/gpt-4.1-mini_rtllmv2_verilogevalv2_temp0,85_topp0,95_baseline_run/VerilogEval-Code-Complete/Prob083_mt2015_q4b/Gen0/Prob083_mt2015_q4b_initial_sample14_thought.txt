The waveform shows output z is 1 only in specific input conditions. From the waveform table, z=1 when:
- At time 0 to 20 ns, x=0 and y=0 -> z=1
- At time 45 to 50 ns and 70 ns, x=1 and y=1 -> z=1
- At time 55 ns, x=0 and y=0 -> z=1

For all other cases, z=0.

So, the output z is 1 if (x=0 and y=0) or (x=1 and y=1). This describes an XNOR operation of inputs x and y.

We can implement this with assign statement: z = ~(x ^ y);
