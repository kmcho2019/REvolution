The waveform describes output z that depends on inputs x and y. Observing the waveform:
- When both x and y are 0, z is 1.
- When either x=1 and y=0, or x=0 and y=1, z is 0.
- When both x and y are 1, z is 1.

This matches the behavior of the XNOR operation (z = ~(x ^ y)), or equivalently z = (x & y) | (~x & ~y).

Therefore, z should be assigned as the XNOR of x and y.
