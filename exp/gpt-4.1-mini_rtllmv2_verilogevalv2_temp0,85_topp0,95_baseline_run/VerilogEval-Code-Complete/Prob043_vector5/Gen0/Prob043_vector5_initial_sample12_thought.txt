To compute all 25 pairwise comparisons of the five 1-bit signals (a, b, c, d, e), we form a 5x5 matrix of comparisons: each output bit corresponds to the comparison of one of the five signals with one of the five signals again. The output bit is 1 if they are equal, otherwise 0. The output vector 'out' has 25 bits, indexed from 24 down to 0. The mapping is: out[24] = comparison of a with a, out[23] = a with b, ..., out[0] = e with e. We can represent the inputs as an array and generate these comparisons by checking equality of bits pairwise, then flattening the results into the output vector accordingly.
