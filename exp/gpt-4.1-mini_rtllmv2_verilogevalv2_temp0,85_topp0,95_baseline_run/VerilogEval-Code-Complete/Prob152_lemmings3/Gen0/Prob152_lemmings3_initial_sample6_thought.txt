We model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- DIG_LEFT: digging left (only when ground=1 and dig=1 while walking left)
- DIG_RIGHT: digging right (same for right)
- FALL_LEFT: falling after walking or digging left
- FALL_RIGHT: falling after walking or digging right

Transitions follow the priority:
1. If ground=0 and not falling or digging, transition to FALL state corresponding to current direction.
2. If currently digging and ground=0, transition to FALL state.
3. If currently falling and ground=1, transition back to walking state in the same direction.
4. If currently walking on ground (not digging or falling) and dig=1, transition to DIG state in current direction.
5. If currently walking on ground and bumped (either bump_left or bump_right=1), switch walking directions.
6. While falling or digging, bump signals have no effect.
7. While falling or digging, dig signals are ignored.

Outputs are driven by the current state:
- walk_left = 1 when in WALK_LEFT
- walk_right = 1 when in WALK_RIGHT
- aaah = 1 when falling (FALL_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT)
- digging = 1 when digging (DIG_LEFT or DIG_RIGHT)

Asynchronous positive edge reset sets state to WALK_LEFT.

We implement state encoding, synchronous state transitions on clk, asynchronous reset, and outputs derived from states.
