Model the Lemming's behavior as a Moore FSM with states encoding the movement direction and current action: walking left, walking right, digging left, digging right, falling left, falling right. On reset, start walking left. Transitions are prioritized as: falling > digging > switching directions.

- When ground=0 and not already falling or digging, transition to falling state matching current direction.
- When falling and ground=1, transition back to walking in the original direction.
- When dig=1 and on ground and walking, transition to digging in the current walking direction.
- When digging and ground=0, transition to falling in the same direction.
- When walking and bumped (left or right), switch walking direction.
- Bumps have no effect when falling or digging.
Outputs are asserted based on the current state.

Implement states as an enum for clarity. Use synchronous state transitions on clk, with asynchronous positive edge reset to walk left.

Outputs:
- walk_left: high in walking_left or digging_left states
- walk_right: high in walking_right or digging_right states
- digging: high in digging states
- aaah: high in falling states
