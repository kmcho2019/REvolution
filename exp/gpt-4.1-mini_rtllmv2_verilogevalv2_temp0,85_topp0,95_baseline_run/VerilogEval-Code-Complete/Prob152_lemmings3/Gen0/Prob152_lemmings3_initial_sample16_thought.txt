Design a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. On async posedge areset, reset to walking left. Transitions prioritize fall (ground=0), then dig (dig=1 and ground=1 and not falling), then bump direction changes if walking on ground. While falling or digging, bumps have no effect. Outputs derive from current state. Store direction separately or encode it in states to resume walking in the same direction after falling or digging.
