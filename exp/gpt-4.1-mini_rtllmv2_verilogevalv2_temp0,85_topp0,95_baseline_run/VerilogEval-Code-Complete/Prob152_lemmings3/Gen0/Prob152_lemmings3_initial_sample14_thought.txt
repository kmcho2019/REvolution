Design a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The Lemming starts walking left after reset. Transitions depend on inputs bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig according to the priority: falling > digging > switching directions.

- States:
  - WALK_LEFT
  - WALK_RIGHT
  - FALLING_LEFT (falling but remember direction)
  - FALLING_RIGHT
  - DIGGING_LEFT
  - DIGGING_RIGHT

- Outputs depend only on states:
  - walk_left: states WALK_LEFT, DIGGING_LEFT
  - walk_right: states WALK_RIGHT, DIGGING_RIGHT
  - aaah: FALLING_LEFT or FALLING_RIGHT
  - digging: DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT

- On ground=0, from walking or digging states, transition to falling state that remembers direction.
- On ground=1 from falling, go back to walking state in remembered direction.
- From walking, if dig=1 and ground=1 and not falling, start digging in the current direction.
- From digging, if ground=0, transition to falling state.
- From walking (not falling or digging), bump_left or bump_right cause switching directions.
- While falling or digging, bumps do not affect direction.
- Bumps at the same cycle as ground changes or while falling/digging are ignored for direction change.

Implement asynchronous posedge reset to WALK_LEFT.
