Model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with four states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground,
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground,
- DIGGING: digging (on ground),
- FALLING: falling (no ground).

Transitions occur on clk rising edge or asynchronous posedge areset. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT.

Priority of behaviors when on ground and walking:
1. Fall if ground=0 -> FALLING
2. Dig if dig=1 -> DIGGING
3. Switch direction if bumped -> WALK_RIGHT or WALK_LEFT accordingly

When falling, ignore bumps and dig commands. When ground returns, go back to the walking state (left or right) stored before falling.

When digging and ground disappears, transition to FALLING.

Outputs are determined solely by the current state:
- walk_left: 1 in WALK_LEFT
- walk_right: 1 in WALK_RIGHT
- aaah: 1 in FALLING
- digging: 1 in DIGGING

Keep a register to remember walking direction before falling to resume correctly.

Implement this FSM with synchronous state updates and asynchronous reset.
