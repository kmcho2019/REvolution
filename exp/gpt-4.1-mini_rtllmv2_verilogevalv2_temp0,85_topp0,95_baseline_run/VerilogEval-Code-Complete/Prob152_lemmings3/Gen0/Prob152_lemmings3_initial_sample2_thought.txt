We model the Lemming as a Moore FSM with these states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- DIGGING_LEFT: digging while walking left (only on ground)
- DIGGING_RIGHT: digging while walking right (only on ground)
- FALLING_LEFT: falling (previous direction left)
- FALLING_RIGHT: falling (previous direction right)

Transitions:
- If ground=0 and not already falling, transition from walking or digging to falling, preserving direction.
- If falling and ground=1, return to walking in the same direction.
- When digging, continue digging until ground=0, then transition to falling.
- If walking and dig=1 and ground=1, start digging in current direction.
- If walking and bump (left or right or both), change walking direction.
- Bumps and dig are ignored when falling or digging.
- Precedence: falling > digging > switching directions.

Output signals:
- walk_left: asserted in WALK_LEFT or DIGGING_LEFT states
- walk_right: asserted in WALK_RIGHT or DIGGING_RIGHT states
- digging: asserted in DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT states
- aaah: asserted in FALLING_LEFT or FALLING_RIGHT states

Asynchronous positive edge areset resets FSM to WALK_LEFT.

We implement the FSM with a state register, next state logic based on inputs and current state, and output logic based on current state.
