The output q is 1 when the 4-bit input (a,b,c,d) interpreted as a binary number is either 0, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, or 15. Converting these values to binary:
0  = 0000
3  = 0011
5  = 0101
6  = 0110
9  = 1001
10 = 1010
12 = 1100
15 = 1111

Observing the pattern, q=1 when the number of 1's in the input is even (0, 2, or 4 ones). This is the definition of an even parity generator.

Therefore, q = ~(a ^ b ^ c ^ d), i.e., the even parity bit of inputs a,b,c,d.

Implement q as the negation of the XOR of a, b, c, and d.
