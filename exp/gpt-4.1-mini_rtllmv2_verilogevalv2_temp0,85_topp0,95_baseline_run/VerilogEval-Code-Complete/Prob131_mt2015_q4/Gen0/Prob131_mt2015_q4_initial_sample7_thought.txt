Instantiate two modules A and two modules B, each with inputs x and y. Connect the outputs of the first A and first B to an OR gate. Connect the outputs of the second A and second B to an AND gate. Then XOR the outputs of the OR and AND gates to produce the final output z. Also implement module A using z = (x^y) & x, and module B matching the waveform behavior. From the waveform, module B outputs 1 when x and y are both 0, or both 1, otherwise 0. So B can be implemented as z = ~(x ^ y).
