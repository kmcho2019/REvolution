Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the branch history. On asynchronous reset, clear the history to zero. On each clock edge, if a misprediction occurs, load the history with train_history concatenated with train_taken (train_history is the older 31 bits, train_taken is the youngest bit). If no misprediction but a prediction is valid, shift the history left by one and insert predict_taken at bit 0. Misprediction handling takes precedence over prediction update.
