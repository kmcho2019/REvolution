Implement a 32-bit shift register that updates on the rising clock edge. On asynchronous reset, clear the history to zero. When a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted=1), load the history with train_history concatenated with train_taken, which forms the correct history before the mispredicted branch plus its actual outcome. If a prediction occurs without a misprediction (predict_valid=1), shift in predict_taken at the LSB side, shifting existing bits towards MSB. The misprediction update takes priority over prediction update. predict_history is continuously assigned from the internal register.
