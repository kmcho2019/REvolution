Design a Moore FSM with states representing how much of the sequence 1101 has been matched so far. The states will be:

- S0: no bits matched
- S1: matched '1'
- S2: matched '11'
- S3: matched '110'
- S4: matched '1101' (sequence found)

Once in S4, start_shifting is set to 1 and stays 1 until reset. The FSM transitions according to input data and resets synchronously on reset. Use a register to hold start_shifting and set it when S4 is reached.
