Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (bit indices 4 and 2 in 0-based indexing). On each clock, shift right by one bit. The new MSB (q[4]) is computed by XORing the output bit (q[0]) with the bits at the tap positions. Specifically, the bits at tap positions are XORed with q[0] before shifting. The output q is reset synchronously to 1 (00001). This ensures maximal length sequence cycling through 31 states excluding all zeros.
