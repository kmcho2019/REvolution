Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. The LFSR shifts right each clock cycle. The new MSB bit (q[4]) is the XOR of the current LSB (q[0]) and the tapped bit at position 2 (index 2 in zero-based, which corresponds to bit 3). All other bits shift right without XOR. The synchronous reset sets q to 1.
