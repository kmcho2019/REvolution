Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. According to the Galois LFSR structure, on each clock cycle, the shift register shifts right by one bit. The new input at the MSB (bit 4 in zero-based indexing) is the XOR of the LSB (q[0]) and the tap bits. For taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based), these correspond to bits 4 and 2 in zero-based indexing. Each tapped bit is XORed with q[0] to produce their new value; bits without taps just shift right unchanged. On synchronous reset, set q to 5'b00001 (decimal 1) to avoid the all-zero state. This ensures maximal-length cycling through 31 states.
