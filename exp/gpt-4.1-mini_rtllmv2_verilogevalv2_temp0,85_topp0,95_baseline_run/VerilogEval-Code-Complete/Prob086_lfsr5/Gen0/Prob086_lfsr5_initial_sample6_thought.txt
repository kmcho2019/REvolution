Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3 (i.e., q[4] and q[2]). On each clock edge, the LFSR shifts right, and the new bit shifted into q[4] is q[0] XOR q[2]. Bits without a tap shift right unchanged, except that those with taps are XORed with q[0] for their next value. The LSB q[0] is replaced by q[1] after shift. On synchronous reset, set q to 5'b00001.
