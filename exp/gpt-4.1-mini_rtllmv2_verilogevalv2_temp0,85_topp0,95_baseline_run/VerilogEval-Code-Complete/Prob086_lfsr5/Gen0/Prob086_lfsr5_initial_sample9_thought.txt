Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock cycle. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (counting from 1 at MSB to 5 at LSB), which correspond to bits q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing. The input bit to the shift register is the XOR of q[0] (LSB) and the tapped bits. On reset, set q to 1 to avoid the all-zero state. The LFSR updates on the rising edge of clk.
