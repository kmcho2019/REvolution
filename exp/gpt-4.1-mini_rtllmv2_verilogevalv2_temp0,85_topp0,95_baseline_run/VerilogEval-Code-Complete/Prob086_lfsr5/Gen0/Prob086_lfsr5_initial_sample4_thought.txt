Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock cycle. The taps are at bits 5 and 3 (bit positions 4 and 2 in zero-based indexing). The feedback bit is the XOR of the LSB q[0] and the tap bits q[4] and q[2]. On each clock, each bit shifts right, but bits with taps are XORed with q[0] before shifting in. The register resets synchronously to 5'b00001 on reset.
