Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR shifting right on each clock cycle. The LSB (q[0]) is the output bit that feeds back. According to the problem, taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based), which correspond to q[4] and q[2] in 0-based indexing. For each tapped bit, XOR it with q[0] to generate its next value; for non-tapped bits, just shift right by one. On synchronous reset, set q to 1 (5'b00001). This will create a maximum-length sequence of 31 states.
