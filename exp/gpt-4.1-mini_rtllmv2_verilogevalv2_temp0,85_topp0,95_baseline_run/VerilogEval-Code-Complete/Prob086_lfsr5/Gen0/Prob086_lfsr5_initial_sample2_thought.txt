Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock edge. The next bit shifted into position q[4] is the XOR of q[0] and q[2] (since taps at bit positions 5 and 3 correspond to q[4] and q[2] respectively). On synchronous reset, set q to 5'b00001 to avoid the all-zero state. The LFSR shifts right, updating each bit accordingly with taps applied.
