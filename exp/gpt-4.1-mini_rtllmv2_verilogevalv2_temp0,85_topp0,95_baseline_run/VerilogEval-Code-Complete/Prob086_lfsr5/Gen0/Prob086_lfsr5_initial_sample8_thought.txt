Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock cycle. The taps are on bits 5 and 3 (1-based), corresponding to q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing. The feedback bit is q[0]. For tapped bits, XOR their current bit with q[0] to get the next state; for untapped bits, shift the previous bit unchanged. On synchronous reset, q is set to 1.
