Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (bits q[4] and q[2]) which shift right every clock cycle. The new bit entering at the MSB position is formed by XORing the output bit (q[0]) with taps. On each clock, q[0] shifts out, and each tapped bit XORs with q[0] to form the new bit, while non-tapped bits just shift right. The synchronous reset sets q to 1.
