From the waveform, the outputs p and q depend on the input a and the clock. The output p updates on the rising edge of the clock: p becomes the same as a at the rising clock edge. The output q updates on the falling edge of the clock: q becomes the same as p at the falling clock edge. Initially, p and q start from 0. Thus, the circuit is a two-stage synchronizer or delay register where p samples a on the rising edge, and q samples p on the falling edge.
