The waveform suggests the circuit has two states stored in p and q. On the rising edge of clock, p and q are updated based on input 'a' and the previous values of p and q.

- When clock rises and a=0, p=0 and q remains its previous value.
- When clock rises and a=1, p=1 and q follows the previous p value.
- On the falling clock edge, outputs p and q do not change.

This behavior resembles a simple state machine where:
- p is set directly by 'a' at rising edge.
- q captures the previous p at rising edge.
