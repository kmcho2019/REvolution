From the waveform, on the rising edge of the clock, the outputs p and q update based on input a and previous states. Observing the patterns:

- At time 25ns (clock=1), p is set to 0.
- At time 90ns, when a=1 and clock=1, p becomes 1 while q remains 0.
- At time 115ns (clock=0), q is updated to p's previous value (q follows p on clock falling edge).
- At time 165ns, with a=1 and clock=1, both p and q become 1.

It appears that:
- On the rising edge of clock, p is assigned the value of input a OR q.
- On the falling edge of clock, q is assigned the value of p.

Hence, the circuit acts as a two-stage sequential circuit where p updates on rising edge and q on falling edge, with p depending on input a and q's previous state.

We will implement this behavior using always blocks triggered on clock edges.
