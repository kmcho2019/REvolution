From the waveform, we analyze the behavior of p and q based on inputs clock and a:
- At clock=1, p seems to latch to the value of a.
- q changes only when clock=0 and seems to follow p from previous cycles.
- More specifically, q updates at the falling edge of clock and takes the value of p before the falling edge.
Thus, the circuit behavior is:
- On rising edge of clock: p <= a
- On falling edge of clock: q <= p
This implements a two-stage register where p captures input a at rising edge, and q captures p at falling edge.
