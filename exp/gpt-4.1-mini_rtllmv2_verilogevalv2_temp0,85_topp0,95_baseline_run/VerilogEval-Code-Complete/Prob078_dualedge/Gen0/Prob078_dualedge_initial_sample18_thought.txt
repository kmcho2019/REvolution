To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in an FPGA without native support, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge and one on the falling edge of the clock. Both capture the input 'd' on their respective clock edges. Then, we combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock to determine which flip-flop's output is currently valid. This effectively creates a flip-flop that updates 'q' on both clock edges.
