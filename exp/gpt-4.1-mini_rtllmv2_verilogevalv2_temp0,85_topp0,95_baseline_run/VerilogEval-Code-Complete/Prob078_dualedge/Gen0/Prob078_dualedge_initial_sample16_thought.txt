To emulate a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in FPGA, use two single-edge flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and one on the negative edge of the clock. Then, combine their outputs in a way that updates the output on both edges. A common approach is to sample the input on both edges in separate flip-flops and then select the most recent value. Here, we store the data sampled on the positive edge and the negative edge, and use a toggle signal to update output 'q' on both edges accordingly.
