To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using FPGA-friendly constructs, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge and one triggered on the falling edge of the clock. Both flip-flops sample the input `d` on their respective edges, and then we combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock to produce the final output `q`. This approach ensures `q` updates on both clock edges, mimicking a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
