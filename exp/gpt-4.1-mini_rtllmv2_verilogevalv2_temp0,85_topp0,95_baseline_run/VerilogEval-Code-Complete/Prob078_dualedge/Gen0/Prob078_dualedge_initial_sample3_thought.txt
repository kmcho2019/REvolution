To build a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using only single-edge flip-flops (which are supported by FPGAs), we can use two flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and another triggered on the negative edge. We sample the input `d` on both edges independently and then combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock. Since the clock is high in the positive half-cycle and low in the negative half-cycle, the output `q` will update on both edges, emulating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
