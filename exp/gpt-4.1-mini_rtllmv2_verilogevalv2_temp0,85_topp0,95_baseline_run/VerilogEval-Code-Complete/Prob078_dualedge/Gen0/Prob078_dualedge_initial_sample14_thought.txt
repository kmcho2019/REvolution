To emulate a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and one on the negative edge of the clock. Each flip-flop samples the input 'd' on its respective clock edge. Then, we combine their outputs using a multiplexer that selects the correct output depending on the clock level. This way, the output 'q' updates on both edges of the clock, behaving like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
