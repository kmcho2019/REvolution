Design a Mealy FSM with three states to detect the sequence "101" on input x. The states represent how much of the sequence has been matched so far:
- S0: No bits matched yet
- S1: Matched '1'
- S2: Matched '10'

On each clock edge, transition between states based on the input x, and output z=1 when the sequence "101" is detected on the current input. Use a negative edge asynchronous reset (aresetn). Overlapping sequences are recognized by transitioning to appropriate states after detection.
