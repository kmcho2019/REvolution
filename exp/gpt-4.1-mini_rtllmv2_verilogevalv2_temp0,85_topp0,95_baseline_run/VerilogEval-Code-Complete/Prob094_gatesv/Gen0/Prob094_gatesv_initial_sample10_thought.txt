For out_both[2:0], compare in[i] and in[i+1] for i=0 to 2; output 1 if both bits are 1.
For out_any[3:1], compare in[i] and in[i-1] for i=1 to 3; output 1 if either bit is 1.
For out_different[3:0], compare in[i] and its left neighbor with wraparound: left neighbor of in[3] is in[0], so out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1)%4].
