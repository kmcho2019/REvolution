For out_both, each bit (0 to 98) is 1 if both in[i] and its left neighbor in[i+1] are 1.
For out_any, each bit (1 to 99) is 1 if either in[i] or its right neighbor in[i-1] is 1.
For out_different, each bit (0 to 99) compares in[i] with its left neighbor, with wrap around: left neighbor of in[99] is in[0].
