For out_both, each bit i (0 to 98) indicates if in[i] and in[i+1] are both 1. Since the neighbor to the left of bit i is in[i+1], out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1].

For out_any, each bit i (1 to 99) indicates if either in[i] or in[i-1] is 1. Since the neighbor to the right of bit i is in[i-1], out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1].

For out_different, each bit i (0 to 99) indicates if in[i] differs from its neighbor to the left, considering wrap-around. The neighbor to the left of in[99] is in[0], so out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1)%100]. Since the vector is 100 bits, (i+1)%100 wraps around correctly.
