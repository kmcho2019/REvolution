Using one-hot encoding, y[1] to y[6] represent states A to F respectively.
We want to find the next-state bits Y2 (for B) and Y4 (for D).

From the state diagram:
- Y2 (B) is set when next state is B.
Check transitions leading to B:

From A(0) --0--> B: y[1] & ~w -> Y2
No other transitions to B.

So,
Y2 = y[1] & ~w;

- Y4 (D) is set when next state is D.
Transitions to D:

B(0) --1--> D: y[2] & w
C(0) --1--> D: y[3] & w
E(1) --1--> D: y[5] & w
F(1) --1--> D: y[6] & w

So,
Y4 = (y[2] | y[3] | y[5] | y[6]) & w;
