First, we analyze the K-map to find minimal SOP expressions considering 'd' as either 0 or 1 to simplify the logic. The K-map is:

       ab
cd  | 01 | 00 | 10 | 11
00  | d  | 0  | 1  | 1
01  | 0  | 0  | d  | d
11  | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1
10  | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1

Mapping inputs to rows and columns:
- Rows: cd = 00,01,11,10 (in order)
- Columns: ab = 01,00,10,11 (in order)

We rewrite the K-map with corresponding variable values:

| cd\ab | 01(b=0,a=1) | 00(b=0,a=0) | 10(b=1,a=0) | 11(b=1,a=1) |
|-------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| 00(c=0,d=0) | d         | 0           | 1           | 1           |
| 01(c=0,d=1) | 0         | 0           | d           | d           |
| 11(c=1,d=1) | 0         | 1           | 1           | 1           |
| 10(c=1,d=0) | 0         | 1           | 1           | 1           |

We treat 'd' (don't care) as 1 where it helps grouping.

Groups:
1) The entire bottom two rows (cd=11 and 10), columns 00,10,11 have output 1 (cells with 1s). That covers cells (11,00), (11,10), (11,11), (10,00), (10,10), (10,11)
2) The top row (cd=00), columns 10 and 11 are 1.
3) There's a 1 at (00,10) and (00,11)
4) The (00,00) is 0; (00,01) is don't-care.

From this, the minimal expression can be:

- For rows cd=11 or 10 (c=1), output is 1 except (11,01)=0 and (10,01)=1 (the cell (10,01) is 1). So for c=1 and b=0 and a=0 (11,00 and 10,00) output 1.

- Let's make explicit expressions:

Expression1: c=1 => output=1 (since almost all cells with c=1 are 1 except (11,01)=0 and (11,00)=1)

Check (11,01) cell is 0, so can't cover full c=1.

Check more precisely:

Cells with c=1,d=1 (11):
(11,00) = 1 (b=0,a=0)
(11,01) = 0 (b=0,a=1)
(11,10) =1 (b=1,a=0)
(11,11) =1 (b=1,a=1)

Cells with c=1,d=0 (10):
(10,00) =1 (b=0,a=0)
(10,01) =1 (b=0,a=1)
(10,10) =1 (b=1,a=0)
(10,11) =1 (b=1,a=1)

So all (10,ab) are 1

Only (11,01) is 0.

So, for c=1 and not (b=0,a=1) => output=1

This can be expressed as c & ~(~b & a)

Or c & (b | ~a)

Second, the top row (cd=00):

(00,00)=0, (00,01)=d, (00,10)=1, (00,11)=1

If we treat (00,01) as 1 (don't care), then the group (00,01),(00,10),(00,11) => 1

This corresponds to c=0,d=0, and a=1 or b=1 (since ab=01,10,11)

So for c=0,d=0 and (a|b) =1

So output = ~c & ~d & (a | b)

For the middle row (01):

(01,00)=0, (01,01)=0, (01,10)=d, (01,11)=d

We can treat d as 0 here to simplify.

So no 1s here.

Thus, final expression:

out = (c & (b | ~a)) | (~c & ~d & (a | b))
