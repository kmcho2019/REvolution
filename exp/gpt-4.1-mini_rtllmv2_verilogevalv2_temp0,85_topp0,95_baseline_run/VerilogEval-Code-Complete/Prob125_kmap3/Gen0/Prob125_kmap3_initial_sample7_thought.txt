First, interpret the Karnaugh map with variables in the order (cd) and (ab) as shown. For each cell, we note the output values and don't-cares. We choose values for don't-cares to simplify the logic expression.

Given the map (rows cd, columns ab):

          ab
     01  00  10  11
cd
00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

We assume a, b, c, d variables as single bits.

Step 1: Assign don't-cares to values that simplify expressions.

- The don't-care at (00,01) can be chosen as 1 (to group with adjacent 1s).
- The don't-cares at (01,10) and (01,11) can be chosen as 1 to form a bigger group.

Step 2: Identify groups of 1s:

Group 1: (cd=00, ab=10 and 11, output=1) plus (00,01) if assigned 1 => row 00, columns 01,10,11 are 1 (three cells)

Group 2: (cd=10 and 11, ab=00,10,11 columns) have 1s - especially columns 10 and 11 are 1 for rows 10 and 11

Group 3: (cd=11, ab=01) = 1

Now, let's rewrite the map with assigned don't-cares as 1:

          ab
     01  00  10  11
cd
00 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

From the above:

- Large group covering rows 10 and 11, columns 10 and 11 => covers all those 1s.

- Also, the 1 at (11,01) and (00,01) and (01,10) and (01,11) can be grouped for simplification.

Let's write the minterms of 1 cells:

(For each cell, variables are abcd, with ab as columns and cd as rows.)

Cell (cd, ab):

- (00,01) = c=0,d=0,a=0,b=1 => a'b d' c' (a' b d' c')
- (00,10) = c=0,d=0,a=1,b=0 => a b' c' d'
- (00,11) = c=0,d=0,a=1,b=1 => a b c' d'
- (01,10) = c=0,d=1,a=1,b=0 => a b' c d
- (01,11) = c=0,d=1,a=1,b=1 => a b c d
- (11,01) = c=1,d=1,a=0,b=1 => a' b c d
- (10,01) = c=1,d=0,a=0,b=1 => a' b c d'
- (10,10) = c=1,d=0,a=1,b=0 => a b' c d'
- (10,11) = c=1,d=0,a=1,b=1 => a b c d'
- (11,10) = c=1,d=1,a=1,b=0 => a b' c d
- (11,11) = c=1,d=1,a=1,b=1 => a b c d

To simplify, let's use grouping:

Group A: All 1s where a=1 and c=don't care, d=don't care, b=don't care:

- Cells (00,10), (00,11), (01,10), (01,11), (10,10), (10,11), (11,10), (11,11)

- These all have a=1.

Group B: All 1s where b=1 and c=1 and d=don't care:

- Cells (11,01), (10,01)

Group C: (00,01) = (a=0,b=1,c=0,d=0)

Combining B and C: b=1 and c=0 or 1, d=don't care except (00,01) is c=0, d=0.

Better to express the logic as:

out = a + (b & c)

Because:

- All cells with a=1 are output=1.

- Also, cells with b=1 and c=1 are output=1 (regardless of a).

- The only exception is (00,01) which is a=0, b=1, c=0, d=0, assigned 1 (don't care assigned 1) which is covered by b=1 & c=0 (but c=0 not c=1). So need to check if out = a + (b & c) covers that cell.

- Cell (00,01) => a=0, b=1, c=0 => a=0, b&c=0 => output=0, but we want 1 here.

So we need to include (b & ~c & ~d) for that cell.

Hence, out = a + (b & c) + (b & ~c & ~d)

Simplify (b & c) + (b & ~c & ~d) = b & (c + ~c & ~d) = b & (c + ~d & ~c) = b & (c + ~c & ~d) = b & (c + ~d & ~c)

Note that (c + ~d & ~c) = c + (~d & ~c)

This is already minimal.

Final expression:

out = a + (b & c) + (b & ~c & ~d)
