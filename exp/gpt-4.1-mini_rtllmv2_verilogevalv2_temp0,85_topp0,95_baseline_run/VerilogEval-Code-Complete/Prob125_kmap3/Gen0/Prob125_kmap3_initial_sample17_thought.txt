First, rewrite the Karnaugh map for clarity and group the cells with '1's considering 'd' as don't-care to simplify the expression. Then implement the simplified Boolean expression in Verilog.

K-map (cd rows, ab columns):

       ab
      01 00 10 11
cd 00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
   01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
   11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Let's mark the cells that are 1 or d (which we can treat as 1 if convenient):

- cd=00, ab=10 or 11 => 1
- cd=11, ab=00,10,11 => 1
- cd=10, ab=00,10,11 => 1
- cd=11, ab=01 => 1
- cd=10, ab=01 => 1
- cd=00, ab=01 => d (so treat as 1 to simplify)
- cd=01, ab=10 or 11 => d (can be 0 or 1, let's choose 0 to simplify)

From this, the cells with 1 or d are:

(00,01)=d (1), (00,10)=1, (00,11)=1

(10,00)=0 (not 1), (10,01)=1, (10,10)=1, (10,11)=1  
(01,10)=d (0 or 1), (01,11)=d (0 or 1), but we choose 0.

Wait, let's check carefully.

For cd=01 row:

All zeros or don't care (treat as 0).

Overall, output=1 when ( (c=0,d=0) and (a=1 or b=1) ) or ( (c=1) and (a=1 or b=1) )

So the expression reduces to: (a or b) and ( (c=0 and d=0) or (c=1) )

But ( (c=0 and d=0) or (c=1) ) = (c + (c' d')) = c + c' d' = c + d'

So expression = (a + b) & (c + d')
