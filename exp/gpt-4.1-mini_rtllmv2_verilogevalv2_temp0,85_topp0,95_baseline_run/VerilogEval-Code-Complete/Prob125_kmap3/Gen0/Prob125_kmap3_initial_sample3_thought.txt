First, let's map the Karnaugh table values to the corresponding minterms of inputs a,b,c,d. The K-map is given with rows as cd and columns as ab in order 01, 00, 10, 11.

From the table:

cd\ab | 01 | 00 | 10 | 11
-------------------------
00    | d  | 0  | 1  | 1
01    | 0  | 0  | d  | d
11    | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1
10    | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1

Let's rewrite in terms of a,b,c,d:

ab is column with order: 01 (a=0,b=1), 00 (0,0), 10 (1,0), 11 (1,1)
cd is row with order: 00 (c=0,d=0), 01(0,1), 11(1,1), 10(1,0)

Let's write the minterms for each cell:

Row 00 (c=0,d=0):
- ab=01 (a=0,b=1): d (don't care)
- 00 (0,0): 0
- 10 (1,0): 1
- 11 (1,1): 1

Row 01 (c=0,d=1):
- 01:0
- 00:0
- 10:d
- 11:d

Row 11 (c=1,d=1):
- 01:0
- 00:1
- 10:1
- 11:1

Row 10 (c=1,d=0):
- 01:0
- 00:1
- 10:1
- 11:1

We can now find the simplified function using the don't cares to cover ones with fewer terms.

The minterms for output=1 are:

- (c=0,d=0, a=1,b=0) -> c=0,d=0,a=1,b=0
- (c=0,d=0, a=1,b=1)
- (c=1,d=1,a=0,b=0)
- (c=1,d=1,a=1,b=0)
- (c=1,d=1,a=1,b=1)
- (c=1,d=0,a=0,b=0)
- (c=1,d=0,a=1,b=0)
- (c=1,d=0,a=1,b=1)

Let's try to group ones for simplification:

Observing the bottom two rows (c=1, d=1 and c=1, d=0):

- For c=1, d=1 or d=0, and a,b in (00,10,11) => output=1
- For c=0,d=0 and a=1,b=(0 or1) => output=1

Hence:

- When c=1 and b=0 or b=1 (except b=01?), but checking b=01 (a=0,b=1) at c=1,d=1 is 0.

At c=1,d=1, ab=01 (0,1) output=0

So for c=1,d=1 and b=0 or b=1 but not b=01, the outputs 1 are when ab=00,10,11.

Similarly, at c=1,d=0, ab=01 output=0, others 1

So for c=1, d=1 or d=0, b=0 or 1 except (a=0,b=1), output=1

Summary:

- Output=1 if c=1 and b=0 or b=1 but a=0,b=1 is 0, so output=1 for all ab except 01 at c=1

- Output=1 if c=0,d=0,a=1,b=0 or 1

From this, the simplified expression is:

- (c and ( (a and ~b) or (a and b) or (~a and ~b) )) = c and (a or ~b)

But ~a and b=1 at c=1 is 0 so exclude that.

Better to write:

At c=1:

Output=1 if b=0 or (a=1,b=1) -> (b=0) or (a=1 & b=1)

At c=0,d=0:

Output=1 if a=1,b=0 or a=1,b=1 = a & (b=0 or b=1) = a

Therefore:

Output = (c & (~b | (a & b))) | (~c & ~d & a)

Simplify (~b | (a & b)) = (~b | a) because if b=0 output=1, if b=1 output=1 if a=1

So output = c & (~b | a) | (~c & ~d & a)

This is the minimized expression.
