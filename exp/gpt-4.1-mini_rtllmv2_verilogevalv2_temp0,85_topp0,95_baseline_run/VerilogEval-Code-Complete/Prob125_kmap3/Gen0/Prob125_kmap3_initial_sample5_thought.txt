Analyze the Karnaugh map to find minimal boolean expression for the output.

The variables are a, b, c, d. The Karnaugh map is given with ab as columns (01, 00, 10, 11) and cd as rows (00, 01, 11, 10).

Interpreting the map entries for each combination of ab and cd:

Rows (cd):
00 = 0 0
01 = 0 1
11 = 1 1
10 = 1 0

Columns (ab):
01 = 0 1
00 = 0 0
10 = 1 0
11 = 1 1

Map (rows=cd, cols=ab):

          ab
   cd   01  00  10  11
   00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
   01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
   11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Let's rewrite the map replacing d with 'don't cares' that we can assign as 1 or 0 for simplification.

Positions:

cd\ab | 01 | 00 | 10 | 11
00(0,0)| d | 0 | 1 | 1
01(0,1)| 0 | 0 | d | d
11(1,1)| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1
10(1,0)| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1

Let's list all minterms where output is 1, or can be 1 due to don't care:

Minterms with 1:
- cd=00, ab=10 -> c=0,d=0,a=1,b=0
- cd=00, ab=11 -> c=0,d=0,a=1,b=1
- cd=11, ab=00 -> c=1,d=1,a=0,b=0
- cd=11, ab=10 -> c=1,d=1,a=1,b=0
- cd=11, ab=11 -> c=1,d=1,a=1,b=1
- cd=10, ab=00 -> c=1,d=0,a=0,b=0
- cd=10, ab=10 -> c=1,d=0,a=1,b=0
- cd=10, ab=11 -> c=1,d=0,a=1,b=1
- cd=10, ab=01 -> c=1,d=0,a=0,b=1

Don't cares can be assigned as 1 to help grouping.

Try to find groups:

Group 1: All entries where c=1 (cd=11 or cd=10). For cd=11 and cd=10 rows:

cd=11, ab=00: 1
cd=11, ab=10: 1
cd=11, ab=11: 1
cd=10, ab=01: 1
cd=10, ab=10: 1
cd=10, ab=11: 1

So, all 1s in rows cd=11 and cd=10 except cd=10, ab=00 is 0.

But cd=11, ab=01 is 1 according to map? No, it's 1 in map? Wait, in cd=11, ab=01 it's 1.

Actually from map:

cd=11, ab=01 is 1

Yes, it's 1.

Similarly cd=10, ab=00 is 0.

So group all cells with c=1 and exclude cd=10, ab=00.

Hence, group where c=1 and (not cd=10, ab=00).

Group 2: For cd=00 row:

cd=00, ab=10 and ab=11 are 1, ab=00 is 0, ab=01 is d (can assign 0 or 1).

Maybe assign d=0 to simplify.

So group cd=00, ab=10 and ab=11 -> a=1, b=x, c=0,d=0

Group 3: cd=11, ab=00 =1: c=1,d=1,a=0,b=0

Isolated 1.

So final minimal expression considering above:

- Group 1: c=1 (since rows cd=11 and 10 are c=1,x)

- Group 2: a=1, c=0, d=0, b= x (from cd=00 row and ab=10,11 columns)

- Group 3: c=1,d=1,a=0,b=0 (from cd=11, ab=00)

But group 1 includes cd=11 and 10 rows (c=1), so c=1 covers most 1's except cd=11,ab=00 (which is 1) and cd=10,ab=00(0).

Wait, cd=11, ab=00 is included in c=1, a=0,b=0,d=1

So c=1 covers all 1's except cd=00, ab=10 and ab=11.

So expression is:

out = c + (a & ~c & ~d)

Using this expression:

- c=1 covers all 1's except when c=0 (cd=00 row), where only a=1 and d=0 leads to output 1.

This fits our map.

Hence:

out = c + (a & ~c & ~d)
