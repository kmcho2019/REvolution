To model the 7458 chip, declare four internal wires representing the outputs of the four AND gates. The first two AND gates produce three-input AND outputs for p1a&p1b&p1c and p1d&p1e&p1f, which are then ORed to form p1y. The other two AND gates produce two-input AND outputs for p2a&p2b and p2c&p2d, which are ORed to form p2y. Use assign statements for the internal AND outputs and the final OR outputs.
