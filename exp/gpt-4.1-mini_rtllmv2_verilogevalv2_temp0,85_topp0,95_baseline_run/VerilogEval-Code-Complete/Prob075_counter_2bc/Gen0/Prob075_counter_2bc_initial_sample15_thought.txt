Implement a 2-bit saturating counter that updates only when train_valid is high. On train_valid=1 and train_taken=1, increment the counter unless it is already at the max (3). On train_valid=1 and train_taken=0, decrement the counter unless it is already at the min (0). When train_valid=0, hold the counter value. Use an asynchronous positive edge reset to set the counter to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken). The counter is updated on the rising edge of clk or the rising edge of areset.
