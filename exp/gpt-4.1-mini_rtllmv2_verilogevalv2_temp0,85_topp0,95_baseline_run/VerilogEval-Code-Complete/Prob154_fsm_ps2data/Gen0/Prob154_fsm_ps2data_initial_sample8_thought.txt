Implement a simple FSM with three states to collect three message bytes. Initially, in IDLE state, discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is found, then transition to BYTE1 state and store the first byte. Then on next clocks, store second byte in BYTE2 state, and third byte in BYTE3 state. Once the third byte is stored, output done=1 and the concatenated 24-bit message (first byte as the MSB). On the next clock, return to IDLE. The reset synchronously clears the state and output signals.
