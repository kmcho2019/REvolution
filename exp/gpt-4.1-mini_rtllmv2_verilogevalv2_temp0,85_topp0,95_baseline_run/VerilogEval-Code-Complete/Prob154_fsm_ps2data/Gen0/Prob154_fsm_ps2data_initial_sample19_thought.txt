Implement a synchronous FSM with states: IDLE (waiting for a byte with in[3]=1), BYTE1 (store first byte), BYTE2 (store second byte), BYTE3 (store third byte). When in IDLE, discard bytes until in[3] is 1, then capture that as byte 1 and move to BYTE2. In BYTE2 and BYTE3 states, capture incoming bytes. After BYTE3, assert done for one cycle with out_bytes set to concatenation of the three bytes. On reset, go to IDLE. The out_bytes and done are registered outputs, updated on the clock edge.
