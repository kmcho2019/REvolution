From the waveform, q depends mainly on input c, and outputs different values for c from 0 to f (hexadecimal).
At c=0, q=b; at c=1, q=e; at c=2, q=a; at c=3, q=d; at c>=4, q=15 (0xf).
So, for c:
- 0: q=b
- 1: q=e
- 2: q=a
- 3: q=d
- 4 to 15: q=4'b1111 (0xf)
The other inputs a, d, e do not affect q in this mapping except at c=2 and c=3 (which directly select a or d), or at c=1 (selects e).
Thus, q is a multiplexer output selecting among b, e, a, d, or 0xf based on c.
