Implement a synchronous 8-bit right shifter with an 8-bit register q initialized to zero. On every rising edge of the clock, shift q one bit to the right, then insert the input bit d into the most significant bit q[7]. Since Verilog does not allow partial assignment to a register within a single nonblocking assignment, we can combine the shift and insertion into one assignment: q <= {d, q[7:1]}.
