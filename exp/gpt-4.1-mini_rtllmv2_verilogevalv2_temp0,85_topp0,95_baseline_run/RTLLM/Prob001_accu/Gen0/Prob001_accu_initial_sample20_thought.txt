Accumulate incoming 8-bit data samples on each clock cycle when valid_in is high and rst_n is not asserted. Maintain a counter to track how many valid data samples have been received. Sum the inputs into a 10-bit register (to hold maximum sum of four 8-bit values: max 4*255=1020, fits in 10 bits). When the counter reaches 4, output the accumulated sum on data_out, assert valid_out for one cycle, then reset the counter and accumulator to prepare for the next set of four inputs.
