The design needs to accumulate four 8-bit input values only when valid_in is asserted. We'll keep a counter to count valid inputs and a register to accumulate their sum. When the count reaches 4, the sum is output on data_out with valid_out asserted for one clock cycle. The sum is 10-bit to accommodate the maximum sum of four 8-bit numbers (4 * 255 = 1020 < 1024). After outputting, the accumulator and counter reset for the next batch. The design is synchronous with active-low reset.
