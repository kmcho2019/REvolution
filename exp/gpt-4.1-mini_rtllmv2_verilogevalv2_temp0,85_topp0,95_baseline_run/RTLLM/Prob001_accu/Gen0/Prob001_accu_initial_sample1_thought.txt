We will create a synchronous module with an active low reset. On each clock cycle, when valid_in is high, the module accumulates the incoming 8-bit data into a register and increments a count. Once four valid inputs have been received, the sum will be output on data_out, valid_out will be asserted for one clock cycle, and then the accumulator and counter reset to accumulate the next four data inputs. The sum is 10-bit to accommodate the maximum sum of four 8-bit numbers (max 255*4=1020, which fits in 10 bits).
