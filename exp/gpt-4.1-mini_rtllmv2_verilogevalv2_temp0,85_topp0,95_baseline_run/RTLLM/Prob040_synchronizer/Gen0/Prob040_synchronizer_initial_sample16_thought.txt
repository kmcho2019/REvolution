The design implements a multi-bit (4-bit) synchronizer for data crossing from clk_a domain to clk_b domain. The data_in and data_en signals are sampled on clk_a, storing data_in in data_reg and data_en in en_data_reg. Then, on clk_b, the enable signal is synchronized through two flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to delay the enable by two clk_b cycles. When the delayed enable (en_clap_two) is high, the stored data_reg is transferred to dataout. The asynchronous resets (arstn and brstn) initialize registers to zero. This approach ensures safe data synchronization using multi-bit mux-based logic, with stable data during data_en high period and output aligned to clk_b.
