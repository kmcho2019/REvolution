Implement a synchronizer module that captures data_in and data_en on clk_a domain with asynchronous reset arstn, then synchronize data_en through two flip-flops in clk_b domain with asynchronous reset brstn. When the delayed enable (en_clap_two) is active, output data_reg on clk_b domain; otherwise hold the previous dataout value. This satisfies the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer requirement with proper asynchronous resets and clock domain crossing.
