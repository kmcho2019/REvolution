The design uses two clock domains clk_a and clk_b. Data and enable signals are sampled and stored on clk_a domain, and the enable is delayed by two flip-flops in the clk_b domain to generate a control signal. When this control signal is high, the stored data is transferred to output dataout on clk_b domain. Reset signals are active low for each domain. The data changes infrequently and data_en is asserted for at least 3 clk_b cycles, ensuring stable synchronization. The output data is held stable if the enable is not active.
