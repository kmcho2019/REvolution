Implement two sets of registers in different clock domains: in clk_a domain, register the input data and the enable signal; in clk_b domain, implement two-stage synchronization of the enable signal from clk_a domain (en_data_reg) to clk_b domain (en_clap_one and en_clap_two). The output data is updated on clk_b when the synchronized enable (en_clap_two) is high. Use async active-low resets for clk_a and clk_b domains respectively.
