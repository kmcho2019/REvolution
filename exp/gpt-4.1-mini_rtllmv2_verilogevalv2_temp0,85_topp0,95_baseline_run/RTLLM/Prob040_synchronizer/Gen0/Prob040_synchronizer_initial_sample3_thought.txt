Implement a multi-bit (4-bit) synchronizer module with two asynchronous clocks clk_a and clk_b. On clk_a domain, register data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively, reset with arstn. On clk_b domain, cascade two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two to delay en_data_reg by two clk_b cycles, reset with brstn. Use en_clap_two as enable to update output dataout with data_reg on clk_b domain. Retain previous output when enable is low. This structure ensures data_in stable under data_en for at least 3 clk_b cycles, and output is properly synchronized to clk_b.
