The design implements a multi-bit synchronizer with two asynchronous clocks clk_a and clk_b. Input data_in and data_en are sampled on clk_a domain into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively, with async reset arstn. Then en_data_reg is synchronized into clk_b domain with two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two with async reset brstn. On clk_b, when en_clap_two is high (which means data_en was high two clk_b cycles ago), the output dataout is updated with the stable data_reg value sampled from clk_a domain, otherwise dataout holds its value. This achieves safe synchronization and controlled data update under given constraints.
