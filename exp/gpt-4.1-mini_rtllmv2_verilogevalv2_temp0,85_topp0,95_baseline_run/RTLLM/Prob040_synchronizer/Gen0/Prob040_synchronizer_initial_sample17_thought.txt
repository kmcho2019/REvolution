Implement the synchronizer module as described:
- Two separate clock domains clk_a and clk_b with respective async resets.
- On clk_a domain, latch data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively.
- Transfer en_data_reg into clk_b domain through two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two.
- Use en_clap_two to gate updating of dataout with data_reg on clk_b domain.
- If en_clap_two is low, dataout retains its previous value.
- Handle resets asynchronously and active-low for both domains.
- Output width is 4 bits matching data_in.
