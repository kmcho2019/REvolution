Implement a multi-bit synchronizer with dual clocks clk_a and clk_b, incorporating two-stage enable synchronization in clk_b domain. Use registers clocked by clk_a to latch data_in and data_en. Then transfer data_en through two flip-flops clocked by clk_b to generate delayed enable signals. Use the delayed enable to control output data loading from data_reg to dataout, while retaining the old value if enable is low.
