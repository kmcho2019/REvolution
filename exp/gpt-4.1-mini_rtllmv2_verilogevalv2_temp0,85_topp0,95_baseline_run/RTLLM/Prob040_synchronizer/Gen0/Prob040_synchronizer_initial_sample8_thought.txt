Implement a multi-bit synchronizer crossing from clk_a domain to clk_b domain using a two-stage enable signal synchronization (en_clap_one, en_clap_two). Data and enable are registered in clk_a domain on arstn reset. Then en_data_reg is synchronized into clk_b domain with two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two. Data output dataout is updated on clk_b domain only when en_clap_two is high, otherwise retains previous value. Both resets arstn and brstn are active low. The data_in and data_en are 4-bit and 1-bit signals respectively. Output dataout is 4-bit.
