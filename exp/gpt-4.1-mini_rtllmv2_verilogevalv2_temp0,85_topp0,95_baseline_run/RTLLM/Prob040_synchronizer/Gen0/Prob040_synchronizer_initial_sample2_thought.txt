Implement the synchronizer module with two clock domains clk_a and clk_b. Use registers clocked by clk_a for data_in and data_en to store their stable values when data_en is high. Synchronize data_en to clk_b domain via two flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two). Use en_clap_two as enable to load data_reg's stable data_in value to dataout in clk_b domain. Use asynchronous active low resets for each clock domain. Maintain dataout value when en_clap_two is low.
