Implement a multi-bit synchronizer module named "synchronizer" that crosses data and enable signals from clk_a domain to clk_b domain. The data_in and data_en signals are sampled on clk_a with asynchronous reset arstn. The data_en signal is then synchronized into clk_b domain through two D flip-flops (en_clap_one, en_clap_two) with asynchronous reset brstn. The output dataout is updated on clk_b clock domain when the delayed enable signal en_clap_two is active, selecting data_reg sampled in clk_a domain. Otherwise, dataout retains its previous value. The 4-bit data and enable signals are properly registered and transferred as described.
