Implement a multi-bit synchronizer module that bridges signals from clk_a domain to clk_b domain using a two-stage enable signal synchronizer in clk_b domain. Data is latched into data_reg and en_data_reg on clk_a domain when data_en is asserted. Then data_en is synchronized into clk_b domain via two flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two). When en_clap_two is high, output dataout is updated with data_reg on clk_b domain. Asynchronous active-low resets (arstn and brstn) are used to initialize all registers to zero.
