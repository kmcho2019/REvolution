Implement a dual-clock domain synchronizer where data_in and data_en are sampled on clk_a domain. The enable signal data_en is double-registered in clk_b domain to create a delayed enable signal. When this delayed enable (en_clap_two) is active, output dataout is updated with the stable data_reg value. All resets are asynchronous active low. The data and enable signals are stored in registers in clk_a domain, then the enable is synchronized across clk_b domain with two flip-flops to avoid metastability and provide proper timing. A mux-like behavior is implemented by controlling output assignment based on delayed enable signal.
