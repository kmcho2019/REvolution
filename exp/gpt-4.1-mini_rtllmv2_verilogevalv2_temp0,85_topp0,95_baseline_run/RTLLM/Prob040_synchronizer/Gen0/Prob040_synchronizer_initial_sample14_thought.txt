Implement a 4-bit synchronizer module with two clocks clk_a and clk_b and asynchronous active low resets arstn and brstn. 
On clk_a domain, latch data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively with asynchronous reset arstn.
On clk_b domain, pass en_data_reg through two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two with asynchronous reset brstn to create a delayed enable signal.
On clk_b domain, output dataout is updated with data_reg when en_clap_two is high; otherwise, dataout holds its previous value.
This satisfies the timing and control requirements for stable data transfer between asynchronous clock domains using a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer.
