Implement a dual-clock domain synchronizer for a 4-bit data input. The data and enable signals are captured on clk_a domain with reset arstn. The enable signal is then passed through two flip-flops on clk_b domain with reset brstn to create a delayed enable control signal. When the delayed enable is high, the data from clk_a domain is transferred to clk_b domain output dataout via a mux controlled by the delayed enable signal. Reset conditions asynchronously clear registers appropriately in each clock domain.
