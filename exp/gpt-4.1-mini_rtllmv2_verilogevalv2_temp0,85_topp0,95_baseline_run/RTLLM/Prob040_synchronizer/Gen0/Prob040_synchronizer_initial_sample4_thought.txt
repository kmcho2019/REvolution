Implement a synchronizer module where input data_in and data_en are sampled on clk_a domain. The data_en signal is delayed by two stages on clk_b domain using en_clap_one and en_clap_two registers. When en_clap_two is high, output dataout on clk_b domain takes the stable data_reg value sampled on clk_a domain. This ensures data_in is stable and data_en is synchronized and delayed correctly between the two clock domains. Reset signals arstn and brstn asynchronously clear the respective registers.
