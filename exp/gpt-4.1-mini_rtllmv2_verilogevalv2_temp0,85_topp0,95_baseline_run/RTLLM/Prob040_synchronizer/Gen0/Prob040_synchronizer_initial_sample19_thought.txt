Implement the synchronizer module with two clock domains clk_a and clk_b. On clk_a domain, register data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively. Then, in clk_b domain, pass en_data_reg through two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two for two-cycle delay. Finally, on clk_b domain, output dataout is assigned data_reg when en_clap_two is high; else retains previous value. Handle asynchronous active-low resets arstn and brstn on respective domains.
