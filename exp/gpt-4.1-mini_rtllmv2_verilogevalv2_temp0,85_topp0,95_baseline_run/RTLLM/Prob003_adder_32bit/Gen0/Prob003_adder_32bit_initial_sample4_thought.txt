Implement a 32-bit adder using two instances of a 16-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA). First, design a 16-bit CLA module that calculates sum and carry-out using generate and propagate signals and a carry-lookahead logic to quickly compute carries. Then, instantiate this 16-bit CLA twice in the 32-bit adder: the lower 16 bits add A[16:1] and B[16:1] with carry-in 0, and the upper 16 bits add A[32:17] and B[32:17] with carry-in equal to the carry-out from the lower 16 bits. Finally, output the concatenated sum and the final carry-out.
