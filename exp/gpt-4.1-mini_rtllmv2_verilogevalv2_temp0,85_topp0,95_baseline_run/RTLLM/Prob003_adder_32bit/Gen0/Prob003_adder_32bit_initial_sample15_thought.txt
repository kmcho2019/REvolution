To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) using 16-bit CLA blocks, I will first design a 16-bit CLA module that takes two 16-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produces a 16-bit sum and a carry-out. The 16-bit CLA will internally compute generate and propagate signals for 4-bit groups to speed up carry calculation.

Then the top-level 32-bit adder module will instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks:
- The lower 16-bit CLA takes the lower halves of A and B with carry-in = 0.
- The upper 16-bit CLA takes the upper halves of A and B with carry-in connected to the carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA.

This hierarchical CLA design ensures fast carry computation by using lookahead logic at both 4-bit and 16-bit levels.

The inputs and outputs will be defined as requested with bit range [32:1] (non-standard indexing but accepted).

The code will follow the requested module and port names exactly.
