Implement a 16-bit CLA block with inputs A, B, carry-in and outputs sum and carry-out. Use generate and propagate signals internally for fast carry computation. Then instantiate two such 16-bit CLA blocks inside the top-level 32-bit adder module. Connect carry-out of the lower 16-bit block to the carry-in of the upper 16-bit block. Finally output the combined 32-bit sum and the carry-out from the upper block as C32.
