The design uses two 16-bit CLA blocks to build a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder. Each 16-bit CLA block computes sum, generate, and propagate signals for its bits and produces the carry-out. The carry-in to the second 16-bit block is the carry-out from the first block. The top module connects these blocks and outputs the final 32-bit sum and the carry-out bit from the most significant position.
