Implement a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) module first, which calculates sum, carry-out, and group generate and propagate signals. Then, implement the top-level 32-bit adder_32bit module by instantiating two 16-bit CLA blocks. The first 16-bit CLA takes carry-in as 0, and its carry-out is connected to the carry-in of the second 16-bit CLA. The overall carry-out is the carry-out of the second 16-bit CLA. This hierarchical approach uses the carry-lookahead architecture for fast addition.
