To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) using two 16-bit CLA blocks, I will first design a 16-bit CLA module. This module will have inputs A[16:1], B[16:1], a carry-in (C0), and outputs sum S[16:1] and carry-out (C16). The CLA logic will generate propagate and generate signals for each bit and compute carry signals efficiently.

Then, the top-level 32-bit adder will instantiate two of these 16-bit CLA blocks: the lower 16 bits and the upper 16 bits. The carry-out of the lower 16-bit CLA will be used as the carry-in for the upper 16-bit CLA. Finally, the outputs from both blocks will be concatenated to form the 32-bit sum, and the final carry-out will be the carry-out of the upper 16-bit block.

This hierarchical approach leverages the CLA structure for fast carry computation while keeping the design modular.
