I will design a 16-bit CLA block that computes the sum, carry-out, and internal carry signals using generate and propagate signals. Then, I will instantiate two such 16-bit CLA blocks inside the 32-bit adder top module. The carry-out of the first 16-bit block will be used as the carry-in to the second 16-bit block to implement the 32-bit addition efficiently with carry-lookahead logic.
