To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelined stages, I will split the 64-bit addition into multiple smaller blocks and pipeline the intermediate sums and carry bits across registers, controlled by clk and rst_n. Each pipeline stage will perform addition of a segment of the inputs plus carry-in from the previous stage, and store the partial sum and carry-out into registers for the next stage. The i_en signal will be registered and propagated through the pipeline to generate the final o_en signal aligned with the output result. The final result will be concatenated from the partial sums, including the final carry-out bit. This pipeline approach balances timing and supports high clock frequencies.
