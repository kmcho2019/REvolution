Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with multiple pipeline stages by splitting the 64-bit addition into smaller chunks (e.g., 16-bit segments) with registers between stages to pipeline the operation. Each stage adds a segment of the operands plus a carry-in, producing a partial sum and a carry-out that is passed to the next stage. The inputs and carry signals are registered to align timing. The output enable signal is pipelined similarly to indicate when the final result is valid. The final output is a concatenation of the partial sums and the last carry-out bit, making a 65-bit result.
