To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers, the addition will be split into smaller stages to allow pipelining, improving throughput by overlapping operations. Here, I will pipeline the 64-bit adder into 8 stages of 8-bit ripple carry adders each. Each stage will add 8 bits along with a carry-in from the previous stage and register the sum and carry-out. Registers will be used to pipeline inputs and carry signals, and the enable signal (i_en) will be propagated through pipeline registers to produce the output enable (o_en) at the final stage.

This approach balances pipeline depth and complexity, allowing each stage to compute part of the sum in one clock cycle. The final sum is assembled from the registered partial sums. The carry-out from the last stage forms the MSB of the 65-bit result.

Reset (rst_n) clears pipeline registers and disables output enable signals.
