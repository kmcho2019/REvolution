To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers, I will break down the 64-bit addition into several smaller pipeline stages, each stage adding a portion of the bits and passing the carry to the next stage. I will synchronize input operands and enable signals through pipeline registers to control timing and ensure stable data flow. Each pipeline stage will store partial sums and propagate carry to the next stage. The output sum and output enable signal will be updated in the last stage. The design will be synchronous with clk and reset asynchronously active-low (rst_n).
