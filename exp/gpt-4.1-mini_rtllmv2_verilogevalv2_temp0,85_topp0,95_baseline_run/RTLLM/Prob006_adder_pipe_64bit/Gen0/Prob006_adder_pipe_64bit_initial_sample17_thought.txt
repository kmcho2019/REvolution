To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers, I will split the 64-bit addition into smaller segments (e.g., 16-bit segments) to form pipeline stages. Each stage will add its segment with the carry input and produce a sum segment and carry output for the next stage. The pipeline registers will store intermediate sums and carry bits, as well as synchronize the input enable signal through the pipeline. The final output will be the concatenation of all sum segments plus the final carry bit, making a 65-bit result. The output enable signal (o_en) will be delayed through pipeline registers to indicate when the output is valid. All pipeline registers and control signals will be synchronous to clk and reset by active low rst_n.
