To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers, I will split the 64-bit addition into multiple stages (e.g., 8 stages with 8 bits each) to create pipeline stages. Each stage computes partial sums and passes the carry to the next stage in the following clock cycle, enabling pipelining of the addition operation. I will register inputs and carry signals to synchronize the pipeline and produce the final 65-bit result (64-bit sum + carry out). The output enable (o_en) will be pipelined as well to indicate when the final result is valid.
