To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelining, I will split the 64-bit adder into smaller stages (e.g., 8 stages of 8 bits each) to allow pipelining. Each stage will add 8 bits with carry-in and produce carry-out. Between stages, I will register both the partial sums and carry-outs, and also pipeline the input enable signal to generate the output enable. The final output will be the concatenation of all 8-bit sums plus the final carry-out bit, resulting in a 65-bit sum. The design will have synchronous reset and use registers to pipeline signals on clk. This approach balances pipelining depth and ripple carry simplicity.
