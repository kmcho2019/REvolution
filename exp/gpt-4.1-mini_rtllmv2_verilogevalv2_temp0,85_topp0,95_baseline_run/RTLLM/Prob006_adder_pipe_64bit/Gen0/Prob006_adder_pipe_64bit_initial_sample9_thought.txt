Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages by splitting the 64-bit addition into smaller chunks (e.g., 16-bit blocks). Each pipeline stage calculates the sum for its 16-bit portion and propagates the carry to the next stage in the following clock cycle. Registers store intermediate sums and carries to enable pipelining, and the i_en signal is synchronized through the pipeline stages to generate o_en. The final sum output is assembled from the pipeline stage outputs, and o_en indicates when the output is valid.
