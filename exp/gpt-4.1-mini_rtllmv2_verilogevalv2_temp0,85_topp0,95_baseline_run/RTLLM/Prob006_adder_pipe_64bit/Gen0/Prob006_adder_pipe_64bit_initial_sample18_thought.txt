To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers, I will split the 64-bit addition into multiple pipeline stages, for example 4 stages each handling 16 bits. Each stage will take its input operands and an incoming carry, compute the partial sum and the outgoing carry, and register the intermediate results. The inputs i_en, adda, addb will be registered at the first stage, and the enable signal will be pipelined along with the data so that o_en goes high when the final result is valid. The output result will be the concatenation of all partial sums plus the final carry out bit, forming a 65-bit result. All registers will be synchronous to clk and reset asynchronously with rst_n. This approach enables throughput improvement by pipelining the addition, although the ripple carry within each stage is combinational. The number of stages and width per stage can be adjusted; here I choose 4 stages of 16 bits each as a good balance.
