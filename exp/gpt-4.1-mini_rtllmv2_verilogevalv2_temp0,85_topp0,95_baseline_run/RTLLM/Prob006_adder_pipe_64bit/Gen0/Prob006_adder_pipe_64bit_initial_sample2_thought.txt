Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline registers to break the addition into stages. The adder will be divided into 4 pipeline stages, each handling 16 bits of addition with carry propagation. Each stage will have registered inputs and outputs to enable pipelining. The input enable (i_en) will be registered and propagated through pipeline registers to generate the output enable (o_en). The 65-bit result will be registered at the output stage. The design resets synchronously with active-low reset, clearing all pipeline registers and o_en.
