To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, the 64-bit adder will be split into smaller blocks (e.g., 8 bits per stage, making 8 pipeline stages). Each stage will take a segment of the operands and the carry-in from the previous stage, outputting a partial sum and carry-out. The carry-out will be registered and passed to the next stage in the next clock cycle. Registers will be inserted to pipeline the inputs and the carry chain, and also to pipeline the enable signal (i_en) so that the output enable (o_en) reflects the valid output data. The final output will be the concatenation of all partial sums and the last carry-out bit. This approach allows the adder to produce valid output after a latency equal to the number of pipeline stages. Reset (active low) will clear registers. The input operands and enable signal are latched each stage to synchronize and pipeline the data.
