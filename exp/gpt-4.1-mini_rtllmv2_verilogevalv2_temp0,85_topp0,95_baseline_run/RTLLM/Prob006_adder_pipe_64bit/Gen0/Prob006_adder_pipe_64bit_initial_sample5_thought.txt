To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelining, I'll split the 64-bit addition into smaller segments (e.g., 16-bit slices) to create pipeline stages. Each stage will add its portion of the inputs plus a carry-in from the previous stage, and register the partial sum and carry-out. The carry-out will be passed as carry-in to the next stage in the next clock cycle. The enable signal (i_en) will be pipelined alongside the data to generate the output enable (o_en) when the final stage's result is valid. The overall result is assembled from all pipeline stages' outputs. This approach balances latency and clock frequency by pipelining the addition, and output enable signals are synchronized accordingly.
