Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined into multiple stages to improve throughput. We can divide the 64-bit addition into 8 stages, each handling 8 bits. Each stage adds its 8-bit slice of the operands plus the carry-in from the previous stage. The carry out of each stage is registered to be passed to the next stage in the next clock cycle, thus creating a pipeline. The input operands and enable signals are also pipelined through registers to align with the stages.

The output result is collected from the concatenation of all stage sums and the final carry-out, forming a 65-bit sum. The o_en output is registered and delayed through the pipeline stages to indicate the validity of the output sum.

The module synchronizes all registers on the rising edge of clk, and asynchronously resets all registers on active low rst_n.
