Implement a frequency divider module that divides the input clock by an even number specified by the parameter NUM_DIV. The counter counts from 0 to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1), and at the end of this count, toggles the output clk_div and resets the counter. This ensures that the output clock frequency is input frequency divided by NUM_DIV. On reset, counter and clk_div are cleared. The counter is 4-bit wide as specified.
