Implement the 8-bit adder using a chain of eight 1-bit full adders connected in series. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each adder is connected to the carry-in of the next adder in the chain. The first full adder uses the module's input carry-in, and the last full adder's carry-out is the module's carry-out.
