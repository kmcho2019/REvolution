Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating 8 full adder modules connected in series. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder becomes the carry-in for the next higher bit full adder. The first full adder uses the module's input cin as carry-in, and the last full adder's carry-out is the module's cout output.
