Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating eight 1-bit full adders in series. Each full adder takes bits a[i], b[i], and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next higher bit full adder. The first full adder's carry-in is the input cin, and the last full adder's carry-out is the module's cout.
