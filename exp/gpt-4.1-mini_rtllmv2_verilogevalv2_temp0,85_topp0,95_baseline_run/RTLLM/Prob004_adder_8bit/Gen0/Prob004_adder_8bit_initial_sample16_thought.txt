Implement an 8-bit adder module named `adder_8bit` by instantiating eight 1-bit full adders in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder takes two bits from inputs `a` and `b` and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each bit-level adder cascades into the carry-in of the next higher bit. The final carry-out from the most significant bit is output as `cout`.
