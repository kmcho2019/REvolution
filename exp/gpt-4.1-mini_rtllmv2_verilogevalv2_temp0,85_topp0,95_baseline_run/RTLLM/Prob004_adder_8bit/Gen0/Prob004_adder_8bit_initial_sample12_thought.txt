Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating 8 full adders in a chain. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder connects to the carry-in of the next, starting with the module's cin input and ending with the final carry-out as cout. This approach uses structural Verilog to compose the 8-bit adder from multiple 1-bit full adders.
