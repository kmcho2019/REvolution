Implement an 8-bit adder using a chain of eight 1-bit full adders connected in series. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next higher bit full adder. The first full adder takes the module's cin as carry-in, and the last full adder's carry-out is the module's cout.
