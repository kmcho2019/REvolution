Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating eight 1-bit full adders connected in series. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each bit-level adder feeds into the carry-in of the next bit-level adder, starting from the module's input carry-in. The final carry-out is the carry-out of the most significant bit adder.
