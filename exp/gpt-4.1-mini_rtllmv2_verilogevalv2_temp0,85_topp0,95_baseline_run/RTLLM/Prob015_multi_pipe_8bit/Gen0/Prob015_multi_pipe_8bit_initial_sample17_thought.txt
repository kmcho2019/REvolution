Design a pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier with registers at each stage to improve throughput.  
1. Register input enable signal mul_en_in in mul_en_out_reg to generate mul_en_out (MSB of mul_en_out_reg).  
2. Register inputs mul_a and mul_b when mul_en_in is asserted.  
3. Generate partial products for each bit of mul_b by ANDing mul_a with mul_b's bits.  
4. Pipeline the summation of partial products over multiple clock cycles using registers sum[0] to sum[6].  
5. In each pipeline stage, add shifted partial products to the previous sum register.  
6. Store the final 16-bit product in mul_out_reg at the last pipeline stage.  
7. Output mul_out_reg when mul_en_out is high; otherwise output 0.  
8. Reset all registers to zero on active-low reset (rst_n=0).  
This approach balances combinational logic and registers to achieve pipelining and higher clock frequency.
