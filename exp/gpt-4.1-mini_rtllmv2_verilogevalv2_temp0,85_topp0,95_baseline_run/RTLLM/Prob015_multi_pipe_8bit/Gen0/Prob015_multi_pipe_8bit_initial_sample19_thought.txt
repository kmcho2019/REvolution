Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelined stages. First, latch input enable and inputs when mul_en_in is active. Generate partial products by ANDing each bit of mul_b with mul_a. Then sum partial products in a pipelined fashion across several clock cycles to spread addition operations, using registers to hold intermediate sums. The output enable signal is delayed through the pipeline stages to indicate valid output. Finally, the complete 16-bit product is output when mul_en_out is active; otherwise zero.
