Design an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a pipelined architecture that processes the multiplication over multiple clock cycles to improve throughput. The pipeline stages include:

1. Input stage: Sample and store mul_a, mul_b and mul_en_in in registers when mul_en_in is asserted.
2. Partial product generation: Compute partial products by ANDing mul_a with each bit of mul_b.
3. Partial sum calculation: Add partial products stepwise over multiple pipeline stages using registers to store intermediate sums.
4. Final product calculation: Sum all partial sums to produce the final 16-bit product.
5. Control pipeline the mul_en_in signal through registers to generate mul_en_out indicating valid output.
6. On reset (active low), clear all registers.

The outputs are registered, and the product is zero when not enabled.

This pipelined approach divides multiplication into multiple clock cycles, improving max clock frequency.

The pipeline depth will be 4 stages:
- Stage 1: Register inputs and generate partial products.
- Stage 2: Add partial products pairwise, store partial sums.
- Stage 3: Add partial sums.
- Stage 4: Final sum and output register.

mul_en_in is delayed through 4 registers to produce mul_en_out.
