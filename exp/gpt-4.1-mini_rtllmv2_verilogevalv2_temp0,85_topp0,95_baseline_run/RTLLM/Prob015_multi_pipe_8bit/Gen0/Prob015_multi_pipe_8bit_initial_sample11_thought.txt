Implement a pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier that registers inputs and enables on the rising edge of clk and active-low reset. The pipeline stages include input sampling, partial product generation using bitwise ANDs, partial sum calculation through registered adders, and final accumulation into mul_out_reg. The enable signals are pipelined alongside the data to ensure output enable syncs with the output product. The final output mul_out is gated by mul_en_out, and reset clears all registers.
