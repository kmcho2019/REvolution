Design a pipelined 8-bit multiplier with clocked input registers and enable signals. On each clock edge, sample the enable and inputs, generate partial products based on multiplier bits, and accumulate sums in pipeline stages using registers. Finally, output the product along with a delayed enable signal, ensuring output is valid only when the enable is active. The pipeline stages include input sampling, partial product generation, partial sum addition, and final product register. Active-low reset clears all registers.
