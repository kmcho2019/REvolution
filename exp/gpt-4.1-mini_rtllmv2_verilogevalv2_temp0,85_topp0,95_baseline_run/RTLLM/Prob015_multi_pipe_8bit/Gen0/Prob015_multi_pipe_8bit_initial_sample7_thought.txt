Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with a pipelined architecture to improve throughput. On each clock cycle with mul_en_in asserted, latch inputs mul_a and mul_b and the enable signal into registers. Generate partial products by ANDing mul_a with each bit of mul_b, shifted accordingly. Use registers to accumulate partial sums stepwise in the pipeline stages. Use a register mul_en_out_reg to propagate the enable signal through the pipeline, with mul_en_out as its MSB indicating valid output. On reset, clear all registers. Output the final product mul_out only when mul_en_out is asserted; otherwise output zero.
