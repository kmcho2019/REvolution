Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining stages to improve throughput. The design registers inputs and enables, generates partial products based on multiplier bits, sums partial products in pipeline registers stage-by-stage, and finally outputs the 16-bit product along with an output enable signal. The pipeline will include input registers, partial product generation, partial sum stages, and final product register, all clocked and reset synchronously with the active-low reset. The output enable pipeline matches the latency of the multiplication pipeline.
