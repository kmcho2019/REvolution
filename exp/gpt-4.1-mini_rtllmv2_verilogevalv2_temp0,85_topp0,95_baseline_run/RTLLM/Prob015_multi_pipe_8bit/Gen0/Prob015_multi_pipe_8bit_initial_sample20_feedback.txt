**Issues identified:**

- **Incorrect Data Dependencies and Pipeline Stage Misalignment:**
  The partial sums at each pipeline stage depend combinationally on registers (`sum_stage1`, `sum_stage2`) and the partial products computed from the current cycle's registered inputs. However, the summations of subsequent stages use the sums computed in the same clock cycle (`sum_stage1` and `sum_stage2`), which are themselves being updated simultaneously in this cycle. This creates combinational feedback or race conditions, as the new values of `sum_stage1` and `sum_stage2` are used before they have been registered, violating the pipeline timing.

- **No Pipeline Register Staging for Partial Sums Between Cycles:**
  The pipeline stages do not separate combinational partial product sums from registered sums for next stage calculations. Each sum stage's calculation happens in the same clock cycle alongside register updates, which breaks the pipeline's purpose and sequencing.

- **Input Sampling and Partial Product Generation Dependency:**
  Although inputs are sampled only when `mul_en_in` is high, partial products depend on the registered inputs every cycle. Due to the pipeline, partial products should be generated from stable, staged registers corresponding to each pipeline stage. The current design does not hold or propagate the inputs through pipeline registers across multiple cycles, leading to invalid partial products in later cycles.
