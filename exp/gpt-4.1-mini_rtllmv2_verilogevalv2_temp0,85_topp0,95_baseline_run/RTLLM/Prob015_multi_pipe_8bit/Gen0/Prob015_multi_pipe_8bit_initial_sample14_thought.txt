To design an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining, I will first register inputs and the enable signal at stage 1. Then, generate partial products for each bit of the multiplier and register them as stage 2. Next, add the partial products in a pipelined manner across subsequent stages to reduce the addition tree delay. Finally, register the full product and the output enable signal at the last stage. The output enable signal propagates through the pipeline registers to indicate valid output. The product is output only when the output enable is asserted; otherwise, zero is output.
