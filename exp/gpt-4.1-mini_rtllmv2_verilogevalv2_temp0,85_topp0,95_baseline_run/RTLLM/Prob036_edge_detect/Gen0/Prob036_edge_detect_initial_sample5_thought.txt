To detect edges on the input signal 'a', we will register its previous value on each clock cycle. Comparing the current and previous values of 'a' will allow us to detect rising (0 to 1) and falling (1 to 0) edges. The outputs 'rise' and 'down' will be set to 1 for exactly one clock cycle following the detection of their respective edges, and will be cleared otherwise. Reset is asynchronous active low to initialize the stored previous value and output signals.
