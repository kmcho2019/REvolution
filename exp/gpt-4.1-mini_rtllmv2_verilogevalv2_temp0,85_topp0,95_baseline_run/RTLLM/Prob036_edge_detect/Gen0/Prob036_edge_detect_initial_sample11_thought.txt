To detect edges on signal 'a', register its previous state at each clock cycle. On each clock's positive edge, compare the current and previous values of 'a'. If the previous was 0 and current is 1, it's a rising edge; if previous was 1 and current is 0, it's a falling edge. The outputs 'rise' and 'down' are set to 1 on the clock cycle after the edge is detected and remain 0 otherwise. The outputs are synchronous to the clock and reset asynchronously with active low reset 'rst_n'.
