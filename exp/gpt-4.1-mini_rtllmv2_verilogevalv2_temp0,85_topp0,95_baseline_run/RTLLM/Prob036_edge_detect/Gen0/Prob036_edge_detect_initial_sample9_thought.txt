To detect edges on a signal 'a', first register the previous value of 'a' at each clock cycle. Then compare the current and previous values to detect rising edges (prev_a=0, a=1) and falling edges (prev_a=1, a=0). The rise and down signals are set to 1 on the clock cycle following the detection of these edges and then cleared until the next respective edge appears. This means rise and down are pulses, one clock cycle wide, synchronized to clk. Use synchronous reset (active low) to initialize registers.
