To detect edges on a signal 'a', store the previous value of 'a' in a register on every clock cycle. Then compare the current 'a' with the previous value to determine if a rising or falling edge occurred. Generate one-cycle pulses for rise and down signals synchronized to the clock. The signals rise and down are set to 1 on the next clock cycle after the edge is detected and then reset to 0 until the next edge occurs. The design uses synchronous reset (active low).
