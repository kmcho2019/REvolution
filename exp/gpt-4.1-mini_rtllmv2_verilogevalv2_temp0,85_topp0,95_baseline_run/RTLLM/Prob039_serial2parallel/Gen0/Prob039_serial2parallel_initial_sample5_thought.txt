Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of valid serial inputs received. On each rising edge of clk, if din_valid is high, shift the din_serial bit into a shift register that stores 8 bits. The most recent serial bit goes into the most significant bit of the shift register, shifting previous bits toward the less significant bits. When the counter reaches 7 (i.e., after receiving 8 bits indexed 0 to 7), output the 8-bit parallel data and set dout_valid to 1 for one clock cycle. Reset counter and dout_valid appropriately. Also handle asynchronous active-low reset.
