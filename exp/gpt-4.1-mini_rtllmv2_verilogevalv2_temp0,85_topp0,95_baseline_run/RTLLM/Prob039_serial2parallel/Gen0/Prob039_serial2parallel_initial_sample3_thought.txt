Implement a synchronous serial-to-parallel converter that shifts in serial data bits on each valid input at the rising edge of the clock. Use a 4-bit counter to count up to 8 bits. The serial bits are shifted into an 8-bit register from the MSB to LSB, meaning the first received bit goes to dout_parallel[7], then the next to dout_parallel[6], and so on until dout_parallel[0]. When 8 bits have been received, set dout_valid high for one clock cycle. Use active-low synchronous reset to clear registers and counter.
