Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of valid serial inputs received. On each rising clock edge, if reset is inactive and din_valid is high, shift din_serial into an 8-bit shift register from MSB to LSB position (shifting left), increment the counter. When the counter reaches 8, output the accumulated 8-bit data on dout_parallel and set dout_valid to 1 for one clock cycle, then reset the counter and dout_valid. Keep dout_valid low when less than 8 bits are collected.
