Design a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages to store intermediate sums. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size zeros at the MSB to align for shifting. Partial products are generated for each bit of mul_b using a generate block. The first pipeline register stores the sum of partial products for bits 0 and 1, the second pipeline register stores the sum for bits 2 and 3. Finally, on the next clock, mul_out is computed by summing the two pipeline registers. All registers are synchronously reset by active-low rst_n.
