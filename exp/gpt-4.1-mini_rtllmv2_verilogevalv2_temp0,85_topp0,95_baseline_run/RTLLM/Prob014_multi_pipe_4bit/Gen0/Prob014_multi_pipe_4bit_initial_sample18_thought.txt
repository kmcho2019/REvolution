Implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two pipeline stages. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size zeros at MSB side to create operands with 8 bits. For each bit of mul_b, generate partial products by left-shifting mul_a when mul_b bit is 1, else 0. In the first pipeline stage, registers store the first half of partial products' sums. In the second pipeline stage, registers sum the remaining partial products and add to the first stage's sum. On reset (active low), all registers and output are cleared to zero. On each clock edge, the pipeline registers are updated and output is the sum of the two pipeline registers, producing the final 8-bit product mul_out.
