The design extends the 4-bit inputs by adding 4 zeros at the MSB side to make them 8 bits. Then, using a generate block, it computes partial products for each bit of the multiplier by left-shifting the multiplicand or zeroing. Two levels of registers are used to pipeline the addition of partial products: the first level accumulates sums of partial products in pairs, and the second level sums the results from the first level. On each clock edge, these registers update or reset on rst_n. Finally, the output is updated with the sum from the second level of registers. This pipeline design breaks the multiply-add operation into stages controlled by clock and reset.
