Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline using registers for intermediate sums. 
Extend the inputs by padding zeros at MSB side to double the width. 
Generate partial products using the multiplier bits: if bit i is 1, partial product = multiplicand shifted left by i; else 0.
In the first pipeline stage, sum the partial products for the lower half bits.
In the second pipeline stage, sum the intermediate sums stored in registers to produce the final product.
Use synchronous reset and clock edge-triggered registers to update intermediate sums and final output.
