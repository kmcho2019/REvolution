The design extends both multiplicand and multiplier by size bits at MSB with zeros. Then, using a generate block, partial products are computed: for each bit of multiplier, if set, the multiplicand is shifted left by the bit index; else zero. These partial products are then accumulated in two pipeline register stages. On clock or reset, registers store intermediate sums or reset to zero. Finally, the output product is computed as the sum of the registers on the next stage clock edge or reset. This pipeline breaks the multiplication addition into two levels of registers for partial sums, ensuring synchronous operation with reset logic.
