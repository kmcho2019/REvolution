Design a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with pipeline stages using two levels of registers to store intermediate sums. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size zeros at the MSB side to align widths for partial product calculation. For each bit of mul_b, generate a partial product by ANDing mul_a with that bit and left-shifting by the bit index, or zero if the bit is 0. In the first clock stage, accumulate partial products 0 and 1 into a register stage1, and partial products 2 and 3 into another register stage2. In the second clock stage, add stage1 and stage2 registers and output the final product. Reset logic clears the registers and output on rst_n low. Use generate block and procedural blocks for synchronous logic.
