The design implements a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages of registers to store intermediate sums. The inputs mul_a and mul_b are zero-extended by 'size' bits at the MSB side, effectively doubling their width to 8 bits internally. For each bit of mul_b, partial products are generated by checking if the bit is 1, then shifting mul_a left by that bit position; otherwise zero. These partial products are added in a pipelined manner using two levels of registers. On reset (active low), all registers and outputs are cleared. On clock edge, the partial products sums are updated first in pipeline registers, then the final sum is updated in the output register, completing the multiplication pipeline.
