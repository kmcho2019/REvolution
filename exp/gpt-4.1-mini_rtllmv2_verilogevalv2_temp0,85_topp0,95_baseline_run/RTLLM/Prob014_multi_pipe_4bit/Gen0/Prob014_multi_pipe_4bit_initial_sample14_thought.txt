Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining using two levels of registers.
Extend inputs by adding 'size' zeros at MSB.
Generate partial products for each bit of multiplier using generate block.
At first clock cycle (or after reset deassertion), registers store partial products sums.
At second clock cycle, registers store sum of previous registers (intermediate sums).
Finally, output product is registered sum of these intermediate sums.
On reset, clear all registers and output.
This approach spreads addition over two pipeline stages.
