The design extends the 4-bit multiplicand and multiplier by adding 4 zero bits at the MSB side to form 8-bit signals. It then generates partial products by checking each bit of the multiplier; if the bit is 1, the multiplicand is left-shifted by the bit index, else zero. The partial products are accumulated in two pipeline stages using registers: the first stage sums partial products 0 and 1, the second stage sums partial products 2 and 3. Finally, on the output stage, these two sums are added and output as the final product. The pipeline registers and output register are updated synchronously on clk's rising edge or asynchronously reset on rst_n falling edge. This structure ensures a two-level pipelined multiplication with two stages of registers storing intermediate sums.
