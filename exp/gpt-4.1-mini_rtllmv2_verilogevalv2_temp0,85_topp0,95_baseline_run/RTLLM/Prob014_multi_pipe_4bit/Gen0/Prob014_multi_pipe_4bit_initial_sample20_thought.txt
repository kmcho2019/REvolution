Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages for partial sum accumulation. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size zeros at the MSB side before partial product generation. Partial products are generated in a generate block by checking each bit of mul_b and left-shifting mul_a accordingly. In the first pipeline stage, registers store sums of pairs of partial products. In the second pipeline stage, registers store the sum of the first stage sums. Finally, the output register holds the final product. Reset logic zeroes all registers asynchronously on rst_n low. Multiplication and additions are synchronous with clk.
