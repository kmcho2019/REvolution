Design a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-level pipeline. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size bits (4 bits) of zeros on the MSB side to prevent overflow during partial product shifts. Use a generate block to create partial products by checking each bit of mul_b. Store these partial products in registers at the first pipeline stage. At the second pipeline stage, sum the partial products to get the final product. Use synchronous reset and clocking for all registers and output. The pipeline has two register levels: one for partial products and one for the accumulated sum (final product). This approach breaks down the multiplication into a pipelined process with two clock cycles latency.
