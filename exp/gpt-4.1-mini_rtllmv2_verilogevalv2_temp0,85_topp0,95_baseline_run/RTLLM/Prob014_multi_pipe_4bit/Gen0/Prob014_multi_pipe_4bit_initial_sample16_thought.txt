Implement a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier with two pipeline stages for partial product summation. First, extend inputs by prepending 'size' zeros on the MSB side to both multiplicand and multiplier. Use a generate block to calculate each partial product based on each bit of the multiplier, left-shifted accordingly or zero if the bit is 0. In the first pipeline stage, sum partial products in pairs and store in intermediate registers. In the second pipeline stage, sum these intermediate registers to get the final product, which is registered as output. Handle synchronous reset (active-low) to clear registers and output.
