Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a pipeline having two levels of registers. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are each extended by prepending 'size' zeros, making them 8 bits wide. For each bit of mul_b, generate the partial product by ANDing mul_a with that bit and left-shifting by the bit index. Sum these partial products in two pipeline stages: first half in first register stage, second half in second register stage. On each clock edge, if reset is inactive, update pipeline registers with sums of corresponding partial products. Finally, combine the two pipeline register values in the last stage and output the result. Reset asynchronously clears registers and output.
