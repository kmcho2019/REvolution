The design extends the inputs by padding zeros at the MSB side to double their width. For each bit of the multiplier, a partial product is generated by left-shifting the multiplicand if the bit is set, otherwise zero. These partial products are added in two pipeline stages with registers: first, sums of partial products in pairs are stored in level-1 registers, then these sums are added in level-2 registers, and finally the output register stores the total product. All registers are synchronous with clk and active-low asynchronous reset.
