The design extends the inputs by adding zero bits at the MSB side to double their width. It generates partial products for each bit of mul_b, where each partial product is mul_a shifted left by the bit index if the multiplier bit is 1, otherwise zero. These partial products are added in two pipeline stages. The first pipeline register stage accumulates the sum of the first half of partial products, and the second pipeline register stage accumulates the sum of the second half. The final product is formed by adding the outputs of these two pipeline registers on the next clock cycle. Synchronous reset (active low) is used to clear the registers and output. The generate block is used to create partial products efficiently, and the pipeline registers hold intermediate sums, ensuring a pipelined multiplication.
