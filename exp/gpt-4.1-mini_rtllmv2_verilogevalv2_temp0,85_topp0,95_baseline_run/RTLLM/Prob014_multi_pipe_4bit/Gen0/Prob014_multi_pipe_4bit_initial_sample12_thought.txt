To design a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining and two levels of registers, first extend mul_a and mul_b by prefixing size zeros. Then, generate partial products by checking each bit of mul_b: if the bit is 1, shift mul_a left by that bit index; otherwise zero. In the first clock cycle, accumulate partial products for lower half bits into first stage registers. In the second clock cycle, accumulate partial products for upper half bits and add the first stage registers to form the final product stored in mul_out.
