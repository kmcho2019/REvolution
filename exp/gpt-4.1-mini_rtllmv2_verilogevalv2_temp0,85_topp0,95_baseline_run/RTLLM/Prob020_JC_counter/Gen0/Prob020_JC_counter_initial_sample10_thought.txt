Implement a 64-bit Johnson counter (torsional ring counter) which shifts right on each clock cycle. On reset (active low), clear the register to 0. On every rising clock edge (if not reset), check the LSB of Q: if 0, shift right and insert a 1 at MSB; if 1, shift right and insert a 0 at MSB. This will generate a Johnson sequence similar to the 4-bit example but extended to 64 bits.
