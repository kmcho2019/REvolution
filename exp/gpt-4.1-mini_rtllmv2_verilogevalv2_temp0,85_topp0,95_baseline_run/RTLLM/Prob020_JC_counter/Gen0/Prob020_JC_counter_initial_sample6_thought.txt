Implement a 64-bit Johnson counter that updates on the rising edge of clk or the falling edge of rst_n. When rst_n is low, reset Q to 0. On each clock cycle, check Q[0]; if it's 0, shift Q right by 1 and insert 1 at MSB; if it's 1, shift right by 1 and insert 0 at MSB. This logic extends the example 4-bit Johnson counter to 64 bits.
