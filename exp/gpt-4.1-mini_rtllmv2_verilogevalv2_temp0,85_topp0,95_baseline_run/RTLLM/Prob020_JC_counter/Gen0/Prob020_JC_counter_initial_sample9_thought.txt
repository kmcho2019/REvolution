Implement a 64-bit Johnson counter that updates on the rising edge of clk or falling edge of rst_n.
On reset (rst_n low), set Q to 0.
Otherwise, check the LSB Q[0]. If 0, shift right by one and put 1 at MSB.
If 1, shift right by one and put 0 at MSB.
This creates a torsional ring behavior as described.
