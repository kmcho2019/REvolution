The design will use a register `data_lock` to hold the first 8-bit data input when `valid_in` is high and a flag to indicate that one piece of data is stored. Upon receiving the second 8-bit data input, the module will concatenate the stored `data_lock` (as higher 8 bits) and current `data_in` (as lower 8 bits) to form the 16-bit output. The output `valid_out` and `data_out` are registered and updated one clock cycle after the second valid input. On reset, all registers are cleared. This ensures that the output only appears after two valid inputs and keeps data consistent across cycles.
