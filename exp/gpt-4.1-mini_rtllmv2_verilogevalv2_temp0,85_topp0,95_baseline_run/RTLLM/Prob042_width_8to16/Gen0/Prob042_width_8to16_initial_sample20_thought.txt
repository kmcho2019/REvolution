Use a register data_lock to store the first 8-bit data when valid_in is high and no previous data is stored (indicated by a flag). When the second 8-bit data arrives, concatenate data_lock (higher 8 bits) and data_in (lower 8 bits) and output them on data_out with valid_out asserted in the next clock cycle. The output valid_out and data_out are registered and only updated when two data inputs have been received. Reset clears all registers and outputs.
