Use an internal flag to indicate when the first 8-bit data has arrived and is stored in a temporary register (data_lock). When valid_in is high and the flag is not set, store the first 8-bit data in data_lock and set the flag. On the next valid_in (flag set), concatenate data_lock (higher 8 bits) and data_in (lower 8 bits) to form the 16-bit output data_out and assert valid_out in the following clock cycle. Clear the flag after output generation to prepare for next pair. valid_out and data_out are updated on the next clock cycle after receiving the second input. On reset, clear all registers and flags.
