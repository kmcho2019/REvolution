Implement a synchronous MAC unit with a 32-bit accumulator register. Use an always block triggered on the positive edge of clk or rst. On reset, clear the accumulator to zero. Otherwise, multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' (32-bit operands) producing a 64-bit intermediate product. Accumulate the lower 32 bits of the product into the 32-bit register 'c'. The output 'c' presents the current accumulated value. Since the accumulator is only 32 bits and the product is 64 bits, either truncate or handle overflow as per requirement. Here, we will simply accumulate the lower 32 bits for simplicity.
