Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit register 'c' that accumulates the product of inputs 'a' and 'b'. On reset, 'c' clears to zero. On each rising clock edge when not in reset, multiply 'a' and 'b' (32-bit each) to get a 64-bit product, then accumulate the lower 32 bits of this product into 'c'. Since 'c' is 32-bit, accumulation is done modulo 2^32, discarding overflow. The output 'c' reflects the current accumulator value.
