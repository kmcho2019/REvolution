Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit output register 'c' that accumulates the product of 32-bit inputs 'a' and 'b' on each clock cycle. On reset (active high), clear 'c' to zero. Multiplying two 32-bit numbers results in a 64-bit product, but since the output 'c' is 32-bit, accumulate only the lower 32 bits of the product to 'c', or alternatively accumulate in 64-bit and truncate output. To avoid overflow issues, accumulate in a wider register internally (64-bit) and output the lower 32 bits to 'c'.
