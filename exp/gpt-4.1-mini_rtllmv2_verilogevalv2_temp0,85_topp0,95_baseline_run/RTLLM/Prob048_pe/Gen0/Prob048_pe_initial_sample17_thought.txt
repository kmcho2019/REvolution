Implement a synchronous MAC unit with a 32-bit accumulator register. On reset, clear the accumulator to zero. On each clock edge, multiply the 32-bit inputs and add the result to the accumulator. Since multiplication of two 32-bit numbers results in a 64-bit product, but the accumulator and output are 32 bits, the multiplication result must be truncated or handled carefully. Here, we will truncate to the lower 32 bits for accumulation, as per the specification of a 32-bit accumulator output.
