Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator (MAC) module named "pe" with synchronous reset. The module multiplies inputs 'a' and 'b' and accumulates the result in a 32-bit register 'c'. On reset, 'c' is cleared to 0. On each clock edge, if not reset, 'c' updates by adding the product of 'a' and 'b'. Handle the product width carefully since 32x32 multiplication results in a 64-bit product; to fit the 32-bit accumulator, truncate or saturate as needed. Here, we will truncate the lower 32 bits of the product for accumulation.
