Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator with a synchronous reset. Use a 64-bit internal register to hold the accumulation result since multiplying two 32-bit numbers results in a 64-bit product, preventing overflow. On reset, clear the accumulator to zero. On each positive clock edge when not in reset, multiply inputs a and b, then add the product to the accumulator. Output the lower 32 bits of the accumulator as c, as specified.
