Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator that resets the 32-bit accumulator register 'c' on reset. On each rising clock edge, if not in reset, multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' (both 32-bit), add the result to the current 'c', and store back in 'c'. Because multiplying two 32-bit numbers produces a 64-bit result, but 'c' is 32-bit, truncate or handle overflow by keeping only the lower 32 bits of the accumulation.
