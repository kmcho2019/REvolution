Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit accumulator register 'c'. On reset, the register clears to 0. On each clock cycle, multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' and add the result to 'c'. Since multiplying two 32-bit numbers can produce a 64-bit result, the accumulator should be at least 64 bits wide to avoid overflow during accumulation. The output 'c' will be the lower 32 bits of the accumulated sum, as per the interface requirements.
