Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit accumulator register 'c'. On each positive clock edge, if reset is asserted, clear 'c' to zero; otherwise multiply inputs 'a' and 'b', then add the product to 'c'. Since multiplication of two 32-bit integers yields a 64-bit result, but the accumulator output 'c' is 32-bit, handle overflow by truncating the lower 32 bits of the accumulation. Use a 64-bit internal register to hold the accumulator value to avoid overflow within accumulation, and output the lower 32 bits as 'c'.
