Implement a sequential multiplying accumulator module named "pe" with 32-bit inputs "a" and "b" and a 32-bit output "c". The accumulator register "c" resets to zero on the positive edge of "rst" and otherwise accumulates the product of "a" and "b" on each positive clock edge. Since multiplying two 32-bit numbers results in up to 64-bit, but the output "c" is only 32-bit, the accumulator will truncate the result to 32 bits by accumulating only the lower 32 bits of the product.
