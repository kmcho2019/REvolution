Use a finite state machine (FSM) to detect the 0->1->0 pattern on data_in. The FSM states will track the progress: IDLE (waiting for 0->1 edge), HIGH_DETECTED (waiting for the final 0 after the 1), and PULSE_END (indicating a pulse detected in the current cycle). On detecting a pulse, data_out is asserted for one clock cycle at the end of the pulse detection. The FSM resets on rst_n. This ensures data_out is high only on the last 0 of the pattern 0->1->0.
