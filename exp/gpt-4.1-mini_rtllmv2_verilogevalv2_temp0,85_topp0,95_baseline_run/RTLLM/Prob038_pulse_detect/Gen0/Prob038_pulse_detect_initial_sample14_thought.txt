Use a simple FSM with four states to detect the pattern 0->1->0, corresponding to the pulse. Start at IDLE state, wait for data_in=0. When data_in goes to 1, move to a second state, then wait for data_in=0 again. On the transition back to 0, output data_out=1 for one cycle, then return to IDLE. Reset asynchronously clears state and data_out.
