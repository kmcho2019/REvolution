Implement a finite state machine with states representing the stages of the pulse detection: IDLE (waiting for 0->1), HIGH (waiting for 1->0), and PULSE_END (detected the pulse). On reset, go to IDLE and output 0. On each clock edge, update the state based on data_in transitions. When the pulse (0->1->0) is detected at the end of the third cycle, output data_out=1 for one cycle, then return to IDLE to detect new pulses.
