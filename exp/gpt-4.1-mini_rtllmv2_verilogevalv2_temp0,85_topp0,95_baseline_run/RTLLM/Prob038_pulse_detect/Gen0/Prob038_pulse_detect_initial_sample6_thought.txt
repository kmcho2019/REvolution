Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE (waiting for data_in=1), HIGH_DETECTED (data_in=1 detected), LOW_DETECTED (detecting the trailing 0), and PULSE_END (pulse detected). On each clock, transition through the states based on data_in. When the pattern 0->1->0 is detected (pulse), assert data_out=1 for one clock cycle at the end of the pulse, then return to IDLE.
