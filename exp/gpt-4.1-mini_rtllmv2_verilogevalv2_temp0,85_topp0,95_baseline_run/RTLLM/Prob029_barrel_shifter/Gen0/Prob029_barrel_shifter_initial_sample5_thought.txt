Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter by cascading three stages of 2-to-1 multiplexers, each stage controlled by one bit of the 3-bit ctrl signal. Each stage shifts the intermediate data by 4, 2, or 1 bit(s) to the left (rotating bits). The mux2X1 modules select between the original bit and the shifted bit at each position, controlled by the corresponding ctrl bit. The output of one stage feeds into the next, allowing efficient variable rotation based on ctrl.
