Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter that rotates the input bits to the right based on the ctrl signal. The shift amount is composed of three bits: ctrl[2], ctrl[1], and ctrl[0], corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. The shifting is done in three stages, each controlled by one bit of ctrl. At each stage, use an array of mux2X1 to select between the unshifted and shifted bits. The shifting is done by rotating bits: for example, a 4-bit rotation shifts bits right by 4 positions with wrap-around. The final output is the result after all three stages.
