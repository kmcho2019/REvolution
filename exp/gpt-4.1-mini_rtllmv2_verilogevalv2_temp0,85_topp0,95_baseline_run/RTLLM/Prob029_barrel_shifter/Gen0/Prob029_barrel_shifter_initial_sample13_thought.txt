Implement the barrel shifter as a sequence of three stages, each conditionally rotating the input by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. Each stage uses 8 instances of a 2-to-1 multiplexer to select between shifted and non-shifted bits based on the corresponding control bit. The mux2X1 module is a simple 1-bit 2-to-1 mux. The final output is the result after all three stages.
