Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter using three stages of 2-to-1 multiplexers. Each stage corresponds to shifting by 4, 2, or 1 bit positions, controlled by ctrl[2], ctrl[1], and ctrl[0], respectively. The mux2X1 module selects between the shifted input and the original input at each bit position. The shift is circular (rotation). The output of one stage feeds into the next, producing the final rotated output.
