Design a barrel shifter that rotates an 8-bit input based on a 3-bit control signal. The shift amount is controlled in stages by ctrl[2], ctrl[1], and ctrl[0], corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 respectively. Each stage uses 2-to-1 multiplexers to select between the shifted version and the original input from the previous stage, enabling efficient rotation without complex arithmetic. The mux2X1 module will be implemented for bitwise selection.
