Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter module that uses three stages of shifting: by 4, 2, and 1 positions, controlled by the bits in ctrl[2:0]. Each stage uses 8 instances of a 2-to-1 multiplexer (mux2X1) to choose between the shifted and unshifted signals. The shifting is circular (rotating). The mux2X1 submodule is defined to select between two bits based on sel.
