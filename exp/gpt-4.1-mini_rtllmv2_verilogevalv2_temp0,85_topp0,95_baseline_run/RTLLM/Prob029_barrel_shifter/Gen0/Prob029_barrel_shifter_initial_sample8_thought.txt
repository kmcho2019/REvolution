Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter that rotates bits to the left by a variable amount specified by a 3-bit control signal. Each bit in the control signal indicates a stage of shifting: 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. Use a mux2X1 submodule to select between shifted and unshifted bits for each stage. The shifts are performed in stages, passing the intermediate output from one stage to the next.
