Implement the barrel shifter as a sequence of three stages of shifts by 4, 2, and 1 bit respectively. Each stage uses 8 instances of a 2-to-1 multiplexer (mux2X1) that select between the current bit and the bit shifted by the stage amount. The selection is controlled by the corresponding control bit in ctrl. The input flows through these stages in order, producing the final output. The mux2X1 module is defined to select one of its inputs based on the sel signal.
