Implement a sequential Radix-4 Booth multiplier that operates on 8-bit inputs a and b.
On reset, the multiplicand and multiplier are sign-extended to 16 bits and loaded into registers.
Use a 5-bit counter to process 16 iterations of multiplication steps.
In each cycle, check the current bit of the multiplier (multiplier[ctr]).
If it is 1, add the multiplicand to the product register.
After processing, shift the multiplicand left by 1 to simulate Booth's shifting.
When the counter reaches 16, set ready signal high to indicate completion.
Output the final product and ready flag.
