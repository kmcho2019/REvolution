Implement a sequential Radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs with a synchronous process triggered on clk or reset. On reset, sign-extend inputs to 16-bit registers multiplier and multiplicand. Use a 5-bit counter to track the 16 steps of the Booth algorithm. In each step, shift the multiplicand left, and if the current multiplier bit is 1, add the multiplicand to the product accumulator. Once all steps complete, set the ready signal high. Output the final product as p.
