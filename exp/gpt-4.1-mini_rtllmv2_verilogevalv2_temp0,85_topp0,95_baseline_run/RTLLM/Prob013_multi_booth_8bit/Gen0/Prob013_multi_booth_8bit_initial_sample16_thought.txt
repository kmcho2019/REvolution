Implement a radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs. On reset, load the multiplicand and multiplier into signed 16-bit registers with sign extension. Use a 5-bit counter to track the operation steps. On each clock cycle (except reset), if counter < 8 (since radix-4 processes 2 bits per iteration for 8-bit inputs), examine 3 bits from the multiplier register for Booth encoding. Based on the 3-bit Booth code, add or subtract multiples of the multiplicand shifted accordingly to the product register. Shift the multiplier and product registers combined accordingly per radix-4 Booth algorithm. Increment counter. When done, set ready signal. Provide synchronous reset and clocked operation. Output the final 16-bit product and ready signal.
