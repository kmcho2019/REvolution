Implement three separate always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST to update Secs, Mins, and Hours respectively. On reset, each register is cleared to zero. Secs increments every clock cycle and rolls over after 59. Mins increments when Secs roll over from 59 to 0, and rolls over after 59. Hours increments when both Secs and Mins roll over from 59 to 0, and rolls over after 23.
