Implement three separate always blocks triggered on the positive edge of CLK or RST. Each block manages one time unit (Secs, Mins, Hours) according to the specified counting and wrapping rules. On reset, all are set to zero. Seconds increment every clock cycle, wrapping from 59 to 0. Minutes increment when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and wrap from 59 to 0. Hours increment when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping from 23 to 0.
