Implement a calendar module with three always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST.
- The seconds counter increments each clock cycle and resets to 0 after 59.
- The minutes counter increments when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, resets to 0 after 59.
- The hours counter increments when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, resets after 23.
All counters reset to 0 on RST.
