Implement three always blocks each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. The first block counts seconds from 0 to 59 and rolls over. The second block increments minutes by 1 when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, rolling over minutes at 59. The third block increments hours by 1 when both minutes and seconds roll from 59 to 0, rolling over at 23. On reset, all values are set to 0. Each time block handles its own wrap-around and increment conditions.
