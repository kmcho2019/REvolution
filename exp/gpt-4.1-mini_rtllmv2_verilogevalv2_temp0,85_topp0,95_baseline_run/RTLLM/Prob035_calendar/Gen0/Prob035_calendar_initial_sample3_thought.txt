Implement the perpetual calendar with three separate always blocks, each triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. On reset, all counters set to zero. Seconds increment every clock cycle, rolling over at 59 to 0. When seconds wrap from 59 to 0, minutes increment by 1 and roll over at 59 to 0. When minutes and seconds both wrap from 59 to 0, hours increment by 1 and roll over at 23 to 0. This approach aligns with the specified logic and ensures proper cascading increments.
