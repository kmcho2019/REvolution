Implement a perpetual calendar with three always blocks each triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. The first block updates seconds, resetting to 0 on 59 or RST. The second block updates minutes only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, wrapping at 59. The third block updates hours only when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0, wrapping at 23. Outputs are 6-bit registers for Hours, Mins, and Secs.
