Implement a calendar module with three always blocks, each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. The seconds block increments seconds from 0 to 59 and resets on 59. The minutes block increments minutes when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and resets at 59 minutes when seconds are 59. The hours block increments hours when minutes and seconds are both 59 and resets at 23 hours, 59 minutes, 59 seconds. Outputs are 6-bit wide.
