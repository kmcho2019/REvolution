Implement a calendar module with three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each always block triggers on posedge CLK or posedge RST. When RST is asserted, reset respective counters to 0. Seconds count from 0 to 59, rolling over to 0 after 59. Minutes increment by 1 when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, minutes count from 0 to 59, rolling over after 59. Hours increment by 1 when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0, hours count from 0 to 23 and then wrap around. Use 6-bit registers for output ports.
