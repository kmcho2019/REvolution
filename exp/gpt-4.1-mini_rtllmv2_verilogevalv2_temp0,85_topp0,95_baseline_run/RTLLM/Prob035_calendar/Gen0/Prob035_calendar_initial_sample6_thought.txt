Implement three separate always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block manages one time unit: seconds, minutes, and hours. Reset sets each to zero. Seconds increment each clock cycle, wrapping at 59 to 0. Minutes increment when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping at 59 to 0. Hours increment when both minutes and seconds wrap, wrapping at 23 to 0.
