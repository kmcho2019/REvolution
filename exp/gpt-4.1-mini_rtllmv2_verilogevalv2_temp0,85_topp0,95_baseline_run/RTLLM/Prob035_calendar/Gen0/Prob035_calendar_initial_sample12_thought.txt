Implement three separate always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST to update seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. On reset, all are cleared to zero. Seconds increment every clock cycle, wrapping from 59 to 0. Minutes increment when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping from 59 to 0. Hours increment when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping from 23 to 0. Use 6-bit registers for each output to hold the necessary ranges.
