Implement the perpetual calendar module with three always blocks each sensitive to posedge CLK and posedge RST. 
- The seconds counter increments every clock cycle, wrapping from 59 to 0.
- The minutes counter increments by 1 when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and wraps from 59 to 0.
- The hours counter increments by 1 when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and wraps from 23 to 0.
On reset, all counters are cleared to zero.
Each counter is declared as 6-bit to hold required ranges.
