Implement the calendar module with three separate always blocks, each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. The seconds block increments seconds from 0 to 59, wrapping around to 0 after 59. The minutes block increments when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, also wrapping from 59 to 0. The hours block increments when minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping from 23 to 0. Reset sets all outputs to zero.
