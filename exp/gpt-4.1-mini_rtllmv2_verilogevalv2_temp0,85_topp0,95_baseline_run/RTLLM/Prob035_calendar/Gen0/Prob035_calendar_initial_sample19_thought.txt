Implement three always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST to update seconds, minutes, and hours independently but coordinated. Each block resets its respective time unit on RST and increments according to the rules: seconds increment every clock, minutes increment when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, and hours increment when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0. Each time unit wraps at its maximum (seconds and minutes at 59, hours at 23).
