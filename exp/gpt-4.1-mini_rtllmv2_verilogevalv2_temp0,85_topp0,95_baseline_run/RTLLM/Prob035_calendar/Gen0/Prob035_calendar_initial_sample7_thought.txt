Implement three sequential always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block resets its respective counter on RST. The seconds block counts from 0 to 59 and wraps around. The minutes block increments only when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and itself wraps from 59 to 0. The hours block increments only when minutes and seconds both wrap from 59 to 0, counting from 0 to 23 and wrapping accordingly.
