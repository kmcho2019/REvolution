Use three sequential always blocks triggered by posedge CLK or RST to separately update seconds, minutes, and hours. Reset sets all to zero. Seconds count from 0 to 59, increment every clock cycle, and wrap to 0 after 59. When seconds wrap, minutes increment, counting from 0 to 59 and wrap to 0 after 59. When minutes and seconds wrap, hours increment, counting from 0 to 23 and wrap to 0 after 23. This matches the described behavior and meets output width requirements.
