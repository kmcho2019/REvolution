Implement a calendar module with separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours as described. Each always block triggers on posedge CLK or posedge RST. On reset, respective counters are set to 0. Seconds count from 0 to 59 and wrap to 0. Minutes increment when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, and minutes count from 0 to 59 and wrap to 0. Hours increment when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0, counting from 0 to 23 and wrap to 0.
