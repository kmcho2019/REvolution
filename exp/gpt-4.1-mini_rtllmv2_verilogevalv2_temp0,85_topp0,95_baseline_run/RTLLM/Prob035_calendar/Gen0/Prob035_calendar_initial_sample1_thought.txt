Implement a perpetual calendar module with three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. On reset, all counters are set to zero. Seconds count from 0 to 59, and upon reaching 59, they reset to 0 and trigger minutes to increment. Minutes count from 0 to 59, and upon reaching 59 with seconds at 59, they reset to 0 and trigger hours to increment. Hours count from 0 to 23, and upon reaching 23 with minutes and seconds at 59, they reset to 0. The outputs are 6-bit wide to accommodate the ranges.
