Implement the 8-bit multiplier using the classic shift-and-add algorithm. Initialize the product to zero. For each bit in the multiplier B, if the bit is 1, add the multiplicand A shifted left by the bit position to the product. The shifting of A corresponds to multiplying by powers of two. Since this is a combinational design, we can use a for-loop inside an always_comb block or a continuous assignment with a generate block to accumulate the partial sums. Finally, assign the accumulated sum to the product output.
