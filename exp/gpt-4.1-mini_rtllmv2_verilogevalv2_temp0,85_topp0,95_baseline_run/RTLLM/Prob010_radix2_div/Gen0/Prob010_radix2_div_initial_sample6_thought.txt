Implement a radix-2 divider that supports both signed and unsigned 8-bit division using sequential logic. On opn_valid, inputs are latched, absolute values computed (if signed), and division starts. The division uses a shift register SR that holds the partial remainder and quotient. Each clock cycle shifts SR left by 1, subtracts the divisor, and decides quotient bit based on subtraction result. After 8 cycles, sign corrections are applied to quotient and remainder. res_valid is asserted when result is ready, cleared on reset or new operation. The final 16-bit result packs remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.
