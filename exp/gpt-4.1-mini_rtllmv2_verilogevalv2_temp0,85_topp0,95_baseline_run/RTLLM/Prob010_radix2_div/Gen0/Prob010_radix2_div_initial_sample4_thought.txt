Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider that supports signed and unsigned 8-bit division. On opn_valid, latch inputs, convert to absolute values for signed operation, initialize SR with dividend shifted left by 1, and NEG_DIVISOR as negative divisor. Use a counter to perform 8 iterations of subtract-and-shift division. Use a combinational subtractor and mux to update SR and cnt every clock cycle. After 8 iterations, finalize quotient and remainder, and adjust signs for signed division if needed. Output the remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits of result. Manage res_valid based on operation progress and reset. The design is fully synchronous with reset and all state held in registers.
