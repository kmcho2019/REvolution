Implement a radix-2 division algorithm on 8-bit operands supporting signed and unsigned operations. On opn_valid, inputs are latched, dividend and divisor are converted to absolute values if signed, and division is performed iteratively over 8 clock cycles using a shift register to hold remainder and quotient bits. A subtraction with negated divisor determines the next quotient bit. After 8 cycles, quotient and remainder are adjusted for sign if necessary and output is marked valid. The result contains remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.
