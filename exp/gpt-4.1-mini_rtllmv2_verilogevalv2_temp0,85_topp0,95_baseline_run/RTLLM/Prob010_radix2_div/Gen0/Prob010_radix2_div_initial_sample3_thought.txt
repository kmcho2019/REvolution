Implement a sequential radix-2 divider with synchronous control signals. The module first captures the input operands and sign flag when opn_valid is asserted and res_valid is low, initializing internal registers accordingly. It converts operands to absolute values when sign=1 to handle signed division, remembering the sign of the quotient. The divider then executes 8 iterations of shift-subtract-restore steps driven by a counter. Each iteration shifts the partial remainder and quotient, performs a subtraction with the divisor, and updates the remainder and quotient bits based on the subtraction result's sign. After completion, if signed division was selected, the quotient and remainder are conditionally negated based on the original signs. Finally, the module outputs a 16-bit result with the remainder in the upper 8 bits and quotient in the lower 8 bits, asserting res_valid until the next operation. The design uses a single clocked process for control and arithmetic, handles reset synchronously, and supports both signed and unsigned division as requested.
