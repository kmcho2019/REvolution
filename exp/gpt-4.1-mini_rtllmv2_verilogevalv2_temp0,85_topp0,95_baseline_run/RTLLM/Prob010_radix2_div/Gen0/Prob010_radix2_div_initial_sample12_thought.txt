Implement a radix-2 division algorithm for 8-bit inputs supporting signed and unsigned division. On opn_valid, latch inputs, convert operands to absolute values if signed, initialize registers including a shift register (SR) containing dividend shifted left by 1, and NEG_DIVISOR as negated absolute divisor. Use a counter to iterate 8 cycles, each cycle doing a subtraction of NEG_DIVISOR from the upper bits of SR. Depending on the subtraction's carry-out (borrow), update the SR accordingly by shifting in the bit. After 8 cycles, adjust quotient and remainder signs for signed division. Output the remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits of result and set res_valid high. Reset and res_valid handled properly.
