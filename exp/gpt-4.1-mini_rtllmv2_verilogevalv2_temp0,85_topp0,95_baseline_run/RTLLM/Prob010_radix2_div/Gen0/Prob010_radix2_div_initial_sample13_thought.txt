Implement a radix-2 division algorithm that operates synchronously over multiple clock cycles.
On a valid input and reset not active, capture inputs, compute absolute values for signed division,
and initialize the shift register (SR), negated divisor, and counters. Then, iteratively perform
subtraction and shift operations to find quotient and remainder bits. After 8 iterations, update SR
with the final remainder and quotient. Finally, adjust signs of quotient and remainder for signed division,
and output them concatenated as per spec. Manage res_valid accordingly.
