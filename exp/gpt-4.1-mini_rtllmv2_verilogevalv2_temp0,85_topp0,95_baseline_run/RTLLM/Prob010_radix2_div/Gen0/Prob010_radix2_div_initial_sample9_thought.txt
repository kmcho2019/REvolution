Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider that works for both signed and unsigned 8-bit integers. On opn_valid, latch inputs and start division. Convert inputs to absolute values if signed operation is requested, keeping track of the signs for final quotient and remainder corrections. Use a shift register SR with 9 bits (8 bits for dividend shifted left by 1) to perform the division bit by bit. NEG_DIVISOR holds the two's complement of the divisor's absolute value to perform subtraction. On each clock cycle during division, perform subtract or keep depending on the sign of the partial remainder. After 8 iterations, adjust signs of quotient and remainder accordingly, then output them with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. Manage res_valid signal appropriately and reset internal states on rst.
