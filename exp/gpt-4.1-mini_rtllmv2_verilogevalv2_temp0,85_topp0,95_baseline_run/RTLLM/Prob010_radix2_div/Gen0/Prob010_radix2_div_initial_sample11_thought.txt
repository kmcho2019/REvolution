Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider for 8-bit operands supporting signed and unsigned division. On opn_valid, capture inputs, convert to absolute values if signed, and initialize registers. Use a shift register (SR) to hold the dividend and quotient bits, with the dividend shifted left by 1 bit initially. Use a negated divisor to perform subtraction and decide quotient bits per cycle. A counter controls the 8 iterations of the division process. At the end, adjust the sign of quotient and remainder based on input signs if signed division. Output the remainder in the upper 8 bits and quotient in the lower 8 bits of the 16-bit result. Manage res_valid for output readiness and allow new operations when ready. Handle synchronous reset.
