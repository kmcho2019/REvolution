Implement a radix-2 division algorithm using a shift register to hold the intermediate remainder and quotient. Support both signed and unsigned division by converting inputs to absolute values and restoring the sign of the quotient and remainder at the end. On opn_valid, load operands and start division; each clock cycle shift and subtract divisor, updating quotient bits accordingly. After 8 cycles, finalize result and set res_valid. Handle reset and clear state properly.
