Implement a radix-2 division algorithm operating synchronously on each clock cycle. Use a shift register SR of 17 bits (to hold dividend shifted left by one bit plus the quotient bits) for the division process. Manage sign handling by converting inputs to absolute values, perform division on unsigned magnitudes, then adjust the signs of the quotient and remainder at the end. Use a counter cnt to count 8 cycles for 8-bit division. When opn_valid arrives, latch inputs and start division. Each cycle performs a subtraction (SR upper bits minus divisor), then shifts in quotient bit accordingly. After 8 cycles, output the remainder (upper 8 bits of SR) and quotient (lower 8 bits), apply sign correction, and set res_valid high until reset or new operation starts.
