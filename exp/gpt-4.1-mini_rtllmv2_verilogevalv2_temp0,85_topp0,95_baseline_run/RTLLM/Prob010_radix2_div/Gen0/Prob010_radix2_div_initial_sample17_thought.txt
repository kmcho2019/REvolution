Implement a radix-2 division algorithm using a shift register and iterative subtraction for 8-bit operands. Handle signed division by converting operands to their absolute values and tracking the sign of the result. On a valid operation, latch inputs, initialize the shift register with dividend shifted left by 1, and NEG_DIVISOR as the negated divisor. Iterate 8 times: shift the shift register left by 1 bit and conditionally subtract divisor if possible, updating the quotient bits accordingly. After completion, adjust signs of quotient and remainder if signed operation, output the result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits, and signal res_valid. Reset and control signals manage operation start and completion.
