Implement a radix-2 divider that supports signed and unsigned 8-bit division. The module runs synchronously with a clock and reset. When opn_valid is asserted and res_valid is low, the division starts by capturing inputs, preparing absolute values, and initializing internal registers. The division is performed iteratively over 8 clock cycles using shift and subtract operations with a shift register holding the remainder and quotient. A counter tracks the number of iterations. Signed operands are converted to absolute values, division is done unsigned, then the final quotient and remainder signs are corrected. The 16-bit output packs remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. res_valid flags when the result is ready.
