Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed and unsigned inputs. On opn_valid, capture inputs, convert to absolute values if signed, and initialize shift registers and counters. Perform iterative subtraction and shift operations each clock cycle, counting up to 8. After completion, restore sign of quotient and remainder if signed division was performed. Manage res_valid to indicate result readiness. The 16-bit output packs remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.
