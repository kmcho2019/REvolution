Implement a radix-2 division module with synchronous operation on clk and rst. On opn_valid, capture inputs and prepare absolute values for signed division, track signs to adjust quotient and remainder signs at the end. Use a 17-bit shift register SR to hold the remainder and quotient bits during the division loop. NEG_DIVISOR holds the negated divisor's absolute value for subtraction. The division loop runs for 8 cycles, shifting and subtracting to build quotient bits. After completion, adjust signs of quotient and remainder according to the original signs if signed operation is selected. Provide res_valid signal to indicate valid output. The 16-bit result concatenates remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).
