Implement a radix-2 division algorithm that works for both signed and unsigned 8-bit integers. On opn_valid, capture and process the inputs: convert them to their absolute values if signed, keep track of the sign of the quotient for final adjustment. Use a shift register (SR) initialized with the absolute dividend shifted left by one bit. Use a negated divisor for the subtraction step. Perform division over 8 cycles using a counter, updating SR based on subtraction results and shifting in the quotient bits. After completion, adjust the quotient sign and remainder sign if the operation is signed. Output the 16-bit result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. Manage res_valid signal accordingly.
