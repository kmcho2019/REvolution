Implement a radix-2 division algorithm on 8-bit signed/unsigned operands. Use a shift register SR initialized with the absolute dividend shifted left by 1. Negate the absolute divisor and perform subtraction steps guided by a counter cnt. Handle signed inputs by taking absolute values and adjusting signs of quotient and remainder at the end. Manage result validity and synchronization with clk and rst. On opn_valid, latch inputs and start the division process. Update SR and cnt each clock cycle until division completes, then output combined remainder and quotient in a 16-bit result.
