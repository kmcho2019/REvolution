Implement a parallel-to-serial converter that loads a 4-bit input every 4 cycles and shifts out one bit per clock from MSB to LSB. Use a 2-bit counter to track which bit to output. When the counter reaches 3 (last bit), load new data and reset counter. valid_out is high only when outputting the MSB (first bit) of the loaded data; for other bits, valid_out is low. The data shift is done by left-shifting the register, bringing MSB out on dout. On reset (active low), clear counter and data.
