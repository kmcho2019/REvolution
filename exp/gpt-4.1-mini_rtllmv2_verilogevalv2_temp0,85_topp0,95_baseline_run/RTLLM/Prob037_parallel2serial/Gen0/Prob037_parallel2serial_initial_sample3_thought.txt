Implement a module with a 2-bit counter and a 4-bit shift register. On reset (active low), clear counter and data. When counter reaches 3, load the parallel input d into data, reset counter to 0, and assert valid_out. On other cycles, shift data left by one bit (MSB to LSB rotation), increment counter, and deassert valid_out. Output dout is always the MSB of data. This achieves MSB to LSB serial output with valid_out indicating the first valid bit.
