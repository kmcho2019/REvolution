Implement a synchronous parallel-to-serial converter that takes 4-bit input and outputs bits serially from MSB to LSB. Use a 2-bit counter to track the bit position. On reset or after outputting the last bit (cnt=3), load new data and assert valid_out=1. On other cycles, shift data left by one bit and output the new MSB, increment counter, and set valid_out=0. The counter increments only when rst_n=1 (active low reset).
