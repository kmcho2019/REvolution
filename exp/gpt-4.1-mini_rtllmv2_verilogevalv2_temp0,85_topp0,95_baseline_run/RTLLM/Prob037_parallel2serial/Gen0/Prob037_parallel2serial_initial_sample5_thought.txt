Use a 4-bit register to hold the parallel data, outputting its MSB first. A 2-bit counter tracks the bit position being output. On reset, initialize the counter and load data. At each clock cycle, output the MSB of the data register on dout, assert valid_out when the first bit (cnt=0) is output, then shift data left circularly and increment counter. When cnt reaches 3 (last bit), load new input data and reset counter.
