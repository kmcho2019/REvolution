Implement a sequential shift-and-accumulate unsigned 16-bit multiplier using registers to hold multiplicand, multiplier, partial product, and a shift count. On reset, clear all registers. On start, load multiplicand and multiplier into registers, then for each bit of the multiplicand, accumulate the shifted multiplier into the product if the bit is set. Maintain a done flag that signals when multiplication is complete. Shift count goes from 0 to 16 to cover all bits. Product output is registered and updated accordingly.
