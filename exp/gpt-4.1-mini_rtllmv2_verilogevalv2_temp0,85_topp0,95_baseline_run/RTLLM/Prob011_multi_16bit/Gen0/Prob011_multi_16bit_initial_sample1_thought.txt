Implement a sequential unsigned 16-bit multiplier using shift and accumulate method. Use a counter 'i' for controlling shifts, and registers to hold the multiplicand (areg), multiplier (breg), and product (yout_r). On reset, clear all registers. On start, load inputs into registers. For each clock cycle when i is between 1 and 16, check the bit at position i-1 of the multiplicand, and if set, accumulate the multiplier shifted by i-1 into the product register. A done flag is set when i reaches 16, indicating multiplication complete.
