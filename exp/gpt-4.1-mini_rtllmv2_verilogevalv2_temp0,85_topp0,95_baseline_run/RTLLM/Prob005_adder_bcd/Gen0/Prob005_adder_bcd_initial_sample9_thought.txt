Perform binary addition of the two 4-bit BCD inputs and the carry-in.
Check if the binary sum exceeds 9 or if there is a carry out of the binary addition.
If so, add 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to correct it to a valid BCD digit and set the carry-out.
Otherwise, the sum is valid BCD and carry-out is zero.
This ensures the output sum is always a valid BCD digit and carry-out signals overflow.
