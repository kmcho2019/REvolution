To implement an odd frequency divider, we use two counters: one tracking positive edges (cnt1) and another tracking negative edges (cnt2). Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1. When a counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock output (clk_div1 or clk_div2) toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of these two divided clocks, which effectively produces a divided clock with a duty cycle close to 50% for odd division factors. The active low reset initializes counters and outputs.
