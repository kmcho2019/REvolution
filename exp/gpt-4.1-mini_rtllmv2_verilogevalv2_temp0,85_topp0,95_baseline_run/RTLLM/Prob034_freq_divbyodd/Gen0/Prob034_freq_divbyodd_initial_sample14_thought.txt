To implement an odd frequency divider, we use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 that count separately on the rising and falling edges of the input clock. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1, toggling intermediate clock signals clk_div1 and clk_div2 when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. These two intermediate clocks are then OR-ed to form the final divided clock output, ensuring a correct odd division with both edges accounted for. The active low reset initializes all counters and outputs.
