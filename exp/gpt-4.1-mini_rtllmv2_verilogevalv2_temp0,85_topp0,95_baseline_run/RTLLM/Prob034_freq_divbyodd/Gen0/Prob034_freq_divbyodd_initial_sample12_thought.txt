To divide the clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, for the positive and negative edges of the input clock, respectively. Each counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When the counters reach half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock output toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 to produce a proper output clock with a frequency divided by the odd number NUM_DIV. The reset signal asynchronously initializes counters and outputs.
