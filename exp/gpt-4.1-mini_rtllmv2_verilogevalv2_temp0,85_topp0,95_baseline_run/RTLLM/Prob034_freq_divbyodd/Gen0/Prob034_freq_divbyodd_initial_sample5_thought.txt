To divide the clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) that count clock edges separately for rising and falling edges. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV - 1 and toggles clk_div1 and clk_div2 respectively when the count reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, which is the midpoint. The outputs clk_div1 and clk_div2 are then OR-ed to produce the final output clock clk_div, providing a symmetrical divided clock with the correct odd division factor. The reset initializes counters and outputs.
