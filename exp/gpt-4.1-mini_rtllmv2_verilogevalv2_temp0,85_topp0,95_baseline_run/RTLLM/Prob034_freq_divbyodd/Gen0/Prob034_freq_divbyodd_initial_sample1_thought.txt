To divide the input clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters: one triggered on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of the input clock. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock (clk_div1 or clk_div2) toggles. The final output clock clk_div is generated by OR-ing these two divided clocks, effectively producing an output clock with frequency input clock divided by NUM_DIV and a 50% duty cycle approximation. The reset signal rst_n asynchronously initializes counters and outputs to zero.
