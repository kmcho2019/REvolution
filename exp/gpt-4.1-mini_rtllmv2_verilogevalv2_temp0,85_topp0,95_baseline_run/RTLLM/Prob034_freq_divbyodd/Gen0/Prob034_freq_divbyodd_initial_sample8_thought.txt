To implement an odd frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters and two intermediate divided clocks, clk_div1 and clk_div2. The counters count from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on rising and falling edges respectively, toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 at halfway points (NUM_DIV/2 rounded down). The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, ensuring a 50% duty cycle or close to it for odd division. Reset initializes counters and outputs.
