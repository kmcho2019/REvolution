Use two counters to track the odd division on both rising and falling edges of the clock. cnt1 increments on the rising edge and toggles clk_div1 at half the NUM_DIV count. cnt2 increments on the falling edge and toggles clk_div2 similarly. The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, producing a clock divided by the odd NUM_DIV parameter. Reset logic initializes counters and outputs asynchronously with active low reset rst_n.
