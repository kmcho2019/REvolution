To divide the input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV, the design uses two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, running on the positive and negative edges of clk respectively. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and toggles clk_div1 or clk_div2 when the counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2, which represents half the division period rounded down. The divided clocks from positive and negative edges are combined with an OR gate to generate clk_div. Reset initializes counters and outputs. This approach ensures a 50% duty cycle output clock for odd division factors.
