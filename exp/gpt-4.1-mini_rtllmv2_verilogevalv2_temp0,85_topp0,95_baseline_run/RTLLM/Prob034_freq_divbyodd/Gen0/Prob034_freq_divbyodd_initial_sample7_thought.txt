To create a frequency divider for odd division factors, we use two counters that count the clock edges separately: one increments on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of the input clock. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When each counter reaches the midpoint (NUM_DIV >> 1), the respective divided clock (clk_div1 or clk_div2) toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of these two signals, ensuring the output frequency is the input frequency divided by the odd NUM_DIV value. The active low reset synchronously resets all counters and outputs.
