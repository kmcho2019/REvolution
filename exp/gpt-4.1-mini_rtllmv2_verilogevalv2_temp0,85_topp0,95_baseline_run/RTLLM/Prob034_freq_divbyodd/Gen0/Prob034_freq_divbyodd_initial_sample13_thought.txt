Use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 incremented on posedge and negedge of clk respectively, each counting up to NUM_DIV-1. When the count reaches half of NUM_DIV, toggle clk_div1 and clk_div2 respectively. The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, producing a clock with frequency divided by the odd NUM_DIV parameter. Reset asynchronously with active low rst_n.
