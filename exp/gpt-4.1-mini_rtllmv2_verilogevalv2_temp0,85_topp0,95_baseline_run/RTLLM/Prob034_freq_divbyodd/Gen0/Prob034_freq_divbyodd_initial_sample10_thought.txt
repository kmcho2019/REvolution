To divide the input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 triggered on the positive and negative edges of clk respectively. Each counter counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1, toggling their respective clk_div signals at half of NUM_DIV counts. By OR-ing the two divided clocks clk_div1 and clk_div2, we generate a clock output with frequency clk/(NUM_DIV). The active low reset rst_n initializes counters and divided clocks.
