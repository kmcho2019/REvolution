To divide the input clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 that count up to NUM_DIV-1 on rising and falling edges respectively. When each counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding clk_div1 or clk_div2 toggles. Finally, clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, generating a clock with the desired odd division. Reset initializes counters and outputs.
