To create a frequency divider by an odd number, we count clock cycles on both rising and falling edges separately using two counters. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When a counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), we toggle its corresponding divided clock output (clk_div1 for rising edge, clk_div2 for falling edge). The final output clock clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively creating a clock with the desired odd division. The asynchronous active low reset initializes counters and outputs.
