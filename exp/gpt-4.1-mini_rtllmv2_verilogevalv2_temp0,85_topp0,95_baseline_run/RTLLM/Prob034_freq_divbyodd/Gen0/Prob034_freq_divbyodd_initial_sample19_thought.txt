To divide the input clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) that count clock cycles and toggle two separate clock dividers (clk_div1 and clk_div2) on positive and negative edges respectively. Each toggling occurs when the counters reach (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, which is the half count for the odd divisor. The final output clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, combining the edges for a 50% duty cycle approximate output. On active low reset, all registers are reset to zero.
