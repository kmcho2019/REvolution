The design uses two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, each counting up to NUM_DIV-1. 
On the rising edge of the input clock, cnt1 increments and toggles clk_div1 at half of NUM_DIV. 
On the falling edge of the input clock, cnt2 increments and toggles clk_div2 at half of NUM_DIV. 
The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively producing a clock divided by an odd number.
The asynchronous active low reset initializes all counters and outputs.
