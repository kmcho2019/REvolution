To implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV, the idea is to use two counters: one triggered on the positive clock edge and the other on the negative edge. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1. We toggle clk_div1 and clk_div2 when their respective counters reach half of NUM_DIV (integer division). This creates two complementary signals that, when OR-ed, produce a clock with a frequency divided by the odd number. The active low reset initializes all counters and outputs.
