Use two counters that count clock edges independently for the rising and falling edges. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When each counter reaches half of NUM_DIV, toggle the corresponding divided clock half (clk_div1 or clk_div2). Finally, OR both clk_div1 and clk_div2 signals to generate the output clk_div, which effectively divides the input clock frequency by the odd number NUM_DIV. The reset signal initializes all counters and output signals.
