To create a frequency divider that divides by an odd number, we'll use two counters to track the rising and falling edges separately. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. When a counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), we toggle the corresponding clk_div output (clk_div1 for rising edge, clk_div2 for falling edge). By OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, we combine their toggling to produce the final divided clock output, which has the correct frequency for an odd divisor. The asynchronous active low reset initializes counters and outputs.
