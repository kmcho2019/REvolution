To divide the input clock frequency by an odd number NUM_DIV, we need to generate a clock signal toggling every NUM_DIV cycles. Since NUM_DIV is odd, we cannot simply toggle the clock at half of NUM_DIV cycles (which would be a fractional number). Instead, we use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, each counting up to NUM_DIV-1, and two internal clock signals, clk_div1 and clk_div2, toggled on rising and falling edges respectively. Both counters count independently, and when each reaches half the NUM_DIV count (integer division), their corresponding clk_div signal toggles. The output clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 to combine both edges and achieve the desired divide-by-odd behavior with a 50% duty cycle approximation. Active low reset initializes all counters and clock signals.
