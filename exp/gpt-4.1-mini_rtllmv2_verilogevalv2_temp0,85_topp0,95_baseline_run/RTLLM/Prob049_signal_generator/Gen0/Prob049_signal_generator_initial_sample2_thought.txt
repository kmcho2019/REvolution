Implement a synchronous process that updates the 'state' and 'wave' registers on the rising edge of the clock or the falling edge of the reset signal. On reset, both registers are cleared to zero. When not in reset, use a state variable to determine whether to increment or decrement the 5-bit wave signal. The state toggles between 0 (incrementing) and 1 (decrementing) when 'wave' reaches the boundary values 31 or 0, respectively. This ensures the generation of a triangle waveform cycling between 0 and 31.
