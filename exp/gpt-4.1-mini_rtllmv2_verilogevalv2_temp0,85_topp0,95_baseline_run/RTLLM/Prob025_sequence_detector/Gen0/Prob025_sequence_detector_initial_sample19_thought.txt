Implement a Moore FSM to detect the sequence 1001 on the serial input data_in. The FSM has five states: IDLE (waiting for '1'), S1 (detected '1'), S2 (detected '10'), S3 (detected '100'), and S4 (detected '1001'). On each clock cycle, transition based on the input bit. When S4 is reached, output sequence_detected is asserted high for one clock cycle. The FSM resets to IDLE when reset_n is low.
