Implement a Moore FSM with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The FSM transitions through states by matching the input bits of the sequence 1001. On each clock cycle, depending on the input bit, the FSM moves to the appropriate next state. When the final bit '1' of the sequence is detected in S3, the FSM transitions to S4, and the output sequence_detected is asserted high for one clock cycle. The FSM then transitions according to overlapping sequence detection to allow detection of sequences in a stream. The reset_n signal is active low and resets the FSM to IDLE state when asserted low.
