Implement a synchronous FSM with states IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4 to detect the 4-bit sequence 1001 on the input stream data_in. On each clock cycle, transition through states according to the next expected bit. When the sequence is detected (reaching S4), assert sequence_detected for one clock cycle. The FSM resets to IDLE when reset_n is low. The design supports overlapping sequences by carefully managing state transitions.
