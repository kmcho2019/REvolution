Implement a Moore state machine with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4 to detect the 4-bit sequence 1001 serially from data_in. The FSM transitions states on the rising edge of clk and asynchronously resets to IDLE on reset_n being low. When the last bit of the sequence is detected (S4), assert sequence_detected for one clock cycle. The sequence detector should support overlapping sequences by properly transitioning states after a partial match.
