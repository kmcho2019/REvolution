Implement a traffic light controller with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green.
The state machine transitions as follows:
- idle: initializes all signals to 0 and immediately transitions to s1_red.
- s1_red: red light on, lasts 10 clocks.
- s3_green: green light on, lasts 60 clocks normally but can be shortened to 10 when pass_request is pressed and remaining green time is > 10.
- s2_yellow: yellow light on, lasts 5 clocks.

The counter counts down every clock cycle, and the state transitions when the counter reaches zero.

The pedestrian button (pass_request) can shorten the green phase if pressed and there are more than 10 clocks left.

State transitions and signal outputs are handled in separate always blocks for clarity.
Previous light signals are stored in p_red, p_yellow, and p_green for state transition detection and control of counter resets.

Output clock reflects the current value of cnt.
