The design implements a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The traffic light cycles through these states with timing controlled by an 8-bit counter. The pedestrian button (pass_request) can shorten the green light period to 10 clock cycles if the remaining green time is greater than 10. Transitions and timing are handled in separate always blocks. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are updated synchronously. The counter reload values correspond to the timing requirements: green = 60, yellow = 5, red = 10. The logic also tracks previous output signals to handle counter reloading correctly on transitions.
