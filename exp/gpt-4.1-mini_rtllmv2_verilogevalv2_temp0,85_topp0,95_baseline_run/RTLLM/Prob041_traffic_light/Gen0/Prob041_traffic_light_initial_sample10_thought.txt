Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM transitions through these states to drive the traffic light signals. The internal counter cnt controls the timing for each state: red for 10 clocks, yellow for 5 clocks, green for 60 clocks. When a pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during green, and remaining green time is greater than 10, shorten green to 10 clocks. The state transition logic is implemented in one always block, the counter logic in another, and the output assignments in a final always block. The outputs red, yellow, green reflect the current state of the lights, and the counter value is output as clock.
