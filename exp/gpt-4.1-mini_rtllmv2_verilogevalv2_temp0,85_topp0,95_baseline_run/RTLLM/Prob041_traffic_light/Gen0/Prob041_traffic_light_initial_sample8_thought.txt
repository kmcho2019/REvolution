Implement a traffic light controller with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The counter counts down from preset values depending on the state: red=10, yellow=5, green=60 normally. When the pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during green, if remaining green time > 10, shorten it to 10. Use a state machine for transitions and a counter for timing. Output signals are assigned from the previous state registers. The counter updates on clock edge or reset, and state transitions occur based on counter expiration.
