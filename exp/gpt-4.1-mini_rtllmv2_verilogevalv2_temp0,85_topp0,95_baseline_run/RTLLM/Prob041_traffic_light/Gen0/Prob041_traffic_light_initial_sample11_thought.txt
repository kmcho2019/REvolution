Implement a finite state machine with states idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green representing red, yellow, and green lights, respectively. Use an 8-bit counter to time each state's duration as specified (red=10, yellow=5, green=60). On a pedestrian pass_request during green, if more than 10 counts remain, shorten green to 10. Use previous light state registers to detect transitions and reset counters accordingly. Output the current counter and traffic light signals. Reset initializes the system and counter.
