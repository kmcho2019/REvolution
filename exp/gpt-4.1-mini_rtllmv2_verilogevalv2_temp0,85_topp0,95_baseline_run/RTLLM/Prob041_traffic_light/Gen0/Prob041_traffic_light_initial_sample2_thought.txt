Implement a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. Use a counter to time each state with different durations: red=10, yellow=5, green=60 clocks. When the pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during green, if the remaining green time is more than 10, shorten it to 10 clocks. Transition states according to count expiration. Save previous output states for control. Outputs reflect current state's light signals.
