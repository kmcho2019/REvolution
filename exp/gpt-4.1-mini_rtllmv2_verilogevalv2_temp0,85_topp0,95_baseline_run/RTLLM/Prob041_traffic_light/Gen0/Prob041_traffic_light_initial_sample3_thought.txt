Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states (idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green). In idle, initialize signals and immediately go to s1_red. Each state sets its corresponding light signal and waits for the appropriate count duration. The count is controlled by an 8-bit counter (cnt), which is loaded according to the timing requirements on entering each state. The pedestrian pass_request can shorten the green time to 10 cycles if more than 10 cycles remain. The counter decrements every clock cycle. Outputs red, yellow, and green are updated synchronously each clock cycle. The clock output reflects the current counter value. The FSM transitions when cnt reaches zero. This design follows the recommended structure and conditions.
