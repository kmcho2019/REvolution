Implement a traffic light controller with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The counter counts down the timing for each state. On reset, initialize counter to 10 (red time). The counter transitions between states based on reaching zero. The pedestrian pass_request can shorten the green time to 10 cycles if remaining green time > 10. Outputs are updated synchronously with the clock. The previous outputs are stored to detect edges for counter reload conditions.
