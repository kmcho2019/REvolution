Implement a finite state machine with states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. 
Use a counter (cnt) to time the duration of each light: red=10, yellow=5, green=60 by default.
On pedestrian button press (pass_request) during green, if remaining green time >10, shorten it to 10.
Track current and previous light signals to control counter reload.
Output signals follow the state and previous values to ensure stable output.
