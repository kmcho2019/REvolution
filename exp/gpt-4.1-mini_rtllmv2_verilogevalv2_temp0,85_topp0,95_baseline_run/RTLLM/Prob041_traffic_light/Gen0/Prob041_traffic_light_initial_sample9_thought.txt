Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The FSM controls three lights: red, yellow, and green, and an 8-bit counter counts down in each state according to specified durations (red 10, yellow 5, green 60). When the pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during green, and remaining green time is greater than 10, the green time is shortened to 10. The counter resets appropriately on state transitions. Outputs are registered and updated on clock edges. The code follows the recommended design track with appropriate handling of the pedestrian button and transitions.
