Implement a traffic light controller with three states: red, yellow, and green, plus an idle state. The counter counts down the time for each state: red for 10 cycles, yellow for 5 cycles, green for up to 60 cycles. When the pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during green and remaining green time is more than 10, shorten green time to 10. Use registers to hold current and previous light signals to detect state changes and properly update the counter. The module outputs the light signals and current count.
