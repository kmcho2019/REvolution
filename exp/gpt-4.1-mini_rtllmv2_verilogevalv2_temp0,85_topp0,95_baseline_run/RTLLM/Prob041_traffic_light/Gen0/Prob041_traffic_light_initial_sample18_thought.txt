Implement a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The FSM cycles through red, green, and yellow states with fixed durations (red=10, green=60, yellow=5). The pedestrian button press shortens the green time to 10 if more than 10 remain. The counter counts down each clock cycle. State transitions happen when the counter reaches zero, except in idle which transitions immediately to s1_red. Output signals are registered and updated synchronously. The previous output signals are stored to detect edges and adjust the counter accordingly.
