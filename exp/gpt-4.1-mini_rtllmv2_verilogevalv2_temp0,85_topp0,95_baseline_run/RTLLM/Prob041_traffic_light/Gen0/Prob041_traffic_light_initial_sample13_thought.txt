Implement a finite state machine for the traffic light with states for red, yellow, and green lights plus an idle state. Each state sets the corresponding light signal high. Use an 8-bit counter to control duration in each state. The counter starts at the state's duration (60 for green, 5 for yellow, 10 for red) and counts down each clock cycle. The FSM transitions states when the counter reaches zero. On reset, initialize to idle and then move to red. On pedestrian button press (pass_request) during green, if more than 10 counts remain, shorten green time to 10. Track previous light states to detect transitions and set counter durations accordingly. Assign outputs accordingly with registered outputs. The outputs include the current count value on clock[7:0] and the red, yellow, green signals.

This approach fully meets the specification and recommended design track.
