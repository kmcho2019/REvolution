Implement a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. On reset, start in idle and then immediately move to s1_red. In s1_red, light is red for 10 cycles, in s2_yellow yellow for 5 cycles, in s3_green green for 60 cycles. Use an 8-bit counter to count down these intervals. If a pedestrian button press (pass_request) happens during green and remaining green time > 10, shorten green time to 10. Also handle transitions between states when the counter expires. Use registers p_red, p_yellow, p_green as next-state light values and assign them to outputs. The module outputs the current count and the three light signals.
