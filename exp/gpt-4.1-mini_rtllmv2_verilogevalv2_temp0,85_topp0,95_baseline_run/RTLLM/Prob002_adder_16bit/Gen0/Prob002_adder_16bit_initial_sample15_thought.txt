Design a 16-bit full adder using two instances of an 8-bit adder. The 8-bit adder will be implemented as a separate module using combinational logic with carry propagation. The first 8-bit adder adds the lower 8 bits of inputs A and B along with the carry-in. The second 8-bit adder adds the upper 8 bits along with the carry-out from the first adder. The outputs of both 8-bit adders combined form the 16-bit sum and the final carry-out.
