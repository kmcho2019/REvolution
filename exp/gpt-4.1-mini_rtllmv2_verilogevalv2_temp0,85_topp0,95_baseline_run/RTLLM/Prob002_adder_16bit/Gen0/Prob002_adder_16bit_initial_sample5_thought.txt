To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first create an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, in the 16-bit adder module, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice: once for the lower 8 bits and once for the upper 8 bits, chaining the carry-out of the first to the carry-in of the second. This hierarchical approach makes the design modular and clean.
