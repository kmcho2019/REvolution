Design an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module: once for the lower 8 bits and once for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower adder is fed as carry-in to the upper adder, and the final carry-out is from the upper adder.
