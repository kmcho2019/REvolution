The 16-bit adder can be built by instantiating two 8-bit adders in series. The first 8-bit adder adds the lower 8 bits of inputs A and B along with the carry-in, producing the lower 8 bits of the sum and an intermediate carry-out. The second 8-bit adder adds the upper 8 bits of inputs A and B along with the intermediate carry-out to produce the upper 8 bits of the sum and the final carry-out. This modular approach promotes code reuse and simplifies the design.
