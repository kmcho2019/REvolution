To implement a 16-bit full adder combinationally, we will create an 8-bit adder module first. The 8-bit adder will take two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, the 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit adders: the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits. The carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder will feed into the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This approach modularizes the design and meets the requirement.
