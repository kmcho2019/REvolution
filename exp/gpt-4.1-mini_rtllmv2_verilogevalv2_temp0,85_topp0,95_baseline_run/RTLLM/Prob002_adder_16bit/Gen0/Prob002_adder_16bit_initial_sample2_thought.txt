Design a small 8-bit adder module first that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder module twice in the 16-bit adder module: the first instance adds the lower 8 bits with the initial carry-in, and the second instance adds the upper 8 bits with the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder. This approach uses modular design and hierarchical instantiation.
