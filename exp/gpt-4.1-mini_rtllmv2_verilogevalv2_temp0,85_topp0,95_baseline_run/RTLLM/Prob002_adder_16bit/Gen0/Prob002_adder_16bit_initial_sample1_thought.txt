To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first design an 8-bit adder module with inputs a, b, and carry-in, and outputs sum and carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module: the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This modular approach simplifies design and promotes reuse.
