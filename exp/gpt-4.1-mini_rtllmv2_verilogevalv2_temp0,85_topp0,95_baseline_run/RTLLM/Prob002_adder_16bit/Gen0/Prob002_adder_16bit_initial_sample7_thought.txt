To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first create an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice in the top-level 16-bit adder module. The first 8-bit adder will handle the lower 8 bits, and its carry-out will be connected as the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder which handles the upper 8 bits. The carry-out of the second 8-bit adder will become the carry-out of the 16-bit adder. This hierarchical approach reuses the 8-bit adder to build the 16-bit adder.
