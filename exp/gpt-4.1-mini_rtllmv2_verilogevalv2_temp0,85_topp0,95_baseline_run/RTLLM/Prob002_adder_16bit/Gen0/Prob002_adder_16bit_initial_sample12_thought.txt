To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first design an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, in the top-level 16-bit adder module (adder_16bit), I will instantiate two 8-bit adders: one to handle the lower 8 bits and one to handle the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will feed into the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This hierarchical design promotes modularity and code reuse.
