Design a 16-bit adder using two instances of an 8-bit adder module. The 8-bit adder module takes two 8-bit operands and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. The 16-bit adder splits the 16-bit inputs into two 8-bit halves, adds the lower halves with the input carry, then adds the upper halves with the carry-out from the lower half addition. This approach achieves modular design and reuse.
