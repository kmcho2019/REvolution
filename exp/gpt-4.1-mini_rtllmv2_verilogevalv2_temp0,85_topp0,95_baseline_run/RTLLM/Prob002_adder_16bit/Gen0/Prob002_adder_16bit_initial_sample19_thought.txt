To implement a 16-bit adder using combinational logic, I will design a smaller 8-bit adder module first. This 8-bit adder will take two 8-bit operands and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, I'll instantiate this 8-bit adder twice in the top-level 16-bit adder module. The lower 8 bits of the operands will be added by the first 8-bit adder, and its carry-out will be connected as the carry-in to the second 8-bit adder that adds the upper 8 bits. The final carry-out from the second 8-bit adder will be the carry-out of the 16-bit adder. This modular approach makes the design scalable and clean.
