To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will create a smaller 8-bit adder module first. This 8-bit adder will take two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, the 16-bit adder module will instantiate two 8-bit adders: one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected as the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder. The final carry-out from the upper 8-bit adder will be the 16-bit adder's carry-out. This hierarchical approach promotes modularity and reusability.
