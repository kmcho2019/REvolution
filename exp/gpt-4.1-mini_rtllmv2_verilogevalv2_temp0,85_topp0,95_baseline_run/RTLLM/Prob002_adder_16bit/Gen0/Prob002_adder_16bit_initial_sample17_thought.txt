To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first design an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs along with a carry-in and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, the top-level 16-bit adder module will instantiate two 8-bit adders: the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits. The carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder will be connected to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder, ensuring proper carry propagation across the 16 bits.
