The 16-bit adder can be constructed by instantiating two 8-bit adders in a ripple-carry fashion. Each 8-bit adder takes two 8-bit operands and a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. The first 8-bit adder processes the lower 8 bits, and its carry-out feeds into the second 8-bit adder, which processes the upper 8 bits. This modular approach reuses the 8-bit adder to build the 16-bit adder.
