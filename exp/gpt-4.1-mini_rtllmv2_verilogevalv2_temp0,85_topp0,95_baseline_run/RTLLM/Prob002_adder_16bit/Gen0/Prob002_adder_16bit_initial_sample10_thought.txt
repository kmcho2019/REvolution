To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first create an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit operands and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module: one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will connect to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. The final carry-out from the upper 8-bit adder will be the 16-bit adder's carry-out.
