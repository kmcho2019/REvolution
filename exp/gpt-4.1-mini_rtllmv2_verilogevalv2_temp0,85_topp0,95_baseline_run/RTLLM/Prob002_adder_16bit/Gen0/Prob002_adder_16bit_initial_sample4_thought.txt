To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will first create an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, the 16-bit adder module will instantiate two 8-bit adders in sequence: the first adder will handle the lower 8 bits with the given carry-in, and the second will handle the upper 8 bits with the carry-out from the first adder as its carry-in. This modular approach improves readability and reusability.
