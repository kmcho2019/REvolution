Design a reusable 8-bit adder module with inputs a, b, and carry-in and outputs sum and carry-out. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice within the 16-bit adder module: the first instance will add the lower 8 bits and the carry-in, producing a carry-out; the second instance will add the upper 8 bits along with the carry-out from the first instance. The final outputs will be the concatenation of the two 8-bit sums and the final carry-out.
