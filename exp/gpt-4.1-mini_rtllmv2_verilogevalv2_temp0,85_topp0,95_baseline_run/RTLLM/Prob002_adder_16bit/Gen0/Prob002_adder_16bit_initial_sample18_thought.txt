To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will design an 8-bit adder module first that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module to handle the lower and upper 8 bits separately. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This hierarchical design simplifies the implementation and adheres to the requirement.
