Implement a 16-bit divider where the dividend is 16-bit and the divisor is 8-bit. 
The division is done combinationally using two always blocks:
- The first always block registers inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg.
- The second always block performs the division logic by extracting the highest bits from the dividend, comparing with the divisor, setting the quotient bit, and updating the remainder.
The division is done bit by bit, shifting the remainder and processing each bit of the dividend.
The quotient (result) and remainder (odd) are output.
