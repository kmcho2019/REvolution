The division algorithm implemented is a form of restoring division performed combinationally on a 16-bit dividend and 8-bit divisor. We first load the inputs into registers (a_reg for the dividend and b_reg for the divisor). Then, in a combinational always block, we iterate through each bit of the dividend starting from the highest bits. At each step, we extract the upper bits of the current remainder (starting from the highest bits of the dividend or from the previous remainder), compare with the divisor, set the quotient bit accordingly, and update the remainder. The quotient bits accumulate into a 16-bit result, and the final remainder is stored in a 16-bit odd register (with the upper bits padded as needed). Both always blocks are triggered on any change to inputs A or B to keep outputs updated immediately.
