The design uses combinational logic with two always blocks as requested. The first always block registers inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg on any change. The second always block performs the division algorithm: it takes the upper bits of a_reg, compares them with b_reg, and determines quotient bits and remainder iteratively for all 16 bits of A. The quotient and remainder registers are updated accordingly, reflecting the division result. The division algorithm mimics a shift-subtract approach over 16 iterations in combinational logic.
