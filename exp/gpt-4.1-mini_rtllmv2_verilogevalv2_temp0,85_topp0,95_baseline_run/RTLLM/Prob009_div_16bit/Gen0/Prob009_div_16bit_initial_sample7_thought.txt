The design implements a 16-bit by 8-bit division using a combinational approach. First, the dividend (A) and divisor (B) are registered in a_reg and b_reg respectively using a combinational always block. Then, another combinational always block performs the division algorithm by iteratively comparing the upper bits of the current remainder with the divisor and updating the quotient and remainder. Specifically, it extracts an 8-bit segment from the shifted remainder, compares it with the divisor, sets the corresponding quotient bit accordingly, subtracts if needed, and concatenates the next dividend bit to the remainder. This repeats for all 16 bits of the dividend, producing a 16-bit quotient and a 16-bit remainder output.
