Implement a combinational 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor using shift-subtract method. Use two always blocks as per specification: the first to latch inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg; the second to perform the division logic combinationally on a_reg and b_reg. The division extracts the top 8 bits of the current remainder, compares with divisor, sets quotient bit accordingly, updates remainder, and proceeds bit by bit from the highest dividend bit down to the lowest.
