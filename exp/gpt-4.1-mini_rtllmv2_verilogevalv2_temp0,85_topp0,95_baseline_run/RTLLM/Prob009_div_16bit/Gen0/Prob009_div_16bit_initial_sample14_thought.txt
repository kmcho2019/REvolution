The approach is to implement a 16-bit by 8-bit combinational divider using a non-restoring style, processing bits from the highest to the lowest. We first load A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg. Then, in a combinational always block, we perform bitwise division by shifting out bits from the dividend and comparing the intermediate remainder with the divisor. At each step, we set the quotient bit depending on whether the remainder is greater or equal to the divisor, subtract if so, then bring down the next bit. Finally, we output the quotient and remainder. Both always blocks are combinational and triggered by A or B changes.
