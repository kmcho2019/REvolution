Implement a 16-bit divider where the divisor is 8-bit, using combinational logic with two always blocks. The first always block captures the inputs into registers. The second always block performs the division iteratively, extracting the top bits from the remainder, comparing with the divisor, updating quotient bits and the remainder accordingly. This simulates a non-restoring division approach where the remainder and quotient are formed bit by bit in a loop over the dividend bits.
