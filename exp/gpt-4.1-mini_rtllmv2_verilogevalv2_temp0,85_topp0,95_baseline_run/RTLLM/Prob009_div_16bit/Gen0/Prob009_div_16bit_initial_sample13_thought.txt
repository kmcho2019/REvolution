The division is implemented as a combinational logic using two always blocks. The first always block captures inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg. The second always block performs the division algorithm: starting with the highest 8 bits of the dividend, it compares to the divisor and shifts in the next dividend bit each iteration. If the portion is greater or equal to the divisor, it subtracts and sets the quotient bit to 1, otherwise 0. This repeats for all 16 bits of the dividend to form the quotient and remainder. The quotient and remainder are output as result and odd respectively.
