Implement a combinational 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor using a restoring division approach. Use two always blocks: 
1) one to latch inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg, 
2) another to perform the division by extracting the top bits of the dividend segment, comparing with the divisor, setting quotient bits, and updating the remainder accordingly. 
This process iterates over all bits of the dividend, shifting in the next bit each time. The quotient and remainder are both 16-bit outputs.
