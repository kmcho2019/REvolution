Implement a combinational 16-bit division by an 8-bit divisor using a restoring division approach. Use two always blocks:
- The first always block simply registers inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg.
- The second always block performs the division combinationally by iteratively shifting bits, comparing the current remainder with the divisor, and updating quotient and remainder accordingly. This simulates the manual long division process. The quotient is built bit-by-bit, and the remainder is updated at each step.
