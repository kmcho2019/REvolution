Use two combinational always blocks: the first to latch inputs into registers a_reg (16-bit) and b_reg (8-bit) whenever inputs A or B change; the second to perform the division using a shift-subtract algorithm on these registers. Initialize remainder and quotient registers to zero. Then for 16 iterations, shift the remainder left by 1, bring down the next bit of the dividend, compare remainder with divisor, and if remainder >= divisor, subtract divisor and set the corresponding quotient bit to 1. Finally, assign quotient and remainder to output ports result and odd.
