The design uses two always blocks as requested. The first always block latches the inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg. The second always block performs the combinational division logic using a shift-subtract approach similar to non-restoring division. It extracts the top bits from the current remainder, compares with divisor, sets quotient bits accordingly, and updates the remainder. The process iterates over each bit of the dividend to produce a 16-bit quotient and 16-bit remainder, complying with the specified approach.
