The division is performed by shifting and subtracting in combinational logic. First, the dividend and divisor are registered in a combinational always block. Then, another combinational always block performs the division algorithm by iteratively extracting bits from the dividend, comparing with the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder registers. The quotient is built bit-by-bit, and the remainder is updated accordingly. This approach avoids sequential logic by unrolling the division steps combinationally.
