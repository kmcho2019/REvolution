Implement a combinational 16-bit by 8-bit divider module using two always blocks. The first always block updates internal registers a_reg and b_reg with inputs A and B respectively. The second always block performs the division algorithm: it initializes quotient and remainder registers, then iteratively compares the highest bits of the remainder concatenated with the next dividend bit against the divisor. If the portion is greater or equal to the divisor, subtract and set quotient bit to 1; otherwise, 0. This mimics the manual long division process combinationally, producing the 16-bit quotient and 16-bit remainder outputs.
