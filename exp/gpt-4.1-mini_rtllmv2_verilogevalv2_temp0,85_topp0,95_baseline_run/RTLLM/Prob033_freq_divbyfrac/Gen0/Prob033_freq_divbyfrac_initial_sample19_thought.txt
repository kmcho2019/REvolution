To achieve a fractional frequency division by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we implement a counter that counts from 0 to 6 (7 cycles total). The divided clock toggles in uneven intervals: one period lasts 4 input clock cycles and the other lasts 3 input clock cycles, alternating. This creates the average division of 3.5. To improve the duty cycle and uniformity, two intermediate divided clocks are generated with a half-clock phase shift relative to each other. We create one intermediate clock on the rising edge of clk, and the other on the falling edge of clk, each using the same counter but delayed or advanced by half a clock cycle. Finally, the outputs of these two clocks are OR'ed to form the final fractional divided clock output with improved duty cycle and uniformity.
