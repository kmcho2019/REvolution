To implement a fractional frequency divider with a division factor of 3.5 (i.e., input frequency divided by 3.5), we double the division factor to 7 and count clock cycles modulo 7. We generate two intermediate clocks with periods of 4 and 3 input clock cycles alternatively to achieve the uneven division. Using double-edge clocking, we generate phase-shifted versions of these clocks by toggling flip-flops on both rising and falling edges of the input clock. One intermediate clock is updated on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of clk to create half-cycle phase shifts. The final output clock is the OR of these two intermediate clocks to produce a smooth fractional divided clock with an approximate 50% duty cycle despite the uneven division periods.
