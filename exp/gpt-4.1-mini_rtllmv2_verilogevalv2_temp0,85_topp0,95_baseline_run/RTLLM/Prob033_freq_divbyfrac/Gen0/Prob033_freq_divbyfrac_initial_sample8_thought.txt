To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 (i.e., a 7/2 division), the design uses a 7-cycle counter to create two uneven clock periods (4 and 3 cycles). By using both edges of the input clock, two intermediate clocks are generated: one aligned with the rising edge and the other phase-shifted by half a clock cycle (aligned with the falling edge). These two intermediate clocks are logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock with a uniform period and improved duty cycle. The reset initializes the counter and outputs. The logic ensures a 3.5 division by effectively toggling the output clock every 3 or 4 input clock cycles, interleaved on rising and falling edges.
