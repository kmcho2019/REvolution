To achieve a 3.5x frequency division using double-edge clocking, the design uses a 7-cycle counter (MUL2_DIV_CLK=7) to create an uneven toggle pattern that alternates between 4 and 3 input clock cycles for each half period of the output clock. This effectively divides the frequency by 7/2 = 3.5. Two intermediate clocks are generated: one toggling on the rising edge of the input clock at specific counts, and another toggling on the falling edge with a half-cycle phase shift. OR-ing these two intermediate clocks produces a final output clock with a uniform period and a duty cycle close to 50%. The reset initializes all registers and the counter.
