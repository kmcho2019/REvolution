To achieve a fractional frequency division by 3.5, we use a double-edge clocking method that cycles through 7 input clock periods (since 3.5*2=7). The counter counts from 0 to 6 (total 7 states), generating two uneven half-periods: 4 cycles high, 3 cycles low (or vice versa). We create two intermediate divided clocks: one triggered on the rising edge of clk, the other on the falling edge, effectively providing half-cycle phase shifts. By OR-ing these two intermediate clocks, the output clock clk_div has a uniform period that is 3.5 times the input clock period, thus achieving fractional frequency division with a better duty cycle. The module uses asynchronous active low reset to initialize the counter and outputs.
