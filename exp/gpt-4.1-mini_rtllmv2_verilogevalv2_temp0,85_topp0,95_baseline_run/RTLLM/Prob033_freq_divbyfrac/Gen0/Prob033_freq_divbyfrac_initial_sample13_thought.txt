To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we create a 7-cycle counter (since 3.5*2=7) to count input clock cycles. We generate two clock signals at half input clock frequency but with different high times (4 and 3 cycles high). One signal is generated on the rising edge, and the other on the falling edge, effectively phase-shifting the signals by half a clock cycle. By OR-ing these two signals, we produce a clock output at 1/3.5 of the input frequency with improved duty cycle uniformity. We use the active-low reset to initialize the counter and outputs.
