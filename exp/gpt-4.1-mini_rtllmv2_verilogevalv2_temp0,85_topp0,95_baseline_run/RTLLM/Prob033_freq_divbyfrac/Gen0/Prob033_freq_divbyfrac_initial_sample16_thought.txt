To achieve fractional frequency division by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, the module counts up to 7 (2 * 3.5) to create two uneven clock periods (4 cycles high, 3 cycles low). Two intermediate clocks are generated: one aligned with the input clock edges and the other phase-shifted by half a clock period (using the opposite clock edge). By OR'ing these two intermediate clocks, the output clock frequency is divided by 3.5 with a more balanced duty cycle. The reset initializes the counter and outputs.
