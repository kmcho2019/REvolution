To implement a fractional frequency divider dividing the clock by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we use a counter that counts from 0 to 6 (7 cycles total). We generate two pulse trains: one high for 4 cycles, the other for 3 cycles, alternating every 7 cycles to achieve the average division of 3.5.

We generate two clock phases: one aligned with the input clock and the other shifted by half a clock cycle (using the negedge of clk). By OR-ing these two generated clocks, we synthesize a fractional clock with uniform frequency and improved duty cycle.

The reset signal rst_n is used to asynchronously reset the counter and output clocks.

This design uses two always blocks clocked on posedge and negedge respectively to generate two intermediate clocks, which are then OR-ed to produce the final output clk_div.
