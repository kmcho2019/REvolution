To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we count through 7 input clock cycles in total. We generate two clock signals: one that is high for 4 cycles and low for 3 cycles, and another phase-shifted by half a clock period that is high for 3 cycles and low for 4 cycles. By OR-ing these two signals, we effectively create a clock with a period equal to 7 input clocks but with a balanced duty cycle close to 50%. The phase shift is achieved by toggling the second clock on the opposite edge of the input clock (i.e., if the first clock toggles on posedge, the second toggles on negedge). Reset logic initializes the counters and outputs. This approach yields a smooth fractional clock output at input frequency divided by 3.5.
