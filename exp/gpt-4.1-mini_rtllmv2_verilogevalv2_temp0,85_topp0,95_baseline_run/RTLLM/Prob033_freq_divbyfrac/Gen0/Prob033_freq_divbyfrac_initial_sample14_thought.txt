To achieve a 3.5x frequency division using double-edge clocking, we count 7 input clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK=7), generating uneven high and low periods (4 and 3 cycles). We create two intermediate clocks: one starts at clk rising edge, the other is phase-shifted by half a clock period (implemented by triggering on clk falling edge). By OR-ing these two clocks, we obtain a fractional frequency divided clock with improved duty cycle. The module resets with an active-low reset signal.
