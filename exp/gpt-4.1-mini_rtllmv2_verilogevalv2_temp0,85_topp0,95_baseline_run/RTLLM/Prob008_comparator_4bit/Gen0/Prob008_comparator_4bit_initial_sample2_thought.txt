Perform a 4-bit subtraction (A - B) using a chain of bit-level subtractors (full subtractors) to generate the result and borrow signals. The borrow out from the MSB indicates if A < B. If no borrow out and the subtraction result is zero, A == B. If no borrow out and the result is non-zero, A > B. Use combinational logic to generate mutually exclusive outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
