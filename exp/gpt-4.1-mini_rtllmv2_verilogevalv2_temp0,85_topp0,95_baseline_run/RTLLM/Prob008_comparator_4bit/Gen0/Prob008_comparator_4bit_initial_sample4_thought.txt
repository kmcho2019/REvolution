Implement a 4-bit comparator by performing a combinational subtraction of B from A using bit-level full subtractors to generate the difference and borrow signals. The borrow chain will determine if A < B (borrow out from MSB). If no borrow out and the difference is zero, then A == B. If no borrow out and difference is non-zero, then A > B. This approach uses multiple bit-level subtractors to generate the borrow and difference signals combinationally and then sets the output flags accordingly.
