Implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence 10011 on a single input bit stream. The FSM transitions through states corresponding to the progress of matching the sequence. On each clock edge, depending on the input, it moves to the next state or falls back appropriately to support overlapping sequences. The MATCH output is asserted when the last bit of the sequence (the final '1' in "10011") is detected. The FSM is synchronous and resets on RST. The states will represent how many bits of the sequence have been matched so far.
