Implement a Mealy FSM with states representing the progress through the sequence "10011". The FSM transitions on the rising edge of CLK or RST, with synchronous reset. The output MATCH is asserted when the last bit of the sequence (1) is detected, immediately on that input. The FSM supports overlapping sequences by returning to appropriate states after detection (loop detection). The FSM states represent how many bits of the sequence have been matched so far.
