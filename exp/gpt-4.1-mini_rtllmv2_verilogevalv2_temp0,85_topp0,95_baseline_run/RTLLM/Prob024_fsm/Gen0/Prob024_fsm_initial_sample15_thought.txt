Design a Mealy FSM with states representing the progress of matching the sequence "10011". On each clock edge, based on the current state and input IN, transition to the next state. When the final sequence is detected at the last input bit, output MATCH = 1 for that clock cycle, otherwise 0. The FSM supports overlapping sequences, meaning after detecting a full sequence, it will immediately check if the new input can be part of a new sequence. Use synchronous reset to initialize states and output.
