Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module that takes two N-bit fixed-point inputs (with Q fractional bits) and outputs their sum in N bits. The module examines the sign bits (MSBs) of the inputs to decide between addition and subtraction of their absolute values. If both inputs have the same sign, add their absolute values and keep the sign. If signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one and assign the sign accordingly. Handle overflow by truncating to N bits (which is natural in fixed-width addition). The internal register `res` stores the intermediate result before assigning to output. The absolute value is computed by taking two's complement if the sign bit is 1. Comparisons of magnitudes are done on absolute values to decide subtraction direction.
