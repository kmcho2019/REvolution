Implement a fixed-point adder handling signed inputs in two's complement format. The MSB indicates sign. The addition logic is:
- If signs of inputs are the same, add absolute values and assign the common sign.
- If signs differ, subtract smaller absolute value from larger absolute value, assign the sign of the operand with the larger magnitude.
We extract sign bits, calculate absolute values, perform addition or subtraction accordingly, and assign the final sign bit. The fractional bits Q are implicitly handled by the fixed-point format; no scaling is needed for addition. Overflow is implicitly handled by two's complement arithmetic; no explicit overflow flag is required.
