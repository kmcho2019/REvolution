Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module that takes two N-bit fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The sign is represented by the MSB of each input (two's complement). The addition logic depends on the signs of the inputs: if they have the same sign, add their absolute values and keep the sign; if different signs, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and assign the sign of the larger operand. Internal registers hold intermediate results. Ensure the output matches the fixed-point representation with consistent precision. Overflow is implicitly handled by the fixed bit width.
