Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module that adds or subtracts two fixed-point numbers represented in two's complement with N bits total and Q fractional bits. The MSB is the sign bit. If the signs match, add the absolute values and preserve the sign. If signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one and assign the sign of the larger operand to the result. Use unsigned arithmetic to compare and calculate absolute values. Store the final result in a register. Output the N-bit result. Overflow is implicitly handled by the fixed bit width and sign bit.
