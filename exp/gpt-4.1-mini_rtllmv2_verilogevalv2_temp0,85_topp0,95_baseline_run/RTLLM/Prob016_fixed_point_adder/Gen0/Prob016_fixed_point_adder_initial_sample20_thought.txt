Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module that adds or subtracts two N-bit fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The sign bit is the MSB. When the signs are the same, simply add the absolute values and assign the sign accordingly. When the signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and assign the sign of the larger operand. The absolute value is computed by masking out the sign bit and treating the rest as unsigned. Handle zero results carefully to assign sign bit as 0 (positive). The internal register res holds the result before assigning to output c. Overflow is implicitly handled by the fixed width of N bits.
