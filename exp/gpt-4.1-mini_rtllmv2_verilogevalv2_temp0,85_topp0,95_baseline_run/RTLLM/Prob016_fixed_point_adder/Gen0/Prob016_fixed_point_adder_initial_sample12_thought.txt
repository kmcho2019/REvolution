Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder with N bits total and Q fractional bits.
Since inputs are signed fixed-point numbers, MSB is sign bit.
If signs of inputs match, add absolute values and keep the sign.
If signs differ, subtract smaller absolute value from larger, and sign of result is sign of the operand with larger absolute value.
The absolute value can be obtained by inverting and adding 1 if negative (two's complement).
Perform addition or subtraction on absolute values, then assign sign bit.
Store the final result in an internal register 'res' and output it.
Handle overflow by restricting to N bits naturally through arithmetic.
