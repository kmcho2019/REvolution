Design a parameterized fixed-point adder that works on signed fixed-point numbers represented in two's complement form with N bits total and Q fractional bits. The module takes two N-bit inputs a and b.

- First, check if the sign bits (MSB) of a and b are the same.
  - If same sign, perform addition on the absolute values (strip sign bit for comparison), set the sign bit of result to the common sign.
- If sign bits differ:
  - Compare absolute values of a and b.
  - Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one.
  - Set the sign bit of result to the sign of the operand with the larger magnitude.
- To handle absolute values, convert inputs to positive magnitude by negating if sign bit is set.
- The internal addition and subtraction are done on (N-1)-bit magnitudes.
- The final output is reconstructed by combining the sign bit and magnitude.
- Since it's fixed point, no additional scaling is needed after addition.
- Overflow is controlled by truncation to N bits. The sign bit is carefully set as per rules.
- The internal register res holds the final N-bit result.

This approach ensures correct fixed-point addition/subtraction respecting sign and magnitude.
