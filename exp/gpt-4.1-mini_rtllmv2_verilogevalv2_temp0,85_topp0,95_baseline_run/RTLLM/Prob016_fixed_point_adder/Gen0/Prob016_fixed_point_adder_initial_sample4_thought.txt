Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder that handles signed inputs with Q fractional bits and total width N. The module determines whether to add or subtract based on the sign bits. If signs are the same, sum the absolute values and keep the sign. If signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and assign the sign accordingly. Use two's complement representation and handle sign bits carefully. Store result in a register 'res' and assign it to output 'c'. No explicit overflow output is required, but the MSB will indicate sign. The fixed-point representation is maintained by simply adding or subtracting the N-bit inputs as integers, but with sign and magnitude logic as specified.
