Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder handling Q fractional bits and N total bits.
The inputs are signed fixed-point numbers in two's complement with N bits, where MSB is sign bit.
When signs match, perform addition of absolute values and keep the sign.
When signs differ, subtract smaller absolute value from larger absolute value and assign sign of the operand with larger magnitude.
The absolute value is computed by two's complement if sign bit is set.
The result is assigned to an internal register 'res' and output 'c'.
Overflow is implicitly handled by the fixed width and sign bit.
