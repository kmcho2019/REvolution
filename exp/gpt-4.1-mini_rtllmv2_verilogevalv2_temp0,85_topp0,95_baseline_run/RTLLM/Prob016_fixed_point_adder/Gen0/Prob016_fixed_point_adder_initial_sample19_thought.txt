Implement a fixed-point adder module parameterized by total width N and fractional bits Q. The sign bit is the MSB. Addition and subtraction depend on the sign bits of inputs a and b. If signs are the same, add absolute values and keep the sign. If signs differ, subtract smaller absolute from larger and set sign accordingly. Absolute value extraction involves masking out the sign bit for comparison and arithmetic. The result is assigned to an internal register res and output c. Overflow is implicitly handled by the fixed N-bit width, keeping the sign consistent.
