Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module where the inputs and output are N-bit signed fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The addition behavior depends on the signs of inputs a and b. If both have the same sign (MSB), add their magnitudes and keep the sign. If signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one and assign the sign of the operand with the larger magnitude. Use internal logic to compute absolute values and compare them. Store the final result in a register and output it. Handle overflow by naturally limiting to N bits.
