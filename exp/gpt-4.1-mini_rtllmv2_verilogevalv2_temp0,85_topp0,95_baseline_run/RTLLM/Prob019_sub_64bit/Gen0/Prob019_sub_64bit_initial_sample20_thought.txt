To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, subtract B from A and assign the result. For overflow detection in signed subtraction: 
- Overflow occurs if A is positive, B is negative, and the result is negative (positive overflow).
- Or if A is negative, B is positive, and the result is positive (negative overflow).
Check the MSB (sign bit) of A, B, and result to determine overflow and set the overflow flag accordingly.
