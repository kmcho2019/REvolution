Implement a 64-bit subtractor module that takes two 64-bit signed inputs, A and B, and produces a 64-bit signed result along with an overflow flag. The subtraction is straightforward: result = A - B. Overflow detection for subtraction is based on the signs of A, B, and the result. Specifically, overflow occurs if:
1. A is positive, B is negative, and the result is negative (positive overflow).
2. A is negative, B is positive, and the result is positive (negative overflow).
The overflow flag is set accordingly.
