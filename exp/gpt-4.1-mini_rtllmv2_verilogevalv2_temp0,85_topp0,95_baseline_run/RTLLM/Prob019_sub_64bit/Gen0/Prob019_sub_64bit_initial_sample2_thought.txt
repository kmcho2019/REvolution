To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, I will subtract B from A to get the result. Since A and B are signed 64-bit values, the overflow conditions arise based on the signs of A, B, and the result:
- Positive overflow: when A is positive, B is negative, and result is negative.
- Negative overflow: when A is negative, B is positive, and result is positive.

I will extract the sign bits of A, B, and the result to check these conditions and set the overflow flag accordingly.
