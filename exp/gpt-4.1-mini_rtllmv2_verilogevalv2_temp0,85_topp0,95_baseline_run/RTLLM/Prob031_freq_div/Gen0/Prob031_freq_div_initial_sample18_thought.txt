Use three separate always blocks triggered on the positive edge of CLK_in or asynchronous reset RST. For CLK_50, simply toggle the output at each clock cycle, effectively dividing the clock frequency by 2. For CLK_10, use a 3-bit counter that counts from 0 to 4; when it reaches 4, toggle CLK_10 and reset the counter. For CLK_1, use a 6-bit counter to count from 0 to 49; when it reaches 49, toggle CLK_1 and reset the counter. Reset all outputs and counters asynchronously when RST is asserted.
