Implement a frequency divider module named freq_div with input clock CLK_in at 100MHz and reset RST. The module generates three output clocks: CLK_50 at 50MHz, CLK_10 at 10MHz, and CLK_1 at 1MHz. CLK_50 toggles every clock cycle (divide by 2). CLK_10 toggles after counting 5 clock cycles (divide by 10). CLK_1 toggles after counting 50 clock cycles (divide by 100). Counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 are used for CLK_10 and CLK_1 respectively. All signals are synchronous to CLK_in with asynchronous reset.
