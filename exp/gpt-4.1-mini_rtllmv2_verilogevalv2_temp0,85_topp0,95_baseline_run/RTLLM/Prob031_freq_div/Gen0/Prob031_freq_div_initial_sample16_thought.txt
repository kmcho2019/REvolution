Implement a frequency divider with three separate counters and clock outputs.  
- CLK_50: toggle on every positive edge to divide by 2.  
- CLK_10: use a 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 4, toggle CLK_10 and reset counter at 4.  
- CLK_1: use a 6-bit counter counting from 0 to 49, toggle CLK_1 and reset counter at 49.  
All counters and outputs reset to 0 on RST.
