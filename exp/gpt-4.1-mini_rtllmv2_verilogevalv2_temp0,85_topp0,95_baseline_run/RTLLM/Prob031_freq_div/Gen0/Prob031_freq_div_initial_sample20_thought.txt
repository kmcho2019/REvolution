Use three separate processes to generate each clock output. For CLK_50, toggle on every positive edge of CLK_in unless reset. For CLK_10, count 5 cycles of CLK_in (0 to 4), then toggle CLK_10 and reset counter. For CLK_1, count 50 cycles of CLK_in (0 to 49), then toggle CLK_1 and reset counter. Reset sets outputs and counters to zero.
