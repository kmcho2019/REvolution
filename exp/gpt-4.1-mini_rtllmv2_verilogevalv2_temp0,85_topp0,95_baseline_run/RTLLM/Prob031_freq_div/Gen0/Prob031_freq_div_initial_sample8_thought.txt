Use three separate always blocks triggered on the positive edge of CLK_in or the reset RST.

1. For CLK_50: toggle CLK_50 on every clock edge when not in reset.
2. For CLK_10: use a 3-bit counter cnt_10 that counts from 0 to 4; toggle CLK_10 when counter hits 4 and reset counter.
3. For CLK_1: use a 6-bit counter cnt_100 that counts from 0 to 49; toggle CLK_1 when counter hits 49 and reset counter.

Reset conditions set the clocks to 0 and counters to 0.
This meets the frequency division requirements: 100MHz / 2 = 50MHz, /10 = 10MHz, /100 = 1MHz.
