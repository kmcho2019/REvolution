Implement a frequency divider module with an input clock of 100 MHz and reset. The module generates three divided clocks: 50 MHz by toggling a flip-flop every clock cycle, 10 MHz by toggling after counting 5 cycles (since toggling at 5 cycles results in 10 cycles per full period), and 1 MHz by toggling after counting 50 cycles. Use synchronous reset to initialize outputs and counters.
