Implement a frequency divider module named freq_div with input clock CLK_in at 100MHz and reset RST. Generate three output clocks:

- CLK_50 by toggling at every clock edge (divide by 2),
- CLK_10 by counting input clock edges, toggling CLK_10 when a 5-cycle count is reached (because toggling at count 4 means half period is 5 cycles, total period 10 cycles),
- CLK_1 by counting input clock edges, toggling CLK_1 when a 50-cycle count is reached (half period 50 cycles, total period 100 cycles).

Use synchronous reset and counters as specified.
