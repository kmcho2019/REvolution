Implement three frequency dividers based on the input 100MHz clock. For 50MHz output, simply toggle CLK_50 on every clock edge. For 10MHz, count 5 clock cycles to toggle CLK_10 (total 10 cycles for a full period). For 1MHz, count 50 clock cycles to toggle CLK_1 (total 100 cycles for a full period). Use synchronous reset to initialize outputs and counters.
