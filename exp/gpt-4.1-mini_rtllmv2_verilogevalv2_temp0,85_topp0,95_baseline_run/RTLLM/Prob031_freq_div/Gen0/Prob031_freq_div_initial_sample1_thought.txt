Implement a frequency divider module named freq_div with input clock CLK_in at 100MHz and reset RST. 
Generate three output clocks: CLK_50 (divided by 2), CLK_10 (divided by 10), and CLK_1 (divided by 100).
CLK_50 toggles every positive edge of CLK_in, so it toggles each cycle (divide by 2).
CLK_10 toggles every 5 cycles (counts 0 to 4), producing a 10MHz clock.
CLK_1 toggles every 50 cycles (counts 0 to 49), producing a 1MHz clock.
Use synchronous reset for all flip-flops and counters.
