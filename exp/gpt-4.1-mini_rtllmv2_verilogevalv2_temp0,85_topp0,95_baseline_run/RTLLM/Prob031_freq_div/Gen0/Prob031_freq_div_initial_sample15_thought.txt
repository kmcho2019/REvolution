Implement a frequency divider module with input clock at 100 MHz. The module generates three output clocks: 50 MHz by toggling every clock cycle (divide by 2), 10 MHz by toggling every 5 cycles (divide by 10), and 1 MHz by toggling every 50 cycles (divide by 100). Use synchronous reset and counters for 10 MHz and 1 MHz clocks, with toggling based on counters reaching specific counts.
