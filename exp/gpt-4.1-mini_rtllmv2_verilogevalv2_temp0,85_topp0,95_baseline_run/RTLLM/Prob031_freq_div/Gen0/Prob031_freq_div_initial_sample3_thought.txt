Implement a frequency divider module with three output clocks derived from a 100 MHz input clock. Use separate counters and flip-flops for each output frequency. CLK_50 toggles every clock cycle (divide by 2). CLK_10 toggles every 5 counts (divide by 10 total), and CLK_1 toggles every 50 counts (divide by 100 total). Asynchronously reset all outputs and counters with RST.
