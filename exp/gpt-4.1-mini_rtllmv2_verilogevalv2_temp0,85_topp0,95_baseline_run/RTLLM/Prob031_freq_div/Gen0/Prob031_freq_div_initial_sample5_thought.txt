Use three separate always blocks triggered by the positive edge of CLK_in or RST for generating each output clock.  
For CLK_50, toggle the output every clock cycle.  
For CLK_10, use a 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 4; toggle CLK_10 when counter hits 4 and reset the counter.  
For CLK_1, use a 6-bit counter counting from 0 to 49; toggle CLK_1 when counter hits 49 and reset the counter.  
Reset sets all outputs and counters to zero.
