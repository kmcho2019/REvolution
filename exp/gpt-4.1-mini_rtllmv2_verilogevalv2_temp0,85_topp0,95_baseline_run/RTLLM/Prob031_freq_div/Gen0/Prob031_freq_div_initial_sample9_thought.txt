Implement a frequency divider module named freq_div that takes a 100MHz input clock and a reset signal. It outputs three divided clocks: 50MHz by toggling output every clock cycle, 10MHz by toggling output every 5 clock cycles (counter counts to 4), and 1MHz by toggling output every 50 clock cycles (counter counts to 49). All counters and output clocks reset to zero upon RST being asserted.
