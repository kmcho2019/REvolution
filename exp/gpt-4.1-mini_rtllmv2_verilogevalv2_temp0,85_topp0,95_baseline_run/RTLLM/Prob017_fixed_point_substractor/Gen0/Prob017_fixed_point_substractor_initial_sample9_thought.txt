To implement the fixed-point subtractor with parameters Q and N, the inputs and outputs are N-bit fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The subtraction needs to handle sign and magnitude carefully.

1. Extract sign bits of inputs a and b.
2. If signs are the same, perform subtraction of magnitudes and keep the sign.
3. If signs are different, perform addition of magnitudes.
   - The result sign depends on the relative magnitudes of a and b.
4. For zero result, explicitly clear the sign bit.
5. Use a register `res` to hold the result before assigning to output `c`.
6. Since inputs are in two's complement fixed-point, magnitude extraction involves taking two's complement if negative.
7. Perform magnitude comparison to determine the sign for addition cases.
8. Finally, convert the magnitude and sign back to two's complement format.

This approach ensures the fixed-point subtraction is accurate, preserves precision, and handles sign correctly.
