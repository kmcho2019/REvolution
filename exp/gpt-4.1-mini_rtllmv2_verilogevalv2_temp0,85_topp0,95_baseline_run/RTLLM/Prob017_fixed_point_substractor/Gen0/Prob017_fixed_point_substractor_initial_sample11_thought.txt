Implement a parameterized fixed-point subtractor that handles sign and magnitude correctly. The inputs and output are N-bit fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The subtraction logic is based on the signs of the operands: if signs are the same, subtract magnitudes and keep the sign; if signs differ, add magnitudes and assign sign based on magnitude comparison. The module will extract sign and magnitude, perform the arithmetic, and then reassemble the fixed-point result. Also, explicitly set sign bit to 0 if the result is zero.
