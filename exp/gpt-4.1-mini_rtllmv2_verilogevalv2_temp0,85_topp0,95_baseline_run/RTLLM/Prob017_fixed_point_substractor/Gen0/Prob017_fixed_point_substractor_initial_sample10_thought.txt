To implement the fixed_point_subtractor with parameters Q and N, the design will interpret the N-bit inputs as fixed-point numbers with Q fractional bits. The sign bit is the MSB (N-1). The subtraction operation is done by considering the signs of a and b:

- If signs are the same, subtract the magnitudes and keep the sign.
- If signs differ, add the magnitudes and determine the sign from magnitude comparison.
- Handle zero result by clearing the sign bit.
The internal register res holds the result. The output c is assigned from res. This way we maintain fixed-point precision and proper sign handling.
