Implement a parameterized fixed-point subtractor module that handles subtraction with sign and magnitude considerations. The inputs a and b are signed fixed-point numbers with N bits total and Q fractional bits. The module will first extract the sign and magnitude of both inputs. If the signs are the same, perform a subtraction of magnitudes and keep the sign. If the signs differ, perform addition of magnitudes and assign the sign according to the relative magnitudes of a and b. Handle zero result by explicitly setting sign bit to zero. The final result is reconstructed from sign and magnitude into a two's complement fixed-point format. Use internal register res for the calculation and assign the output c accordingly.
