Implement a fixed-point subtractor module parameterized by total bit width N and fractional bits Q. Inputs a and b are N-bit fixed-point numbers (signed). The subtraction depends on the signs of a and b:

- If both have the same sign, perform subtraction of their absolute values and assign the sign of the inputs to the result.
- If signs differ, perform addition of absolute values and determine the result sign by comparing magnitudes.
- Handle zero result by forcing the sign bit to 0.
- Use a register res to store the intermediate result and assign to output c.
- The sign bit is the MSB (N-1).
This approach ensures correct signed fixed-point subtraction with the defined precision.
