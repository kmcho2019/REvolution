Design a module "instr_reg" with clk and active-low rst. Use two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On posedge clk, if rst is low, clear both registers. If fetch == 2'b01, load data into ins_p1; if fetch == 2'b10, load data into ins_p2; otherwise keep previous values. Output ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (high 3 bits and low 5 bits), and ad2 is the entire ins_p2 register.
