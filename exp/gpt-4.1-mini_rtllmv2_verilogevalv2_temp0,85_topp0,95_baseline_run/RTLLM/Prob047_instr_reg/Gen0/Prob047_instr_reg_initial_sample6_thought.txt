Create a module instr_reg with clk, rst, fetch, and data as inputs. Use two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from different fetch sources. On reset (rst low), initialize both registers to zero. On the rising edge of clk, update ins_p1 if fetch == 2'b01, and ins_p2 if fetch == 2'b10, else keep previous values. Derive outputs ins as the upper 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 as the lower 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 as the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
