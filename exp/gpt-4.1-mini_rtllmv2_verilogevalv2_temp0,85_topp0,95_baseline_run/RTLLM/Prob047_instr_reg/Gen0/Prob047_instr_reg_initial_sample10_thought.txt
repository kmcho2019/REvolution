Implement a synchronous instruction register with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On reset (active low), clear both registers. On each rising clock edge, update ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01 and update ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10, otherwise keep their previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1's upper 3 bits and lower 5 bits respectively, and ad2 is directly the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
