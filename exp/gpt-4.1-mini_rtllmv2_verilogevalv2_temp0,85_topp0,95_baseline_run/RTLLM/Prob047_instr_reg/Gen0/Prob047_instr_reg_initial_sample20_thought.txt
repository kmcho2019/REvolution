Create a module named instr_reg with inputs clk, rst, fetch, and data. Inside, define two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is low, reset both registers to zero. Otherwise, if fetch is 2'b01, load data into ins_p1; if fetch is 2'b10, load data into ins_p2; else retain previous values. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are derived as follows: ins is the upper 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 is the lower 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 is the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
