Implement a synchronous instruction register module with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is asserted low, reset both registers to zero. Otherwise, load data into ins_p1 if fetch is 2'b01, or into ins_p2 if fetch is 2'b10, retaining previous values if neither. The outputs are derived as follows: ins is the upper 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 is the lower 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 is the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
