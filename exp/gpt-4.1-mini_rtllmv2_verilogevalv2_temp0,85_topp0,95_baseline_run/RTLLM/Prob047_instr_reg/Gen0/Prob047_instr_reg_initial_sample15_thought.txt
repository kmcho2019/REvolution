Create a module instr_reg with clk, rst, fetch, and data as inputs. Inside, define two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is low, clear both registers. Otherwise, based on fetch, load data into ins_p1 if fetch is 01, into ins_p2 if fetch is 10, else keep previous values. Assign outputs ins as high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 as low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 as full ins_p2.
