The module instr_reg uses a clock and active low reset to control two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2. On reset, both registers are cleared to zero. On each rising clock edge, depending on the fetch control signal, the data input is stored either in ins_p1 (when fetch is 2'b01) or in ins_p2 (when fetch is 2'b10). If fetch is neither 01 nor 10, the registers retain their previous values. The outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (ins being the upper 3 bits and ad1 being the lower 5 bits), while ad2 is directly from ins_p2.
