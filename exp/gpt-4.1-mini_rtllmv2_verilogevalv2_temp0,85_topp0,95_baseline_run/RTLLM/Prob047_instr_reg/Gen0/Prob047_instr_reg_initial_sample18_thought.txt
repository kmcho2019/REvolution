Implement a synchronous module with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 that update on clk rising edge.
On reset (active low), both registers reset to zero.
When fetch = 2'b01, load data into ins_p1.
When fetch = 2'b10, load data into ins_p2.
Otherwise, retain previous values.
Outputs ins (upper 3 bits of ins_p1), ad1 (lower 5 bits of ins_p1), and ad2 (full 8 bits of ins_p2).
