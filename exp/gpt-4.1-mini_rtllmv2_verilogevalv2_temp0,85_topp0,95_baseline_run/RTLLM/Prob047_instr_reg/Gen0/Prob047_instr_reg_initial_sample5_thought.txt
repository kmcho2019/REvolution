Create a synchronous instruction register with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On reset (active low), clear both registers. On each rising clock edge, load data into ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01, into ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10, else retain values. Outputs are derived by splitting ins_p1 into ins (upper 3 bits) and ad1 (lower 5 bits), and output ad2 is the full ins_p2.
