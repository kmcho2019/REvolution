Implement a synchronous instruction register module with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2). On the rising edge of clk, if rst is low, clear both registers. Otherwise, update ins_p1 if fetch == 2'b01, update ins_p2 if fetch == 2'b10, else retain old values. The outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1's upper 3 bits and lower 5 bits, respectively. The output ad2 is the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
