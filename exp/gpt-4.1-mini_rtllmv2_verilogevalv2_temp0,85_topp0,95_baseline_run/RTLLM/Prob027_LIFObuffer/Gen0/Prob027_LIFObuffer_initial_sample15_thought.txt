Implement a 4-entry stack using an array of 4-bit registers and a 3-bit stack pointer (SP) initialized to 4 to indicate empty state. On reset, clear all entries and set SP to 4. On write (RW=0) with EN high and not full, decrement SP and store dataIn at that location. On read (RW=1) with EN high and not empty, output data from SP, clear that location, and increment SP. Update EMPTY flag when SP==4 and FULL flag when SP==0.
