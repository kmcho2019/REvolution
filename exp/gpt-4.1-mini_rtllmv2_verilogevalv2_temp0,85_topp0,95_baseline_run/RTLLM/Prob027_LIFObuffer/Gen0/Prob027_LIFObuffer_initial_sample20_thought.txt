Implement a 4-entry 4-bit wide stack as an array with a stack pointer (SP) starting at 4 (empty). On reset, initialize SP to 4 and clear memory. On each clock edge when enabled, if RW=0 and not full, push dataIn by decrementing SP and storing data. If RW=1 and not empty, pop dataOut by reading from SP and incrementing it. Update EMPTY when SP == 4 and FULL when SP == 0.
