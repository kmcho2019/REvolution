Implement a 4-entry stack (LIFO) buffer with a 4-bit data width. Use a 2-bit stack pointer SP that points to the next free location for push (write) operations. Initialize SP to 4 (empty state) on reset and clear memory. On write (RW=0) and not full, decrement SP and store dataIn. On read (RW=1) and not empty, output the top dataOut, clear the memory, and increment SP. Update EMPTY and FULL flags based on SP (EMPTY when SP=4, FULL when SP=0). All operations occur synchronously on the rising edge of Clk when EN is high. Rst is synchronous active high.
