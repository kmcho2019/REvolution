To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using an illegal sensitivity list, we can create two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and the other on the negative edge of the clock. Each flip-flop captures the input 'd' on its respective clock edge. Then, we combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock to produce the final output 'q'. This approach ensures 'q' updates on both edges of the clock, effectively emulating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
