To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in FPGAs without illegal sensitivity lists, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of clk and another on the negative edge of clk. Each flip-flop captures the input d on its respective clock edge. Then, we use a multiplexer controlled by the clock signal to select which flip-flop output to use, effectively giving us a sampled output at both edges of clk.
