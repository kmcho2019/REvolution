A dual-edge triggered flip-flop can be implemented by creating two flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and the other on the negative edge of the clock. Then, a multiplexer selects the output corresponding to the current clock level to produce the final output. This approach effectively samples the input on both clock edges. Since FPGAs only support single-edge triggered flip-flops, this method avoids illegal sensitivity lists and uses standard positive and negative edge triggered flip-flops separately.
