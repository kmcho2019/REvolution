To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA without direct support, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and another on the negative edge of the clock. Both flip-flops sample the input 'd' on their respective edges, and then we combine their outputs through a multiplexer driven by the clock to produce the final output 'q'. This way, 'q' updates on both edges of the clock.
