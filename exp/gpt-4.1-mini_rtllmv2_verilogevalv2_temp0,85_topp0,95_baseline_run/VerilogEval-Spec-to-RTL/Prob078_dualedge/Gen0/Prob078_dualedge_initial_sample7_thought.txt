To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA without using an always block sensitive to both edges (which is not allowed), we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other on the negative edge. Both capture the input `d` on their respective edges. Then, we combine their outputs using a simple logic (like a mux or an OR gate) to produce a single output `q` that updates on both edges effectively. This way, the output changes on both rising and falling edges of the clock.
