To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in an FPGA, which does not support triggering on both edges directly, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and one on the negative edge of the clock. Both flip-flops capture the input 'd' on their respective clock edges. Then, we use a multiplexer controlled by the clock signal to select the output from the appropriate flip-flop. This way, on the positive half-cycle, the output reflects the positive-edge triggered flip-flop's output, and on the negative half-cycle, it reflects the negative-edge triggered flip-flop's output, effectively creating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop behavior.
