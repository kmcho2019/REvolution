A dual-edge triggered flip-flop captures the input 'd' on both the rising and falling edges of 'clk'. Since we cannot use an always block sensitive to both edges directly, we can emulate this behavior by using two single-edge flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of the clock and one triggered on the falling edge (achieved by using the inverted clock). We then combine their outputs to produce the final output 'q'. One common method is to use a toggle flip-flop or latch that updates on both edges via two flip-flops clocked by clk and its inverse, and then multiplex or OR their outputs appropriately. Here, we implement two registers sampling 'd' on posedge and negedge respectively and then latch the output using a simple two-stage approach to create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop behavior.
