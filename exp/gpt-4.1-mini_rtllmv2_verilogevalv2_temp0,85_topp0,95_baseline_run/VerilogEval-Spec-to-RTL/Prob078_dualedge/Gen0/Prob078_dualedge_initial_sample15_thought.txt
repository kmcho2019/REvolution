To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using an always block sensitive to both edges, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of clk and the other triggered on the falling edge of clk. Each flip-flop captures the input d at its respective clock edge, and then we combine their outputs with a multiplexer that selects which flip-flop output to use based on the clock level. This way, the output q updates on both edges of clk, emulating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
