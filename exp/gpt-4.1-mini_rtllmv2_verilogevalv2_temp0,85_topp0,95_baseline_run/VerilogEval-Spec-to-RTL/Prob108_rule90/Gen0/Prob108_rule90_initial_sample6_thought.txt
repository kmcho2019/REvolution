Implement a 512-bit register q to hold the state of the cellular automaton. On a positive clock edge, if load is high, load q with the input data. Otherwise, update each bit of q according to Rule 90: the next state of cell i is the XOR of its left neighbor (q[i-1]) and right neighbor (q[i+1]). For the boundaries, assume neighbors outside the range are zero. Implement the next state combinationally, then update q on the clock edge.
