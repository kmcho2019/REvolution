Implement a Moore finite state machine with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The FSM state updates on the positive edge of clk, and asynchronously resets to WALK_LEFT on the positive edge of areset. When in WALK_LEFT state, walk_left output is 1 and walk_right is 0; when in WALK_RIGHT state, walk_right is 1 and walk_left is 0. On each clock, if bump_left or bump_right is 1 (even both), the state toggles to the opposite walking direction.
