Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The current state drives the outputs: walk_left or walk_right. On each clock's positive edge, if asynchronous reset (areset) is asserted, reset to WALK_LEFT. Otherwise, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted (either or both), switch direction: if walking left and bumped, switch to right; if walking right and bumped, switch to left.
