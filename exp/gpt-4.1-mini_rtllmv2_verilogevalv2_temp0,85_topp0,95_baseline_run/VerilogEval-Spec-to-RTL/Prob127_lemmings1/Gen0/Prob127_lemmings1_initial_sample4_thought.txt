Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. On each clock edge, if bumped on the left, transition to WALK_RIGHT. If bumped on the right, transition to WALK_LEFT. If both bumps are 1, still switch directions. Outputs are one-hot: walk_left=1 when in WALK_LEFT, walk_right=1 when in WALK_RIGHT.
