Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. On asynchronous positive edge reset, state is set to WALK_LEFT. On each clock positive edge, if bump_left or bump_right is high, transition to the opposite state. If both bumps are high, also switch states. Outputs are walk_left = 1 when in WALK_LEFT state, walk_right = 1 when in WALK_RIGHT state.
