Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The output walk_left is 1 in WALK_LEFT state, walk_right is 1 in WALK_RIGHT state. On reset (asynchronous, positive edge), state is set to WALK_LEFT. On each positive clock edge, if bump_left or bump_right is 1 (including both), the state toggles direction: WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT or WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT.
