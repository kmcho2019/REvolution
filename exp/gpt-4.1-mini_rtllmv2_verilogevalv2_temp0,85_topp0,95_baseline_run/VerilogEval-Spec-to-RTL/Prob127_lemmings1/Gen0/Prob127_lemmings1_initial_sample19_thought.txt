Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The state changes on the positive edge of clk or asynchronously resets on the positive edge of areset. On reset, set state to WALK_LEFT. When in WALK_LEFT, output walk_left=1 and walk_right=0; when in WALK_RIGHT, output walk_left=0 and walk_right=1. On each clock, if bump_left or bump_right is 1 (or both), the FSM switches to the opposite direction.
