Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The output walk_left is 1 in state WALK_LEFT, and walk_right is 1 in state WALK_RIGHT. On areset (async posedge), reset to WALK_LEFT. On each clock posedge, if bumped on the left or right (or both), switch directions. If walking left and bumped, switch to walk right; if walking right and bumped, switch to walk left.
