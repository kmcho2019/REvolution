Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. The state transitions occur on the positive edge of clk or asynchronously on the positive edge of areset. On areset, set state to WALK_LEFT. On each clock edge, if bumped on the left, transition to WALK_RIGHT; if bumped on the right, transition to WALK_LEFT; if both bumps occur simultaneously, transition to the opposite direction as well. Outputs are derived solely from the state: if in WALK_LEFT, walk_left=1 and walk_right=0; if in WALK_RIGHT, walk_left=0 and walk_right=1.
