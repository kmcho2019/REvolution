To add two 8-bit 2's complement numbers and detect overflow, perform an 8-bit addition with carry out. Overflow occurs if the sign of the result does not match the expected sign given the inputs: specifically, if the carry into the sign bit and the carry out of the sign bit differ. Another way is to check if the sign bits of inputs are the same but differ from the result's sign bit.
