Implement an 8-bit signed adder that adds inputs a and b. The sum is assigned to s. To detect signed overflow, check if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the sign bit of the result s. Specifically, overflow occurs if the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of the sign bit, or equivalently if the sign of the sum does not match the sign of the inputs when the inputs have the same sign.
