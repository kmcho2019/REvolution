To add two 8-bit signed numbers and detect overflow, perform the addition using a 9-bit wire to hold the sum including the carry out. The overflow in signed addition occurs if the sign bits of the operands are the same, but the sign bit of the result differs. This can be detected by checking the carry into and out of the sign bit, or equivalently by checking if the sign of the sum differs from the operands' sign when operands have the same sign.
