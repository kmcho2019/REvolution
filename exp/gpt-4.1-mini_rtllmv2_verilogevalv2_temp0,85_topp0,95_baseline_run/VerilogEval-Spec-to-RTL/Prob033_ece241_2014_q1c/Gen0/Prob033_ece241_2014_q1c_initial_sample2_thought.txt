To add two 8-bit 2's complement numbers and detect overflow, we perform an 8-bit addition with carry. The overflow in signed addition occurs if the carry into the most significant bit (MSB) differs from the carry out of the MSB. Alternatively, overflow can be detected by checking if the sign bits of inputs are the same and the sign bit of the result differs. We will implement an 8-bit adder, compute the sum and the overflow accordingly.
