Perform an 8-bit signed addition of inputs a and b, producing the sum s.
Detect signed overflow by checking if the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of the sign bit.
The overflow can be computed using the formula:
overflow = (carry_in_to_msb) XOR (carry_out_of_msb)
where carry_in_to_msb is the carry into bit 7, and carry_out_of_msb is the carry out of bit 7.
