To add two 8-bit 2's complement numbers a and b, and detect signed overflow, perform an 8-bit addition with a carry out. The sum output is the 8-bit result of the addition. Overflow in signed addition occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same, but the sign bit of the result is different. More concretely, overflow = (~a[7] & ~b[7] & s[7]) | (a[7] & b[7] & ~s[7]). This condition can be computed after addition.
