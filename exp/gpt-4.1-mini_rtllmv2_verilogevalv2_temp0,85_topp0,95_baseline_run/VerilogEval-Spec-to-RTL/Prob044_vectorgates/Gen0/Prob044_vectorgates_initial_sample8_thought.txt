The module takes two 3-bit inputs a and b. We compute the bitwise OR by using the '|' operator on the vectors, resulting in a 3-bit vector. The logical OR checks if either a or b is nonzero (using '||'), yielding a 1-bit output. For out_not, we invert b and place it in the upper 3 bits, and invert a and place it in the lower 3 bits. This can be done by concatenating the two inverted vectors.
