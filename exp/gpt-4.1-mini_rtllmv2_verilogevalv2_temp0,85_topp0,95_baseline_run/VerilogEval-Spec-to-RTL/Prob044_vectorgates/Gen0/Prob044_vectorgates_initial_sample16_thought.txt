The module TopModule has two 3-bit inputs a and b, and three outputs:
- out_or_bitwise: bitwise OR of a and b (3 bits)
- out_or_logical: logical OR of a and b treated as a single entity, output is 1 if any bit in a or b is 1, else 0
- out_not: 6-bit output consisting of the bitwise NOT of b in bits [5:3], and bitwise NOT of a in bits [2:0]

The implementation will directly assign these outputs using Verilog operators.
