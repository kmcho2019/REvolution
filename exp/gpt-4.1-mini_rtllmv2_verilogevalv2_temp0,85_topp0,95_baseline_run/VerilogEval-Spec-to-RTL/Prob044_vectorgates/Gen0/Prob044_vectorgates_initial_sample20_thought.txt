Implement a module TopModule with two 3-bit inputs a and b. Compute the bitwise OR of a and b and assign it to out_or_bitwise. Compute the logical OR by checking if either a or b is non-zero and assign the result to out_or_logical. Compute the bitwise NOT of b and place it in the upper 3 bits of out_not and the bitwise NOT of a and place it in the lower 3 bits of out_not.
