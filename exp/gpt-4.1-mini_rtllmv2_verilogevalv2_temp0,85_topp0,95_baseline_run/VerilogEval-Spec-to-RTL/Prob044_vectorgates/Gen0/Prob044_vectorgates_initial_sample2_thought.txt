The module takes two 3-bit inputs `a` and `b`. It outputs the bitwise OR of `a` and `b` as a 3-bit vector `out_or_bitwise`. The logical OR of `a` and `b` is a single bit output `out_or_logical`. Since logical OR operates on entire vectors, we reduce `a` and `b` individually using OR-reduction to get single bits, then OR those to get the final logical OR. Finally, the output `out_not` is a 6-bit vector where the upper 3 bits [5:3] are the bitwise NOT of `b` and the lower 3 bits [2:0] are the bitwise NOT of `a`.
