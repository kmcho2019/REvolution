Implement a module named TopModule with two 3-bit inputs 'a' and 'b'.
- Compute bitwise OR of a and b and assign to out_or_bitwise (3 bits).
- Compute logical OR of a and b (treating each as a multi-bit vector, so logical OR of (a != 0) || (b != 0)) and assign to out_or_logical (1 bit).
- Compute bitwise NOT of b and place in bits [5:3] of out_not.
- Compute bitwise NOT of a and place in bits [2:0] of out_not.
