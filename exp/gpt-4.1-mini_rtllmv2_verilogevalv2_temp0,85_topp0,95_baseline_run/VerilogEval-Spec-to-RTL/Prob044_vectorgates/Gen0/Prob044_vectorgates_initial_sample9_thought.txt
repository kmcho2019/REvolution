The module takes two 3-bit inputs a and b. We compute the bitwise OR of a and b and assign it to out_or_bitwise. For the logical OR, we treat a and b as boolean values where any non-zero is true, so we use the logical OR operator (||) and assign the result to out_or_logical. For out_not, we concatenate the bitwise NOT of b (upper 3 bits) with the bitwise NOT of a (lower 3 bits).
