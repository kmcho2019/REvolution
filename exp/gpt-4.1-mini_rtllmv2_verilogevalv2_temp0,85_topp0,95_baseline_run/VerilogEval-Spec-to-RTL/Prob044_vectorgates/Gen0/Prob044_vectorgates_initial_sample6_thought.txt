Implement a module TopModule with two 3-bit inputs a and b.
- Compute bitwise OR of a and b for out_or_bitwise.
- Compute logical OR of a and b for out_or_logical; logical OR should return 1 if any bit in a or b is 1.
- Compute bitwise NOT of b and place it in the upper half (bits 5:3) of out_not.
- Compute bitwise NOT of a and place it in the lower half (bits 2:0) of out_not.
