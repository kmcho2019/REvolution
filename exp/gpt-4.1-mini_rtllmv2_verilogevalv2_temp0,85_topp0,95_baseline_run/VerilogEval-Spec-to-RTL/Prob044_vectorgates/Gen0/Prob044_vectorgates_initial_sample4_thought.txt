Implement a module TopModule with 3-bit inputs a and b. Compute out_or_bitwise as the bitwise OR of a and b. Compute out_or_logical as the logical OR of the entire 3-bit vectors a and b (i.e., output 1 if any bit in a or b is 1). Compute out_not as a 6-bit vector with bits [5:3] as the bitwise NOT of b and bits [2:0] as the bitwise NOT of a.
