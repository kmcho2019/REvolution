The module TopModule has two 3-bit inputs a and b. We need to produce three outputs: 
- out_or_bitwise: bitwise OR of a and b (3 bits)
- out_or_logical: logical OR of the entire a and b (single bit)
- out_not: 6 bits, where bits [5:3] is bitwise NOT of b, and bits [2:0] is bitwise NOT of a

We will use bitwise operators for the bitwise OR and NOT, and use reduction OR (|) operator on a and b to get single bit logical OR output. 
Then, concatenate the inverted b and a for out_not.
