The module samples the input 'a' on the rising edge of clk. When 'a' is high at the clock edge, the output q is set to 4 and remains there as long as 'a' stays high. When 'a' transitions to low at a clock edge, q starts incrementing by 1 at each subsequent rising clock edge, wrapping around from 6 back to 0. The output q is 3 bits wide, and the initial value is undefined (x).
