The circuit updates the 3-bit output q on the rising edge of clk. When input a is high, q is held at 4 continuously. When a goes low, q starts incrementing by 1 on each rising edge of clk, wrapping around after 7 (3-bit max). So: 
- If a=1, q=4
- If a=0, q increments on each rising clock edge modulo 8.
