From the waveform, q updates on the rising edge of clk. When a=1, q remains constant at 4. When a=0, q increments by 1 on each rising clk edge, wrapping from 7 back to 0. The initial value of q at time 0 is unknown (x), so we'll initialize q to 4 on reset (or at start). The module has no reset input, so we'll initialize q to 4 at declaration.

Implementation:
- q is a 3-bit register
- At every positive edge of clk:
  - If a==1, q <= 4 (fixed)
  - Else q <= q + 1 (mod 8)
