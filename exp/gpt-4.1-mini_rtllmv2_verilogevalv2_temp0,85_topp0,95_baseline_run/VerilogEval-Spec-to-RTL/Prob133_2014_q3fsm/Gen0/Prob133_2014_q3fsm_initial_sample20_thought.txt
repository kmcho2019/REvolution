Implement a FSM with three states: A (initial/reset), B (collecting w samples), and C (output z based on count). In state A, stay until s=1, then move to B. In B, collect w samples for 3 cycles, counting how many times w=1. After 3 cycles, move to C where z=1 if exactly two samples were 1, else z=0. In C, output z for one cycle and then return to B to start next 3-cycle collection. Use a 2-bit counter for cycles and a 2-bit counter for w count.
