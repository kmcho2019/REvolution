Design a FSM with states: A (initial), B0, B1, B2, and B3. 
- A: wait for s=1 to go to B0.
- B0-B2: count w=1 occurrences over three cycles, storing counts.
- B3: output z=1 if exactly two w=1's detected in last three cycles; else z=0.
After B3, go back to B0 to check next three cycles.
Use a 2-bit counter to track the number of w=1's.
Reset synchronously to A with z=0.
