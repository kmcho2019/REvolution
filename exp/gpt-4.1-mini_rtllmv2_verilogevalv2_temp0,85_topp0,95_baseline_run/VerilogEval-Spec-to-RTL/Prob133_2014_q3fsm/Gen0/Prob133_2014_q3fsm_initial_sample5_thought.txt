Implement a FSM with these states: A (initial/reset), B0, B1, B2, B3. 
- In state A, wait for s=1 to move to B0.
- In states B0 to B3, count the number of w=1s in three clock cycles:
  * B0: first cycle, record w
  * B1: second cycle, accumulate w
  * B2: third cycle, accumulate w
  * B3: evaluate if exactly two w=1s; output z=1 if yes, else 0; then go back to B0 for next window.
Use a 2-bit counter to count how many w=1s in the three cycles.
s input is ignored outside of A.
z is registered and synchronous, outputted in the cycle after the third w is observed.
Reset initializes FSM to A and clears counters and output.
