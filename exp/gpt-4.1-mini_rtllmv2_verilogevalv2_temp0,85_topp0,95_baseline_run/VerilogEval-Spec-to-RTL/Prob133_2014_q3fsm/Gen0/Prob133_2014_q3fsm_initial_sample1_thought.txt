Design a FSM with states representing A (waiting for s=1), and a counting phase that collects three w inputs, counts how many times w=1, and then outputs z=1 if exactly two out of three w inputs are 1. Since the counting is done over three cycles, we can implement a small counter (0 to 3) and a register to accumulate the number of times w=1. We can use a simple two-state FSM: state A (waiting for s=1), and state B (counting w inputs). During state B, we count cycles and accumulate the number of w=1 inputs. After three samples, output z accordingly for one cycle, then reset the counter and accumulator and continue counting the next three cycles. The output z is registered and updated after the three cycles. Reset synchronously brings the FSM back to state A, clears counters and output.
