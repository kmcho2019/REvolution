Implement a 4-state FSM: A (reset state), B0, B1, B2 for counting the three cycles of w input. In state A, wait for s=1 to move to B0. In states B0 to B2, count how many times w=1 in three cycles using a 2-bit counter. After three cycles, output z=1 if count of w=1 is exactly 2, else 0. Then repeat the counting cycle by returning to B0. Use synchronous reset and positive edge clock.
