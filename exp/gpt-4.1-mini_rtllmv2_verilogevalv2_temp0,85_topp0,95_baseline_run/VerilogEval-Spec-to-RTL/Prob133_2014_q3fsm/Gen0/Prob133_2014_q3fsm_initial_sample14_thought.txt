Implement a finite state machine with states: A (reset state), B0, B1, B2, B3. In state A, the FSM waits for s=1 to transition to B0. In states B0 to B3, the FSM counts the number of times w=1 in three consecutive cycles using a 2-bit counter. The states B0 to B3 represent counting cycles 0 to 3. On reaching B3 (end of 3 cycles), output z is asserted if exactly two of the three w inputs were 1; otherwise, z is 0. Then the FSM resets the count and continues cycling through B0 to B3 as long as s remains 1 (though s is not used in these states). Reset is synchronous active high, and all sequential logic triggers on the positive clock edge. This approach uses 4 states total, minimizing states and enabling counting via a counter variable.
