Implement a module with a D flip-flop that is clocked on the positive edge of clk. The D input is the XOR of 'in' and the current output 'out'. Since the output of the flip-flop is fed back into the XOR gate, this creates a toggle behavior when 'in' is 1. There is no reset, so the initial value of 'out' will be unknown ('x') unless explicitly initialized. The output 'out' is driven by the flip-flop.
