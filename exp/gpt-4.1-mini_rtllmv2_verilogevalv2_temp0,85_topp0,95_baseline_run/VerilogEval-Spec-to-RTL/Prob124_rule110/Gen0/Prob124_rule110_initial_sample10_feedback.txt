   bit left = (i == 511) ? 1'b0 : q[i+1];

   This matches the problem description, but the naming is counterintuitive and prone to confusion since 'left' is actually q[i+1] (cell to the right on a conventional horizontal array) and 'right' is q[i-1] (cell to the left). While logically consistent with the problem's indexing, this unconventional naming could lead to misunderstanding. However, this alone would not cause the large number of mismatches unless the logic is flawed elsewhere.
