Implement a 512-bit register `q` to hold the current state of the cellular automaton. On each positive clock edge, if `load` is high, load `data` directly into `q`. Otherwise, update each cell of `q` according to Rule 110 using its left (i+1), center (i), and right (i-1) neighbors. Boundary cells assume neighbors outside the range are zero. The next state is computed combinationally for all bits, then registered at the clock edge.
