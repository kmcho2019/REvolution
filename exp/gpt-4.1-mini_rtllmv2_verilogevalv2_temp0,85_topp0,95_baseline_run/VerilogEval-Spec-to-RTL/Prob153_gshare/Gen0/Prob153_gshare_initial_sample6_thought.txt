Implement a 7-bit global history register (GHR) and a 128-entry pattern history table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters. For prediction, XOR the 7-bit pc with the 7-bit GHR to index the PHT, output prediction and the history used for prediction. On predict_valid, update the GHR with predicted branch direction at next clock edge.

For training, if train_valid is high, update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history using the actual outcome (train_taken). If the branch was mispredicted (train_mispredicted), restore GHR to train_history at next clock edge. If training and prediction happen in the same cycle, training takes precedence on GHR update. The PHT update happens on clock edge.

Use asynchronous reset to clear GHR and initialize PHT entries to weakly not taken (2'b01).

The module thus maintains and updates GHR and PHT according to the given rules.
