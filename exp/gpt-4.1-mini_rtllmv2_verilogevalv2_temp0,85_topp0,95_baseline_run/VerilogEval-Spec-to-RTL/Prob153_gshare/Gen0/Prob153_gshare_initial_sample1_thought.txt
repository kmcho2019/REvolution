Implement a 7-bit global history register (GHR) updated on predictions and possibly restored on mispredictions during training. Use a 128-entry PHT (2-bit saturating counters) indexed by XOR of 7-bit PC and 7-bit GHR. Prediction interface uses current GHR and PC to read PHT and outputs prediction and history used. On prediction, update GHR with predicted direction at next clock edge. Training interface updates PHT entries based on actual outcome and misprediction status; if mispredicted, restore GHR to training history value. On simultaneous prediction and training, training takes priority for GHR update. PHT updates occur on clock edge, so prediction output reflects PHT before training update. Reset is asynchronous active-high, clearing GHR and setting PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10). Output predict_history is the GHR used for prediction (before update).
