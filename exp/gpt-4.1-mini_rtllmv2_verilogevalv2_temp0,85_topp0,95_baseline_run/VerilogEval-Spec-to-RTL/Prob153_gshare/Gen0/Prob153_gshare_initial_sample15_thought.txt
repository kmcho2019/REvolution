Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT (2-bit saturating counters). The index is computed by XORing the 7-bit PC and the 7-bit history. Prediction interface: on predict_valid, output the prediction and the history used for prediction; also update a "next_history" register to be committed on the next clock edge if no misprediction training has higher priority. Training interface: on train_valid, update the PHT entry according to actual outcome; if mispredicted, recover the global history register to the training branch's history plus its actual outcome (append train_taken), discarding any speculative history updates from younger branches. On simultaneous predict and train with misprediction, training takes precedence, so update the global history with training recovery, ignoring prediction update. The PHT is read combinationally to allow prediction to see the state before training updates at the clock edge. Asynchronous active-high reset clears the global history and PHT entries to weakly taken (e.g., 2'b10). Outputs reflect combinational reads. This design handles the priority and timing constraints specified.
