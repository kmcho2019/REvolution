Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of PC and history. Prediction interface: when predict_valid=1, output prediction (taken if counter MSB=1) and current history, then update history with predicted outcome on next clock edge unless a training misprediction takes precedence. Training interface: when train_valid=1, update the PHT entry based on actual outcome; if mispredicted, restore history to train_history (state after mispredicted branch). If both train (with misprediction) and predict occur simultaneously, training takes precedence in updating history. Prediction reads PHT synchronously (combinational), reflecting state before training update in the same cycle. Asynchronous active-high reset initializes history and PHT to weakly taken (2'b10). All updates happen on posedge clk.
