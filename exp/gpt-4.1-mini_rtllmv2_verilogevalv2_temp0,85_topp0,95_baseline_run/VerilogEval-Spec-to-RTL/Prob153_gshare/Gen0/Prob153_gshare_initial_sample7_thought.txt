Implement a gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters. Prediction uses xor of pc and global history as index. On predict_valid, output prediction and the history used, and update global history with predicted direction on next clock. On train_valid, update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc xor train_history with actual outcome. If mispredicted, restore global history to train_history to recover from misprediction. If train_valid and predict_valid both set, train takes precedence for global history update. Use asynchronous active-high reset to initialize global history and PHT entries to weakly taken (2). The prediction output is taken if counter MSB is 1. Prediction history output is the current global history before update.
