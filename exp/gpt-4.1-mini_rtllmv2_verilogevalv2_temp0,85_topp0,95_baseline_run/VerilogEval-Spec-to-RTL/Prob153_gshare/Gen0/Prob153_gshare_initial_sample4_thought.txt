Implement a 128-entry PHT with 2-bit saturating counters, indexed by gshare: XOR of 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history. Maintain a 7-bit global history register. 

Prediction interface:
- When predict_valid is high, compute index = predict_pc ^ global_history.
- Output predicted taken (MSB of 2-bit counter) and the current global history.
- On next clock edge, update global history by shifting in predicted taken, unless a training mispredict occurs.

Training interface:
- When train_valid is high, update PHT at index = train_pc ^ train_history according to train_taken.
- If train_mispredicted is asserted, restore global history to train_history (the state after the mispredicted branch) on next clock edge.
- If both train_mispredicted and predict_valid occur same cycle, training takes precedence in updating global history.

Handle asynchronous reset by clearing global history and initializing PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10).

Ensure prediction reads current PHT state before training updates it at clock edge.
