Implement a 64-bit register that loads data synchronously when load is high.
When load is low and ena is high, shift the register contents according to the amount control:
- For left shifts (00 and 01), shift in zeros from the right.
- For right shifts (10 and 11), perform arithmetic right shifts, i.e., replicate the MSB on the left.
The shift amounts are either 1 or 8 bits, depending on amount.
Output the current register value on q.
