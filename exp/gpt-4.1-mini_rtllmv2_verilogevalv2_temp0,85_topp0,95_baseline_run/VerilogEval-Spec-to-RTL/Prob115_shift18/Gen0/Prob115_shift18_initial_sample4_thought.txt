Implement a 64-bit register that on every rising clock edge either loads the input data if load is high or shifts the current register value if ena is high and load is low. The shift direction and amount are controlled by the 2-bit amount input. For left shifts, zeros are shifted in from the right. For right shifts, the arithmetic shift replicates the sign bit (MSB) when shifting right. Shifts by 1 or 8 bits are supported according to the amount input. If neither load nor ena is high, the register retains its value.
