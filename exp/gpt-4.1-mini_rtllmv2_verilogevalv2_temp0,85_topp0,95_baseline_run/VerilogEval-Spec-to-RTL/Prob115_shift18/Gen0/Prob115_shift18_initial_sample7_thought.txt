Implement a 64-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk. On load high, the register loads the input data directly. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift the register contents according to amount:
- 00: shift left by 1 bit, zero-fill LSB
- 01: shift left by 8 bits, zero-fill LSBs
- 10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit, replicate MSB into vacated bit
- 11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits, replicate MSB into vacated bits

The output q reflects the current register content.
