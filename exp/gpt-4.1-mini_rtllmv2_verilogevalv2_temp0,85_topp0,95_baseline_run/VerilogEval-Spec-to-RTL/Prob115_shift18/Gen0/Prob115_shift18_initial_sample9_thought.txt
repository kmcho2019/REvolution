Implement a 64-bit register that loads data synchronously on load=1. When load=0 and ena=1, shift the register contents according to the amount input: left shift by 1 or 8 bits, or arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits. The arithmetic right shift should replicate the sign bit (bit 63) for the vacated bits. When ena=0 and load=0, hold the current value.
