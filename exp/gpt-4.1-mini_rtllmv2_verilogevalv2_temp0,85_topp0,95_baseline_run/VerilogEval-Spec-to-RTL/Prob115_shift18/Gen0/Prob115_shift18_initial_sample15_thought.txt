Implement a synchronous 64-bit shift register with a load input. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load the 64-bit data into the register. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift the register based on amount. 
amount specifies direction and shift amount: 00=shift left by 1, 01=shift left by 8, 10=shift right arithmetic by 1, 11=shift right arithmetic by 8. 
For left shifts, fill shifted-in bits with 0. For arithmetic right shifts, fill with sign bit (MSB). Output the current register content on q.
