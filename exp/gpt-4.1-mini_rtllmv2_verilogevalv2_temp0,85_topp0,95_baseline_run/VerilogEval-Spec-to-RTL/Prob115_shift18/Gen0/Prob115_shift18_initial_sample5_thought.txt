Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load data into q. Else if ena is high, shift q according to amount:
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit, fill with 0 on the right
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits, fill with 0 on the right
- 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit, fill with sign bit on left
- 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits, fill with sign bits on left
Maintain the output q as the current register value.
