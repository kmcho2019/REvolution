Implement a 64-bit register that on each clock edge either loads the input data (when load is high) or shifts its current contents (when ena is high and load is low). The shift amount and direction is controlled by the 2-bit amount input. For left shifts, zeroes are shifted in from the right. For right shifts, perform arithmetic right shift by replicating the MSB (sign bit) for the shifted-in bits. If ena is low and load is low, maintain current state.
