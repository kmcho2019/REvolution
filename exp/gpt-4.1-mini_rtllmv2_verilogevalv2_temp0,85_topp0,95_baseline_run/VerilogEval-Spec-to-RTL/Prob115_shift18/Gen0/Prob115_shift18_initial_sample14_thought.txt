Implement a 64-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk. If load is high, synchronously load data into q. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift q according to amount: shift left by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b00 and 2'b01 respectively, or arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b10 and 2'b11 respectively. For arithmetic right shift, replicate the MSB to fill shifted-in bits.
