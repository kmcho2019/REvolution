Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load on clk. On each clock edge, if load is high, load data into the register. If load is low and ena is high, shift the register according to amount:
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit, fill LSB with 0
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits, fill lower 8 bits with 0
- 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit, fill MSB with original MSB (sign extension)
- 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits, fill upper 8 bits with original MSB (sign extension)
Output q is the current register value.
