Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and enable signals. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load data into the register. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift according to amount: shift left by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b00 and 2'b01, and shift right by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b10 and 2'b11. For right shifts, perform arithmetic right shift, i.e., replicate the sign bit during shifting. Output q is always the current register content.
