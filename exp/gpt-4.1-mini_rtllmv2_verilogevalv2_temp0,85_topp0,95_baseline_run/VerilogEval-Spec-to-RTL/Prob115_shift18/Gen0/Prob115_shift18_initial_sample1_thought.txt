Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift enable. On each clock edge, if load is high, load the 64-bit data into the register. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift the register contents either left or right depending on amount. For left shifts, shift in zeros from the right. For right shifts, perform arithmetic right shifts, i.e., fill the vacated bits on the left with the original MSB bit. The amount signal determines the shift direction and the shift amount (1 or 8 bits). If neither load nor ena is asserted, retain the current value.
