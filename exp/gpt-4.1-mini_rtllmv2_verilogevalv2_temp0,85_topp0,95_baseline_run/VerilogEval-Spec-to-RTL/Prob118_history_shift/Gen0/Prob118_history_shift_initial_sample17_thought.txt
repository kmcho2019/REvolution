Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold global branch history. On async positive edge of areset, reset history to zero. On every posedge clk, if train_mispredicted is asserted, update history with {train_history, train_taken} (train_taken is the youngest bit). Else if predict_valid is asserted, shift history right by one and insert predict_taken at LSB. Output current history on predict_history.
