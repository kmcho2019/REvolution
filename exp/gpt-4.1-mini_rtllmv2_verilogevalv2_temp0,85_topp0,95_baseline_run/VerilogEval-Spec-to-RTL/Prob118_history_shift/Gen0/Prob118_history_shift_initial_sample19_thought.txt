Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the global branch history. On a positive clock edge, if areset is asserted asynchronously, reset history to zero. Otherwise, if a misprediction (train_mispredicted=1) occurs, load the history with {train_history, train_taken} (train_history concatenated with train_taken as the LSB). If no misprediction but a prediction is valid (predict_valid=1), shift the history left by one and insert predict_taken at LSB. The misprediction takes precedence over prediction. The predict_history output reflects the current history register. The shift register's youngest bit is predict_history[0].
