Implement a 32-bit shift register updated on the positive edge of clk. On areset, reset to zero asynchronously. On each clock edge, if train_mispredicted=1, load predict_history with {train_history[31:0], train_taken}, shifting train_taken into LSB side. Else if predict_valid=1, shift in predict_taken into LSB (shift right by 1, insert predict_taken at bit 0). predict_history output is the current register value. Misprediction update takes precedence over prediction update.
