Implement a 32-bit shift register representing the global branch history. The register is asynchronously reset by areset to 0. On each clock edge, if train_mispredicted is high, update the register with {train_history, train_taken} (train_history shifted left by 1 plus train_taken) to rollback the history due to pipeline flush. Otherwise, if predict_valid is high (and no misprediction), shift in predict_taken at LSB side to update predicted history. predict_history output is the current value of the history register. Misprediction takes precedence over prediction.
