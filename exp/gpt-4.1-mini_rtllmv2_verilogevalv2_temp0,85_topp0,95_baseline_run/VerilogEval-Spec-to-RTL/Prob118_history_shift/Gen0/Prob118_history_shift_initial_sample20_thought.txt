Implement a 32-bit shift register for the branch history, updated on the positive clock edge.
On asynchronous positive edge areset, reset history to 0.
On clock edge, if train_mispredicted is asserted, load the history with {train_history, train_taken} (train_history is bits [31:1], train_taken is bit 0).
Else if predict_valid is asserted, shift history left by 1 and insert predict_taken at LSB.
Otherwise, retain current history.
Output predict_history is the current history.
