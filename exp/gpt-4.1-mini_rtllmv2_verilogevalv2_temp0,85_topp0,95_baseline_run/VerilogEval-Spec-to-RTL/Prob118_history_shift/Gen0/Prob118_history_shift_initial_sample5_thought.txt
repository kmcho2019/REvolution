Implement a 32-bit register to hold the global branch history. On the positive edge of clk, asynchronously reset the register to zero if areset is asserted. Otherwise, if a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted=1), update the history with the concatenation of train_history and train_taken (train_history is the older 31 bits, train_taken is the newest bit). This takes precedence over prediction updates. If no misprediction but a prediction is valid (predict_valid=1), shift the current history left by one and insert predict_taken into LSB. Output the current history as predict_history.
