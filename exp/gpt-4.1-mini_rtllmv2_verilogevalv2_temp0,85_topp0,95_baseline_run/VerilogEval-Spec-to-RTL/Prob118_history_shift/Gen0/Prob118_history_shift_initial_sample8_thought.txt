Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the global branch history. On the positive clock edge, check for asynchronous reset first and clear the register to zero if active. Otherwise, if a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted=1), load the history with the concatenation of train_history and train_taken. If no misprediction but a prediction is valid, shift in predict_taken into the LSB side of the history register, pushing the older bits towards MSB. Output the current history register as predict_history. The misprediction update takes priority over the prediction update.
