Implement a 32-bit shift register updated on the positive clock edge. On async positive edge of areset, reset history to zero. On each clock, if train_mispredicted is asserted, load history with {train_history, train_taken}. Else if predict_valid is asserted, shift history right by 1 bit and insert predict_taken at LSB. Output predict_history is the current history register. Misprediction update takes precedence over prediction update.
