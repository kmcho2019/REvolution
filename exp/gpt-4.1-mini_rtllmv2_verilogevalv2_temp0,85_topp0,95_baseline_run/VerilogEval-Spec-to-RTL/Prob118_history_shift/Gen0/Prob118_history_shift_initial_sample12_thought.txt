Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the global branch history. On the positive edge of clk, if areset is asserted, reset the register to zero. Otherwise, if train_mispredicted is asserted, load the register with train_history concatenated with train_taken, where train_history occupies the upper 31 bits and train_taken the LSB. If train_mispredicted is not asserted and predict_valid is asserted, shift the register right by one bit and shift in predict_taken into bit 0. Output predict_history is the current state of the shift register. Since misprediction takes precedence over prediction, the corresponding logic is prioritized accordingly.
