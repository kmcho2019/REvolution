Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold global branch history. On reset (asynchronous, posedge), clear the register to zero. On clock rising edge, if a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted=1), load the history register with {train_history, train_taken} concatenated to form the new 32-bit history (train_taken is the youngest branch, so it should be the LSB). Otherwise, if predict_valid=1, shift the register left by 1 bit and insert predict_taken at LSB. If neither condition applies, hold the current history. The output predict_history is assigned from the history register. Misprediction takes priority over prediction when both occur simultaneously.
