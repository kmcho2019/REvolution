Implement a 32-bit shift register for the global history, updated on the positive clock edge. On async positive edge reset, clear the history to zero. On each clock cycle:
- If train_mispredicted is high, load predict_history with train_history concatenated with train_taken (train_history[31:0] and train_taken as LSB).
- Else if predict_valid is high, shift predict_taken into the LSB side of the history.
- Else keep history unchanged.
Output predict_history reflects the current history register.
