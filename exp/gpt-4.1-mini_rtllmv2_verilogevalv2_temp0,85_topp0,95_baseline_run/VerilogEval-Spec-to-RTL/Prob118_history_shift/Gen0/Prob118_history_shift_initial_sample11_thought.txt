Implement a 32-bit global history shift register updated on the positive edge of clk. On asynchronous positive edge of areset, reset the history to zero. When train_mispredicted is asserted, update the history with {train_history, train_taken} (train_history is 31 bits of older history, train_taken the latest actual branch outcome), taking priority over predict_valid. Otherwise, if predict_valid is asserted, shift the current history left by 1 and insert predict_taken at LSB. Output predict_history always reflects the current history register.
