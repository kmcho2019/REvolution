The output q is updated on the rising edge of clk. From the waveform, q changes only at clk posedge and depends on the input 'a' at that edge. Observing the waveform: when a=0 at clk posedge, q=1; when a=1 at clk posedge, q=0. Initially q is unknown. So q is the inverted value of 'a' sampled at the rising edge of clk.
