The output q is updated on the positive clock edge. From the waveform, q only changes on rising clock edges. Initially, q is unknown (x). On the first rising edge when a=0, q becomes 1 and stays 1 on subsequent rising edges while a=0. When a=1 on a rising edge, q becomes 0 and stays 0 on subsequent rising edges while a=1.

Thus, q follows the negation of a on each rising edge of clk, effectively q <= ~a at each positive clock edge.
