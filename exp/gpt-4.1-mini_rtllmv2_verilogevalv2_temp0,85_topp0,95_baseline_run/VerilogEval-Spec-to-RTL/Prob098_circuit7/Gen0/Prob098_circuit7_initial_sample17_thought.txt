The output q is a one-bit register updated on the positive edge of clk. Observing the waveform, q changes only on rising clock edges and depends on the input a at that time. 

From time 15ns (clk rising edge), q=1 when a=0; at 45ns (clk rising edge), q=0 when a=1. 

Detailed observation:
- At clk rising edges, if a=0, q becomes 1.
- If a=1, q becomes 0.

So the behavior is q <= ~a at the positive edge of clk.
