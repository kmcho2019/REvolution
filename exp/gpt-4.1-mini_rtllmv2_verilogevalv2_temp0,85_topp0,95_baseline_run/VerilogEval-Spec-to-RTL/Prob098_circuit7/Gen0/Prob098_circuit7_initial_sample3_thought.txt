The output q is a state stored in a flip-flop updated on the positive edge of clk. Based on the waveform, q initializes to 0 (unknown at time 0). At the 1 edges of clk (5ns, 15ns, 25ns, ...), q changes only when a is 1; specifically, when a is 1, q toggles (inverts), and when a is 0, q holds its value. This means q_next = q ^ a at each posedge clk.
