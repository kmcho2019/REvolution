The output q is updated on the positive clock edge. From the waveform, q starts undefined and then takes the value 1 when a is 0 at the clock edge, and stays 1 while a remains 0 at the clock edges. When a becomes 1 at a clock edge, q becomes 0 and remains 0 as long as a stays 1. Thus, q is the inverse of the input a, sampled synchronously on the positive edge of the clock. Implement q as a D flip-flop with input ~a.
