The output q updates only on the positive edge of clk. From the waveform, q becomes 1 when a=0 at posedge clk and stays 1 as long as a=0 at the clk edges. Once a=1 at a posedge clk, q becomes 0 and remains 0 on subsequent clock edges. This behavior matches a latch that is set to 1 when a=0 and reset to 0 when a=1 on the clock edge. In other words, q = ~a sampled on posedge clk. Implement q as a flip-flop that captures the inverted input a on each posedge clk.
