The output q is updated only on the positive clock edge. From the waveform, q becomes 1 at time 15ns when clk rises and a=0, and remains 1 for all subsequent clk rising edges while a=0. At time 45ns when clk rises and a=1, q changes to 0 and remains 0 for all subsequent clk rising edges with a=1. Thus, q is a stored state that updates to the inverse of a on each rising clk edge, i.e., q <= ~a at each positive clk edge.
