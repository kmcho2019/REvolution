The output q is updated only on the positive clock edge. From the waveform:

- At 15ns (posedge clk, a=0), q becomes 1
- At 35ns (posedge clk, a=1), q remains 1
- At 45ns (posedge clk, a=1), q becomes 0

Analyzing changes:

- When a=0 at posedge clk, q is set to 1 and remains 1.
- When a=1 at posedge clk, q is set to 0 and remains 0.

Thus, q is the inverse of a sampled at the positive clock edge (q <= ~a). Implement a positive edge triggered flip-flop that samples the inverse of a.
