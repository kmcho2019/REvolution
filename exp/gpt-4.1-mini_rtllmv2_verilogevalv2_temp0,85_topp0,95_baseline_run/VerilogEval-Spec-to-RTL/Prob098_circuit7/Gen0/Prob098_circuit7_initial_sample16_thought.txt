The output q is updated on the positive edge of clk. From the waveform:
- Initially q is unknown.
- When clk rises with a=0, q becomes 1 and stays 1 as long as a=0 on subsequent clock edges.
- When a changes to 1 at a rising edge of clk, q becomes 0 and stays 0 as long as a=1.
So q reflects the inverse of a sampled at the rising edge of clk.
