The output q updates only on the positive edge of clk. Observing the waveform, q holds its previous value when a=0 at clk rising edge, and toggles or resets when a=1. Checking transitions:

- At time 15ns (clk rising edge), a=0, q=1 (previous q was undefined, so assume it initializes to 0 or unknown, but q becomes 1 when a=0).
- At time 35ns (clk rising edge), a=1, q remains 1.
- At time 45ns (clk rising edge), a=1, q becomes 0.
- Subsequent rising edges with a=1 keep q=0.

Notice from 15ns to 35ns with a=0, q=1. At 35ns with a=1, q=1, then at 45ns with a=1, q=0, and stays 0 with a=1.

It looks like:

- When a=0 at clk rising edge, q sets to 1.
- When a=1 at clk rising edge, q clears to 0.

Thus, q behaves like a latch that sets q=1 when a=0 and resets q=0 when a=1 on rising clk edge.

We can implement this behavior as a synchronous set/reset flip-flop:

On posedge clk:
  if a == 0: q <= 1
  else: q <= 0

This matches the waveform, including initial x values at time 0.
