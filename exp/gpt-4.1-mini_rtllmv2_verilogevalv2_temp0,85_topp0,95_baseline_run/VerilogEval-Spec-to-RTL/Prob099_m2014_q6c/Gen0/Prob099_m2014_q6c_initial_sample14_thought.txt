We have a one-hot encoded state machine with states A-F encoded on y[5:0].
The transitions depend on input w (the 0/1 on transitions is w).
We want to derive logic equations for the next-state signals Y1 and Y3
corresponding to next state bits y[1] and y[3] respectively.
From the given transitions, identify which states transition to y[1] (B) and y[3] (D) on given inputs.
Then write the next-state equations for Y1 (next y[1]) and Y3 (next y[3]) as combinational logic from current y and w.
Finally, implement the module with inputs y[5:0], w and outputs Y1 and Y3, computing Y1 and Y3 as next-state bits.
