Here, OFF is a typedef enum constant of type logic [0:0], but 1'b0 is a simple 1-bit literal. Some Verilog simulators require explicit casting when assigning between different types, especially SystemVerilog enums and logic vectors, and do not automatically treat enum constants as compatible with plain logic literals.

Additionally, the declaration of state and next_state as variables of type state_t (a typedef enum logic [0:0]) introduces type-checking complications in simulators that expect classic Verilog rather than full SystemVerilog support.
