The module will take a 32-bit input vector and reverse the order of its bytes. Since each byte is 8 bits, the byte at positions [31:24] should move to [7:0], the byte at [23:16] should move to [15:8], the byte at [15:8] should move to [23:16], and the byte at [7:0] should move to [31:24]. This can be achieved by concatenating the bytes in reverse order.
