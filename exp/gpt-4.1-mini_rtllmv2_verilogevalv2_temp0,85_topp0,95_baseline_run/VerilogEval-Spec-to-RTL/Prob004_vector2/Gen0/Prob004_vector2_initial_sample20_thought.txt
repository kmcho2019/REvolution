To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit input vector, we need to rearrange the bytes such that the least significant byte becomes the most significant byte, the second least significant byte becomes the second most significant byte, and so forth. Specifically, for the 32-bit input consisting of bytes B0 (bits 7:0), B1 (bits 15:8), B2 (bits 23:16), and B3 (bits 31:24), the output should be concatenated as {B0, B1, B2, B3} reversed to {B3, B2, B1, B0}.
