Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are asserted according to the current walking state; aaah is asserted in FALLING. On async reset, go to WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, transition to FALLING regardless of bumps. While FALLING, bumps don't affect direction or state. When ground returns to 1, resume walking in the direction prior to falling. When in WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT and bumped on either side (or both), switch walking direction. Use a register to store the walking direction before falling so it can be restored after falling ends. Implement async reset and synchronous state transitions on posedge clk.
