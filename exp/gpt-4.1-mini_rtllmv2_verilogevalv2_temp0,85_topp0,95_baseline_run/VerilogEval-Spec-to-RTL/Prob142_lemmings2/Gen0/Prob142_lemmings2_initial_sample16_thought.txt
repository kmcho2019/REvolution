Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs depend only on the current state. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. When on ground (ground=1), if bumped on either side, switch direction accordingly. If ground=0, transition to FALLING and output aaah=1. While FALLING, ignore bumps and wait for ground=1 to resume walking in the previous direction.
