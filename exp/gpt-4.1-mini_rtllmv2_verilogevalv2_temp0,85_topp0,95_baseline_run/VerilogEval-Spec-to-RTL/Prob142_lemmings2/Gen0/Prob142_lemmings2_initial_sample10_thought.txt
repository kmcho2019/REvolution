Implement a Moore finite state machine with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. When on the ground (ground=1), the Lemming walks either left or right. If bumped on left or right (or both) while walking, it switches direction. If ground goes low, transition to FALLING state, but retain the direction prior to falling in a register. While falling, the Lemming outputs aaah=1, and walk_left and walk_right are 0. When ground goes high again, return to the walking state with the saved direction. Bumps during falling or during transition don't affect direction.
