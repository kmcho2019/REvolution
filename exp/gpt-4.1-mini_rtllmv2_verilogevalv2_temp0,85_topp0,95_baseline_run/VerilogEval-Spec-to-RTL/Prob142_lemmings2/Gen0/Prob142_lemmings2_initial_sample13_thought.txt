Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the FSM starts in WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, FSM transitions to FALLING state regardless of bumps. While falling, bumps do not affect direction. When ground returns to 1, FSM transitions back to the previous walking state before falling. When walking, if bumped on either side (or both), the FSM switches direction. Outputs walk_left and walk_right are asserted according to the walking states; aaah is asserted only in FALLING state. Asynchronous reset sets FSM to WALK_LEFT state.
