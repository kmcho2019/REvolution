Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. 
- On reset, go to WALK_LEFT.
- When walking left or right:
  - If ground is 0, transition to FALLING.
  - If bumped on left or right (or both), switch direction accordingly.
- When falling:
  - If ground becomes 1, return to the previous walking direction.
  - Bumps during falling do not affect direction or cause transitions.
Output signals:
- walk_left is 1 in WALK_LEFT state,
- walk_right is 1 in WALK_RIGHT state,
- aaah is 1 in FALLING state.
Store previous walking direction in a register to resume after falling.
