Model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with three states:
- WALK_LEFT: walk_left=1, walk_right=0, aaah=0
- WALK_RIGHT: walk_left=0, walk_right=1, aaah=0
- FALLING: walk_left and walk_right retain their last walking direction, aaah=1

On reset, state is WALK_LEFT.
Transitions:
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT:
  - If ground=0, move to FALLING (retain direction).
  - Else if bumped (bump_left or bump_right is 1), switch walking direction.
  - Else remain in current walking state.
- From FALLING:
  - If ground=1, return to previous walking direction state.
  - Else remain in FALLING.

Bumps during FALLING do not affect direction.
Bumps occurring in same cycle as ground disappearance (when leaving walking states) do not affect direction, as the transition to FALLING occurs immediately.

Implement with asynchronous posedge reset, and synchronous posedge clock FSM logic.

Store walking direction in state encoding. During FALLING, output walk signals based on stored walking direction and aaah=1.
