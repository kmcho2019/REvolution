Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right indicate walking direction states only, and aaah is asserted only when in FALLING state.

- On async reset, go to WALK_LEFT.
- While walking:
  - If ground=0, transition to FALLING.
  - If bump_left=1 (or bump_left and bump_right both 1), transition to WALK_RIGHT.
  - If bump_right=1, transition to WALK_LEFT.
- While falling:
  - Stay in FALLING while ground=0.
  - When ground=1, return to previous walking direction state (stored in a register).
- Bumps have no effect during falling or on ground transitions involving falling.
- The FSM is Moore, so outputs are based on current state.
- Use asynchronous reset.

States encoding:
- 2'b00: WALK_LEFT
- 2'b01: WALK_RIGHT
- 2'b10: FALLING

Store previous walking direction to recover after falling.
