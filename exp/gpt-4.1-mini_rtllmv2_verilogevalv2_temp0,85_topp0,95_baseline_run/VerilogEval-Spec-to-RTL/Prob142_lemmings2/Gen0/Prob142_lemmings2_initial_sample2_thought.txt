Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the FSM starts in WALK_LEFT. While walking, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted, the FSM switches walking direction accordingly, including when both bumps are asserted. If ground goes low, the FSM transitions to FALLING state, asserting aaah=1 and no walking signals. While falling, bump inputs do not affect direction. When ground goes back high, the FSM returns to the previous walking state it was in before falling, resuming that walking direction.
