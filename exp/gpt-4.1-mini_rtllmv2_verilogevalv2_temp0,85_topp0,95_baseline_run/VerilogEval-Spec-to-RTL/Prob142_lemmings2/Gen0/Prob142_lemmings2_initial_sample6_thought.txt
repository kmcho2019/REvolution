Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the Lemming starts walking left. When on the ground and bumped (left or right or both), the Lemming switches walking direction. When ground goes low, transition to FALLING state, asserting aaah=1. While falling, bumps do not affect direction. When ground goes high again, return to the previous walking state (left or right). The output signals walk_left, walk_right, and aaah depend only on the current state (Moore machine).
