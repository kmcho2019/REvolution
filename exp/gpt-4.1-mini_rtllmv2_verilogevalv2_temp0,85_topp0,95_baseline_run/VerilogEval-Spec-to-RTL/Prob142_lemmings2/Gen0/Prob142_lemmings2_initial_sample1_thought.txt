Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. While on ground, the Lemming walks either left or right. If bumped on left, transition to WALK_RIGHT; if bumped on right, transition to WALK_LEFT; if bumped on both sides, also switch direction. When ground goes low, transition to FALLING, remembering the last walking direction. While falling, ignore bumps and assert aaah output. When ground returns high, transition back to the previous walking state. Outputs depend solely on the current state.
