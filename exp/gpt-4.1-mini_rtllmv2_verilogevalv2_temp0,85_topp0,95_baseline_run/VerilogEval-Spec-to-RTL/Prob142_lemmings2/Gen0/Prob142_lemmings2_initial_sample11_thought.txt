Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING.
- On asynchronous reset (posedge areset), go to WALK_LEFT.
- When ground=0, transition to FALLING state and assert aaah=1.
- When ground=1 again, return to the previous walking state (the one before FALLING).
- When in walking states and bumped on left, go to WALK_RIGHT.
- When in walking states and bumped on right, go to WALK_LEFT.
- If bumped on both sides, switch direction.
- Being bumped during falling or at the moment of ground disappearing/appearing does not affect the walking direction or state transitions.
- Outputs (walk_left, walk_right, aaah) are determined only by the state (Moore).
