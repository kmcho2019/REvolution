Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. When on ground (ground=1), the Lemming is either walking left or right. If bumped on left or right (or both), the Lemming switches walking direction. When ground goes to 0, the FSM transitions to FALLING state, outputting aaah=1. While falling, bumps are ignored and the direction is preserved. When ground returns to 1, the FSM returns to the previously stored walking direction state. Outputs are determined solely by the current state: walk_left=1 if WALK_LEFT, walk_right=1 if WALK_RIGHT, aaah=1 if FALLING.
