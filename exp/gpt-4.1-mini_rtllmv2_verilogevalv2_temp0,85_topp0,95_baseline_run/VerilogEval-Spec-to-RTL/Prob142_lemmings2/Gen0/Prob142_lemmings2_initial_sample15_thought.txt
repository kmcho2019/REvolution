Model the Lemming as a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. 
- Initially, the Lemming walks left.
- If bumped on the left, switch to walking right.
- If bumped on the right, switch to walking left.
- If bumped on both sides, switch direction regardless.
- If ground goes low, switch to FALLING state and output aaah=1.
- While falling, bumps do not change walking direction.
- When ground returns high, return to the previous walking state.
- Asynchronous active high reset sets state to WALK_LEFT.
Outputs depend solely on the current state.
