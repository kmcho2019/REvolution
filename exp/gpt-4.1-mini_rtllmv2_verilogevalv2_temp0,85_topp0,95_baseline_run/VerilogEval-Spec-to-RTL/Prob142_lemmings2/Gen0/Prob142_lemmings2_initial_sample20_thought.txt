Model the Lemming with a Moore FSM having three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the Lemming starts in WALK_LEFT. When on the ground (ground=1), the Lemming walks either left or right. If bumped on left or right (or both), it switches walking direction. If ground=0, it transitions to FALLING state and asserts aaah=1. While falling, bumps do not affect direction. When ground returns to 1, transition back to the previous walking direction state and deassert aaah. Use an asynchronous positive edge reset to initialize state to WALK_LEFT.
