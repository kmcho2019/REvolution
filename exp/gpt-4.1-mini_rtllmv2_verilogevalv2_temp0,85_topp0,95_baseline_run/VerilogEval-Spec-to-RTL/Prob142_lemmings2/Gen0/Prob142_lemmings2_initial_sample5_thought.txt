Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right correspond to the walking states, and aaah corresponds to the falling state. The FSM asynchronously resets to WALK_LEFT on areset. On each clock edge, if not falling (ground=1), check bump inputs to switch direction. If ground=0, transition to FALLING state and keep track of the previous walking direction to restore it once ground returns to 1. Bumps during falling or ground transition do not affect direction.
