Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. While on the ground (ground=1), the Lemming walks left or right. If bumped on the left, it moves to WALK_RIGHT; if bumped on the right, it moves to WALK_LEFT; if both bumps, also switches direction. When ground goes to 0, transition to FALLING state, output aaah=1, and keep previous walking direction stored. While falling, bumps are ignored. When ground goes back to 1, return to the stored walking direction state. areset asynchronously resets to WALK_LEFT. Outputs depend only on state (Moore machine).
