Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right correspond to the walking states, and aaah is asserted in the FALLING state. The machine resets asynchronously to WALK_LEFT. 

Transitions:
- From WALK_LEFT: if ground=0, transition to FALLING; else if bumped on left or right, switch direction to WALK_RIGHT.
- From WALK_RIGHT: if ground=0, transition to FALLING; else if bumped on left or right, switch direction to WALK_LEFT.
- From FALLING: if ground=1, return to the previous walking direction; otherwise remain in FALLING.
- Bumps during FALLING do not affect direction or state.

Direction changes do not occur during transitions to or from FALLING, and bumps during ground changes do not affect direction. We store the walking direction in a reg to remember it when falling.

Asynchronous reset puts FSM into WALK_LEFT state.
