Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are asserted only in the WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states respectively. The aaah output is asserted only in the FALLING state. On asynchronous reset, the FSM goes to WALK_LEFT. When ground is 0, transition to FALLING state, preserving the last walking direction internally. When ground becomes 1 again, return to the preserved walking state. Bumps only affect direction when in walking states and only if ground=1; bumps during falling or during transitions to/from falling do not affect direction.
