The code implements a Moore FSM with three states (WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING), and stored direction before falling in saved_dir. The asynchronous reset and state register logic is mostly correct, resetting to WALK_LEFT and saving WALK_LEFT direction initially.

However, the simulation log reports 154 mismatches on walk_left and walk_right signals starting early (time 270) and no mismatches on aaah, implying the FALLING state transitions and outputs are working correctly, but the walking direction updates are not matching expected behavior.

Examining the next state logic in detail reveals issues:

1. **Direction reversal on bump while walking**:  
   - For WALK_LEFT state, bump_left or bump_right causes transition to WALK_RIGHT.
   - For WALK_RIGHT state, bump_left or bump_right causes transition to WALK_LEFT.  
   This matches the spec that any bump (left, right, or both) causes direction change.

2. **Missing handling of simultaneous bumps**:  
   The problem states a bump on both sides simultaneously also causes direction change. The code uses `(bump_left || bump_right)`, which covers that correctly.

3. **Updating saved_dir only on walking states and ground high**:  
   The saved_dir register updates only when `ground` is high and the next_state is a walking state (`WALK_LEFT` or `WALK_RIGHT`). This is intended to remember the walking direction before falling.

4. **Potential issue: saved_dir updated too early or with incorrect next_state**:  
   Because saved_dir updates within the sequential block on every clock edge when conditions are met, if bumps occur in the same cycle that the Lemming transitions between walking states, saved_dir could be updated with a direction that does not reflect the pre-fall walking direction consistently.

5. **Effect on direction restoration after falling**:  
   The FSM resumes walking in the saved_dir state after falling when `ground` becomes 1. If saved_dir was incorrectly updated during bumps or near falling transitions, the FSM would restore the wrong direction after a fall.

6. **No separation of saved_dir updates and state updates**:  
   In the sequential block, the `state` register updates first, then saved_dir updates if conditions hold. Since saved_dir updates based on `next_state` (combinational), not the current state, it can cause saved_dir to be set to a direction that has not stabilized or is in transition, violating the problem's requirement that bumps during falling or ground transitions do not affect direction.

7. **Bumps affecting direction during ground loss or regain cycles**:  
   The problem specifies that bumps during falling, during ground disappearance cycle, or during ground reappearance cycle should not affect direction. The code's combinational next state logic may cause direction flips in these edge cycles if bump signals are present, because bump signals are considered immediately in the next_state logic.

Therefore, the primary cause of mismatches in walk_left and walk_right outputs appears to be the incorrect updating of walking direction (`saved_dir`) in timing or conditions that allow bump signals to improperly affect the walking direction state, violating the problem's requirement to ignore bumps during falling and ground transition cycles.

In summary, the code's handling of saved_dir and bump inputs around ground transitions and falling states is flawed, causing the walking direction outputs to mismatch expected simulation results.
