From the waveform, analyze the relationship between inputs x, y and output z.
z=1 except in these cases: (x=1,y=0), (x=0,y=1).
And when (x=1,y=1), z=1.
The only time z=0 is when exactly one of x or y is 1, the other is 0.

Thus, z = NOT(x XOR y) = x XNOR y

So the module outputs 1 when x and y are equal, else 0.
