From the waveform, analyze the output z with respect to inputs x and y:
- z is 1 when both x and y are 0 (time 0-20ns, 55ns)
- z is 0 when either x=1,y=0 or x=0,y=1 (except when both are 1)
- z is 1 when both x and y are 1 (45ns, 70ns)

This can be summarized as:
z = 1 if (x AND y) OR (NOT x AND NOT y)
z = (x & y) | (~x & ~y)

This is an XNOR function of x and y.

Implement z as the XNOR of x and y.
