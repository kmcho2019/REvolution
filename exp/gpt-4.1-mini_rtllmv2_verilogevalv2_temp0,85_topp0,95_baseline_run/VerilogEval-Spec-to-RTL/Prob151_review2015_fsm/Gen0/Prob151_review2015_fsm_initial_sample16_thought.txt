Implement a finite state machine with the following states:
- IDLE: searching for the pattern 1101 on the serial data input.
- SHIFT: after detecting the pattern, assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles to shift in the next 4 bits.
- COUNT: assert counting, wait for done_counting to go high.
- DONE: assert done, wait for ack to go high before returning to IDLE.

Detect pattern 1101 by shifting in data bits into a 4-bit shift register each clock. When the register equals 4'b1101, move to SHIFT state.

Use a 2-bit counter in SHIFT to count 4 cycles of shift_ena.

All state transitions and outputs are synchronous with clk, with active high synchronous reset to IDLE.
