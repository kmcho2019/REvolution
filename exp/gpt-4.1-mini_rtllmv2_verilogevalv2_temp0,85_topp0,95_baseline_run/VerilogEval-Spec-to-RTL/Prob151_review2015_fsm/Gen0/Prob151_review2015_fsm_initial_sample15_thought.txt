Implement an FSM with states: IDLE (searching for pattern), SHIFT (shifting in 4 bits), COUNT (waiting for done_counting), DONE (assert done and wait for ack). Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101. On detection, move to SHIFT state and assert shift_ena for 4 cycles (using a counter). Then move to COUNT state, assert counting, wait for done_counting input. Once done_counting is high, move to DONE state, assert done, wait for ack input. On ack, return to IDLE. Synchronous reset sets state to IDLE and clears outputs.
