The FSM has the following states: IDLE (searching for pattern), SHIFT (shifting in 4 bits), COUNT (waiting for done_counting), DONE (assert done and wait for ack). In IDLE, it looks for the pattern 1101 by shifting in bits serially. Once detected, move to SHIFT state asserting shift_ena for 4 cycles. Then go to COUNT asserting counting until done_counting is high. Then assert done and wait for ack. On synchronous reset, go to IDLE.
