Implement an FSM with states: IDLE (search for pattern), SHIFT (shift in 4 bits with shift_ena), COUNTING (wait for done_counting), DONE (assert done and wait for ack). Detect pattern 1101 by shifting in data bits and checking last 4 bits. On reset, go to IDLE. On detecting pattern, move to SHIFT and assert shift_ena for 4 cycles. Then go to COUNTING and assert counting until done_counting is high. Then go to DONE, assert done, wait for ack to return to IDLE.
