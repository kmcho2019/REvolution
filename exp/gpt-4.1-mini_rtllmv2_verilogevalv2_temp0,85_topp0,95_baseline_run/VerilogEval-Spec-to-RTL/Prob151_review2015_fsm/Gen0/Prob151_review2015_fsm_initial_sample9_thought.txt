Implement a synchronous FSM with the following states:
- IDLE: looking for the pattern 1101 on the serial 'data' input. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern.
- SHIFT: once pattern detected, assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles to shift in 4 more bits.
- COUNTING: after shifting 4 bits, assert counting output and wait for done_counting signal.
- DONE: once done_counting is high, assert done output and wait for ack.
- After ack is high, return to IDLE state.

The FSM is reset synchronously on reset. Outputs are asserted in corresponding states.
