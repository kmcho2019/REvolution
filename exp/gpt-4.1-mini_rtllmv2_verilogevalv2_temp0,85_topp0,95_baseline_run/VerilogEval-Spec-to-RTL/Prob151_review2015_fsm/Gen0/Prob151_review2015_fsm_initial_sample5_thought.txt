Implement a finite state machine with the following states:
- IDLE: searching for the 1101 pattern on the serial data input.
- SHIFT: after detecting the pattern, enable shifting by asserting shift_ena for 4 clock cycles.
- COUNT: after shifting, assert counting output and wait until done_counting is asserted.
- DONE: assert done output and wait until ack is asserted.
On reset, return to IDLE state. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101 on data input.
Use a 2-bit counter to count the 4 shift cycles.
Outputs shift_ena, counting, and done are asserted only in their respective states.
