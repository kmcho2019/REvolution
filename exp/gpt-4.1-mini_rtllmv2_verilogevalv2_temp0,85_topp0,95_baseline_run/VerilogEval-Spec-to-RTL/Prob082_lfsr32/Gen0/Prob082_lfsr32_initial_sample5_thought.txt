Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1. Since bit positions start from 1 at the MSB, this corresponds to q[31], q[21], q[1], and q[0] in zero-based indexing. On each clock edge, if reset is high, q is set to 32'h1; otherwise, the LFSR shifts right by one bit. The feedback bit is the XOR of q[0] with the tapped bits. In a Galois LFSR, bits with taps XOR their next value with q[0], while bits without taps just shift the previous bit. Implement this logic synchronously on the positive clock edge.
