Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1. The LFSR shifts right on every positive clock edge. The feedback bit is the XOR of the LSB (q[0]) and the tap bits. Specifically, if q[0] is 1, then the tapped bits get XORed with q[0] to form the next value; if q[0] is 0, the bits simply shift right without XOR. On synchronous active-high reset, q is set to 32'h1. The module updates q on every positive clock edge.
