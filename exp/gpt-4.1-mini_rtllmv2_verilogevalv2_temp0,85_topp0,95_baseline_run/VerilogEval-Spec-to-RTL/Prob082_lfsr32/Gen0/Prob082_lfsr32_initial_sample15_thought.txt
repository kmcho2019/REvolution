Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. The feedback bit is q[0], and taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 means we XOR q[0] with those tapped bits when computing the next state for the corresponding bits. Since it's a Galois LFSR, the XOR only affects tapped bits, others just shift right. On reset (active high synchronous), q is set to 32'h1.
