Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based index). In a Galois LFSR, the feedback bit is the output bit q[0]. For each tapped bit, if the output bit is 1, the tapped bit gets XORed with 1 (i.e., flipped) during the shift. The shift is to the right. Implement synchronous reset to 32'h1 on the positive clock edge.
