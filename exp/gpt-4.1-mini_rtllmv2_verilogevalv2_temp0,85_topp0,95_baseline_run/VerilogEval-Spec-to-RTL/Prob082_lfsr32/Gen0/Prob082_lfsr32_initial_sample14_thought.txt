Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. Use synchronous active high reset to initialize q to 1. For the Galois LFSR, taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1 mean we XOR the LSB (q[0]) with these tapped bits to create the feedback applied during the shift. In a Galois LFSR shifting right, if the output bit (q[0]) is 1, the tapped bits get XORed with it before shifting; if 0, the register just shifts right without XOR. The taps are usually applied conditionally based on q[0]. The LSB output bit q[0] is used to conditionally XOR taps during the shift.
