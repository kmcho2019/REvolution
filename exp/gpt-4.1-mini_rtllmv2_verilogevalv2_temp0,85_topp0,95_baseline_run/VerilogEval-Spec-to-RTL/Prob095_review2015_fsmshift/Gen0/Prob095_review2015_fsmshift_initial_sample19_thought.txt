Implement a synchronous FSM with a counter that enables shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. On reset (active high synchronous), the counter is loaded with 4 and shift_ena is asserted. Each clock cycle, the counter decrements until it reaches zero, after which shift_ena is deasserted and remains low until the next reset. Since the problem states "whenever the proper bit pattern is detected" but gives no input or pattern details, we only implement the reset behavior as described.
