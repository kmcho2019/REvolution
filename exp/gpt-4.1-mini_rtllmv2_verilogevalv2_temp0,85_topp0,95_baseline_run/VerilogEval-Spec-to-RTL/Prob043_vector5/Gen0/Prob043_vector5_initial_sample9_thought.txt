The output is a 25-bit vector representing the pairwise equality comparisons of the inputs a, b, c, d, e with each other. The order of bits in the output vector is from (a vs a) at bit 24 down to (e vs e) at bit 0, scanning in row-major order over a 5x5 matrix of comparisons. Each bit is 1 if the two bits compared are equal, which can be implemented as the XNOR of the two bits.
