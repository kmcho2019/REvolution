The goal is to generate a 25-bit output vector representing the equality of each pair of bits from inputs a, b, c, d, and e. Each bit in the output corresponds to one comparison. The order is such that the highest index (24) corresponds to comparing a with a, then a with b, a with c, etc., down to the lowest index (0) comparing e with e. To compare if two bits x and y are equal, we use the XNOR operation (x ~^ y). We'll generate all 25 comparisons in a nested fashion and concatenate the results accordingly.
