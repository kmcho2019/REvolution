Implement 16 D flip-flops that update on the positive edge of clk. Use synchronous, active-low reset to clear all flip-flops to 0. Control writing to each byte with byteena bits: if byteena[1] is high, update upper byte q[15:8] with d[15:8], else keep previous value; similarly for lower byte q[7:0] with byteena[0]. On resetn low, synchronously clear all q to 0.
