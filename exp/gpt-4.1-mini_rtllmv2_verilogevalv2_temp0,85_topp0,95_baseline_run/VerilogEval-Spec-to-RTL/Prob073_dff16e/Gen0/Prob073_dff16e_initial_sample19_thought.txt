Implement 16 D flip-flops updated on the positive edge of clk. On each clock, if resetn is low (synchronous reset), q is cleared to 0. Otherwise, update q selectively based on the byteena bits: if byteena[1] is set, update the upper byte q[15:8] with d[15:8]; if byteena[0] is set, update the lower byte q[7:0] with d[7:0]. If a byteena bit is clear, keep the corresponding byte of q unchanged.
