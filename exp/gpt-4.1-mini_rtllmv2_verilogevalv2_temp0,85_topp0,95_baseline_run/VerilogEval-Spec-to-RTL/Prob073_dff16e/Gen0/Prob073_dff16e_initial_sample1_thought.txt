Implement a synchronous active-low reset and clocked D flip-flops for a 16-bit register. Use byteena[1] to enable writing the upper byte (bits 15:8) and byteena[0] to enable writing the lower byte (bits 7:0). On the positive edge of clk, if resetn is low, reset all bits to 0. Otherwise, update only the bytes enabled by byteena, preserving the other bytes.
