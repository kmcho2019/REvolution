Implement a Moore FSM that counts consecutive 1's in the input stream. The FSM states represent how many consecutive 1's have been seen so far (0 to 6). When a zero is received, the FSM resets to state 0. If after seeing 5 consecutive 1's a zero is received, assert 'disc' on the next cycle to discard that zero. If after seeing 6 consecutive 1's a zero is received, assert 'flag' on the next cycle to indicate frame boundary. If a 1 is received in state 6 (thus 7 consecutive 1's), assert 'err' on the next cycle. The FSM transitions and outputs are synchronous, and outputs are asserted for one clock cycle after detecting the condition.
