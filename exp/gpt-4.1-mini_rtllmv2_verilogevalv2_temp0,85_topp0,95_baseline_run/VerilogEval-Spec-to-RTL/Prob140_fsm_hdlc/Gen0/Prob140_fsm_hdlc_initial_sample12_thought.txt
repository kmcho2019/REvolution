Implement a Moore FSM with states representing how many consecutive 1s have been seen since last zero. The FSM counts 1s from 0 up to 6+. When seeing a 0 after 5 consecutive 1s, the FSM outputs disc on the next cycle. When seeing a 0 after 6 consecutive 1s, the FSM outputs flag on the next cycle. If 7 or more consecutive 1s are seen (i.e., input=1 in state 6), output err on the next cycle. The FSM resets to state 0 on synchronous reset. Outputs disc, flag, and err are registered signals asserted for one full clock cycle following the detection of respective conditions.
