Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q as four 4-bit fields. On each clock edge, increment the least significant digit. When a digit reaches 9 and is incremented, reset it to 0 and enable increment for the next digit. The ena output signals indicate when the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits should increment, i.e., when the previous digit rolls over from 9 to 0. Provide synchronous reset to clear all digits and disable ena signals.
