Implement a 4-digit BCD counter using a 16-bit register for the digits. The least significant digit (ones) counts from 0 to 9 and rolls over to 0 when incremented. When the ones digit rolls over, enable ena[0] goes high for one clock cycle, indicating the tens digit should increment. Similarly, when the tens digit rolls over, ena[1] goes high to increment the hundreds digit, and when the hundreds digit rolls over, ena[2] goes high to increment the thousands digit. The reset synchronously sets the counter to 0. The ena output is 3 bits, with ena[0] for incrementing the tens digit, ena[1] for hundreds, and ena[2] for thousands.
