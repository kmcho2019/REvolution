Implement a 4-digit BCD counter that increments the ones digit on every clock cycle. When a digit rolls over from 9 to 0, the next digit should increment on the next clock cycle, indicated by the enable signals for digits [3:1]. Use synchronous active-high reset to clear the counter and disable all enables. The output ena[2:0] correspond to digits 1 to 3 (tens to thousands) enable signals. The counter increments on each clock edge, and enables are asserted for one cycle when the lower digit rolls over.
