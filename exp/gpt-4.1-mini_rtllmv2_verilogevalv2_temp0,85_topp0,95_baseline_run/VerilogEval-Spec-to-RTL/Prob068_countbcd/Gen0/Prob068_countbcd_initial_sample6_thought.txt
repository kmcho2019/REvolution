Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q[15:0], each digit 4 bits. On every clock edge, increment the ones digit; if it rolls over from 9 to 0, enable the tens digit increment (ena[0]); similarly, carry over to hundreds (ena[1]) and thousands (ena[2]). Output ena signals as one-hot enables for incrementing digits 1 to 3. Synchronous active-high reset clears all digits to 0. Use a sequential always block triggered on posedge clk for counting and reset.
