Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with each digit stored in 4 bits within q. On each clock tick, increment the ones digit. When it rolls over from 9 to 0, enable and increment the tens digit, and similarly propagate carries to hundreds and thousands digits. The ena output is a 3-bit signal indicating when to increment digits 1 (tens), 2 (hundreds), and 3 (thousands). Reset synchronously sets q and ena to zero.
