Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with each digit stored in 4 bits within a 16-bit register q. The least significant digit (ones) increments every clock cycle unless reset. For each digit, if the lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0, generate an enable signal to increment the next digit. Outputs ena[2:0] correspond to the enable signals for digits 1 (tens), 2 (hundreds), and 3 (thousands). The counting is synchronous and reset is active-high, also synchronous.
