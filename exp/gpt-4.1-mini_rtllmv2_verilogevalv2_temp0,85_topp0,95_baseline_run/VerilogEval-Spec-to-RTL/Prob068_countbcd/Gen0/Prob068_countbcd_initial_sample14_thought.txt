Implement a 4-digit BCD counter where each digit is 4 bits. The ones digit increments every clock cycle. When it rolls over from 9 to 0, enable the tens digit to increment on the next clock. Similarly, when the tens digit rolls over from 9 to 0, enable the hundreds digit to increment, and likewise for the thousands digit. The ena signals indicate when each upper digit should be incremented (ena[0] for tens, ena[1] for hundreds, ena[2] for thousands). Use synchronous active-high reset to clear the counter.
