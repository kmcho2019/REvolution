Implement a synchronous FSM with states IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. In IDLE, discard bytes until in[3] is 1, then latch byte as byte1 and move to BYTE2. In BYTE2, latch byte as byte2 and move to BYTE3. In BYTE3, latch byte as byte3 and move to IDLE, asserting done signal for one cycle and outputting the concatenated bytes as out_bytes. Synchronous reset clears state and outputs.
