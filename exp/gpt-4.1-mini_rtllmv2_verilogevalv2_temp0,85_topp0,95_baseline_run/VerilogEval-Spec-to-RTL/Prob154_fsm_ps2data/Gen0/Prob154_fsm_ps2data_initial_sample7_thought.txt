Implement a FSM with states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. In IDLE state, discard bytes until in[3]=1 (indicating start of message). Then store byte as first byte and move to BYTE2, store second byte and move to BYTE3, store third byte and assert done output for one cycle while outputting concatenated 3-byte message on out_bytes. After that go back to IDLE and repeat. Use synchronous reset to clear state and outputs.
