Implement a synchronous FSM with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2. 
In IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is received, then move to BYTE1. 
In BYTE1 and BYTE2 states, collect the incoming bytes into a shift register to build the 3-byte message.
After receiving the third byte, assert done for one cycle and output the collected 24-bit message. 
Then return to IDLE to look for the next message. 
Reset is synchronous active-high and clears state and outputs.
