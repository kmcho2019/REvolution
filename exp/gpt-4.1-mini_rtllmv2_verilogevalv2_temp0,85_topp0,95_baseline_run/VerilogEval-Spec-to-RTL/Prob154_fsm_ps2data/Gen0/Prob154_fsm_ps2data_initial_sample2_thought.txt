Implement a 2-state FSM: IDLE and COLLECT. In IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3] = 1 is received, then capture it as the first byte of the message and move to COLLECT state. In COLLECT state, collect the next two bytes and then assert done signal for one cycle while outputting the assembled 24-bit message (first byte in bits 23:16, second in 15:8, third in 7:0). Use a byte counter to track how many bytes have been collected. Reset synchronously resets the FSM and data registers.
