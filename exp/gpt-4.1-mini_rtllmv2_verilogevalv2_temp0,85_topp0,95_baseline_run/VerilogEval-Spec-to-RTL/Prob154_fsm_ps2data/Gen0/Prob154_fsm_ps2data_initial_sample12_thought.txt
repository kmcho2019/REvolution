Implement a 2-state FSM: IDLE and COLLECT. In IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3] = 1 is seen, then transition to COLLECT. In COLLECT, collect 3 bytes (including the first one that triggered the transition). After the 3rd byte, assert done for one cycle, output the 24-bit concatenated message (first byte at [23:16], second at [15:8], third at [7:0]), then return to IDLE. Use synchronous reset to clear state and outputs. done is asserted only for one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is received.
