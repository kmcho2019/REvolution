Implement an FSM with three states: IDLE, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In IDLE, wait for a byte with bit 3 set (in[3]==1), then capture it as the first byte. Transition to BYTE2 state, capture the next byte as the second byte. Transition to BYTE3, capture the third byte, then output the combined 24-bit message with done=1 for one cycle. After signaling done, return to IDLE to look for the next message. Use synchronous reset to reset state and outputs. The output message is arranged as {byte1, byte2, byte3} with byte1 in [23:16], byte2 in [15:8], byte3 in [7:0].
