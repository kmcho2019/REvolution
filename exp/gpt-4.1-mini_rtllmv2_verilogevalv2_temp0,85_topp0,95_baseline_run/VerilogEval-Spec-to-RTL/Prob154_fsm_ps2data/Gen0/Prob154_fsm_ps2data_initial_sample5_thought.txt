Implement an FSM with four states: IDLE (discard bytes until in[3]=1), BYTE1 (capture first byte), BYTE2 (capture second byte), BYTE3 (capture third byte and signal done). After done is asserted for one cycle, return to IDLE. The 24-bit output concatenates the three bytes with the first byte in the highest byte, second in the middle, third in the lowest byte. The reset is synchronous active high. The done signal is asserted for exactly one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is received. Outputs are registered.
