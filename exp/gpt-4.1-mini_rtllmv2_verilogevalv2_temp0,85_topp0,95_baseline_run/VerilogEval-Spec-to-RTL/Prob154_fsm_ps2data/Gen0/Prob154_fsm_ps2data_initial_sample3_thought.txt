We design a finite state machine (FSM) with three states to collect 3-byte messages from a byte stream. The FSM discards bytes until it finds a byte with in[3]=1, marking the start of a message (byte 1). It then collects the next two bytes. Upon receiving the third byte, it asserts done and outputs the 24-bit message in out_bytes, formatted with the first byte in the most significant 8 bits. The reset is synchronous active-high. The done signal is asserted for one cycle immediately after the third byte is received and the message is assembled.
