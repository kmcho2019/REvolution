Implement a FSM with three states: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3.
- In IDLE, wait for a byte with in[3] = 1 (indicating start of message).
- When such byte is received, latch it as byte 1, transition to BYTE2.
- In BYTE2, latch second byte, transition to BYTE3.
- In BYTE3, latch third byte, assert done for one cycle, then return to IDLE.
out_bytes will be a concatenation of the three latched bytes.
Use synchronous reset active high.
done asserted for one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is latched.
