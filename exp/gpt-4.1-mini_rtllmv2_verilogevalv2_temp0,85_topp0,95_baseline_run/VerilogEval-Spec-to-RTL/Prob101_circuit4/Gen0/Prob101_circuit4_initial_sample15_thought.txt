The output q is 1 whenever c=1 or b=1, except when a=1 and b=0 and c=0; in that case q=0 regardless of d. This means q = (b | c) & ~(a & ~b & ~c). Simplifying, q= (b | c) | (a & b) since if b=1, q=1; if c=1, q=1; if a=1 and b=0 and c=0, q=0; otherwise q=0. Another way to express it is q = (b | c) | (a & b) but since (a & b) is already included in b=1, this term is redundant. Therefore q = b | c. Checking the waveform confirms q=1 whenever b=1 or c=1. The only exception is when b=0 and c=0, q=0 regardless of a and d. So the simplest implementation is q = b | c.
