From the waveform, q is 1 whenever c=1 or b=1, except when a=0 and b=0 and c=0 (q=0 regardless of d). 
Also, d does not affect q when a=0 and b=0 and c=0 or when a=1. 
Effectively, q is 1 if c=1 or b=1, regardless of d, or if a=1 and (b=1 or c=1). 
Simplifying, q=1 if (b OR c) is 1; when both b and c are 0, q is 0.
Therefore, q = b OR c.
