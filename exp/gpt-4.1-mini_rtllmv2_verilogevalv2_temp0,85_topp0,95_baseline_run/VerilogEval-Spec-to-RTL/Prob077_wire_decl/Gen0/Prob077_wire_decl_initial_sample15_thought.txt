Implement the TopModule with inputs a, b, c, d and outputs out and out_n.
Create two intermediate wires to hold the outputs of the two AND gates:
- wire_and1 = a & b
- wire_and2 = c & d
Then OR the two wires into out:
- out = wire_and1 | wire_and2
Finally, out_n is the inversion of out:
- out_n = ~out
