To implement a 4-bit wide, 256-to-1 multiplexer, the input is a 1024-bit vector composed of 256 groups of 4 bits. The 8-bit selector selects which group of 4 bits to output. Since each group is 4 bits, the selection index should be multiplied by 4 to get the starting bit index. The output is the 4 bits starting at in[sel*4 +: 4].
