For out_both, each bit except for the MSB (bit 99) checks if the bit and its left neighbor are both 1. For bit i (0 to 98), out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1]. out_both[99] = 0.

For out_any, each bit except the LSB (bit 0) checks if the bit or its right neighbor is 1. For bit i (1 to 99), out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1]. out_any[0] = 0.

For out_different, each bit compares in[i] with its left neighbor with wrap-around. For bit i, left neighbor is (i+1) mod 100. So, out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1)%100].
