To generate the outputs:
- out_both[i] = 1 if in[i] and in[i+1] are both 1, for i=0 to 98; out_both[99] = 0.
- out_any[i] = 1 if in[i] or in[i-1] is 1, for i=1 to 99; out_any[0] = 0.
- out_different[i] = 1 if in[i] is different from its left neighbor, with wrapping, i.e. for i=0 to 99, left neighbor is in[(i+1)%100].

We will handle edge cases as specified and implement these combinationally.
