For out_both: For bits 0 to 98, out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1]. For bit 99, out_both[99] = 0.

For out_any: For bits 1 to 99, out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1]. For bit 0, out_any[0] = 0.

For out_different: For all bits 0 to 99, out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1) mod 100], which means the vector is treated circularly with the left neighbor of in[99] being in[0].
