Implement the three output vectors by comparing bits of the input vector as specified:
- out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1], for i=0 to 98, and out_both[99] = 0.
- out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1], for i=1 to 99, and out_any[0] = 0.
- out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1) mod 100], wrapping around so that index 99's neighbor is 0.
