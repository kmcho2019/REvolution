- Rows: x[3]x[2]
- Columns: x[1]x[0]

          00  01  11  10  (x[1]x[0])
    00 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |  (x[3]x[2] = 00)
    01 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |  (x[3]x[2] = 01)
    11 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |  (x[3]x[2] = 11)
    10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |  (x[3]x[2] = 10)

We will write the output f as a sum of minterms where the output is 1. Implement a combinational logic assigning f = 1 for those minterms and 0 otherwise.
