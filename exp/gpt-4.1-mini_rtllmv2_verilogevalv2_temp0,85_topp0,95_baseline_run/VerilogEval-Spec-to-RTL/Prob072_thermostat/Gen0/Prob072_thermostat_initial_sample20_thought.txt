The TopModule should check the mode input to decide between heating and cooling. In heating mode (mode=1), turn on the heater if too_cold is asserted and keep the aircon off. In cooling mode (mode=0), turn on the aircon if too_hot is asserted and keep the heater off. The fan should be on if the heater or aircon is on, or if fan_on is asserted by the user. The outputs are combinational based on inputs.
