The TopModule implements a simple thermostat control logic. When in heating mode (mode=1), if too_cold is asserted, heater turns on and the fan turns on to circulate air. Air conditioner is always off in heating mode. When in cooling mode (mode=0), if too_hot is asserted, aircon turns on and fan turns on. Heater is always off in cooling mode. Additionally, if the user requests fan_on, the fan is turned on regardless of heater or aircon states.
