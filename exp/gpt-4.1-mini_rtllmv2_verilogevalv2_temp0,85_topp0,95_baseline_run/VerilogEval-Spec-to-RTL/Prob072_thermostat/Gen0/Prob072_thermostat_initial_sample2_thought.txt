The thermostat controller operates in two modes: heating (mode=1) and cooling (mode=0). In heating mode, the heater is on if too_cold is asserted; the air conditioner is off. In cooling mode, the air conditioner is on if too_hot is asserted; the heater is off. The fan is on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests the fan (fan_on=1). This logic can be implemented combinationally.
