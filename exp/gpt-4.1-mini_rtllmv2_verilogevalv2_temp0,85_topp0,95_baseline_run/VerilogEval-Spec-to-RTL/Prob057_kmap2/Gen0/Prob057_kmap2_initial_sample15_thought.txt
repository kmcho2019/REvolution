The Karnaugh map is a 4-variable map with variables c and d as row indices and a and b as column indices. I will decode the map into its minterms and then derive the boolean expression for the output. Alternatively, I can directly implement the logic by decoding the K-map.

Mapping the K-map:

cd\ab | 00 | 01 | 11 | 10
-------------------------
 00   |  1 |  1 |  0 |  1
 01   |  1 |  0 |  0 |  1
 11   |  0 |  1 |  1 |  1
 10   |  1 |  1 |  0 |  0

Variables: a,b,c,d (all one bit)

First, let's write down the minterms where output is 1:

1) cd=00 (c=0,d=0)
   ab=00( a=0,b=0): 1 -> m0
   ab=01( a=0,b=1): 1 -> m1
   ab=10( a=1,b=0): 1 -> m2
2) cd=01 (c=0,d=1)
   ab=00( a=0,b=0): 1 -> m4
   ab=10( a=1,b=0): 1 -> m6
3) cd=11 (c=1,d=1)
   ab=01( a=0,b=1): 1 -> m13
   ab=11( a=1,b=1): 1 -> m15
   ab=10( a=1,b=0): 1 -> m14
4) cd=10 (c=1,d=0)
   ab=00( a=0,b=0): 1 -> m8
   ab=01( a=0,b=1): 1 -> m9

So minterms: 0,1,2,4,6,8,9,13,14,15

Let's write minterms in decimal:

m0: a=0,b=0,c=0,d=0 -> decimal 0
m1: a=0,b=1,c=0,d=0 -> decimal 1
m2: a=1,b=0,c=0,d=0 -> decimal 2
m4: a=0,b=0,c=0,d=1 -> decimal 4
m6: a=1,b=0,c=0,d=1 -> decimal 6
m8: a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0 -> decimal 8
m9: a=0,b=1,c=1,d=0 -> decimal 9
m13:a=0,b=1,c=1,d=1 -> decimal 13
m14:a=1,b=0,c=1,d=1 -> decimal 14
m15:a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1 -> decimal 15

Now, to write the boolean expression in sum of minterms:

out = Σ m(0,1,2,4,6,8,9,13,14,15)

We can implement this by creating a 4-input lookup using the inputs a,b,c,d with suitable logic or using the minterm sum.

Alternatively, a more compact form can be obtained by grouping in K-map, but since the problem didn't require simplification, I will directly implement the sum of minterms by writing the minterm expressions and OR them.

Each minterm is:

m0: !a & !b & !c & !d
m1: !a & b & !c & !d
m2: a & !b & !c & !d
m4: !a & !b & !c & d
m6: a & !b & !c & d
m8: !a & !b & c & !d
m9: !a & b & c & !d
m13: !a & b & c & d
m14: a & !b & c & d
m15: a & b & c & d
