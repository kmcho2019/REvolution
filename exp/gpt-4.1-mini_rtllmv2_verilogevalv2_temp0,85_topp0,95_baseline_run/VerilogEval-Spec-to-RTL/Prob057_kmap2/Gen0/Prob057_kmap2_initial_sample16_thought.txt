The Karnaugh map is a 4-variable map with variables a and b on columns, and c and d on rows. The order for ab is 00, 01, 11, 10 and for cd is 00, 01, 11, 10, indicating Gray code ordering. We will implement the function by writing the minterms corresponding to the cells where the output is 1. The minterms are the combination of variables a,b,c,d with appropriate complements according to the cell coordinates. Finally, we can OR all minterms to get the output.
