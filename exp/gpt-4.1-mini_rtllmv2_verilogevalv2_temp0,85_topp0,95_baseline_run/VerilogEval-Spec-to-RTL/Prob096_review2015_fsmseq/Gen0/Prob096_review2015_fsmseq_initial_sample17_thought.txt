Implement a synchronous FSM that detects the sequence 1101 on the input data stream. 
The FSM has states representing how much of the sequence has been matched so far:
- S0: no match yet
- S1: matched '1'
- S2: matched '11'
- S3: matched '110'

When the last bit '1' in the sequence is matched (transition from S3 on data=1), assert start_shifting to 1. 
Once asserted, start_shifting remains 1 forever until reset is asserted synchronously on clock edge.

Reset synchronously brings FSM back to initial state and clears start_shifting.
