Implement a finite-state machine (FSM) that detects the bit sequence 1101 on the input data stream. The FSM will have states representing how much of the sequence has been matched so far. When the sequence 1101 is detected, the output start_shifting is set to 1 and remains 1 until reset is asserted. The reset is synchronous and active high. The FSM transitions happen on the positive edge of clk.
