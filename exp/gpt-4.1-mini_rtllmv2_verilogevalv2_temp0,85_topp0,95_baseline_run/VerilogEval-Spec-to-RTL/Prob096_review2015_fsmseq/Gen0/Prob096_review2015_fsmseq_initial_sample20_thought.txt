Implement a finite state machine (FSM) to detect the sequence 1101 in the input bit stream. The FSM will have states representing partial matches of the sequence. On detecting the full sequence 1101, the output start_shifting will be set to 1 and remain asserted until a synchronous reset is asserted. The FSM transitions and output logic will be updated on the positive edge of clk, and reset will synchronously reset the FSM and clear start_shifting.
