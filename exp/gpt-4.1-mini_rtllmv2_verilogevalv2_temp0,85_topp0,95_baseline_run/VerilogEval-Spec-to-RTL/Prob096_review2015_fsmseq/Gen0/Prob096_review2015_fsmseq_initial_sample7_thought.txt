Implement a synchronous FSM that searches for the sequence "1101" in the serial input 'data'. Use a set of states to track how much of the sequence has been matched so far. When the full sequence is matched, set the output start_shifting to 1 and keep it high until a synchronous reset occurs. The FSM transitions and output logic will be updated on the positive clock edge, with synchronous active-high reset clearing the FSM and output.
