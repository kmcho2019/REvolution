Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (indexing bits 4 and 2 in zero-based Verilog). The feedback is the XOR of these tap bits with the LSB before shifting. On reset, the register is set to 1. The output q is the current state of the LFSR.
