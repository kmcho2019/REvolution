Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing), which correspond to q[4] and q[2] in 0-based indexing. The feedback bit is the XOR of q[0] with these tapped bits, which is used to update the appropriate bits according to the Galois LFSR structure. On synchronous active-high reset, the register is set to 1. The output q is the current state of the LFSR.
