Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (indexing bits from 1 to 5). The shift register shifts right each clock cycle. The input bit to the highest bit (bit 4 in zero-based indexing) is computed by XORing the LSB (q[0]) with q[2] (the tap at position 3). Other bits shift right; bits with taps are XORed with LSB before shifting in. On synchronous reset (active high), q is set to 1. Use positive edge-triggered always block.
