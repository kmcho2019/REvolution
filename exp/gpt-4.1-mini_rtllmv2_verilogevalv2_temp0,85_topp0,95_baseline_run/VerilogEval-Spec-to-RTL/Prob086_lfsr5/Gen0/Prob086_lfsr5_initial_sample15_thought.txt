Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing from MSB). The feedback comes from XORing the output bit (q[0]) with the tapped bits to generate the next state bits. Since this is a Galois LFSR, the taps affect the bits during the shift. We will use synchronous reset to set q to 1 on reset. The shift direction is right, so q shifts towards q[0]. The maximal length polynomial for 5 bits with taps at 5 and 3 is x^5 + x^3 + 1.
