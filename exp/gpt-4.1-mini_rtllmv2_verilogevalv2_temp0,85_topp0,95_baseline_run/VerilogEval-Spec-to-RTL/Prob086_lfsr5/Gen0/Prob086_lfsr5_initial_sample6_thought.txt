Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3 (indexing bits from 1 for clarity).
In 0-based indexing (Verilog), bit 4 and bit 2 are tapped.
The LFSR shifts right each clock cycle.
The feedback bit is the LSB before shift.
For each tapped bit, the new bit is old_bit XOR feedback.
For non-tapped bits, the new bit is the previous bit shifted in.
The reset synchronously sets the register to 1.
