Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 means taps at q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing. On each clock edge, the register shifts right by one bit, and for the tapped bits, their next values are XORed with the current LSB (q[0]). The synchronous reset sets q to 1 (00001). The LFSR operates on the positive edge of clk.
