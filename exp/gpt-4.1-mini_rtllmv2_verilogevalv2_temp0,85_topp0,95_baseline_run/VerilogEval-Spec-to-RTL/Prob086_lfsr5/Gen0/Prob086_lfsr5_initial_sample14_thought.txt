Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (counting from 1 as MSB). In Galois configuration, the LSB (q[0]) is shifted out, and taps are XORed with this bit to determine the next bit values for those tap positions. For taps at bit 5 and 3, when q[0] is 1, the bits at positions 5 and 3 will be XORed with 1 to produce their next state; otherwise, they just shift right. The all-zero state is avoided by resetting to 1 (00001). The output q is a 5-bit register updated on the positive edge of the clock, with synchronous active-high reset.
