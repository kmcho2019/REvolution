Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (indexing from 1). 
In Verilog, bits are usually indexed from 0, so bit 5 corresponds to q[4] and bit 3 to q[2]. 
The LFSR shifts right each clock cycle. The feedback is taken from q[0] (LSB). 
If q[0] is 1, the bits at tap positions are XORed with q[0] for the next state; 
otherwise, bits shift right normally. On synchronous reset, output q is set to 1 (i.e., 5'b00001).
