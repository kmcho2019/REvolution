Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3 (1-based indexing). In zero-based indexing for a 5-bit register q[4:0], these correspond to q[4] and q[2]. On each clock rising edge, if reset is active, q is set to 1. Otherwise, the LFSR shifts right by one bit. The new leftmost bit q[4] is computed as the XOR of the old q[0] and q[2] (the tap bits XORed with the output bit q[0]). For other bits with taps, the new bit is the XOR of the old bit and q[0]; for bits without taps, the new bit is just the old bit shifted right. This yields a maximal-length LFSR that cycles through 31 non-zero states.
