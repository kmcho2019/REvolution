Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. In a 5-bit register indexed [4:0], taps at bit 5 and 3 correspond to bits 4 and 2 (0-based indexing). On each clock cycle, the LSB (q[0]) is the feedback bit. For each tap position, if the tap is set, the next bit value is XORed with the feedback bit. The shift is right, so bits move from higher indices to lower indices. On reset, set the register output to 1. The LFSR advances on each positive clock edge.
