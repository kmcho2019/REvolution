Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, where the shift is to the right. The taps correspond to bits q[4] (bit 5) and q[2] (bit 3) in zero-based indexing. On each clock cycle, the new MSB is computed by XORing the tap bits with the LSB before shifting. The LSB is shifted out, and the rest of the bits shift right, with tapped bits XORed with the LSB. On reset, the output q is set to 1 (i.e., 5'b00001). The LFSR is updated on the positive clock edge and synchronous reset.
