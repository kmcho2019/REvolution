Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. The LFSR shifts right on each clock cycle. The feedback bit is the current LSB (q[0]). Bits at tap positions will XOR their current value with the feedback bit for the next state. On synchronous reset, the register will be set to 1. The LFSR is updated on the positive edge of the clock.
