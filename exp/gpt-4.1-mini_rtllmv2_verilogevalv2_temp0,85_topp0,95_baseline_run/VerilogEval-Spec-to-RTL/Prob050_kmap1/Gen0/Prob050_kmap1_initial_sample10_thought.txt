The Karnaugh map corresponds to a function of three variables a, b, and c. The map is:

      a
  bc  0   1
  00 | 0 | 1
  01 | 1 | 1
  11 | 1 | 1
  10 | 1 | 1

From the K-map, the output is 0 only when (a=0, b=0, c=0).
In all other cases, output is 1.

This can be expressed as:
out = NOT (a=0 and b=0 and c=0) = !(~a & ~b & ~c) = a + b + c

So the output is the OR of a, b, and c.
