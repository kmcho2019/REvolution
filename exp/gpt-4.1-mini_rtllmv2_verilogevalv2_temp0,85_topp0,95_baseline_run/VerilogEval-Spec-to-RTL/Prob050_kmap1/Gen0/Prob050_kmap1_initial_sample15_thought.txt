The Karnaugh map specifies the output for all combinations of inputs a, b, and c. 
From the map, the output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0; in all other cases the output is 1.
Thus, the output can be expressed as the negation of (a=0, b=0, c=0), i.e., out = !(~a & ~b & ~c).
Equivalently, out = a + b + c.
