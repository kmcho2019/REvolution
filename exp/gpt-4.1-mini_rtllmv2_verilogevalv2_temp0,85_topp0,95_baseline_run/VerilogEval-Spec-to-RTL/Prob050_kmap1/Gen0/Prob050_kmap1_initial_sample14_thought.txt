The Karnaugh map describes the output for inputs a, b, c as follows:

          a
   bc   0   1
   00 | 0 | 1 |
   01 | 1 | 1 |
   11 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 1 | 1 |

From the map:
- For bc=00 and a=0, output=0
- For bc=00 and a=1, output=1
- For all other combinations of bc, output=1 regardless of a.

This means the output is 0 only when b=0, c=0, and a=0, otherwise output=1.

So output = NOT(b=0 AND c=0 AND a=0) = NOT(~b & ~c & ~a) = a + b + c

Thus, the output can be implemented as OR of a, b, and c.
