First, interpret the Karnaugh map to find the minimal Boolean expression for 'out' as a function of inputs a, b, and c.

The Karnaugh map is:

          a
     bc   0   1
     00 | 0 | 1 |
     01 | 1 | 1 |
     11 | 1 | 1 |
     10 | 1 | 1 |

So, for a=0:
- bc=00 -> 0
- bc=01 -> 1
- bc=11 -> 1
- bc=10 -> 1

For a=1:
- all entries = 1

From this, the output is 1 except when a=0 and bc=00.

Another way to write the function is:

out = a + (b OR c)

Because when a=1, output=1; when a=0, output=1 if b=1 or c=1 (since bc=00 means b=0 and c=0).

Hence, the expression is out = a + b + c
