The Karnaugh map describes a 3-variable function f(a,b,c) with the following truth values:

bc\a | 0 | 1
00   | 0 | 1
01   | 1 | 1
11   | 1 | 1
10   | 1 | 1

From the K-map, the function is 0 only when a=0 and b=0 and c=0; in all other cases output is 1.

Thus, the output can be expressed as:
out = !(~a & ~b & ~c)
which simplifies to:
out = a + b + c

So the module outputs 1 if any of the inputs is 1.

We will implement this expression in Verilog.
