From the Karnaugh map, we can deduce the output function. The map is:

       a
   bc  0   1
   00 | 0 | 1
   01 | 1 | 1
   11 | 1 | 1
   10 | 1 | 1

This can be interpreted as:

- When bc = 00 and a=0, out=0
- For all other combinations, out=1 except (bc=00, a=0)

This means out is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0

Therefore, the function is the complement of (a=0, b=0, c=0), or equivalently:

out = a + b + c

We implement out as a logical OR of inputs a, b, and c.
