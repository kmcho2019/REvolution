The given Karnaugh map specifies the output for all combinations of a, b, and c. The map is:

       a
bc    0   1
00    0   1
01    1   1
11    1   1
10    1   1

From the map, the output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0. For all other input combinations, the output is 1.

Thus, the output is the negation of (a=0, b=0, c=0), which can be expressed as:
out = (a) OR (b) OR (c)

This is the simplest expression implementing the logic.
