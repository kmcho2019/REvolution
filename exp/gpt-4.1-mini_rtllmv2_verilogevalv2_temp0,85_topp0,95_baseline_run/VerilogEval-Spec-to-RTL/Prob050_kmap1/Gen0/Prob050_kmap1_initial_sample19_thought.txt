First, analyze the Karnaugh map to find the minimal Boolean expression. The K-map is:

       a
   bc 0  1
   00 0  1
   01 1  1
   11 1  1
   10 1  1

Observing the values, the output is 1 for all inputs except when a=0, b=0, c=0.

So the output can be expressed as the complement of (a=0,b=0,c=0), i.e.,

out = NOT (NOT a AND NOT b AND NOT c) = a + b + c

Therefore, the output is the OR of inputs a, b, and c.
