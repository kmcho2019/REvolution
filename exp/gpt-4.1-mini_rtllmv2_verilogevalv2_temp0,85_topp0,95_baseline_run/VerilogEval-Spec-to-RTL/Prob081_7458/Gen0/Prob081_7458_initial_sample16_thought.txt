Implement the 7458 functionality by creating four intermediate wires representing the outputs of the AND gates. Then combine these wires with OR gates to produce the outputs p1y and p2y. Specifically, p1y is the OR of two 3-input AND gates (p1a&p1b&p1c, p1d&p1e&p1f), and p2y is the OR of two 2-input AND gates (p2a&p2b, p2c&p2d).
