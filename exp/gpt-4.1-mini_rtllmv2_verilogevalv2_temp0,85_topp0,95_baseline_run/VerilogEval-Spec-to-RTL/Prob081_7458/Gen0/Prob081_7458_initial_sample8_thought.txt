To implement the TopModule with the functionality of the 7458 chip, I will first create four intermediate wires representing the outputs of the four AND gates: two 3-input AND gates for p1y, and two 2-input AND gates for p2y. Then, the outputs p1y and p2y will be driven by ORing the results of their corresponding AND gates. This modular approach improves readability and matches the chip's gate structure.
