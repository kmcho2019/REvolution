The design intent is to detect the pattern 1101 on the serial input `data`, then read 4 delay bits MSB first, and finally count for (delay+1)*1000 cycles while outputting the remaining delay in `count`, asserting `counting` during this time, and then asserting `done` and waiting for `ack` before restarting.
