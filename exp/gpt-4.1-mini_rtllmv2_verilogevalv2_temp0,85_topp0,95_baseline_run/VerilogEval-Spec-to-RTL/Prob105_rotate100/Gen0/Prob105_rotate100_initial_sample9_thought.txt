Implement a 100-bit shift register that on each rising clock edge either loads the input data when load is high, or rotates left or right by one bit if ena indicates rotation direction, or holds its value otherwise. For rotation, the left shift takes the MSB and moves it to the LSB, and the right shift takes the LSB and moves it to the MSB. When ena is 2'b01, rotate right; when 2'b10, rotate left; else hold.
