Implement a 4-state FSM with states A, B, C, and D, encoded with 2-bit state codes. On synchronous active-low reset (resetn=0), FSM transitions to state A. The transitions from A prioritize r[0], then r[1], then r[2], assigning grants g[0], g[1], or g[2] respectively by moving to states B, C, or D. In grant states B, C, and D, the FSM continues granting as long as the corresponding r[i] remains asserted; otherwise it returns to A. Use one always block for next-state logic, one always block for state registers, and continuous assignments for outputs g based on the current state.
