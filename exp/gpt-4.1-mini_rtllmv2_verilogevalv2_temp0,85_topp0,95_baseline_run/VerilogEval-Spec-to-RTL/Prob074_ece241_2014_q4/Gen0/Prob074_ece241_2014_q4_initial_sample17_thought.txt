The design consists of three D flip-flops, each clocked on the positive edge of clk and initialized to zero. For each flip-flop, its D input is determined by applying a specific two-input gate between input x and the current flip-flop output (or its complement):
- XOR gate: inputs x and the flip-flop's current output Q.
- AND gate: inputs x and the complement of the flip-flop's current output Q.
- OR gate: inputs x and the complement of the flip-flop's current output Q.

Each gate's output feeds its respective flip-flop's D input. On every clock edge, the flip-flops update their outputs. Finally, the outputs of all three flip-flops are input to a three-input NOR gate whose output drives z.

A synchronous reset can be implemented to initialize the flip-flops to zero at start.
