Implement a TopModule with clk, x inputs and z output. Create three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or) initialized to zero. For each gate:

- XOR gate input: x and ff_xor output.
- AND gate input: x and complemented ff_and output.
- OR gate input: x and complemented ff_or output.

The outputs of these gates feed into their respective flip-flops on the positive clock edge. The flip-flop outputs are connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce output z.

Use synchronous reset to initialize flip-flops to zero.
