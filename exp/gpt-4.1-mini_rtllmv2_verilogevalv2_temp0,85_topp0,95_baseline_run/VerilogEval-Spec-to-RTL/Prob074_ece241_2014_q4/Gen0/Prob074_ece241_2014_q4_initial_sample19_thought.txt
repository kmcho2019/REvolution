Implement three D flip-flops with initial zero values. For each flip-flop, compute its D input based on the current input x and the flip-flop's current output (or its complement) through the specified gates: XOR with Q, AND with ~Q, OR with ~Q. On each positive clock edge, update the flip-flops with the computed D inputs. Finally, output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
