Create three D flip-flops initialized to zero. Each flip-flop input is the output of a corresponding two-input gate with x as one input and the flip-flop's current output or its complement as the second input: XOR with its own Q, AND with its own Q complement, OR with its own Q complement. On the rising edge of clk, each flip-flop updates its output. The outputs of all three flip-flops feed into a three-input NOR gate, generating output z.
