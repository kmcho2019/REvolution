Implement three D flip-flops, each driven by a two-input gate with input x and the flip-flop's output or complement as the second input. Specifically, the first flip-flop gets D = x XOR Q, the second D = x AND ~Q, the third D = x OR ~Q. The outputs Q of all three flip-flops feed a 3-input NOR gate to produce output z. Initialize all flip-flops to zero at the start.
