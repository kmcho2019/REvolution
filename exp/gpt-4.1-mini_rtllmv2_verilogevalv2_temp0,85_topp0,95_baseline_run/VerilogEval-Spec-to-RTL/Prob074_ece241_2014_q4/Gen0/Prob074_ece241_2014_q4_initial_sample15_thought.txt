Implement three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or) each initialized to 0. On each clock edge, compute the D input for each flip-flop as follows:

- ff_xor_d = x XOR ff_xor_q
- ff_and_d = x AND ~ff_and_q
- ff_or_d = x OR ~ff_or_q

Update the flip-flops on the positive clock edge. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs. This means z = ~(ff_xor_q | ff_and_q | ff_or_q).
