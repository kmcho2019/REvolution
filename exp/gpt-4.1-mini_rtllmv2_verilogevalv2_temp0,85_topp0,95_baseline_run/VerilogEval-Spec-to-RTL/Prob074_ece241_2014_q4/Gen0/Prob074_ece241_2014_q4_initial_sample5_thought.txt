Implement three D flip-flops that store the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates respectively. Each gate's first input is the external input x, and the second input depends on the current state (the flip-flop outputs or their complements). On each positive clock edge, update the flip-flop outputs. Finally, output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs. Initialize the flip-flops to zero using an asynchronous reset at the beginning.
