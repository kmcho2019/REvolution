Implement three D flip-flops, each storing the output of a two-input gate that takes the input x and the state of its own flip-flop output (or complement) as the second input. Specifically:
- XOR gate input: x and current Q of XOR flip-flop
- AND gate input: x and complement of Q of AND flip-flop
- OR gate input: x and complement of Q of OR flip-flop
On each clock positive edge, update the flip-flops with their respective gate outputs. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
Initialize all flip-flops to zero on reset.
