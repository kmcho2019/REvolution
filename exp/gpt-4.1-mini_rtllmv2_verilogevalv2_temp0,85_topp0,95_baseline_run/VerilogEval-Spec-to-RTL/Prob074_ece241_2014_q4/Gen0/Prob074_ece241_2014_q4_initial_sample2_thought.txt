Implement three D flip-flops initialized to zero. For each flip-flop, compute the D input based on the specified gate with x and either the flip-flop output or its complement as the second input: 
- XOR with flip-flop output
- AND with complement of flip-flop output
- OR with complement of flip-flop output.
On each positive clock edge, update flip-flops with their respective D inputs. The output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.
