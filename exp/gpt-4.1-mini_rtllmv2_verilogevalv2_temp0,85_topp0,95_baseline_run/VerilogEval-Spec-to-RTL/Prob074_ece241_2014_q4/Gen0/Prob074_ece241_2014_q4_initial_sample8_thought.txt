Implement a module with three D flip-flops, each taking as input the output of their respective gates (XOR, AND, OR). Each gate receives input x and the respective flip-flop output or its complement as the second input. The flip-flop outputs are then fed into a three-input NOR gate to produce output z. Initialize the flip-flops to zero on reset (assumed at start). All flip-flops trigger on the positive edge of clk.
