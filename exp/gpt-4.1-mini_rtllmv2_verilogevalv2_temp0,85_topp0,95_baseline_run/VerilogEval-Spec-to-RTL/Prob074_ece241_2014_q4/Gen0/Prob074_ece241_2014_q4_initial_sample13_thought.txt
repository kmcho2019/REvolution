Implement three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or), each updated on the positive edge of clk. For each flip-flop's D input:
- XOR gate: inputs are x and the current output of ff_xor.
- AND gate: inputs are x and the complement of ff_and's output.
- OR gate: inputs are x and the complement of ff_or's output.
After the flip-flops are updated, the outputs of the three flip-flops are fed into a three-input NOR gate, whose output drives z. All flip-flops initialize to zero at start.
