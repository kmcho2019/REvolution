Implement three D flip-flops (q_xor, q_and, q_or) each updated on the positive edge of clk. For each, compute the D input as follows:
- D_xor = x XOR q_xor
- D_and = x AND ~q_and
- D_or = x OR ~q_or

After updating the flip-flops, output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs (q_xor, q_and, q_or). Initialize all flip-flops to 0.
