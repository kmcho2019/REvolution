Also, `data_shift` is not shifted or updated as a whole vector, which is unusual for a shift register. This likely causes incorrect data capture.

   The `bit_count` register is assigned in two separate always blocks: one with reset in the main FSM block (in the same block as state and data_shift) and another always block solely for bit_count update. This creates multiple drivers for the same register, which is illegal in synthesis and may cause simulation mismatches. The conflicting assignments mean the value of `bit_count` is not well-defined and likely causes the FSM to malfunction during data reception.
