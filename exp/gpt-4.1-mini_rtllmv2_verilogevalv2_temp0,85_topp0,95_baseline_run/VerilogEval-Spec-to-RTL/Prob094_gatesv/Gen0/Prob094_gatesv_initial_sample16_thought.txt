Implement the TopModule with 4-bit input 'in' and 4-bit outputs: out_both, out_any, and out_different. 
- out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1] for i = 0 to 2; out_both[3] = 0 (no left neighbor for in[3])
- out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1] for i = 1 to 3; out_any[0] = 0 (no right neighbor for in[0])
- out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1)%4], treating vector as circular with wrap-around
