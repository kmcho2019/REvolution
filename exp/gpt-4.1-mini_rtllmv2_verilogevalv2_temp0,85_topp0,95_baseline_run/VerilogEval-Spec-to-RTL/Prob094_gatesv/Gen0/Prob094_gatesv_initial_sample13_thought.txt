Implement the TopModule with 4-bit input and three 4-bit outputs. For out_both, only bits 0 to 2 are computed by ANDing the bit with its left neighbour (higher index), bit 3 is zero. For out_any, only bits 1 to 3 are computed by ORing the bit with its right neighbour (lower index), bit 0 is zero. For out_different, each bit compares the bit with its left neighbour, with wrapping around for bit 3 to bit 0.
