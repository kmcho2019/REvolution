Implement the TopModule with a 4-bit input vector. 
- For out_both, assign bits 0 to 2 to the AND of in[i] and in[i+1], and zero out the last bit since in[3] has no left neighbor.
- For out_any, assign bits 1 to 3 to the OR of in[i] and in[i-1], and zero out the first bit since in[0] has no right neighbor.
- For out_different, since it wraps around, assign each bit to the XOR of in[i] and in[(i+1)%4], i.e., for i from 0 to 3, out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1)%4].
