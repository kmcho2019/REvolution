Implement the TopModule with four-bit input and three four-bit outputs.
For out_both, bits [0..2] compare in[i] & in[i+1], out_both[3] = 0 as no left neighbor.
For out_any, bits [1..3] compare in[i] | in[i-1], out_any[0] = 0 as no right neighbor.
For out_different, each bit compares in[i] with in[(i+1)%4] because left neighbor wraps around.
Use combinational assignments for outputs.
