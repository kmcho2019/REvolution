For out_both, each bit i (0 to 2) will be the logical AND of in[i] and in[i+1]. out_both[3] will be 0 since in[3] has no left neighbor.

For out_any, each bit i (1 to 3) will be the logical OR of in[i] and in[i-1]. out_any[0] will be 0 since in[0] has no right neighbor.

For out_different, each bit i (0 to 3) will be set if in[i] is different from its left neighbor (in[(i+1)%4]), with wrap-around.
