Implement a Moore FSM with states representing the Lemming's behavior: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Walking states encode direction (left or right). On bump signals, if walking, switch direction. On ground=0 and not digging, transition to falling. On dig=1 and walking on ground, transition to digging. Falling ignores bump and dig inputs, returns to walking on ground=1. Digging continues until ground=0, then transition to falling. Outputs reflect the current state: walk_left or walk_right when walking, aaah when falling, digging when digging. Asynchronous positive edge reset sets state to walking left.
