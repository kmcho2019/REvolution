Design a Moore FSM with states representing the Lemming's behavior:
- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- DIGGING_LEFT
- DIGGING_RIGHT
- FALLING_LEFT
- FALLING_RIGHT

The output signals are derived purely from the current state:
- walk_left = 1 for WALK_LEFT and DIGGING_LEFT
- walk_right = 1 for WALK_RIGHT and DIGGING_RIGHT
- digging = 1 for DIGGING_LEFT and DIGGING_RIGHT
- aaah = 1 for FALLING_LEFT and FALLING_RIGHT

Transitions:
- On reset, state = WALK_LEFT.
- If ground=0, transition to falling state corresponding to current walking direction.
- While falling, stay in falling until ground=1, then return to walking state matching falling direction.
- While walking on ground (ground=1 and not falling or digging):
  - If dig=1, start digging in current walking direction.
  - Else if bumped left or right (one or both), switch walking direction.
- While digging on ground:
  - If ground=0, transition to falling state.
  - Else continue digging.
- Bumps have no effect during falling or digging.
- dig=1 ignored during falling or no ground.
- When ground changes or bumps happen simultaneously with falling or digging, ignore bumps during fall or dig.

Implement asynchronous posedge reset, synchronous posedge clock FSM with next state logic and Moore outputs.
