Implement a Moore FSM with states representing the Lemming's behavior: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The walking states track direction. On areset, the FSM goes to walk left. If ground=0, the FSM goes to falling regardless of bump or dig signals. While falling, the direction is held and bump/dig have no effect. When ground returns to 1, the FSM goes back to walking in the held direction. When on ground and not falling, if dig=1, the FSM goes to digging and holds direction. While digging, if ground=0, the FSM goes to falling. While digging or falling, bump signals have no effect. When on ground and not digging/falling, bump signals cause direction switching. The outputs are derived directly from the states: walk_left=1 in walk left state, walk_right=1 in walk right state, aaah=1 in falling, digging=1 in digging. The FSM is synchronous with asynchronous posedge reset.
