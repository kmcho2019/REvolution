Model the Lemming behavior using a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Use a state register updated on the positive clock edge with asynchronous active-high reset to walk left state.

- States:
  * WALK_LEFT
  * WALK_RIGHT
  * FALLING
  * DIGGING_LEFT
  * DIGGING_RIGHT

- Outputs depend only on state:
  * walk_left = 1 for WALK_LEFT or DIGGING_LEFT
  * walk_right = 1 for WALK_RIGHT or DIGGING_RIGHT
  * aaah = 1 in FALLING
  * digging = 1 in DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT

- Transitions:
  On ground=0 and not already FALLING or DIGGING, go to FALLING.
  When ground=1 from FALLING, return to previous walking direction.
  When dig=1 and walking on ground, start digging in current walking direction.
  When digging and ground=0, transition to FALLING.
  When bump_left or bump_right while walking (and not falling/digging) switch direction accordingly.
  Bump signals ignored when falling or digging.

Use a "prev_walk_dir" or encode walking direction in state to remember walking direction through fall/dig. Implement all conditions with priority fall > dig > bump.

Implement asynchronous reset to WALK_LEFT state.
