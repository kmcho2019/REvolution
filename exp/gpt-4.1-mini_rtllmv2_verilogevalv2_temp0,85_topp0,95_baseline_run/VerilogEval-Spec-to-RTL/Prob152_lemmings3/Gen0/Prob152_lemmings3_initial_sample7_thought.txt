Model the Lemming behavior as a Moore FSM with four states:

- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- FALL: falling (aaah)
- DIG: digging (on ground)

On reset, state is WALK_LEFT.

Transitions:
- If ground=0 and not digging or falling, go to FALL.
- If in FALL and ground=1, return to previous walk direction.
- If in WALK states and dig=1, enter DIG state.
- If in DIG and ground=0, transition to FALL.
- If in WALK states and bump_left or bump_right, reverse direction (walk right if bumped left, walk left if bumped right or both).
- Bumps during FALL or DIG do not change direction.
- Falling has highest precedence, then digging, then direction switching.
Outputs are according to the current state (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, digging).

Implement asynchronous positive edge reset to WALK_LEFT.
Remember to store walking direction separately for restoration after fall.
