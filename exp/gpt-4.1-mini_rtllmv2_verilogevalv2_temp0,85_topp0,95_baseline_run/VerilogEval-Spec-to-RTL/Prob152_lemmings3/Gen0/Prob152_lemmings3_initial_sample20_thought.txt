Implement a Moore FSM with states representing the Lemming's behavior: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Use separate states for walking left and right, and digging left and right, to remember direction. On asynchronous posedge areset, reset to walking left. On each clock edge, update state according to inputs and current state, respecting the precedence: falling > digging > switching direction. Falling happens when ground=0; digging starts only if dig=1 and on ground while walking; bumping affects walking direction only if currently walking (not falling or digging). Outputs are derived from the state.
