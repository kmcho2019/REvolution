Implement a Moore FSM with four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, and DIGGING. Outputs depend only on the state. On asynchronous reset, state goes to WALK_LEFT. Transitions:

- If ground=0 (falling condition) and not already falling or digging, transition to FALLING (aaah=1).
- If falling (FALLING state) and ground=1, return to the previous walking direction.
- If digging is commanded (dig=1) while walking and ground=1, transition to DIGGING.
- If digging and ground=0, transition to FALLING.
- While walking (left or right), if bumped (left or right), switch direction.
- While falling or digging, bump inputs are ignored.
- Falling has highest priority, then digging, then switching direction.

Outputs:
- walk_left = 1 in WALK_LEFT
- walk_right = 1 in WALK_RIGHT
- aaah = 1 in FALLING
- digging = 1 in DIGGING
