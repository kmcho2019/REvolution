Implement a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The current walking direction is saved in the walking states and preserved through falling and digging. Transitions are prioritized: fall (ground=0) > dig (dig=1 and ground=1 and not falling) > switch direction (bump signals). Bump signals only change direction when walking (not falling or digging). On areset (async posedge), reset to walking left state. Outputs are derived from the current state.
