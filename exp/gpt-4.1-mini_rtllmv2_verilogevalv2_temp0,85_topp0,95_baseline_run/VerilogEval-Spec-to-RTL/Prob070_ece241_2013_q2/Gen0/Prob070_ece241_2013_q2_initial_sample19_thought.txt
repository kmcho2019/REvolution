First, interpret the 4-bit input as a number with inputs (a,b,c,d), where 'a' is the most significant bit and 'd' is the least significant bit. The numbers to output logic-1 are 2, 7, and 15. The numbers to output logic-0 are 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14. The numbers 3,8,11,12 never occur.

We need to find the minimal sum-of-products (SOP) and product-of-sums (POS) expressions for the output to be 1 for 2,7,15.

First, write the minterms for outputs=1:

2 = 0010 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0)

7 = 0111 (a=0,b=1,c=1,d=1)

15=1111 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1)

We express out_sop as the sum of minterms for these three inputs minimized.

Similarly, out_pos is the product of maxterms where output is 0. Because 3,8,11,12 never occur, we don't consider them.

We can simplify the expressions:

- Write the truth table for the 16 combinations, mark 1 for 2,7,15, 0 for the others given, X for don't cares (3,8,11,12).

- Minimize using Karnaugh map or Boolean algebra.

Finally, implement the two minimized equations in Verilog.

Note: The inputs are single bits: a,b,c,d.

Example for minterm 2 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0): ~a & ~b & c & ~d

Similarly for others.

We will compute the minimized SOP and POS expressions.

Let's do K-map simplification:

Inputs: a b c d

List of 1's: 2(0010), 7(0111), 15(1111)

List of 0's: 0(0000),1(0001),4(0100),5(0101),6(0110),9(1001),10(1010),13(1101),14(1110)

Don't care: 3(0011),8(1000),11(1011),12(1100)

K-map:

Let's define variables order for K-map: ab rows, cd columns

ab\cd: 00,01,11,10

00: 0(0000),1(0001),3(0011),2(0010)

01: 4(0100),5(0101),7(0111),6(0110)

11: 12(1100),13(1101),15(1111),14(1110)

10: 8(1000),9(1001),11(1011),10(1010)

Mark 1's:

2(0010) => ab=00, cd=10 => row 00, col 10: 1

7(0111) => ab=01, cd=11 => row 01, col 11: 1

15(1111)=> ab=11, cd=11 => row 11, col 11: 1

Mark 0's:

0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14 as 0

Don't cares: 3,8,11,12

Fill K-map:

cd\ab | 00 | 01 | 11 | 10

00    | 0  | 0  | 12X | 8X

01    | 1  | 0  | 13 | 9

11    | 3X | 5  | 15 | 11X

10    | 2  | 6  | 14 | 10

Wait, the columns are cd=00,01,11,10, rows ab=00,01,11,10.

Let's construct the K-map as:

          cd

       00  01  11  10

ab 00  0   1   3X  2

   01  4   5   7   6

   11  12X 13  15 14

   10  8X  9  11X 10

Wait, the earlier order was rows ab, columns cd:

Row ab=00 -> a=0,b=0

Row ab=01 -> a=0,b=1

Row ab=11 -> a=1,b=1

Row ab=10 -> a=1,b=0

Columns cd=00 -> c=0,d=0

Columns cd=01 -> c=0,d=1

Columns cd=11 -> c=1,d=1

Columns cd=10 -> c=1,d=0

So now fill with values (1=output=1, 0=output=0, X=don't care):

ab\cd| 00| 01| 11| 10

00   | 0 | 0 | X | 1  // 0(0000)=0,1(0001)=0,3(0011)=X,2(0010)=1

01   | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0  //4(0100)=0,5(0101)=0,7(0111)=1,6(0110)=0

11   | X | 0 | 1 | 0  //12(1100)=X,13(1101)=0,15(1111)=1,14(1110)=0

10   | X | 0 | X | 0  //8(1000)=X,9(1001)=0,11(1011)=X,10(1010)=0

Now, group 1's and include don't cares to minimize:

1) The 1 at (00,10) for 2

2) The 1 at (01,11) for 7

3) The 1 at (11,11) for 15

Possible groups:

- Group the two 1's at (01,11) and (11,11) with don't care at (10,11) (which is X), so can group (01,11), (11,11), and (10,11)(X) to cover c=1,d=1.

This group corresponds to c=1,d=1, b and a varies over 0 and 1.

Thus, one product term: c & d

- For the 1 at (00,10) (2), can it be grouped with don't cares?

Check neighbors: (00,11)=X (3) and (01,10)=0 (6)

We can group (00,10) and (00,11) (X) horizontally:

Group: (00,10) and (00,11) => a=0,b=0,c=1,d=x

Product term: ~a & ~b & c

So, sum of products is:

out_sop = (~a & ~b & c) + (c & d)

Now for product of sums (POS):

POS is 0 for 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14

At these points output=0.

We form maxterms for these zeros and minimize.

From the K-map, the zeros are at positions not covered by the groups:

We can find the minimal POS by taking complement of the SOP and simplifying.

Using De Morgan:

out = (~a & ~b & c) + (c & d)

out' = (~(~a & ~b & c) & ~(c & d)) = (a + b + ~c) & (~c + ~d)

Therefore,

out_pos = (a + b + ~c) & (~c + ~d)

Final expressions:

out_sop = (~a & ~b & c) + (c & d)

out_pos = (a + b + ~c) & (~c + ~d)
