Implement a module TopModule with inputs clk, a, b and outputs out_assign, out_always_comb, out_always_ff. The out_assign is the XOR of a and b using an assign statement. The out_always_comb is the XOR of a and b implemented inside an always_comb block. The out_always_ff is a registered output updated on the positive edge of clk, capturing the XOR of a and b, thus delayed by one clock cycle.
