Implement a TopModule with inputs clk, a, b and outputs out_assign, out_always_comb, out_always_ff.
- out_assign is driven by a continuous assign statement with XOR of a and b.
- out_always_comb is driven inside an always @(*) block with XOR of a and b.
- out_always_ff is driven inside an always @(posedge clk) block with registered XOR of a and b.
