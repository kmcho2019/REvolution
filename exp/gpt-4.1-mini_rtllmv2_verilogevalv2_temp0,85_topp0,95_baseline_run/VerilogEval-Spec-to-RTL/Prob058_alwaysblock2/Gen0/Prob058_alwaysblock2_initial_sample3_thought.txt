Implement a TopModule with inputs clk, a, b and outputs out_assign, out_always_comb, out_always_ff. 
- out_assign is assigned the XOR of a and b using an assign statement.
- out_always_comb is assigned the XOR of a and b inside an always_comb block.
- out_always_ff is registered output of the XOR of a and b inside an always_ff block triggered on posedge clk, thus delayed by one clock cycle.
