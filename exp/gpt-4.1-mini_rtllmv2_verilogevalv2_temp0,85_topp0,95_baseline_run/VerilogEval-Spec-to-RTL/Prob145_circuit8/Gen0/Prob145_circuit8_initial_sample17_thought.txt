The outputs p and q are sequential and change on the clock edges. Observing the waveform:

- At the first rising edge (around 25ns clock=1), p=0, q=x.
- At falling clock edges, q updates to the previous p value.
- p updates on rising clock edges and is set to 'a & q' (1 only when both a and q are 1).
- q updates on falling clock edges to the previous p.

Essentially, the circuit is a two-bit shift register with feedback on p:
- On rising edge: p <= a & q
- On falling edge: q <= p

Initial values are unknown (x) and then settle to 0.

Implementing this with sequential always blocks triggered on posedge and negedge of clock.
