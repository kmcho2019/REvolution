Analyze the waveform to infer the behavior of outputs p and q based on clock and input a.

- p and q are initially unknown (x).
- At the first rising clock edge (25ns), p is set to 0 and q is x.
- q remains 0 until time 115ns, where on clock low q changes from 0 to 1 and p becomes 1.
- p updates mostly on rising clock edges when a=1 (e.g., at 90ns, 100ns, 110ns, and 165ns).
- q updates on falling clock edges, becoming 1 at 115ns and stays 1 until 175ns, then drops back to 0.
- The output p is set to the input a sampled at the rising edge of clock when q=0.
- When q=1, p follows the input a sampled at the rising edge of clock.
- At falling clock edges, q is updated to p's value from the last rising edge.
- Essentially, q is the delayed version of p updated on falling edge.
- On falling edge, q <= p.
- On rising edge, p <= a.
- On falling edge, q <= p.
- At start, p=0, q=0.

Implement two registers p and q updated on clock edges accordingly.
