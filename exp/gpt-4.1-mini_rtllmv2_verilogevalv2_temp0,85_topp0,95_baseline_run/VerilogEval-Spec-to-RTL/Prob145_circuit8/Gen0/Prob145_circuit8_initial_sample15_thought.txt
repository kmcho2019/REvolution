The circuit has two outputs p and q that are both 1-bit registers. 

- On reset (initially), outputs are unknown.
- On the first clock rising edge (time 25ns), p is 0 and q is unknown (maybe q is uninitialized).
- q gets updated only on clock falling edges.
- p gets updated on clock rising edges.
- When clock is low, changing a doesn't affect outputs until the next clock edge.
- On rising edge of clock, if a=1, p=1; otherwise p=0.
- On falling edge of clock, q takes the value of p at the last rising edge.
- After some cycles, q is basically a delayed version of p by one half clock cycle.
- The logic implements a kind of flip-flop with p updated on rising edge from input a, and q updated on falling edge from p.

Thus, implement p as a rising-edge triggered register that follows a, and q as a falling-edge triggered register that follows p.

At initialization, outputs are 0.
