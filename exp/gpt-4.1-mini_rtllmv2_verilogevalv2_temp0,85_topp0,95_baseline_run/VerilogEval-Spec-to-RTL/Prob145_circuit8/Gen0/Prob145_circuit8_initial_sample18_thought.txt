From the waveforms, it appears the outputs p and q are sequential and depend on previous states and input a, synchronized with clock.

Key observations:
- p and q are initially unknown (x).
- On clock high (posedge), p seems to latch input a if q is 0; otherwise, p follows a different rule.
- q changes mostly on clock low; when clock is low, q seems to latch previous p value.
- Specifically, on posedge clock, p updates to a & ~q.
- On negedge clock, q updates to p.

This suggests a flip-flop pair with p updating on posedge clock as p <= a & ~q and q updating on negedge clock as q <= p.

Implementing p as a positive edge triggered register and q as a negative edge triggered register will match the behavior.

Initialize p and q to 0.
