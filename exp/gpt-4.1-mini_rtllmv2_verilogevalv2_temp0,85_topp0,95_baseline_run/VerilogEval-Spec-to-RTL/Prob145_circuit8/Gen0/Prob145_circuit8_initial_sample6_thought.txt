The waveform shows a sequential behavior with outputs p and q changing on clock edges and based on input a.

- On the rising edge of clock, if a=1, p is set to 1; otherwise, p is cleared to 0.
- q only changes on the falling edge of clock.
- On falling edge of clock, if p was 1 at the previous clock cycle, q is set to 1; otherwise, q is cleared.
- Initially, both p and q are unknown (x).
- q holds its value during clock=1 periods.
- The output q is updated at falling edge of clock based on the stored p.

Hence, the design needs:
- A register p that updates on rising edge of clock based on input a.
- A register q that updates on falling edge of clock based on the previous p.
- Both outputs are registered outputs.

Implementing this requires two always blocks triggered on posedge and negedge clock respectively.
