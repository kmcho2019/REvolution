The circuit has two outputs p and q and is sequential. From the waveform:
- Initially at time 0 p and q are unknown (x).
- At the first clock=1 edge (25ns) with a=0, p=0 and q=x.
- When clock=0, p and q hold values until the next clock=1.
- p changes to 1 only on clock rising edge when a=1, otherwise p=0 on clock=1 edges.
- q is set to 1 on clock falling edge if previously p was 1; q holds its state during clock=0.
- After q is set, p is reset to 0 on the next clock=1 edge unless a=1 again.
- When clock goes from 1 to 0, q copies p's previous value.
- p updates only on rising edge of clock to a, q updates only on falling edge of clock to p.

Hence:
- On posedge clock: p <= a
- On negedge clock: q <= p

Initialize p and q to 0 at start for defined behavior.

This implements a one-bit latch behavior where p follows a at clock rising edge, and q samples p at clock falling edge.
