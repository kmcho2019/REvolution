The module has two outputs p and q and one input a, with a clock input. From the waveform:

- At first, p and q are unknown.
- At the first rising edge of clock (at 25ns), p becomes 0 and q unknown.
- At the next falling clock edge (55ns), p=0, q=0.
- When clock is low, p remains 0 regardless of a.
- At 90ns rising edge, when a=1, p becomes 1, q remains 0.
- At 115ns falling edge, p changes from 1 to 1, q changes from 0 to 1.
- At subsequent rising edges when a=1 and q=1, p=1, q=1.
- When clock is low and a changes, p and q hold their values.
- At the falling edge of clock at 175ns, p and q go to 0.

From this, it seems p is set to a at rising clock edges when q=0, q is set to p at falling clock edges.

In summary:

- At rising edge of clock: p <= a & ~q (p set only when q=0)
- At falling edge of clock: q <= p

This gives the observed behavior: q follows p at falling edge, p is loaded from a only if q=0 at rising edge.

Implementing this sequential logic with two flip-flops updating on opposite clock edges.

Initial values unknown, so initialize p and q to 0.
