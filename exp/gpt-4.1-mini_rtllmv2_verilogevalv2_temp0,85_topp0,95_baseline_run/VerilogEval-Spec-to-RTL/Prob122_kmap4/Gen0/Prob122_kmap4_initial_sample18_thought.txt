The Karnaugh map shows a repeating pattern of 0s and 1s depending on the values of inputs a, b, c, and d. By analyzing the map, the output is 1 when the pair (a,b) and (c,d) differ in parity in a specific pattern. Observing the map, the output is 1 if exactly one bit differs between (a,b) and (c,d) in their respective positions in the 2-bit numbers formed by ab and cd. 

The simplest way to express this is: out = a XOR b XOR c XOR d.

Checking the map confirms this:
- For (a,b,c,d) = (0,0,0,0): out = 0^0^0^0 = 0 (matches)
- For (0,1,0,0): out = 0^1^0^0 = 1 (matches)
- and so on...

Therefore, the output is the XOR of all inputs.
