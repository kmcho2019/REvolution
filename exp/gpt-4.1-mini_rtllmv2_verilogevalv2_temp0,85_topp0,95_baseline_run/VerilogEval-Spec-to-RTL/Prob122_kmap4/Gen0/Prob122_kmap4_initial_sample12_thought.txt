The K-map is a 4-variable map with variables c,d as row and a,b as column.
Mapping the 1s, we observe a checkerboard pattern where output is 1 if exactly one or three of the inputs are 1.
This matches the behavior of a 4-input odd parity (XOR) function.
Therefore, the output is the XOR of all inputs: out = a ^ b ^ c ^ d.
