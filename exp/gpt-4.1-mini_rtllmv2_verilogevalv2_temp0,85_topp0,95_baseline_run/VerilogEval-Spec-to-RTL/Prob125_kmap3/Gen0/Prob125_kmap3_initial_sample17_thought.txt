The Karnaugh map specifies the output for combinations of inputs a, b, c, and d, where d is a don't-care input and can be chosen freely to simplify the logic. I'll treat d as a don't-care and ignore it to minimize the logic expression.

First, organize the map with variables:

Rows are cd: 00, 01, 11, 10 (in that order)
Columns are ab: 01, 00, 10, 11 (in that order)

The table:

          ab
     01  00  10  11
cd
00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Let's map each cell to variables a,b,c,d:

Positions:

ab = 01 => a=0, b=1
ab = 00 => a=0, b=0
ab = 10 => a=1, b=0
ab = 11 => a=1, b=1

cd = 00 => c=0, d=0
cd = 01 => c=0, d=1
cd = 11 => c=1, d=1
cd = 10 => c=1, d=0

Now list all minterms where output=1 (ignoring d cells):

From the map, the outputs of 1 are at:

(00,10): cd=00 (c=0,d=0), ab=10 (a=1,b=0)
(00,11): cd=00 (c=0,d=0), ab=11 (a=1,b=1)
(11,00): cd=11 (c=1,d=1), ab=00 (a=0,b=0)
(11,10): cd=11 (c=1,d=1), ab=10 (a=1,b=0)
(11,11): cd=11 (c=1,d=1), ab=11 (a=1,b=1)
(10,10): cd=10 (c=1,d=0), ab=10 (a=1,b=0)
(10,11): cd=10 (c=1,d=0), ab=11 (a=1,b=1)
(10,01): cd=10 (c=1,d=0), ab=01 (a=0,b=1)

Cells with d (don't-care) can be assigned as 0 or 1 to simplify.

Also, the 1s at (11,00) and (10,01) are important.

Let's write all ones minterms explicitly as Boolean expressions:

- (00,10): c=0,d=0,a=1,b=0 => ~c & ~d & a & ~b
- (00,11): ~c & ~d & a & b
- (11,00): c & d & ~a & ~b
- (11,10): c & d & a & ~b
- (11,11): c & d & a & b
- (10,10): c & ~d & a & ~b
- (10,11): c & ~d & a & b
- (10,01): c & ~d & ~a & b

Now, try to group and simplify.

Group 1: (00,10) and (00,11)
= ~c & ~d & a & (b + ~b) = ~c & ~d & a

Group 2: (11,10), (11,11), (10,10), (10,11)
= c & (d + ~d) & a & (b + ~b) = c & a

Group 3: (11,00) and (10,01)
= c & ((d & ~a & ~b) + (~d & ~a & b)) = c & ~a & (d & ~b + ~d & b)
Note that (d & ~b + ~d & b) is the XOR of d and b.

So group 3 = c & ~a & (b ^ d)

Finally, include group 1 and group 2:

Output = (~c & ~d & a) + (c & a) + (c & ~a & (b ^ d))

Notice that (c & a) covers some cases from group 2.

Alternatively, we can combine (~c & ~d & a) + (c & a) as a & (~c & ~d + c)

But since d is don't-care, we can assign d to simplify expressions.

Since d is don't-care, let's set d=0 in the circuit to simplify.

Then (b ^ d) becomes b ^ 0 = b

Therefore group 3 = c & ~a & b

Group 1 = ~c & ~d & a = ~c & a (since d=0)

Group 2 = c & a

So output = (~c & a) + (c & a) + (c & ~a & b)

= a + c & ~a & b

= a + (c & ~a & b)

This is the minimal expression.

To implement:

out = a | (c & ~a & b)

We can implement this easily.

d is unused.
