Implement a finite state machine with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. The FSM waits in IDLE, discarding bytes until it sees a byte with in[3]=1. Then it transitions to BYTE1, marking the first byte received. On the next two bytes, it goes to BYTE2 and BYTE3, then asserts done for one cycle immediately after the third byte is received, and returns to IDLE to search for the next message. The reset is synchronous and active high.
