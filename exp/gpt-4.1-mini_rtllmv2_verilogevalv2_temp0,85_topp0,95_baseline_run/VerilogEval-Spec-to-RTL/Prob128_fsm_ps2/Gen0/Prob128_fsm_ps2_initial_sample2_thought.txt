Implement a 3-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Initially in IDLE, discard bytes until in[3]==1, then move to BYTE2. Next cycle, receive byte 2 and move to BYTE3. Next cycle, receive byte 3 and assert done=1, then return to IDLE to look for next message. done is asserted for one cycle after receiving the 3rd byte. Synchronous reset clears the FSM to IDLE. Input bytes arrive every clock cycle.
