Implement a simple FSM with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. In IDLE, wait for a byte with in[3]=1 to indicate the start of a message. Then transition through BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3 states on each clock cycle with new bytes. After receiving the third byte (BYTE3), assert done for one clock cycle and return to IDLE to look for the next message. Reset synchronously returns FSM to IDLE and clears done.
