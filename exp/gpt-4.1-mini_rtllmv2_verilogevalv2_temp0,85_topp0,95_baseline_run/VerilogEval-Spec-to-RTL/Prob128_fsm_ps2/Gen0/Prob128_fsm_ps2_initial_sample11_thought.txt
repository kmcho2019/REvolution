Implement a three-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Initially in IDLE, wait for a byte with in[3] = 1. When found, transition to BYTE2 after first byte received. Then after second byte, transition to BYTE3. After third byte, assert done for one cycle and return to IDLE to find next message start. Use synchronous reset to go to IDLE state. done is asserted for one cycle immediately after third byte receipt.
