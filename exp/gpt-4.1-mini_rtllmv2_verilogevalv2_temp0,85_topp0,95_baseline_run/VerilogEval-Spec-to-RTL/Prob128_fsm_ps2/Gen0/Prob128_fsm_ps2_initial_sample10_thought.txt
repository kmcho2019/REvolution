Implement an FSM with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. The FSM waits in IDLE state discarding bytes until it receives a byte where in[3] = 1 (start of message). It then moves to BYTE1 (first byte received), then BYTE2 and BYTE3 on subsequent clocks, capturing bytes. After BYTE3, it asserts done for one clock cycle and returns to IDLE to wait for next message. Reset synchronously brings FSM to IDLE and clears done signal.
