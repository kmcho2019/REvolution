Implement the combinational circuit for output f based on the given truth table.
From the table, f is 1 in these input conditions:
- x3=0, x2=1, x1=0 or 1 -> when x3=0, x2=1
- x3=1, x2=0, x1=1
- x3=1, x2=1, x1=1

So the boolean expression can be derived as:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & ~x2 & x1) | (x3 & x2 & x1)
