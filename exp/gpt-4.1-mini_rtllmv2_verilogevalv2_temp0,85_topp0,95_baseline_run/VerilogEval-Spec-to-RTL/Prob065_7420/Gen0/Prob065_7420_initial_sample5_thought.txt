The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate outputs the NAND of its four inputs. For the module TopModule, the first NAND gate takes inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and outputs p1y. The second NAND gate takes inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d and outputs p2y. The outputs are the NAND of the four inputs respectively.
