The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each gate takes four inputs and outputs the NAND of these inputs. For the TopModule, the first NAND gate will take inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and output p1y, and the second NAND gate will take inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d and output p2y. The output of a NAND gate is the negation of the AND of all inputs.
