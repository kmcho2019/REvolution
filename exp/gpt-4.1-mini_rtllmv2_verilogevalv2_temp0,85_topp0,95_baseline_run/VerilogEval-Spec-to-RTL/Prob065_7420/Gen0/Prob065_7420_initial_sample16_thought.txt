The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each gate outputs the NAND of its four inputs. The module TopModule should instantiate two such gates: one taking inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and outputting p1y, and the other taking inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d and outputting p2y. The NAND operation can be implemented directly using the Verilog nand primitive or using assign statements.
