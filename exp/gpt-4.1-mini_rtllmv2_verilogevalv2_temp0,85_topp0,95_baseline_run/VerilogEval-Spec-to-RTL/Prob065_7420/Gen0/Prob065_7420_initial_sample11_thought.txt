The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate outputs the negation of the AND of its four inputs. Therefore, to implement the TopModule, we instantiate the two 4-input NAND gates by performing a NAND operation on the respective inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d), producing outputs p1y and p2y.
