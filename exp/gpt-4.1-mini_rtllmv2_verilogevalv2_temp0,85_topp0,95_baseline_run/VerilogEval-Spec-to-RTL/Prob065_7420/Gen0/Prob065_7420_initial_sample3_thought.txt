The 7420 chip has two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate takes four inputs and produces one output which is the NAND of the inputs. We will map p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d as inputs to the first NAND gate with output p1y, and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d as inputs to the second NAND gate with output p2y. The implementation will directly assign the NAND of inputs to outputs.
