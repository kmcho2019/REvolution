The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate outputs the negation of the AND of its four inputs. To implement this, I will create a TopModule with inputs p1a to p1d for the first NAND gate, and p2a to p2d for the second NAND gate. The outputs p1y and p2y will be the NAND results of their respective inputs.
