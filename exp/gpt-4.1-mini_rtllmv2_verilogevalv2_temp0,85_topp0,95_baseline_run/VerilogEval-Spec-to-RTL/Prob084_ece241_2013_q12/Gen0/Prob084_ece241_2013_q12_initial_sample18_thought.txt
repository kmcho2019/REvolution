Implement an 8-bit shift register with synchronous enable. On each clock edge, if enable is high, shift the bits right and shift in S at Q[0]. The outputs of the 8 flip-flops are stored in a vector Q[7:0]. The output Z is selected by a 3-to-8 multiplexer controlled by inputs A,B,C as a 3-bit address to select which Q bit to output.
