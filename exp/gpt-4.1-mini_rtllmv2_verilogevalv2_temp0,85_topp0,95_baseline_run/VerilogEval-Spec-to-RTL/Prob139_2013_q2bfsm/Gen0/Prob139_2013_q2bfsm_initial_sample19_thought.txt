Design an FSM with the following states to implement the described behavior:
- A: Initial state (after reset), f=0, g=0
- B: After reset de-asserted, output f=1 for one clock cycle
- C0: Waiting for x sequence 1 (first bit)
- C1: Waiting for x sequence 0 (second bit)
- C2: Waiting for x sequence 1 (third bit)
- D: g=1 after detecting x sequence, start monitoring y for up to 2 cycles
- E: g=1 permanently (y detected within 2 cycles)
- F: g=0 permanently (y not detected within 2 cycles)

On reset (resetn=0), go to A.
After resetn=1, go to B (f=1 one cycle).
Then monitor x for 1,0,1 sequence across three cycles.
Upon sequence detection, set g=1 (state D).
In D, count up to two cycles waiting for y=1.
If y=1 within two cycles, go to E (g=1 permanent).
Else go to F (g=0 permanent).

Outputs f and g are registered outputs updated on clock edges.
