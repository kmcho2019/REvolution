The module has a 1-bit state stored in a flip-flop updated on the clock's positive edge. The output q depends combinationally on inputs a, b, and the current state. From the waveform, q often matches b when state is 0, and q often matches a when state is 1, but with exceptions. Checking transitions, the state toggles to 1 when (a=0,b=1,state=0), resets to 0 when (a=1,b=1,state=0), and when state=1, q equals state except when (a=1,b=0) where q=0.

Summarizing logic:

- Next state = (state & ~(a & b)) | (~state & ~a & b)
- q = (~state & b) | (state & a & ~b) | (state & ~a & ~b) is not consistent, but from waveforms q = (b & ~state) | (state & a & ~b) approximately.
- Actually, from waveforms, q = (state & a & ~b) | (~state & b) fits the data.

Implement a flip-flop holding state, update it as above on posedge clk. q is combinational from a, b, and state as above.

Output 'state' is the flip-flop output.
