From the waveform, the flip-flop state updates on the positive clock edge. The output q depends on the previous state and inputs a and b. Observing state and q changes:

- state is the flip-flop output.
- q is combinational output derived from current inputs and the stored state.

From waveform:

- At 15ns (clk rising), state goes to 0.
- At 45ns (clk rising), a=0, b=1, state=0, q=1
- At 65ns, a=1, b=1, state=0, q=0
- At 75ns, a=0, b=0, state=1, q=1
- At 95ns, a=1, b=1, state=1, q=1

We can deduce:

- The next state depends on current a, b, and state.
- q seems to be equal to the next state.

Finding a logic equation for next state:

By analyzing transitions, next state = (state & ~a & ~b) | (~state & b) | (a & ~b)

Simplify:

- state is retained if a=0 and b=0 (state & ~a & ~b)
- If b=1 and state=0, next state=1 (~state & b)
- If a=1 and b=0, next state=1 (a & ~b)

Output q = next state.

Implement a flip-flop storing state, update on posedge clk, and combinational logic for q as next state.
