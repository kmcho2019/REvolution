From the wave:
- The initial state is unknown.
- When b=1 and a=0 with state=0, q=1 and state stays 0.
- When b=1 and a=1 with state=0, q=0 and state stays 0.
- When state=1 and b=1 and a=1, q=1 and state=1.

Summarizing:

- q = (state & a) | (b & ~state)
