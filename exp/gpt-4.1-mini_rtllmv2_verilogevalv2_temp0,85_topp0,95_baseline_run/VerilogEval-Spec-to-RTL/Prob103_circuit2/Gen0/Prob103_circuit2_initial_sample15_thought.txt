Analyze the given truth table and derive the logic equation for q based on inputs a, b, c, d.
By mapping the output q for each input combination, q = (~a & ~b & ~c & ~d) 
+ (~a & ~b & c & d) + (~a & b & c & ~d) + (~a & b & ~c & d)
