Implement a 3-state Mealy FSM to detect the sequence "101" on input x, asserting output z when the last bit of the sequence is detected. The FSM is asynchronously reset on the negative edge of aresetn. The states represent how much of the sequence "101" has been matched so far, allowing overlap detection. The FSM transitions and output z are determined by current state and input x. The reset initializes to state S0. The FSM is clocked on the positive edge of clk, and reset is asynchronously triggered on the negative edge of aresetn.
