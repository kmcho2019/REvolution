Implement a 3-state Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "101" on input x. The FSM will have states representing how much of the sequence has been matched so far: 
- S0: no match yet,
- S1: matched '1',
- S2: matched '10'.

The output z is asserted when the next input completes the sequence "101" (i.e., when in S2 and x=1). The FSM uses a negative edge asynchronous reset (aresetn) to reset to S0. The sequential logic triggers on the positive edge of clk, and the state transitions and output are implemented in a combinational always block. The FSM also supports overlapping sequences.
