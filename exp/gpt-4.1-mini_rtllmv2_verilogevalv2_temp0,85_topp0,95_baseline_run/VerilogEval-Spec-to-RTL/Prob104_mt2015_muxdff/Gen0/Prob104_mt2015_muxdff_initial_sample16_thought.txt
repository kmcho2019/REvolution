The TopModule will implement a single bit flip-flop with synchronous load functionality. 
Inputs are clk, L (load enable), q_in (data input), r_in (load data). 
Output Q is the stored bit. 
If L is high on clk's rising edge, Q loads r_in; else Q loads q_in. 
This matches the behavior needed to instantiate 3 times in full_module for each bit of q.
