The TopModule will implement one bit of the three-bit register with load enable (L), clock (clk), and asynchronous inputs q_in (current q bit) and r_in (corresponding r bit). Inside, a 2:1 multiplexer selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and the selected value is stored in a D flip-flop triggered on the positive edge of clk. The output Q is the stored bit. This matches the behavior needed to factor full_module into three instances of TopModule.
