The TopModule should implement one bit of the 3-bit register in full_module. It has a 2:1 multiplexer that selects between loading the input bit r_in when L is asserted or shifting the current bit Q otherwise. The flip-flop captures the output of the mux on the rising edge of clk. The mux inputs are q_in (shifted bit from other flip-flop) and r_in (the load bit), controlled by L. The flip-flop output is Q.
