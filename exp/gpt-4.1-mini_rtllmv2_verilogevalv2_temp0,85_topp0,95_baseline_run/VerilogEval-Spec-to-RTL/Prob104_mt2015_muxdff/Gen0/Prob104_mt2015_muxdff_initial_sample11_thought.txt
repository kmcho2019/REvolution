The TopModule will be a 1-bit register with a synchronous load. It takes inputs clk, L (load enable), q_in (data input from the feedback logic), and r_in (data input from the load vector). The output Q is the stored bit. On the positive edge of clk, if L is high, Q loads r_in, else it loads q_in. This implements one flip-flop with synchronous load controlled by L. The full_module can instantiate three such TopModules for the three bits of q.
