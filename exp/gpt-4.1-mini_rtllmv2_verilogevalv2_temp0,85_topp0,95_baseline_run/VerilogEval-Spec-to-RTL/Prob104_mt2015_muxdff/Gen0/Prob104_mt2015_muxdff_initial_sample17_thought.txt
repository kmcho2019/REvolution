Implement TopModule as a single-bit storage element with synchronous load enable. It has inputs clk, L (load enable), q_in (data input), r_in (load data input), and output Q (stored bit). When L is high at the rising edge of clk, the output Q loads r_in; otherwise, it loads q_in. This effectively combines a 2:1 multiplexer controlled by L, and a D flip-flop clocked by clk.
