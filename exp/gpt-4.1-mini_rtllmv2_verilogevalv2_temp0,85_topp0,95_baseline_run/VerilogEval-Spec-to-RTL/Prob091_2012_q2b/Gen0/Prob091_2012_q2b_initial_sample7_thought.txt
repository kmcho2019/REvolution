The FSM has 6 states encoded one-hot in y[5:0] as:
A=000001, B=000010, C=000100, D=001000, E=010000, F=100000.

We need to determine the next state inputs for flip-flops y[1] (B) and y[3] (D),
which correspond to states B and D respectively.

From the FSM transition table and the one-hot encoding:
- Next state B (y[1]) is active when:
  - From A (y[0]) with input 1: A(0) --1--> B
  - From y[1] itself? No self-transition to B.
  - From other states? No other transitions to B.
  Therefore: Y1 = y[0] & w

- Next state D (y[3]) is active when:
  - From B (y[1]) with input 0: B(0) --0--> D
  - From C (y[2]) with input 0: C(0) --0--> D
  - From E (y[4]) with input 0: E(1) --0--> D (note E's output is 1, but transition depends on input w=0)
  - From F (y[5]) with input 0: F(1) --0--> D
  Therefore:
    Y3 = (y[1] & ~w) | (y[2] & ~w) | (y[4] & ~w) | (y[5] & ~w)
       = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

Note: y[0] = y[0], y[1] = y[1], etc., consistent with y[5:0].

Hence,
Y1 = y[0] & w
Y3 = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])
