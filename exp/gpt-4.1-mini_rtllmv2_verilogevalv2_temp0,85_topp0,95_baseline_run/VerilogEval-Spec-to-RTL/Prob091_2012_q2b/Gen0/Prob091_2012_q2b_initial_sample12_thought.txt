The FSM is one-hot encoded in y[5:0] for states A-F. The outputs Y1 and Y3 correspond to the inputs of flip-flops y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D).

From the transitions, the input for each flip-flop is the OR of the conditions that cause the FSM to enter that state on the next clock cycle.

For Y1 (flip-flop y[1], state B):
Transitions to B are:
- A --1--> B
So, Y1 = y[0] & w

For Y3 (flip-flop y[3], state D):
Transitions to D are:
- B --0--> D
- C --0--> D
- E --0--> D
- F --0--> D
So, Y3 = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

We'll implement these output logic equations combinationally.
