The FSM has six states represented by a one-hot encoding on y[5:0]. The input w controls transitions. The outputs Y1 and Y3 are the inputs to state flip-flops y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D).

We determine Y1 and Y3 by examining all transitions leading to states B and D:

- Y1 (input to y[1], state B) is high if the next state is B.
  From transitions:
    - A --1--> B  : when y=A (y[0]=1) and w=1
  So, Y1 = y[0] & w

- Y3 (input to y[3], state D) is high if next state is D.
  From transitions:
    - B --0--> D : y[1]=1 & ~w
    - C --0--> D : y[2]=1 & ~w
    - E --0--> D : y[4]=1 & ~w
    - F --0--> D : y[5]=1 & ~w
  So, Y3 = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

Implementing these as combinational logic completes the task.
