The module has a 6-bit one-hot encoded state input y and a 1-bit input w.
We must implement the next-state inputs for the flip-flops corresponding to y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D).

From the FSM transitions and one-hot encoding:
- y[1] corresponds to state B
- y[3] corresponds to state D

To derive Y1 (next input for y[1]):

Transitions to B:
- From A (y[0]) with input=1 → B
- From F (y[5]) with input=1 → C (not B)
No other transitions to B, so:
Y1 = (y[0] & 1) when w=1, but 1 means w=1
So Y1 = y[0] & w

To derive Y3 (next input for y[3]):

Transitions to D (y[3]):
- From B (y[1]) with input=0 → D
- From C (y[2]) with input=0 → D
- From E (y[4]) with input=0 → D
- From F (y[5]) with input=0 → D

So Y3 = (y[1] & ~w) | (y[2] & ~w) | (y[4] & ~w) | (y[5] & ~w)

Simplify Y3:
Y3 = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

Hence, the outputs Y1 and Y3 are combinational logic derived by inspection of FSM transitions and one-hot coding.
