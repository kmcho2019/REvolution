The FSM is one-hot encoded with states A to F represented as y[0] to y[5]. The transitions are given for each state with input w=0 or w=1.

We need to derive the next-state logic inputs for y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D), i.e., Y1 and Y3.

From the transitions:

- Transitions to B (y[1]):
  - From A on w=1: A (y[0]) --1--> B (y[1])
  - From F on w=1: F (y[5]) --1--> C (y[2]), so no B here.
  So only from A with w=1.

- Transitions to D (y[3]):
  - From B on w=0: B (y[1]) --0--> D (y[3])
  - From C on w=0: C (y[2]) --0--> D (y[3])
  - From E on w=0: E (y[4]) --0--> D (y[3])
  - From F on w=0: F (y[5]) --0--> D (y[3])

So next-state logic:

Y1 = (y[0] & w)

Y3 = (y[1] & ~w) | (y[2] & ~w) | (y[4] & ~w) | (y[5] & ~w)

Implement these equations directly.

Note: y[3] is the one-hot bit for state D.
