The FSM uses one-hot encoding for states A-F mapped to y[5:0]. The outputs Y1 and Y3 correspond to the inputs of the flip-flops y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D) next state inputs.

By inspecting the state transitions:

- To go to B (y[1]) next state:
  From A on input 1: A(0) --1--> B(1)
  From no other state transitions lead to B.

Thus, Y1 = (y[0] & w)  // y[0] is A, w is input

- To go to D (y[3]) next state:
  From B on 0: B(1) --0--> D(3)
  From C on 0: C(2) --0--> D(3)
  From E on 0: E(4) --0--> D(3)
  From F on 0: F(5) --0--> D(3)

So, Y3 = (y[1] & ~w) | (y[2] & ~w) | (y[4] & ~w) | (y[5] & ~w)
     = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])
