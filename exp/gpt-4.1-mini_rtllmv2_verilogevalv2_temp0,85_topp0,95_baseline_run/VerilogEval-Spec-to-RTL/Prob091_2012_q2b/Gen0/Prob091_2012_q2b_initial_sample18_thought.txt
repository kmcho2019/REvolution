The FSM uses a one-hot encoding for states with y[0] for A, y[1] for B, y[2] for C, y[3] for D, y[4] for E, y[5] for F.

We want to derive Y1 and Y3 which are the inputs to the flip-flops for states B (y[1]) and D (y[3]) respectively.

From the state transition table:

- Transitions to B (y[1]):
  - From A (y[0]) on input w=1: A->B
  - From F (y[5]) on w=1: F->C (not B)
  - From other states no transitions to B.
  So only from A on w=1.

Y1 = (y[0] & w)

- Transitions to D (y[3]):
  - From B (y[1]) on w=0: B->D
  - From C (y[2]) on w=0: C->D
  - From E (y[4]) on w=0: E->D
  - From F (y[5]) on w=0: F->D
  So D is reached from states B, C, E, F on w=0.

Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w

Therefore:

Y1 = y[0] & w
Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w

These are the input signals for flip-flops y[1] and y[3] respectively.

The module will take inputs y (6-bit) and w (1-bit), and output Y1 and Y3 accordingly.
