The module implements the next-state logic for a one-hot encoded FSM with states A-F represented by bits in y[5:0]. Each state transitions depend on input w. The output Y1 and Y3 are the inputs to flip-flops y[1] and y[3] respectively, meaning they represent the next-state values for states B and D.

By inspecting the FSM transitions:

- Y1 (next input to y[1], state B):
  B is entered from A on w=1.
  So Y1 = A & w = y[0] & w

- Y3 (next input to y[3], state D):
  D is entered from B on w=0: B & ~w = y[1] & ~w
  D is entered from C on w=0: C & ~w = y[2] & ~w
  D is entered from E on w=0: E & ~w = y[4] & ~w
  D is entered from F on w=0: F & ~w = y[5] & ~w
  So Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w

This logic directly produces the next state inputs for B and D.
