Implement a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling left, falling right, digging left, digging right, and splattered. The walking states track direction, the falling states track direction and count fall cycles, the digging states track direction and continue digging until ground=0, then transition to falling. On bumps during walking, the direction switches. Falling duration is counted; if falling longer than 20 cycles and ground returns, transition to splattered. areset asynchronously resets to walking left. Outputs are derived purely from the current state (Moore). Priority: falling > digging > switching directions.
