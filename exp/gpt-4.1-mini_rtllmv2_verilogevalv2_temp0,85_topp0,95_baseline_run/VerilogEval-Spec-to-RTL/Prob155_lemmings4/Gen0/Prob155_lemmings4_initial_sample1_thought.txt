Implement a Moore FSM with states encoding walking direction (left/right), falling, digging, and splattered (dead). The walking states are split into walk_left and walk_right. When ground=0 and not digging, transition to falling state (keep track of direction). Falling increments a fall counter each cycle; if fall counter > 20 upon hitting ground, transition to splattered state. When ground returns during falling, return to previous walking direction. If dig=1 while walking and ground=1, start digging in current direction. Digging continues until ground=0, then transition to falling. Bumps cause direction switch only when walking and not digging or falling, bumps have no effect in falling or digging. Outputs reflect state: walk_left/walk_right in walking; aaah in falling; digging in digging; all zero in splattered. Use async posedge areset to reset to walk_left state and zero fall counter.
