Implement a Moore FSM with states encoding walking direction, falling, digging, and splattered. Outputs depend only on states. The direction (walk left or right) is stored in the state. Handle transitions on clock rising edge or async reset (areset). Priority of events on walking: if ground=0, transition to falling; else if dig=1, transition to digging; else check bump signals to switch walking direction. When falling, count cycles; if count > 20 when ground returns, go to splattered. Digging continues until ground=0, then transition to falling. While falling or digging, bumps and dig inputs have no effect. Outputs are driven by current state. On reset, start walking left.
