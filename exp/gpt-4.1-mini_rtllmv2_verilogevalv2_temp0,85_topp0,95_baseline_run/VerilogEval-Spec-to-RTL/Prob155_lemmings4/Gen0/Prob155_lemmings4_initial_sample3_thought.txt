Model the Lemming behavior with a Moore FSM with these main states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING, and SPLATTERED. Walking states hold the direction. On bump signals, while walking and on ground, switch directions. Falling occurs when ground=0; while falling, the falling counter increments each cycle. If falling ends (ground=1) and the counter > 20, transition to SPLATTERED state. Digging starts only if dig=1 while walking on ground and continues until ground=0, then transitions to FALLING with the original walking direction remembered. Outputs are based on the current state only. Asynchronous reset sets the FSM to WALK_LEFT. The FSM handles precedence: falling > digging > bump direction switch. Bump signals have no effect in falling or digging states. SPLATTERED state outputs all zeros permanently until reset.
