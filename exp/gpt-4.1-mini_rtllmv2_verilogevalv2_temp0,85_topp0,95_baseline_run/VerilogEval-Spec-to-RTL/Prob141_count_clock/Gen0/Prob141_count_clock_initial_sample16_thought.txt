Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters increment seconds on each ena pulse. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 0 and increment minutes; similarly for minutes to hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD with special wrap-around from 12 to 1. The pm output toggles after 11:59:59 increments to 12:00:00 (i.e., at hour wrap). On synchronous reset, set time to 12:00 AM (hh=12 BCD, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). All counters are updated on the positive edge of clk, considering reset and ena as described.
