Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. Use BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. On each 'ena' pulse, increment seconds. When seconds reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment minutes. Similarly for minutes rolling over to hours. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When hours roll from 11 to 12, toggle PM. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). All counters are updated on the positive edge of clk with synchronous reset. The outputs are the BCD representations of hours, minutes, seconds, and the pm flag.
