Implement a 12-hour BCD clock with separate BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Use the ena pulse to increment the seconds counter once per second. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 00 and increment minutes. Similarly, when minutes reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12. When hours increment past 12, reset to 1 and toggle the pm bit. Reset synchronously sets the clock to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). All counters are updated on the positive edge of clk with synchronous reset and only when ena is asserted (except reset which has highest priority).
