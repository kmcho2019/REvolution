Implement a 12-hour clock with separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, all driven by a fast clk but incrementing only when ena pulses once per second. The seconds and minutes counters count from 00 to 59 in BCD, rolling over to the next digit and triggering minute/hour increments accordingly. The hour counter counts 1-12 in BCD with proper rollover, and the pm bit toggles at 12:00 (noon and midnight). Reset synchronously sets time to 12:00 AM (hours=12, minutes=00, seconds=00, pm=0). All counting and transitions happen on positive edge of clk.
