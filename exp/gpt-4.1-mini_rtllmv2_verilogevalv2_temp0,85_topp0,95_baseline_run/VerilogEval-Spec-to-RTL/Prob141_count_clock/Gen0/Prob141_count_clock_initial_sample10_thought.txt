Implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator using BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The clock increments once per second when ena is asserted. On synchronous reset, set time to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, rolling over properly. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD, incrementing and toggling pm at rollover from 11:59:59 to 12:00:00 and from 12:59:59 to 1:00:00 accordingly. All counters use separate BCD digits, and increment logic handles BCD digit rollovers.
