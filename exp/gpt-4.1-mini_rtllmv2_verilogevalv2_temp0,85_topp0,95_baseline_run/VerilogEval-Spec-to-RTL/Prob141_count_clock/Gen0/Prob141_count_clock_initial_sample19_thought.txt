Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, and a PM indicator. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). On each pulse of ena, increment seconds by 1. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 00 and increment minutes. Similarly, when minutes reach 59 and increment, reset minutes to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD. When hours increment from 11 to 12, toggle the pm bit. When hours increment from 12, wrap to 1. Use two BCD digits per hh, mm, ss, output as 8-bit values (tens in bits [7:4], units in bits [3:0]). All operations synchronous on posedge clk with synchronous reset. Reset has priority over ena.
