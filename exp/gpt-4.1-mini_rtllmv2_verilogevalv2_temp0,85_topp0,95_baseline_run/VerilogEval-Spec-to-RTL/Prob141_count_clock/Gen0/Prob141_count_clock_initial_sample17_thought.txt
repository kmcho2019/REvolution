Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. On each positive edge of clk, if reset is asserted, reset all counters to 12:00:00 AM. If enable is asserted and not reset, increment seconds BCD counter. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset seconds to 00 and increment minutes. When minutes reach 59 and increment, reset minutes to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD, and wrap to 1 after 12. The pm signal toggles when hours wrap from 11 to 12 (i.e., when hour increments from 11 to 12, pm toggles). Implement proper BCD increment logic for seconds, minutes, and hours. Output hh, mm, ss are 8-bit buses with two BCD digits each.
