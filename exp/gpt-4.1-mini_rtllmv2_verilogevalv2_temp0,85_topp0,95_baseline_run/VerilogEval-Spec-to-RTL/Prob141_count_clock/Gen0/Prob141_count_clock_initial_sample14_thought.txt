Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. On each clock pulse, if reset is high, reset time to 12:00 AM. Otherwise, on ena pulse, increment seconds BCD counter (00 to 59), rolling over to 00 and incrementing minutes. Minutes increment similarly, rolling over at 59 and incrementing hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD with special rollover logic. Keep track of AM/PM state with a pm bit toggled when hour rolls from 11 to 12. Outputs are pm (1 for PM, 0 for AM) and BCD hour, minute, second values on 8-bit outputs (two BCD digits each).
