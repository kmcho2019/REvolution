However, the simulation failures show widespread mismatches on 'pm', 'hh', and 'mm', highlighting issues in the time incrementation logic beyond seconds.

Key problematic areas:

1. **Hour Increment Logic in `inc_hour_12` Function:**

   - The function attempts to increment a 12-hour BCD hour counter, rolling over from 12 to 01 with a carry that should toggle 'pm'.
   - The rollover condition uses:
     ```verilog
     if ((new_hour[7:4] > 4'd1) || (new_hour > 8'd12) || (new_hour == 8'd00)) 
     ```
     This mix of bitwise comparison for the tens digit (BCD) and decimal comparisons of an 8-bit vector causes ambiguity and incorrect behavior.
   - The comparison `(new_hour > 8'd12)` treats the BCD hour as a pure 8-bit binary value rather than as two separate BCD digits, which leads to incorrect rollover detection.
   - The use of `8'd` decimal values to compare BCD digits is incorrect and results in invalid hour values, causing erroneous resets and carry signals.
   - Because of this, the hour counter likely fails to increment correctly between "01" and "12" and may skip values or reset unexpectedly, causing mismatches in 'hh'.

2. **Minute Increment Logic in `bcd_inc60` Function:**

   - The function increments two-digit BCD minutes from 00 to 59 with a carry beyond 59.
   - Inside `bcd_inc60`, after incrementing the low digit, it increments the high digit if the low digit overflows. Then, if the high digit exceeds 5, it resets to 0 and generates a carry out.
   - However, the condition uses `if (high > 4'd5)` to detect overflow, which might be off by one if the high digit is exactly 5 and the low digit is 9.
   - This might cause incorrect carry generation and minute rollovers to fail or behave inconsistently, leading to mismatches in 'mm'.

3. **Mixing Binary and BCD Arithmetic:**

   - Several comparisons in the code treat BCD-encoded values as binary numbers, leading to erroneous conditions.
   - For example, in `inc_hour_12`, the condition `(new_hour > 8'd12)` is invalid for BCD since '12' in BCD is `0001_0010` (decimal 18 in binary).
   - This likely results in incorrect rollover detection, causing the hour counter to malfunction.

4. **Register Declarations Inside Always Block:**

   - The code declares local regs such as `sec_inc`, `min_inc`, `hr_inc`, and rollover flags inside the always block, which is syntactically legal in SystemVerilog but may cause synthesis or simulation tools to interpret them unexpectedly if targeting pure Verilog-2001.
   - While this might not cause the simulation failures observed, it is a potential source of confusion.

5. **AM/PM Toggle Logic:**

   - The pm flip is toggled only when there is an hour rollover detected via `rollover_hr`.
   - Since the hour rollover signal is misgenerated due to the flawed hour increment logic, the pm signal will not toggle correctly, producing many mismatches.
