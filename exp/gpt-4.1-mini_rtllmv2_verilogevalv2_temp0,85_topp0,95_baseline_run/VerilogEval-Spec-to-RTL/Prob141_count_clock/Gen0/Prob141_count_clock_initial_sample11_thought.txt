Implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator using BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. On each ena pulse (once per second), increment the seconds counter. When seconds reach 59 and wrap, increment minutes; when minutes reach 59 and wrap, increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD. The pm flag toggles when the hour rolls from 11 to 12. Synchronous active-high reset sets time to 12:00 AM. All counters are updated on the positive clock edge, with reset having highest priority.
