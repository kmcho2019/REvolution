Implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator using BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters increment only when ena is high on the positive edge of clk. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, incrementing in BCD. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment minutes. Similarly for minutes to hours. Toggle the pm bit when hours roll from 11 to 12 (indicating the transition between AM and PM). Use synchronous reset with priority over enable.
