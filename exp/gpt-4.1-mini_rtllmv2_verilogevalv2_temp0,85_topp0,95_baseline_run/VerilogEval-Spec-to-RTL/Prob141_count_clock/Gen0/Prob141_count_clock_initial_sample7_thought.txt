Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The clock increments once per second indicated by the ena signal. On reset, set the time to 12:00:00 AM. Use synchronous logic triggered on the positive edge of clk. The seconds and minutes counters count from 00 to 59 in BCD (two 4-bit digits), and the hour counter counts from 01 to 12 in BCD. The pm output indicates if it's PM (1) or AM (0). On increment, seconds increment, and on rollover, minutes increment, and so on. When hours roll from 11 to 12, toggle pm. After 12, hours roll back to 01.
