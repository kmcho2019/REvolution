Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each time the ena signal pulses (once per second), increment the seconds counter. When seconds reach 59 and roll over to 00, increment minutes. Similarly, when minutes roll over from 59 to 00, increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD. When hour rolls over from 12 to 1, toggle the pm indicator. On synchronous reset, set time to 12:00 AM. All counters are coded in BCD with proper rollovers.
