Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Use synchronous reset to set the clock to 12:00:00 AM. On each ena pulse, increment seconds; if seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes; if minutes roll over from 59 to 00, increment hours. Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD, with a PM flag toggled when hour rolls from 11 to 12. The outputs hh, mm, ss are 8-bit BCD with two digits each.
