Implement a 12-hour clock using BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters increment on the ena pulse, which represents one-second increments. Seconds count from 00 to 59, minutes count from 00 to 59, and hours count from 01 to 12. The pm output is a 1-bit signal indicating PM when asserted. Reset synchronously resets the time to 12:00 AM (hours = 12, minutes = 00, seconds = 00, pm = 0). The counters will roll over appropriately, toggling pm when the hour transitions from 11 to 12. The hh, mm, and ss outputs are 8-bit wide, with each two BCD digits (high nibble tens digit, low nibble units digit).
