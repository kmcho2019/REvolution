Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with a BCD representation for hours, minutes, and seconds. On each positive edge of clk, if reset is asserted, reset the time to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). Otherwise, if ena is asserted, increment seconds; when seconds reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment minutes. When minutes reach 59 and increment, reset to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD; when incrementing from 11, next is 12; after 12, wrap to 1 and toggle pm bit. All counters are in two BCD digits each (hh[7:4] and hh[3:0] for tens and units respectively). Ensure correct BCD incrementing and rollover behavior.
