Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The clock increments the seconds BCD counter on each pulse of ena. When seconds reach 59 and increment, it resets to 00 and increments the minutes BCD counter. Similarly, when minutes reach 59 and increment, it resets to 00 and increments the hours BCD counter. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD; after 12, it resets to 1 and toggles the pm flag. The reset synchronously sets the clock to 12:00 AM. The outputs hh, mm, and ss are 8-bit BCD representations (two digits each). The pm output indicates whether the clock is in PM (1) or AM (0).
