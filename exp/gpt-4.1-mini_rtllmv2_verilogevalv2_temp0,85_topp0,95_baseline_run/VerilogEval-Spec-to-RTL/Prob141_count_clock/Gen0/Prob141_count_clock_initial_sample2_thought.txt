Implement a 12-hour clock counter with BCD outputs for hours, minutes, and seconds. Use three separate counters: seconds, minutes, and hours. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD. The PM flag toggles when hours roll over from 11 to 12. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM (hours=12, minutes=0, seconds=0, pm=0). On each ena pulse, increment seconds; when seconds roll over, increment minutes; when minutes roll over, increment hours; when hours roll over from 12 to 1, toggle pm. Use synchronous reset, synchronous counters triggered on posedge clk.
