Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM. On each ena pulse, increment seconds in BCD, rolling over to minutes, then hours with correct 12-hour wrap-around. The hour counts from 1 to 12 in BCD, with 12 represented as 0x12 in BCD (binary 0001 0010). The pm flag toggles when hour rolls from 11 to 12. Use synchronous logic triggered on positive clk edge with reset having highest priority.
