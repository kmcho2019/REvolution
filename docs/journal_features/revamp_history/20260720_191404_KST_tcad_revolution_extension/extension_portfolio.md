## Recommended Journal Package

State the smallest coherent conference-to-journal delta supported by isolated
evidence. Separate:

- primary algorithmic contribution;
- supporting reliability or generalization contribution;
- evaluation and reproducibility improvements;
- negative findings retained as characterization;
- limitations and unresolved reviewer objections.

Do not fill this section by combining individually weak candidates. If no
minimal package qualifies, record `PORTFOLIO_READY` or `PIVOT_REQUIRED`.

Wave 1 produced no primary package. H5 is useful negative-map evidence that
role-aligned correction can increase direct repairs without preserving
per-seed valid-PPA coverage or establishing PPA causality. Historical no-C-F
remains retrospective characterization only, and hardened paired reporting is
an evaluation contribution rather than an algorithmic journal thesis.

Wave 2 retired H9 before spend and retired H10 after a complete two-seed suite.
H10's exact activation preserved coverage but did not broaden repair and
materially regressed continuous PPA surfaces. Its full evidence package passed
raw audit and byte-for-byte report reproduction. Nearby feedback variants are
barred because they add knobs or state without a distinct measured premise.

No third Wave-2 mechanism is opened. The remaining lanes are either already
tested-negative, directly occupied by related work, not cleanly isolatable, or
unsupported by fresh classic evidence. An unused wave slot is a ceiling, not a
quota. Discovery therefore closes with no `PAPER_CANDIDATE` and no `VIABLE`
candidate.

reproducible decisions, and negative portfolio are complete, but
`PORTFOLIO_READY` requires at least one `VIABLE` direction and none remains.

weaknesses remain useful future-work targets, but forcing another nearby card
