# H6 Candidate Decision

## Outcome

`HISTORICAL_SUPPORT`

## Decision Rationale

The no-C-F arm is a simple, natural subtraction of an unvalidated conference
component. It improves canonical mean HV-AUC and coverage over five historical
full-RTLLM seeds while mean final HV is above classic.

The final-HV confidence interval crosses zero, wins and losses are balanced,
and seed 1001 loses materially. More importantly, the current seed roles,
noninferiority margins, and AUC gate were frozen after these outcomes existed.
The result is retrospective support outside the candidate outcome state
machine, not a `VIABLE` prospective result, primary PPA improvement, or proof
that fusion is harmful.

## Allowed Claim

In the historical matched full-RTLLM test, removing C-F had higher mean final
HV, HV-AUC, and coverage, but the final-HV effect was mixed across seeds and
statistically unresolved. This is retrospective characterization only.
