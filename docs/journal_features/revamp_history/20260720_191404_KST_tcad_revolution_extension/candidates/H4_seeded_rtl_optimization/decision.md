# H4 Candidate Decision

## Outcome

`RETIRED`

Allowed outcomes: `PAPER_CANDIDATE`, `VIABLE`, `RETIRED`, `BLOCKED`.

## Decision Rationale

H4 was a contingent generalization stage, not an independently supported
algorithmic mechanism. Its prerequisite was a confirmed REvolution extension
whose behavior could then be tested on seeded RTL optimization. Both discovery
waves closed without a `VIABLE` or `PAPER_CANDIDATE` method, so no treatment
exists to generalize.

No code, benchmark role, admission event, model call, synthesis evaluation, or
live result was created for H4. `RETIRED` records the absent prerequisite; it is
not evidence that seeded RTL optimization is infeasible or unimportant.

## Allowed Claim And Paper Role

H4 contributes no manuscript claim. A future program may study seeded RTL
optimization after freezing a distinct method, baseline, task contract, and
equivalence-aware PPA endpoint. It must not relabel this unrun card as negative
experimental evidence.
