## Probe step (before any live spend)

Run the descriptor probe (June-25 pattern) for every arm profile:
resolve axes with `requires_ppa=false`, confirm extraction on a sample
of existing V2-anchor candidates, confirm archive mechanics
(grid-quantile binning for 3-D arms; CVT centroid init for 8-D), and
record the probe JSONs under this lane before launch. Any axis that
fails to resolve or collapses on the probe sample kills that arm before
spend.
