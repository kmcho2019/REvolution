# Cover Letter To Senior Advisor

We need a direction decision for the TCAD extension of the ASP-DAC 2026
REvolution paper.

The recent natural-QD campaign was extensive and methodologically useful. It
corrected an operator-contamination problem in earlier QD experiments, ran
operator-fair full-RTLLM variants, and found a five-seed near miss in S07.
However, no QD/MAP-Elites variant beat classic REvolution on final PPA
hypervolume over the full 46-design reference-complete RTLLM suite. S07
improves HV-AUC and coverage but reaches only 98.7% of classic final HV.

We therefore do not recommend presenting S07 as the main journal win. The
choice is now between:

1. writing a carefully scoped characterization paper from the current QD
   evidence; or
2. reopening the method around a simpler, evidence-derived extension with a
   better chance of improving final HV and larger-design functionality.

Our recommendation is the second path, with strict limits. First isolate
descriptor-free Pareto selection inside the conference REvolution loop. If it
is competitive, extend it to a verified-RTL-seeded optimization mode for
larger modules. Keep the QD campaign as the analysis that explains why the
simpler design was chosen.

We ask the advisor to judge four points:

- Is descriptor-free Pareto REvolution a sufficient and natural journal
  method when paired with the existing QD negative map and operator
  ablations?
- Is verified-RTL-seeded optimization a legitimate extension of an RTL
  generation paper, or does it change the problem too far?
- Which result threshold would make the extension TCAD-worthy rather than
  merely publishable?
- Should the current QD characterization be a central contribution,
  supporting ablation, or appendix material?

The bundle foregrounds negative evidence and known measurement limitations.
It is intended for a critical direction review, not for approval of a
preselected method.
