## Review

- Strength: visually polished and easy to parse at a glance.
- Weakness: less rigorous than the manual metric figure; arrows and shaded area
  may invite questions about exact HV geometry.
- Recommendation: keep the manual figure for metric definition slides. Use this
  only as a decorative or intuition-building visual.
